# F4 Religious & Ceremonial Objects: the decided tree (kind-level test)

Category F4 holds objects made for religious or spiritual practice and for the dead: what is set up to be venerated, what a practitioner holds or carries to pray, what is handled in a rite, what is lit, burned or set out, what is read or cast in divination, what holds or marks the dead, and parts made for one of these objects. Religious dress, worn jewellery, published books, art made as art and food and drink as eaten are in other categories.

Levels: category, sub-family, leaf, kind. Answer with the kind id of the one kind that fits the object best.

Tradition or sect (Christian, Jewish, Buddhist, Hindu, Shinto, Muslim, neopagan and others), occasion, life-cycle stage, the figure shown, material, size, capacity, installation, power source, user (adult, child, pet), region, era, maker and price never decide the kind. A named form (a tradition's own name for a kind, a style, a published design) sits below the kind and is not part of this test.

---

## F4.1 Altars, shrines and sacred images

What is set up to be venerated. Leaf axis: the form of the sacred focus (altar or shrine; flat image; three-dimensional image; relic and its container).

### F4.1.1 Altars and shrines
- F4.1.1.01 Household altar or shrine: cabinet, shelf or box shrine kept in a home or room to hold sacred images, name tablets or talismans.
- F4.1.1.02 Shrine boundary rope: rope hung to mark a sacred space or object.

### F4.1.2 Flat images
- F4.1.2.01 Devotional panel painting: sacred picture painted on a rigid panel, framed or mounted to stand or hang.
- F4.1.2.02 Devotional scroll painting: sacred picture painted or printed on cloth or paper and mounted as a hanging scroll or rolled for hanging.
- F4.1.2.03 Altarpiece: large sacred picture or carved screen made to stand behind or above an altar.

### F4.1.3 Three-dimensional images
- F4.1.3.01 Devotional statue: sculpted figure of a holy person, deity or saint made to be venerated; the figure is not a kind.
- F4.1.3.02 Devotional cross: cross, with or without a figure on it, made to stand, hang or be held in veneration.
- F4.1.3.03 Nativity set: boxed or composed set of figures and a stable that shows the birth of Jesus, sold and compared as one set.

### F4.1.4 Relics and reliquaries
- F4.1.4.01 Relic: body part or belonging of a holy person, or a thing touched to one, kept for veneration.
- F4.1.4.02 Reliquary: case or container made to hold and show a relic.

## F4.2 Personal devotion aids

What the practitioner holds, counts, kneels on or carries to pray. Leaf axis: how the aid is used (counted; knelt or sat on; turned or flown; held, carried or used to bless).

### F4.2.1 Counted
- F4.2.1.01 Prayer beads: string or loop of beads, knots or counters made to count prayers, with or without a cross, tassel or guide bead.

### F4.2.2 Knelt or sat on
- F4.2.2.01 Prayer rug: rug or mat made to kneel, stand or prostrate on in prayer.

### F4.2.3 Turned or flown
- F4.2.3.01 Prayer wheel: cylinder holding written prayers, turned by hand, wind or water.
- F4.2.3.02 Prayer flag: cloth flag printed with prayers or sacred images, hung to be blown by the wind.

### F4.2.4 Held, carried or used to bless
- F4.2.4.01 Devotional medal: loose medal of a saint or sacred subject made for devotion.
- F4.2.4.02 Prayer card: printed card with a sacred image or prayer, kept or carried for devotion.
- F4.2.4.03 Holy-water font: small basin for holy water, hung or stood by a door or in a room.
- F4.2.4.04 Holy-water bottle: bottle or flask made to hold blessed water.
- F4.2.4.05 Carried protective charm: small charm carried (not worn as jewellery) for luck, protection or a named blessing.

---

## F4.3 Ritual vessels, implements and instruments

What is handled in the rite. Leaf axis: how the object is handled in the rite (vessel; hand implement; text-bearing object; sound-maker).

### F4.3.1 Vessels
- F4.3.1.01 Ritual cup: cup or goblet made to hold wine or another drink used in a rite.
- F4.3.1.02 Monstrance: display vessel with a window or frame, made to show a consecrated host.
- F4.3.1.03 Ritual food plate: plate or tray made to hold the symbolic foods of a rite.

### F4.3.2 Hand implements
- F4.3.2.01 Aspergillum: sprinkler for holy water.
- F4.3.2.02 Torah pointer: slim rod ending in a pointing hand, held to follow the text when reading a scroll aloud.
- F4.3.2.03 Ritual sceptre: short sceptre held in the hand in a rite.
- F4.3.2.04 Ritual knife: knife or blade made to be held in a rite, not to cut.

### F4.3.3 Text-bearing objects
- F4.3.3.01 Doorpost scroll case: case made to hold a doorpost scroll and be fixed to a doorpost.
- F4.3.3.02 Doorpost scroll: handwritten parchment strip of sacred text made to be put in a case and fixed to a doorpost.
- F4.3.3.03 Phylacteries: pair of small boxes holding handwritten sacred passages, with straps, made to be strapped to the arm and head in prayer.
- F4.3.3.04 Handwritten Torah scroll: handwritten scroll of the Five Books on parchment, made to be read in the rite.

### F4.3.4 Sound-makers
- F4.3.4.01 Ritual horn: horn or shell made to be blown in a rite.
- F4.3.4.02 Hand bell: bell held by a handle and rung in a rite or service.
- F4.3.4.03 Standing bowl bell: bowl-shaped bell that rests on a cushion or stand and is struck with a striker, made for a rite.
- F4.3.4.04 Slit drum: hollow wooden block struck to keep rhythm in chanting.

## F4.4 Light, incense and offerings

What is lit, burned or set out. Leaf axis: what is lit, burned or set out (lamp and candle; incense and its burner; offering vessel and stand).

### F4.4.1 Lamps and candles
- F4.4.1.01 Hanukkah lamp: nine-branch lamp or candle holder, eight places for the festival lights and one for the helper light.
- F4.4.1.02 Seven-branch menorah: seven-branch lamp or candle holder.
- F4.4.1.03 Oil lamp for worship: lamp burning oil or ghee, made for worship.
- F4.4.1.04 Eternal light: lamp kept burning before a sanctuary or altar.
- F4.4.1.05 Altar candlestick: candle holder made to stand on or by an altar in a rite.
- F4.4.1.06 Ritual candle: candle made for a rite or devotion, including altar, votive, procession, grave and memorial candles.
- F4.4.1.07 Advent wreath: wreath with candles used to count the weeks before Christmas.
- F4.4.1.08 Ritual lantern: lantern made to be hung or set out in a rite or as a shrine offering.

### F4.4.2 Incense and burners
- F4.4.2.01 Ritual incense: incense made for a rite, in stick, cone, resin or church grade.
- F4.4.2.02 Incense burner: standing or hanging vessel for burning incense.
- F4.4.2.03 Swung censer: incense burner hung on chains, to be swung.

### F4.4.3 Offering vessels and stands
- F4.4.3.01 Offering vessel: bowl, cup or vase made to hold water, rice, tea, flowers or other offerings.
- F4.4.3.02 Offering stand: stand or tray made to hold offerings.

## F4.5 Divination and esoteric tools

What is read or cast. Leaf axis: deck, or other tool.

### F4.5.1 Decks
- F4.5.1.01 Tarot deck: 78-card deck with major and minor suits, sold for divination.
- F4.5.1.02 Oracle deck: deck of cards with its own set of images and messages, any number of cards, sold for divination.
- F4.5.1.03 Lenormand deck: 36-card deck of the Lenormand pattern, sold for divination.

### F4.5.2 Other tools
- F4.5.2.01 Crystal ball: sphere used for scrying.
- F4.5.2.02 Spirit board: lettered board with a pointer, made for divination.
- F4.5.2.03 Rune set: set of stones, wood or tiles marked with runes, made for casting.
- F4.5.2.04 I Ching coin set: coins or sticks made for casting the I Ching.
- F4.5.2.05 Divination pendulum: weight on a chain or cord, made to be dangled for divination.

---

## F4.6 The dead

What holds the remains, marks the dead, or covers the body or coffin. Leaf axis: coffin; urn; marker; furnishing.

### F4.6.1 Coffins
- F4.6.1.01 Coffin or casket: box that holds a body for burial or cremation.

### F4.6.2 Urns
- F4.6.2.01 Cremation urn: container for cremated remains, of any capacity from a keepsake portion to a companion pair; not a worn piece of jewellery.
- F4.6.2.02 Scattering urn: container made to release ashes on land, water or from the air.
- F4.6.2.03 Urn vault: outer box or liner made to hold an urn in the ground.

### F4.6.3 Markers
- F4.6.3.01 Grave marker: stone or plaque set on or at a grave.
- F4.6.3.02 Memorial tablet: tablet that stands for a dead person, inscribed with a name, kept at an altar or home.

### F4.6.4 Furnishings
- F4.6.4.01 Funeral pall: cloth laid over a coffin.
- F4.6.4.02 Burial shroud: cloth or set of cloths that wrap the body.

---

## F4.7 Parts and accessories made for one religious or funerary object

Leaf axis: the object served.

### F4.7.1 For Torah scrolls
- F4.7.1.01 Torah finial: ornament fitted to the top of a scroll's rollers.

### F4.7.2 For coffins
- F4.7.2.01 Coffin plate: plate fixed to a coffin, naming the dead.
- F4.7.2.02 Coffin pillow: pillow made for the inside of a coffin.

### F4.7.3 For cremation urns
- F4.7.3.01 Urn appliqué or medallion: plaque, appliqué or medallion made to be fixed to an urn.

## In-category precedence

- A part or accessory made for one F4 object goes to F4.7 first. A doorpost scroll case is not such a part: it is the object fixed in the rite and is F4.3.3.
- Otherwise test in this order: the dead (holds remains, marks the dead, covers the body or coffin), divination, light, incense and offerings (lit, burned or set out), sacred focus, rite implement, personal aid.
- A lit object is F4.4, not F4.1: a lamp or candle made for a rite is a lamp or candle even when it stands at an altar or marks a memorial; a candle made for a rite or devotion, grave lights included, is a ritual candle.
- An urn that holds ashes is F4.6 whatever its decoration; a keepsake urn that is not worn stays a cremation urn, with capacity as an attribute and not a kind.
- A set of objects of one kind is filed as that kind; a mixed set is filed by its item of greatest value; a kit sold, standardised and compared as one object is a kind (the nativity set).
- A bowl-shaped bell, drum or horn made for a rite is F4.3.4; the same instrument made for meditation or music is not in F4.
- A deck, board or set made for divination is F4.5; the same made for card play or a board game is not in F4.
- A scroll or case made to be fixed or worn in a rite is F4.3.3; a ritual implement that holds food or drink is F4.3.1 and the food itself is not in F4.
- A holder or case made for one object follows that object; a case made to hold and show a relic is a reliquary.
