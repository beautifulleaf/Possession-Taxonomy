# Shaping, sanding & finishing > Chisels, gouges & mortisers
- 068 · bench (striking) chisel — chisel made to be struck with a mallet, strong tang or socket and hooped or reinforced handle.
- 069 · paring chisel — long, thin chisel pushed by hand for fine paring.
- 070 · mortise chisel — thick, deep-bladed chisel struck to chop mortises.
- 071 · woodturning gouge — long-handled gouge for shaping wood on a lathe.
- 072 · mortiser — machine that cuts square mortises with a hollow chisel and auger.
