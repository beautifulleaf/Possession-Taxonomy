# Ladders, lifting & site machines > Earth-moving & site machines
- 181 · excavator — machine with a boom, arm and bucket on a swinging house, tracked or wheeled (mini to full size).
- 182 · skid steer loader — compact loader steered by skidding wheels or tracks, with a lift arm.
- 183 · telehandler — loader with a telescopic boom.
- 184 · forklift — truck that lifts loads on forks.
- 185 · dumper — machine with a tipping body for hauling material on site.
- 186 · plate compactor — vibrating plate machine that compacts soil and aggregate.
- 187 · cement mixer — drum machine that mixes cement, mortar or concrete.
- 188 · backhoe loader — wheeled machine with a front loader bucket and a rear digging arm.
