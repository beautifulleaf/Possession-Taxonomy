# Shaping, sanding & finishing > Routers & moulders
- 090 · router — power tool with a bit spinning in a base, for edges, grooves and recesses.
- 091 · spindle moulder — stationary machine with a vertical spindle and cutter for moulding profiles.
- 092 · CNC router — computer-controlled gantry machine that routes along programmed paths.
- 093 · biscuit jointer — hand-held power tool that plunges a small circular blade sideways to cut slots for joining biscuits.
