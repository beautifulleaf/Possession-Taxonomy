# Shaping, sanding & finishing > Lathes, mills & presses
- 094 · wood lathe — machine that turns timber between centres or on a chuck against hand-held tools.
- 095 · metal (engineering) lathe — machine that turns metal with a fixed cutting tool on a carriage.
- 096 · milling machine — machine that cuts metal with a rotating multi-tooth cutter against a movable table.
- 097 · reloading press — lever-operated press that holds dies to resize, prime and seat components of ammunition cartridges.
- 098 · combination lathe-mill — metal lathe with a milling head on a column over the bed, one machine for turning and milling.
