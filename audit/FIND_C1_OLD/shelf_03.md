# Cutting & sawing > Axes & splitting tools
- 032 · axe — long-handled tool with a bladed head for felling, hewing or splitting wood, of any pattern.
- 033 · splitting maul — heavy-headed tool with a wedge face on one side and a flat sledge face on the other, for splitting logs.
- 034 · splitting wedge — steel wedge driven into wood with a hammer or maul to split it.
- 035 · log splitter — machine (hydraulic or other power, not tractor-driven) that forces a log against a wedge.
- 036 · kindling splitter — cast-iron frame with a fixed upward blade, into which a small log is struck with a hammer to split kindling.
