# Ladders, lifting & site machines > Hand trucks, carts & pallet trucks
- 177 · sack truck [hand truck] — upright two-wheel carrier with a toe plate for tilting and moving loads.
- 178 · dolly — low platform carrier on wheels or casters.
- 179 · hand cart — hand-pushed multi-wheel cart or trolley for goods.
- 180 · pallet truck [pallet jack] — hand-pulled or powered low truck whose forks slide under a pallet and lift it just clear of the floor.
