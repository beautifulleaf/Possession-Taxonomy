# Measuring & testing > Test & inspection
- 152 · multimeter — instrument that measures several electrical quantities for trade and electronics work.
- 153 · detector [stud, pipe and cable detector] — handheld device that senses studs, metal pipes or live cables behind a surface.
- 154 · moisture meter — instrument that measures moisture in timber or building materials.
- 155 · infrared thermometer — handheld non-contact thermometer that reads surface temperature from infrared radiation.
- 156 · inspection camera [endoscope] — camera on a flexible probe with a screen, for looking inside walls, pipes and machines.
- 157 · sound level meter — instrument that measures sound pressure level in decibels.
- 158 · clamp meter — electrical meter whose jaws close round a conductor to measure current without breaking the circuit.
- 159 · oscilloscope — instrument that displays electrical signals as waveforms, handheld or bench (ScopeMeter is a Fluke name).
