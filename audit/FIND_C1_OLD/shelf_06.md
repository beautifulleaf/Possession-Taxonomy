# Drilling & driving > Spanners, wrenches & sockets
- 048 · combination spanner — fixed wrench with an open end and a ring end of the same size.
- 049 · adjustable wrench — wrench with a movable jaw set by a screw.
- 050 · socket wrench (ratchet) — ratcheting drive handle that turns interchangeable sockets; a socket set is filed as this kind.
- 051 · torque wrench — wrench that indicates or limits the tightening torque applied.
- 052 · impact wrench — power wrench with an impact mechanism and square drive for sockets and heavy fasteners.
- 053 · pipe wrench — heavy adjustable wrench with a hook jaw and serrated teeth that bite round pipe.
- 054 · basin wrench — long-shafted wrench with a pivoting jaw for tap and fitting nuts behind basins.
- 055 · box spanner — tubular spanner with hexagon ends, turned by a bar through a hole.
