# Drilling & driving > Drills
- 037 · brace — hand-cranked drill with a sweep handle and a chuck.
- 038 · drill/driver — hand-held rotary tool with chuck and clutch for drilling and driving screws.
- 039 · hammer drill — drill/driver with an added percussive mode and ordinary chuck, for masonry and concrete.
- 040 · rotary hammer — hand-held tool with an electro-pneumatic hammer mechanism and an SDS-type bit holder, for heavy drilling and chiselling in masonry.
- 041 · drill press [pillar drill] — drill on a column with a table and feed lever, bench or floor mounted.
- 042 · magnetic drill — portable drill press on an electromagnetic base that clamps to steel, usually with annular cutters.
- 043 · hand drill [wheel brace] — hand-cranked geared drill with a side wheel and a chuck, for small holes.
