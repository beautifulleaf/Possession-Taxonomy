# Drilling & driving > Screwdrivers & drivers
- 044 · screwdriver — hand tool with a handle and tip that turns screws.
- 045 · electric screwdriver — small powered driver for light screwdriving, without an impact mechanism.
- 046 · impact driver — hand-held power driver with an impact mechanism and a quarter-inch hex bit holder.
- 047 · hex key — bent or T-handled bar with a hexagonal end for socket screws.
