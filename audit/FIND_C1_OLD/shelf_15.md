# Fastening, joining & clamping > Pry bars & wreckers
- 112 · wrecking bar — flat or round steel bar with a curved claw end for prying and demolition.
- 113 · pry bar [flat bar] — thin flat steel bar with bevelled ends and nail slots, for prising trim and boards.
- 114 · nail puller [cat's paw] — short bar with a claw tip driven under a nail head to lever it out.
- 115 · lifting bar [pinch bar] — long heavy straight bar with a chisel or point end, used as a lever to lift slabs and heavy items.
