# Fastening, joining & clamping > Pliers, clamps, vices & workbenches
- 129 · pliers — hand tool with two pivoted jaws to grip, bend or cut.
- 130 · clamp — tool that presses and holds workpieces together by a screw, bar or lever.
- 131 · bench vise — jaws fixed to a bench that hold a workpiece.
- 132 · workbench — work surface made for tool work, with or without vises, dog holes and storage.
- 133 · sawhorse — trestle-type support pair or single frame carrying a workpiece at working height.
- 134 · trestle — frame support for a board or workpiece, used in pairs.
- 135 · router table — table with a router mounted beneath its top and a fence, holding the work to be passed over the bit.
- 136 · saw stand — folding or wheeled stand that holds a mitre or table saw at working height, often with outrigger supports.
