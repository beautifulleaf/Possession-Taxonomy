# Shaping, sanding & finishing > Files, rasps & sharpening
- 073 · file — hardened steel bar with cutting ridges for removing metal or wood by hand.
- 074 · whetstone — block of natural or synthetic abrasive used with water or oil to sharpen blades (honing guides go with it).
- 075 · bench grinder — electric motor with grinding wheels on a stand, for sharpening and shaping.
- 076 · sharpening system — machine or jig system for sharpening edges, such as a slow wet wheel with jigs.
- 077 · rasp — steel bar or blade with individually raised teeth, for fast shaping of wood and soft materials.
