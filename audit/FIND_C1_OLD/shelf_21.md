# Measuring & testing > Length & level
- 137 · tape measure — coiled flexible rule in a case.
- 138 · spirit level — straight body with liquid vials showing level or plumb.
- 139 · laser distance measurer — handheld device that measures distance with a laser, for building and trade work.
- 140 · line laser level — self-levelling laser projecting horizontal and vertical lines for setting out.
- 141 · rotary laser level — laser with a spinning head that sweeps a 360-degree level plane, read by a detector on site.
- 142 · optical level [dumpy level] — telescope on a tripod that sights a level line onto a staff, for site levels.
