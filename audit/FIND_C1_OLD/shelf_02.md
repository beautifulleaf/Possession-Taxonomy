# Cutting & sawing > Knives & multi-tools
- 029 · utility knife — knife with a replaceable or snap-off blade for cutting materials in a trade or workshop.
- 030 · pocket knife — small folding or locking knife carried in a pocket for general cutting (not a weapon blade pattern).
- 031 · pocket multi-tool — pocket-size tool with pliers and folding implements (knife, scissors, drivers, file, saw) in its handles.
