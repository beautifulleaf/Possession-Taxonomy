# Hand tools > Planes
- 074 · Smoothing plane — short hand plane for final surface smoothing (Stanley No. 1 to 4-1/2 pattern).
- 075 · Jack plane — medium-length hand plane for general stock removal (No. 5, 5-1/2, 6).
- 076 · Jointer plane — long hand plane for straightening edges and faces (No. 7, 8).
- 077 · Block plane — small one-hand plane with a low-angle bevel-up blade, for end grain and chamfers.
- 078 · Shoulder plane — narrow plane whose blade spans the full width of the sole, for trimming rebates and tenon shoulders.
- 079 · Router plane — plane with a cutter hung below a flat base, for levelling grooves and recesses to depth.
- 080 · Plough plane — plane with a fence and depth stop and interchangeable narrow cutters, for grooves along the grain.
- 081 · Japanese plane (also called: kanna · pull plane) — Japanese plane with a wooden body and blade set into it, cutting on the pull stroke.
