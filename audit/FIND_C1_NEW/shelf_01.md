# Power tools > Drills & drivers
- 001 · Drill driver (also called: drill/driver · cordless drill) — hand-held rotary tool with chuck and clutch for drilling and driving screws.
- 002 · Hammer drill — drill/driver with an added percussive mode and ordinary chuck, for masonry and concrete.
- 003 · Rotary hammer (also called: SDS drill) — hand-held tool with an electro-pneumatic hammer mechanism and an SDS-type bit holder, for heavy drilling and chiselling in masonry.
- 004 · Electric screwdriver — small powered driver for light screwdriving, without an impact mechanism.
- 005 · Impact driver — hand-held power driver with an impact mechanism and a quarter-inch hex bit holder.
- 006 · Impact wrench — power wrench with an impact mechanism and square drive for sockets and heavy fasteners.
- 007 · Magnetic drill — portable drill press on an electromagnetic base that clamps to steel, usually with annular cutters.
