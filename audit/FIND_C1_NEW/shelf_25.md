# Welding & soldering > Welders & plasma cutters
- 155 · MIG welder — welding machine that feeds a continuous wire electrode through a gun with shielding gas.
- 156 · TIG welder — welding machine using a tungsten electrode and a separate filler rod under shielding gas.
- 157 · Stick welder (also called: arc welder · MMA welder) — welding machine that burns coated rod electrodes held in a clamp.
- 158 · Multi-process welder — one welding machine set up to run more than one of the MIG, TIG and stick processes.
- 159 · Plasma cutter — machine that cuts metal with an electric arc through a constricted gas jet, sold and compared with welders.
