# Power tools > Routers, planers & multi-tools
- 021 · Router — power tool with a bit spinning in a base, for edges, grooves and recesses.
- 022 · Power planer — hand-held electric plane with a rotating cutter drum.
- 023 · Biscuit jointer (also called: plate joiner) — hand-held power tool that plunges a small circular blade sideways to cut slots for joining biscuits.
- 024 · Oscillating multi-tool (also called: multi-tool) — hand-held power tool whose blade, pad or scraper vibrates through a small arc, for plunge cuts, sanding and scraping.
- 025 · Rotary tool (also called: Dremel-type tool) — slim hand-held high-speed motor with a collet or chuck for small accessories (engraving, cutting, sanding, polishing).
