# Measuring & detecting > Tape measures, levels & lasers
- 132 · Tape measure — coiled flexible rule in a case.
- 133 · Spirit level — straight body with liquid vials showing level or plumb.
- 134 · Laser measure (also called: laser distance measurer) — handheld device that measures distance with a laser, for building and trade work.
- 135 · Line laser (also called: cross-line laser level) — self-levelling laser projecting horizontal and vertical lines for setting out.
- 136 · Rotary laser (also called: rotating laser level) — laser with a spinning head that sweeps a 360-degree level plane, read by a detector on site.
- 137 · Optical level (also called: dumpy level · builder's level) — telescope on a tripod that sights a level line onto a staff, for site levels.
