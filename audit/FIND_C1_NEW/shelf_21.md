# Workshop machinery > Lathes, mills & presses
- 126 · Wood lathe — machine that turns timber between centres or on a chuck against hand-held tools.
- 127 · Metal lathe (also called: engineering lathe) — machine that turns metal with a fixed cutting tool on a carriage.
- 128 · Milling machine — machine that cuts metal with a rotating multi-tooth cutter against a movable table.
- 129 · Lathe-mill (also called: combination lathe-mill) — metal lathe with a milling head on a column over the bed, one machine for turning and milling.
- 130 · Reloading press — lever-operated press that holds dies to resize, prime and seat components of ammunition cartridges.
- 131 · Reloading die — die for a reloading press.
