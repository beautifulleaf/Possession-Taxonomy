# Measuring & detecting > Testers, detectors & cameras
- 147 · Multimeter — instrument that measures several electrical quantities for trade and electronics work.
- 148 · Clamp meter — electrical meter whose jaws close round a conductor to measure current without breaking the circuit.
- 149 · Oscilloscope — instrument that displays electrical signals as waveforms, handheld or bench (ScopeMeter is a Fluke name).
- 150 · Stud, pipe and cable detector (also called: stud finder · wall scanner) — handheld device that senses studs, metal pipes or live cables behind a surface.
- 151 · Moisture meter — instrument that measures moisture in timber or building materials.
- 152 · Infrared thermometer — handheld non-contact thermometer that reads surface temperature from infrared radiation.
- 153 · Inspection camera (also called: endoscope · borescope) — camera on a flexible probe with a screen, for looking inside walls, pipes and machines.
- 154 · Sound level meter — instrument that measures sound pressure level in decibels.
