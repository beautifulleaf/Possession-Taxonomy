# Hand tools > Chisels, files & sharpening
- 082 · Bench chisel (also called: striking chisel) — chisel made to be struck with a mallet, strong tang or socket and hooped or reinforced handle.
- 083 · Paring chisel — long, thin chisel pushed by hand for fine paring.
- 084 · Mortise chisel — thick, deep-bladed chisel struck to chop mortises.
- 085 · Turning gouge (also called: woodturning gouge) — long-handled gouge for shaping wood on a lathe.
- 086 · File — hardened steel bar with cutting ridges for removing metal or wood by hand.
- 087 · Rasp — steel bar or blade with individually raised teeth, for fast shaping of wood and soft materials.
- 088 · Whetstone (also called: sharpening stone · waterstone) — block of natural or synthetic abrasive used with water or oil to sharpen blades (honing guides go with it).
- 089 · Sharpening system (also called: wet grinder · sharpening jig) — machine or jig system for sharpening edges, such as a slow wet wheel with jigs.
