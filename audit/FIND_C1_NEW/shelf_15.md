# Hand tools > Axes & log splitting
- 094 · Axe — long-handled tool with a bladed head for felling, hewing or splitting wood, of any pattern.
- 095 · Splitting maul — heavy-headed tool with a wedge face on one side and a flat sledge face on the other, for splitting logs.
- 096 · Splitting wedge — steel wedge driven into wood with a hammer or maul to split it.
- 097 · Kindling splitter — cast-iron frame with a fixed upward blade, into which a small log is struck with a hammer to split kindling.
- 098 · Log splitter — machine (hydraulic or other power, not tractor-driven) that forces a log against a wedge.
