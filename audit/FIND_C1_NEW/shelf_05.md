# Power tools > Nail guns, staplers & heat tools
- 026 · Nail gun (also called: nailer) — tool that drives collated nails, powered by air, gas or electric/battery drive.
- 027 · Staple gun — tool that fires staples into a surface.
- 028 · Rivet gun — tool that sets rivets.
- 029 · Glue gun — hand-held electric tool that melts and dispenses adhesive sticks.
- 030 · Heat gun — hand-held electric hot-air tool for stripping, shrinking and softening.
