# Power tools > Power saws
- 008 · Circular saw — hand-held power saw with a rotating toothed disc for straight cuts in timber and sheet.
- 009 · Jigsaw — hand-held power saw with a short, vertically reciprocating blade for curves and cut-outs.
- 010 · Reciprocating saw (also called: recip saw · sabre saw) — hand-held power saw with a long blade driven back and forth in line with the body, for demolition and rough cuts.
- 011 · Track saw (also called: plunge saw) — circular saw whose blade plunges down from a sprung housing and that runs on a guide rail for straight sheet cuts.
- 012 · Mitre saw (also called: chop saw · compound mitre saw) — power saw whose blade is carried on a pivoting arm over a table, for crosscuts and angle cuts (sliding and compound are attributes).
