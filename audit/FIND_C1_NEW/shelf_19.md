# Workshop machinery > Planing, moulding & routing machines
- 114 · Jointer (also called: surface planer · planer (machine)) — machine with infeed and outfeed tables and a cutterhead that flattens a face and squares an edge.
- 115 · Thicknesser (also called: thickness planer) — machine that planes boards to an even thickness against a bed.
- 116 · Planer-thicknesser — single machine combining jointer tables and a thicknessing bed on one cutterblock.
- 117 · Spindle moulder — stationary machine with a vertical spindle and cutter for moulding profiles.
- 118 · Mortiser — machine that cuts square mortises with a hollow chisel and auger.
- 119 · Router table — table with a router mounted beneath its top and a fence, holding the work to be passed over the bit.
- 120 · CNC router — computer-controlled gantry machine that routes along programmed paths.
