# Hand tools > Pliers, knives & multi-tools
- 064 · Pliers — hand tool with two pivoted jaws to grip, bend or cut.
- 065 · Utility knife — knife with a replaceable or snap-off blade for cutting materials in a trade or workshop.
- 066 · Pocket knife — small folding or locking knife carried in a pocket for general cutting (not a weapon blade pattern).
- 067 · Multi-tool (also called: pocket multi-tool) — pocket-size tool with pliers and folding implements (knife, scissors, drivers, file, saw) in its handles.
