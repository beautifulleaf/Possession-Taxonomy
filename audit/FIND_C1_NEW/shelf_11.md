# Hand tools > Clamps, vices & workbenches
- 068 · Clamp — tool that presses and holds workpieces together by a screw, bar or lever.
- 069 · Bench vice (also called: bench vise) — jaws fixed to a bench that hold a workpiece.
- 070 · Workbench — work surface made for tool work, with or without vises, dog holes and storage.
- 071 · Sawhorse — trestle-type support pair or single frame carrying a workpiece at working height.
- 072 · Trestle — frame support for a board or workpiece, used in pairs.
- 073 · Saw stand (also called: mitre saw stand) — folding or wheeled stand that holds a mitre or table saw at working height, often with outrigger supports.
