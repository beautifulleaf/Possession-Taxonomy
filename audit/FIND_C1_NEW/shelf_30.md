# Ladders, lifting & moving > Sack trucks & trolleys
- 187 · Sack truck (also called: hand truck) — upright two-wheel carrier with a toe plate for tilting and moving loads.
- 188 · Moving dolly (also called: dolly · furniture dolly) — low platform carrier on wheels or casters.
- 189 · Hand cart (also called: platform trolley) — hand-pushed multi-wheel cart or trolley for goods.
- 190 · Pallet truck (also called: pallet jack) — hand-pulled or powered low truck whose forks slide under a pallet and lift it just clear of the floor.
