# Hand tools > Pry bars & wrecking bars
- 090 · Wrecking bar (also called: crowbar) — flat or round steel bar with a curved claw end for prying and demolition.
- 091 · Pry bar (also called: flat bar) — thin flat steel bar with bevelled ends and nail slots, for prising trim and boards.
- 092 · Nail puller (also called: cat's paw) — short bar with a claw tip driven under a nail head to lever it out.
- 093 · Lifting bar (also called: pinch bar) — long heavy straight bar with a chisel or point end, used as a lever to lift slabs and heavy items.
