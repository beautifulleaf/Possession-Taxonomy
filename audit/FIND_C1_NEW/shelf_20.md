# Workshop machinery > Drill presses, grinders & sanders
- 121 · Drill press (also called: pillar drill) — drill on a column with a table and feed lever, bench or floor mounted.
- 122 · Bench grinder — electric motor with grinding wheels on a stand, for sharpening and shaping.
- 123 · Spindle sander (also called: bobbin sander · oscillating spindle sander) — bench machine with an abrasive sleeve on a spindle that rotates and moves up and down through a table, for curves.
- 124 · Belt and disc sander — bench machine combining a sanding belt and a sanding disc on one motor.
- 125 · Dust extractor (also called: dust collector · shop vac) — extraction unit for dust from workshop machines and power tools.
