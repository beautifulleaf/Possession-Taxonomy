# Ladders, lifting & moving > Ladders
- 170 · Stepladder (also called: step ladder) — free-standing hinged A-frame ladder.
- 171 · Platform stepladder (also called: platform step ladder) — step ladder with a top platform to stand on.
- 172 · Combination ladder — ladder that can be set up in more than one form (step, extension, other) from one set of rails.
- 173 · Extension ladder — ladder with sliding sections that lean against a support.
- 174 · Telescopic ladder — ladder whose rungs and rails telescope into each other.
- 175 · Single ladder (also called: leaning ladder) — one-section ladder leaned against a support.
- 176 · Roof ladder — ladder with wheels and a ridge hook, laid on a pitched roof.
- 177 · Tripod ladder (also called: garden ladder · orchard ladder) — ladder with a single rear leg forming a tripod, for uneven ground and hedges.
