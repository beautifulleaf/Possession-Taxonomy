# Power tools > Breakers, chasers & cut-off saws
- 031 · Demolition hammer (also called: breaker · jack hammer) — powered percussion tool that drives a chisel or point by hammer blows only, with no rotation, to break concrete, masonry and tile (jackhammer and pneumatic or petrol breakers are this kind, `drive` an attribute; provisional until a page selling demolition hammers or breakers as a type is fetched; memo section 14).
- 032 · Wall chaser (also called: chasing saw) — hand-held machine with twin diamond discs that cuts parallel channels in masonry for cables and pipes.
- 033 · Cut-off saw (also called: disc cutter · concrete saw) — hand-held engine or electric saw with a large abrasive or diamond disc behind a guard, for concrete, stone and steel.
