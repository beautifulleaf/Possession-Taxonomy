# Measuring & detecting > Precision measuring & marking out
- 138 · Caliper — sliding gauge measuring outside, inside and depth dimensions.
- 139 · Micrometer — screw gauge for precise outside, inside or depth measurement.
- 140 · Dial indicator — gauge with a plunger or lever and a dial showing small displacement.
- 141 · Height gauge — gauge on a base for measuring or marking height.
- 142 · Marking gauge (also called: kebiki) — tool that scribes a line parallel to an edge.
- 143 · Square (also called: try square · combination square) — right-angle tool for marking and checking squareness, including combination squares.
- 144 · Angle finder — dial, bevel or digital gauge that measures and transfers angles.
- 145 · Hardness tester — instrument that tests the hardness of a material.
- 146 · Coordinate measuring machine (also called: CMM) — machine that probes a part to measure its geometry in three axes.
