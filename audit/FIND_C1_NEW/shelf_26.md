# Welding & soldering > Soldering & brazing
- 160 · Soldering iron — hand-held heated tip for melting solder, usually in electrical and electronics work.
- 161 · Soldering station — temperature-controlled power base with a soldering iron handpiece and stand.
- 162 · Gas torch (also called: soldering torch · blowtorch) — hand-held butane or propane torch for soldering, brazing and heating in trade and repair work.
- 163 · Fume extractor — unit that draws away soldering or welding fumes.
