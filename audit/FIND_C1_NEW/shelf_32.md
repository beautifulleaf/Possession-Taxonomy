# Site equipment & power > Site plant & mixers
- 197 · Excavator — machine with a boom, arm and bucket on a swinging house, tracked or wheeled (mini to full size).
- 198 · Backhoe loader — wheeled machine with a front loader bucket and a rear digging arm.
- 199 · Skid steer (also called: skid steer loader) — compact loader steered by skidding wheels or tracks, with a lift arm.
- 200 · Telehandler — loader with a telescopic boom.
- 201 · Forklift — truck that lifts loads on forks.
- 202 · Site dumper (also called: dumper) — machine with a tipping body for hauling material on site.
- 203 · Plate compactor — vibrating plate machine that compacts soil and aggregate.
- 204 · Cement mixer — drum machine that mixes cement, mortar or concrete.
