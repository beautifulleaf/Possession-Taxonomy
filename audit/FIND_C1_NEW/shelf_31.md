# Site equipment & power > Generators, compressors & pumps
- 191 · Inverter generator — engine-driven generator with inverter electronics for stable output.
- 192 · Open-frame generator (also called: conventional portable generator) — engine-driven generator in an open frame with a directly coupled alternator.
- 193 · Air compressor — machine that makes compressed air for tools, in a general workshop or site role.
- 194 · Submersible pump (also called: drainage pump · sump pump) — pump that sits under water to remove or drain it.
- 195 · Transfer pump — pump that moves liquid from one container to another.
- 196 · Surface pump (also called: pressure pump) — surface-mounted general-purpose pump that draws water and delivers it under pressure.
