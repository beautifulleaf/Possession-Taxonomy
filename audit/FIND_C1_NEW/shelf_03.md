# Power tools > Sanders & grinders
- 013 · Angle grinder — hand-held power tool with a disc on a right-angle head, for grinding and cutting.
- 014 · Die grinder — straight-bodied high-speed hand tool for burrs and mounted points.
- 015 · Random orbital sander (also called: random-orbit sander · ROS) — hand-held sander with a round pad moving in orbit and rotation.
- 016 · Belt sander — sander with a continuous abrasive belt over two rollers.
- 017 · Polisher — power tool with a soft pad or mop that rotates to polish a surface.
- 018 · Sheet sander (also called: orbital finishing sander) — hand-held sander with a rectangular pad moving in small orbits, taking a cut-down abrasive sheet.
- 019 · Detail sander (also called: delta sander · mouse sander) — hand-held sander with a small triangular (delta) pad that vibrates, for corners and narrow areas.
- 020 · Drywall sander — sander with a large round head on a long pole and dust extraction, for plasterboard walls and ceilings.
