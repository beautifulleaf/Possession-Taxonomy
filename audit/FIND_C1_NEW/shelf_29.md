# Ladders, lifting & moving > Jacks, stands & hoists
- 178 · Trolley jack (also called: floor jack) — wheeled hydraulic floor jack with a lifting arm.
- 179 · Bottle jack — hydraulic jack in a vertical bottle form.
- 180 · Scissor jack — jack lifted by a screw closing a diamond of linked arms.
- 181 · Axle stand (also called: jack stand) — stand that supports a lifted vehicle.
- 182 · Transmission jack — jack with a saddle that lifts and holds a gearbox for removal and fitting.
- 183 · Engine hoist (also called: engine crane) — folding crane that lifts engines.
- 184 · Engine stand — wheeled stand that holds an engine by its block and lets it rotate for rebuild work.
- 185 · Chain hoist — hand or electric hoist with a chain.
- 186 · Winch — drum and rope or cable device for pulling or lifting.
