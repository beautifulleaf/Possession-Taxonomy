- All house, flat, land, mobile and building kinds (about 40 with entries): vernacular and regional types, buildings and styles removed as commodity kinds (about 140 entries → 0). New: Ritz-Carlton (2000), Trump International, Fairmont, St Regis, Banyan Tree, Kempinski, Marriott International, Accor, Hilton, Banyan Group. Sherry-Netherland dropped (a precedent building, not a brand). Roles: Four Seasons (origin, from earlier source), YOO (origin for non-hotel brands, per Knight Frank via the article). Tiers: Marriott, Accor, Hilton Global group; Four Seasons, Aman, Ritz-Carlton, Mandarin Oriental, Trump Independent; the rest carry no tier.
