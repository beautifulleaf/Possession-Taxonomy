# E1PZ report: Puzzles, fidgets & desk toys

**Before:** 3 puzzle kinds (Jigsaw, 3D jigsaw, Mechanical puzzle with 7 archetypes) plus Fidget toy (4 archetypes). **After:** new department, 5 shelves, 28 kinds, 86 archetypes (Jigsaw 2/5; Twisty 5/21; Mechanical by family, Slocum's ten categories, 10/34; Fidgets 6/16; Desk 5/10). Old Puzzles shelf, Mechanical puzzle and Fidget toy removed. "Skill toys & fidgets" is now "Skill toys", with new kind Electronic handheld toy (6 archetypes).

**Brands:** 215 to 238 (+23): DaYan, MoYu, QiYi, YJ, ThinkFun, Smart Games, Karakuri Creation Group, Jean-Claude Constantin, Antsy Labs, Tangle Creations, Spin Master, Milton Bradley, Maxfield & Oberton, James Industries; designers van Deventer, Coffin, Cutler, Kamei, Yoshigahara, Rubik, Slocum, Piet Hein, Mèffert. Gigamic already existed; Ravensburger gained a source.

**Corrections:**
- Pop it: 1975 is the Costers' (Theora Concepts) design; FoxMind acquired it in 2009, showed silicone in 2014, Target sale 2019 (old "2009 silicone" was wrong).
- Speedcube: GAN is not the originator; DaYan led c. 2011-2015, GAN356 Air UM (2016) was the first mass-produced magnetic cube, QiYi rose in 2018.
- Squishy: Japanese origin, no year found; 2017 is its US arrival. Stress ball date dropped.
- Spinner: boom 2017, Torqbar c. 2014, Hettinger patent 1993 with no bearing.
- 1,000-piece puzzle is a type; puzzle globe only under 3D jigsaw; duplicate Cast Quartet/NEWS models removed.
- New owner rule applied: no activity kinds; WCA only in attributes.

**Unsourced, left out:** Neocube, haptic coins and slider makers, Giiker/Rubik's Connected, Pelikan Puzzles, Face Turning Octahedron, vanish puzzles, Katamino designer, GAN's 2011 first cube (speedsolving.com blocked). Parallel tools had no credit. Karakuri, Constantin and slider sources are maker or retailer pages.
