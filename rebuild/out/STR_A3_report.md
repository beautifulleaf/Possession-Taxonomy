# STR_A3 report: Jewellery

**Before → after.** Departments 10 → 10. Shelves 14 → 28. Kinds 85 → 109. Archetypes about 227 → 145 (generic ones demoted, see below). `validate.py --v3` prints OK. Backups are in rebuild/work/STR_A3/bak/.

**Category.** Added `ax` (where on the body, or one regional tradition) and a 10-step placement `key`. The lead already fitted RULES.md and is unchanged.

**Departments.** Each has `t` and `ax`. Shelves were regrouped so each answers its department's question:
- Rings now by purpose: stone-set, bands and fashion, identity and pledge, novelty and memorial.
- Earrings by where the ornament sits: lobe, hanging, wrapped or clipped.
- Necklaces by what makes the piece: strand, neck-fitted, hanging ornament.
- Bracelets by build: rigid, strand, special job.
- Brooches by what they fasten.
- Head by hair, face or teeth.
- Body-worn pieces by ankle, toe, waist or torso.
- Piercing jewellery by form: bar, post or ring, stretched ear.
- Traditional forms by region.

**Kinds split.** "Ear cuff & jacket" became Ear cuff and Ear jacket. "Tiaras & crowns" became Tiara and Crown.

**Kinds promoted.** Kanzashi, Binyeo and Fazan went from archetypes of Hairpin to kinds in the new East Asian shelf.

**Kinds added** (reference in brackets): Giardinetti ring, Birthstone ring, Midi ring, Promise ring, Acrostic ring (Wikipedia Ring); Chandelier earring, Threader earring (Wikipedia Earring); Silicone wristband (Wikipedia Bracelet); Fibula (Wikipedia Brooch); Collar pin; Chatelaine; Spiral barbell and Nipple shield (Wikipedia Body piercing jewellery); Crown (Wikipedia List of jewellery types). The same list was the whole-space check for every shelf.

**Generic archetypes demoted** to types or definitions:
- Signet face shapes, Diamond eternity ring.
- Diamond solitaire stud, Pearl stud, Creole hoop, Sleeper.
- Cultured Akoya pearl strand, Ribbon choker, Viking neck ring.
- The five locket types, Shell cameo, Hardstone cameo, Victorian charm, Mid-century charm.
- Diamond line bracelet, Medical ID bracelet, Chain-link cufflink, Flag pin, Button badge.
- Victorian mourning, Cameo and Edwardian platinum brooches, Bandeau, Circlet.
- Solid, open-face, iced and double-cap grills.
- Double-flare plug, Single-flare plug, Flesh tunnel, Crescent taper, Spiral.
- Agadez cross and the Hei-tiki archetype (duplicates of their kinds).

**Maker ranges moved to `r`.** Miriam Haskell, Schiaparelli, Gorilla Glass, Kaos Softwear and Diablo Organics. Penannular brooch moved to Fibula.

**Moves.** None new; the existing Hair clip and Patch entries stay. Championship ring was not added because F3 holds it; it is linked instead.
