**Before → after:** departments 11 → 12; shelves 26 → 43; kinds 168 → 219; archetypes 410 → 377.

**Category:** added `ax` and a 12-step `key` with a precedence note; lead kept. All departments and shelves have `t`/`ax`; flat `t` lists became grouped `tt`.

**Restructured:** Knives now cut by job (daggers, hunting, bayonets, thrown); Firearms by gun type with a new Muzzle-loading shelf; Air guns by power source; Archery split into bows, crossbows, other launchers, projectiles, accessories; Ammunition into rounds, components, equipment; Armour by body part with Modern body armour as its own department; Militaria into inert pieces and replicas.
