**Structure.** Category `ax` and `key` (7 steps plus precedence) added. Every department has `t` and `ax`; every shelf has `t`, and `ax` where it holds more than three kinds. Every kind with attributes now has grouped `tt` (flat `t` removed).

- Controllers: Pads & sticks, Pointing & motion, Driving, Flying, Music/dance/party.
