# A5 Clocks & Watches — rebuild report (pilot)

**Brand mode:** Wristwatches, Pocket watches and Collectible clocks are variety; Everyday clocks and Timers are tier; Straps, storage & parts is mixed.

**Counts (before → after):** 7 → 6 departments · 64 → 74 kinds · 0 → 247 archetypes with 668 versions (reference numbers and generations) · 126 → 75 flat models · 31 → 12 kinds with no entries (these are parts kinds carrying `t` types, plus Tower clock and Time clock, kept at kind level). Brand register: 105 brands in 15 segments. Traditions: 6. Moves: 1 (Chess clock → E1, to merge with "Game clock").

**What changed**
- W1/W2: major brands and independents are now set out as model family → reference → detail (e.g. Submariner 6204 → 126610LV "Starbucks").
- W3: added Integrated-bracelet sports watch, Everyday, Jewellery, Chronometer, Electric & early quartz, and Avant-garde kinds. "Other time-only watches" is gone. Field watch is filled.
- W4/W5: pocket watches are now sorted by mechanism (early, lever, railroad, dollar, complicated). Collectible clocks are sorted by form → maker/period.
- W6–W9: as approved. Clocks split into everyday and collectible. Clock parts are one kind. Modding parts are listed by what they fit. The atomic clock is dropped and cuckoo sources are now maker-based (VdS).

**Sources:** Wikipedia, Wikidata SPARQL, maker archives (AP Chronicles, Rolex, Patek, Seiko, Omega), auction houses and reference articles. 435 distinct URLs are logged in `work/A5/sources.tsv`.

**Not done / caveats**
- Watchbase blocked automated access (HTTP 403), so I did not work around it; references come from the sources above instead.
- Wikipedia and WDQS rate-limited this proxy heavily, so the brand register is not yet checked by capture–recapture.
- Some brands have no retrieved page: Petermann Bédat, Rémy Cools, Andersen Genève and Ming appear only as model makers.
- Not attested, so left out: Tissot PRX, Omega flat-link "1171", rally strap.
