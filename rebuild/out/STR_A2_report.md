# STR_A2 Footwear structure report

**Before → after:** departments 9 → 9; shelves 19 → 28; kinds 83 → 97; archetypes 297 → 253 (44 demoted, listed in work/STR_A2/demoted.json). Category now has `ax` and a nine-step `key`; lead unchanged (it already fits RULES.md). Every department and shelf has `t` and `ax`; every kind has `tt`, written by hand.

**Regrouped:** Sneakers by lineage (basketball; court and canvas; running and terrace; skate, street and designer). Running, walking & outdoor split into running, walking and hiking, water. Dress shoes by fastening (lace-ups, strap, slip-ons). Heels by how the foot is held. Boots by purpose (dress and fashion, work and military, western and riding, weather); Over-the-knee, Knee-high, Jodhpur moved accordingly. Sandals by what holds the foot. Traditional footwear by region.

**Merged/renamed:** "Shoe polish and waterproofing" merged into "Shoe polish and conditioner" (models and ranges carried over); "Fit aids" renamed "Insole & heel grip"; "Slippers" (one kind, 10 models) split into Scuff, Moccasin, Clog, Slipper boot, Novelty slipper, models distributed by name.

**Added kinds (sourced):** Platform shoe, Go-go boot, Rigger boot, Waraji, Jipsin, Tiger-head shoe, Giveh, Bast shoe (lapti), Abarka, Bakya, Veldskoen (List of shoe styles); five slipper kinds (Slipper article). No new archetypes for them: no maker or originator pages were retrieved.

**References (coverage):** Wikipedia List of shoe styles (dress, heels, traditional, boots, sneakers shelves), Boot, Sandal, Slipper, Sneakers. Firecrawl credits ran out, so Boot, Sandal and Slipper came via WebFetch. Care shelves were not checked against a fetched specialist list; they keep their earlier retailer sources.

**Generic archetypes demoted to types/aliases:** cap-toe, plain-toe, brogue grades, plain derby, longwing, country derby, single/double monk, soft-sole moccasin, traditional espadrille, classic/dress western boot, Grecian/Roman sandal, Mexican huarache, two-tooth geta, all zōri sub-types, regional jutti/kheri/opanak names, paduka, babouche, klomp, valenki, kamik and shoe-tree variants. Their maker versions went into `m`; versions without a maker were dropped.

**Moves:** none new; the existing Motorcycle boot move stands. Brands file untouched.
