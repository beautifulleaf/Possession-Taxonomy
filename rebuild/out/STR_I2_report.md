# STR_I2 structure report (I2 Digital Media & Items)

**Before → after:** 5 departments → 5; 16 shelves → 20; 70 kinds → 80 (6 platform kinds replace 1, plus 5 new); 150 archetypes → 123 (29 demoted, 2 added).

**Category:** added `ax` and a five-step `key`. Lead kept (no rule conflict).

**Departments:** each has `t` and `ax`. Fonts regrouped into 3 shelves (strokes; hand and display; technical and other-script). Creative assets regrouped into 6 shelves (the 9-kind graphics shelf split into Artwork & layouts and Presets & motion). Game items gained a Currency & passes shelf. Domains & tokens now has Registered names (domain name, blockchain name) and NFTs. Every shelf has `t`; shelves over 3 kinds have `ax`, kinds ordered along it.

**Split/renamed:** "Platform profile item" split into Platform trading card, Profile badge, Profile background, Avatar and frame, Chat emoticon, Chat sticker pack (renames file: Platform profile item → Platform trading card). Blockchain name moved from NFTs to Registered names. No kinds merged or moved to other categories.

**Added kinds (with source):** Pixel and bitmap typeface (Wikipedia Bitmap font), Stock video clip (Stock footage), In-game currency (Virtual currency), Battle pass (Battle pass; archetypes Dota 2 Compendium, Fortnite Battle Pass), Ticket or membership token (Non-fungible token).

**Coverage references fetched:** Typeface classification (fonts), Non-fungible token (tokens), Loot box and Battle pass and Virtual currency (game items), Top-level domain (domains; types now follow its IANA classes), Stock footage. Not usable: Unity Asset Store (404), Digital magazine (disambiguation), Sound effect (no library content). Creative assets and Personal media were not checked against a whole-space reference beyond these.

**Types:** all `t` lists replaced by hand-written `tt` groups, each with one question; named products avoided except software-compatibility labels (Procreate, After Effects, Kontakt, Lightroom).

**Generic archetypes demoted** (versions kept in `m`/`r`): CS2 gloves, Fortnite pickaxe and glider, Rocket League car cosmetics, Dota 2 cosmetic sets, Roblox avatar items, Fortnite outfit, Dota 2 courier, WoW flying mounts, Animal Crossing furniture, FFXIV housing, Second Life virtual goods, Habbo rare furni, CS2 sticker, CS2 weapon charm, CS2 graffiti, CS2 music kit, CS case key, Tournament sticker capsule, Steam animated profile background, Steam Points Shop game profile, Serum presets, HDR light probe lighting. Pattern houses (Butterick, McCall's, Simplicity, Burda Style, Ravelry) and sample services (Splice, Loopmasters) became ranges in `r`.

**Cross-links added:** 14 `see` links (C4 sewing and knitting patterns and embroidery machine, D1 3D printer, I1 3D modelling software, sampler, orchestral sample library, font manager, F1 full score, C5 diary or planner, F3 non-sports trading card, I3 audiobook subscription, and two within I2). All targets checked in the reader files.
