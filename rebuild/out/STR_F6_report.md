# STR_F6 report (Natural History & Specimens)

Counts before → after: departments 9 → 10, shelves 40 → 44, kinds 308 → 345, archetypes 1,191 → 1,098 (92 demoted, none dropped). Validator: OK. File 584 KB.

**Structure.** Category `ax` and a 10-step `key` added; lead reworded. "Minerals & crystals" (19 shelves) became two departments: Minerals: non-silicates (8 shelves by chemical class; quartz, opal and chalcedony sit with oxides) and Minerals: silicates (6 shelves by structure: single-unit, paired-unit, ring, chain, sheet, framework and zeolite). Gemstones now 4 shelves; Meteorites 4 (chondrites, achondrites, irons, impact glass); Rocks by family (igneous, sedimentary, metamorphic, curiosities); Shells by animal group (4); Taxidermy by preservation; Elements 2; Display 2. Every department has `t` and `ax`; every shelf has `t`, and `ax` where over three kinds, with kinds ordered along it.

**Kinds.** Every old `t` list rewritten by hand as `tt` with named axes. Added 37: Arsenopyrite, Covellite, Tetrahedrite, Millerite, Cryolite, Ilmenite, Pyrolusite, Brucite, Chromite, Strontianite, Hydrozincite, Jarosite, Pyrope, Petalite, Thomsonite (Wikipedia pages, fetched); Cut kunzite, Cut zircon; Tusk shell, Sand dollar, Cuttlebone, Chiton shell; ten rocks (Gabbro, Andesite, Pumice, Tuff, Conglomerate, Shale, Chert and flint, Schist, Gneiss, Quartzite; List of rock types); Element coin; Display dome, Specimen stand, Museum putty, Gem jar, Perky box, Riker mount. "Specimen display" was split into the six display kinds (renames file: Specimen display → Display dome). Brachiopod shell moved to the corals shelf; no kind removed, none moved to another category.

**References per shelf.** Minerals: Nickel–Strunz and Mindat-style classes; gems: List of gemstones by species; meteorites: Meteorite classification; rocks: List of rock types; shells: Seashell; fossils: Gondwana Fossils category list; elements: Element collecting; taxidermy: NatSCA; display: minerals.net, kk.org (Riker). The University Products page returned 403 and was not used.

**Demoted generic archetypes** (now models in `m`, versions kept): diamond colour grades, lab-growth methods, star and colour-change corundum, pearl shapes, forms of native gold and silver, mount styles, tusk, egg and feather types, element cubes and ampoules, trace-fossil types, crystal-habit entries such as "Biotite books", and similar (full list in work/STR_F6/demoted.json).

**Cross-links.** 66 `see` links: cut gem ↔ mineral pairs, amber ↔ insect in resin, fossil ↔ modern shell, coral and urchin pairs, display kinds, plus required Marble → E1 Marble and Jet → G1 Jet.

**Unsourced / open.** Strunz placements of a few silicates (neptunite, petalite, astrophyllite, grandidierite) come from memory and should be spot-checked. Moonstone page was disambiguation only. Kind definitions for new minerals were written from general knowledge; `s` is the fetched page.
