# ICONS report (2026-10-31)

Strict rule applied: origins only where a fetched page says "first"; landmarks only where a fetched MoMA collection or ADI Compasso d'Oro page confirms the item. Backups: rebuild/work/ICONS/bak/. Sources: rebuild/work/ICONS/sources.tsv. All eight files pass validate.py --v3.

## Added
- B6 Robot vacuum cleaner: Electrolux Trilobite (origin, 1996) - Wikipedia, Robotic vacuum cleaner ("introduced the first robotic vacuum cleaner").
- B6 Upright vacuum cleaner: Dyson DC02 (landmark) - MoMA works/88172. Cylinder vacuum cleaner: Spalter, Castiglioni, 1956 (landmark) - MoMA works/1367. Wet and dry vacuum cleaner: Bidone Lavatutto (landmark) - ADI page, Compasso d'Oro XV edition, 1989.
- B7 Electric fan (design & vintage): Behrens fan GB1 (MoMA works/1603) and Ariante (ADI, Compasso d'Oro 1979); Fan heater: Braun Heater-Ventilator H 3 (MoMA works/4135); Portable air conditioner: Candyzionatore (ADI, Compasso d'Oro 1970; the page does not say "portable", placement is a judgement).
- B8 Baby monitor: Zenith Radio Nurse (origin, 1937) - Wikipedia, Baby monitor.
- H2 Hearing aid: Akouphone (origin, c. 1895) - Wikipedia, Hearing aid. Manual wheelchair: Carna (MoMA works/1512) and Bob Hall racing wheelchair (MoMA works/2533).
- F5 Charge card: Diners Club card (origin, 1950) - Wikipedia, Credit card ("first general purpose charge card").
- F5 Transit card: canon of 681 entries from Wikipedia's List of public transport smart cards, read in three windows (the last holds only references), sectioned by region; nl, sel and coverage line set. Same-name cards in several cities are one entry with versions. Rows naming no card were skipped. Turkey's repeated "Kentkart" rows are one entry. 344 operators added to F5_brands.json as stubs. No origin is marked: the page names no first fare card.
- C8 Wall tile and Floor tile: tt group "Tradition" (Delft, Encaustic, Zellige, Majolica and Talavera, Azulejo, Iznik, Cuerda seca, Cement tile) with a see-note to F2 (visual-art taxonomy). No entries.
- Brand registers extended in B6, B7, B8, H2, F5 for makers used in mk (country only where a page gives it).
