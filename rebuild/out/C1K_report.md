# C1K report: Knives, multi-tools & axes (knives part)

**Before:** shelf "Pocket knives & multi-tools", 6 kinds (Folding knife 7 archetypes, Traditional folding knife 7, Swiss Army knife 4, Multi-tool 9, Fixed-blade bushcraft knife 2, Utility knife 2). Axes shelf untouched.
**After:** 6 shelves for knives, 14 kinds, 56 archetypes: Folding knives by mechanism (slip-joint 5, friction/locking-collar 2, lockback 3, liner/frame lock 6, crossbar 3, compression 2); Swiss Army knives (8); Fixed blades & utility (4+2); Multi-tools (plier 11, keychain/wearable 4); Knife sharpeners (1); Everyday-carry tools (pen 2, pry bar/keychain tool 3).

**Brands added (5):** ESEE, Ka-Bar, Fisher Space Pen, Tuff-Writer, Nite Ize. **Traditions added (4):** Laguiole knife, Opinel and Savoie knives, Sheffield cutlery, Solingen cutlery.

**Moved/merged/removed:** "Folding knife" split by lock; "Traditional folding knife" split into slip-joint and friction/collar kinds; Opinel, Higonokami and Laguiole retained as archetypes; Multi-tool split plier vs keychain; Swiss officer's-knife versions Huntsman/Climber became archetypes (Climber dropped, unsourced); Stanley Quickslide moved to Utility knife. Dropped SKU-level m entries: Civivi Elementum II and Mini Praxis, Real Steel Solis, The James Brand Folsom (lock types unconfirmed). Balisong and automatic knives left to E4. Sharpening system (woodworking) untouched; the pocket sharpener kind is separate. Dept criteria: added "Lock or opening mechanism", replaced "Lock and action".

**Corrected:** Spyderco Paramilitary 2 and Para 3 (2016) re-sourced; Sebenza versions and Umnumzaan/Mnandi/Inkosi from Wikipedia; Leatherman versions from its model table; Gerber MP600 versions; Soldier knife generations.

**Could not source (left out or carried unverified):** Climber and Cybertool years; Spartan/Huntsman years; Bugout and Griptilian designer/years; Buck 112 Ranger; Delica, Worker and Kershaw Leek lock types (placement kept from earlier data, s pages from earlier rounds not re-fetched); Opinel/Higonokami old versions carried over; Cold Steel Voyager/Recon 1; Ontario RAT-1; Ka-Bar, ESEE, Nite Ize country/founding years; Sheffield trademark marks.
