# V4_C1 report (Tools, Hardware & Materials)

Validate: `validate.py --v3 out/C1.json` OK. About 40 fetches (none via Firecrawl). Backups and the dropped lists are in rebuild/work/V4_C1/ (bak/, dropped.json, sources.tsv).

## Hand planes (Woodworking hand tools)
Entries before → after: 40 → 176 across 14 plane kinds (Bench 4→48, Block 4→36, Rebate 3→22, Scraper 2→16, Specialist 16, Plough 10, and so on; new kinds Scrub, Circular, Dado, Match and tonguing, Specialist).
Reference: Patrick's Blood and Gore (supertool.com, pages 1–15), one entry per Stanley number, letter/prefix variants (C, H, A, S, W, M, J) as versions, grouped by number series. Fine Woodworking supports the series grouping.
Not sourced: Record, Lie-Nielsen and Veritas versions (no fetched page ties them to a number). Numbers #14, #53, #91 had no data; #11, #80 and #90 each name two different planes and appear as two entries. The pages were read through a summariser, so a heading may be missing. Spokeshave and Moulding-plane entries not in the guide were dropped.

## Knives and multi-tools

Not sourced: Mora years; Victorinox years for gallery models; Leatherman limited editions are omitted.

## Power tools (market-first)
New shelf "Power tool brands": DeWalt, Black+Decker, Craftsman (Stanley Black & Decker), Milwaukee, Ryobi (Techtronic), Bosch, Makita, Hilti, Festool, each with own/tier/cty. The 17 battery platforms gained own/tier/cty where a fetched page gives the owner. Market panel (16 facts): GLG ranking 2021 (TTI, Stanley Black & Decker, Bosch, Makita, Hilti), regional shares 2020 (Asia-Pacific 35 %, North America 32 %, Europe 29 %), country shares 2020, and 2024–2025 group revenues. Not sourced: brand-level shares, Black+Decker founding year, Porter-Cable/AEG country. Craftsman purchase year differs between pages (2016 vs 2017); the Craftsman page's 2017 is used. Brand register: added Stanley Black & Decker, Techtronic Industries, TTS Tooltechnic Systems, Craftsman.
