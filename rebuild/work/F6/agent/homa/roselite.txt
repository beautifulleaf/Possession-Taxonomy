Roselite                                                                  Ca2 (Co, Mg)(AsO4 )2 • 2H2 O

Crystal Data: Monoclinic. Point Group: 2/m. Crystals are elongated along [100],
showing {011}, {111}, {010}, {110}, to 1.3 cm; commonly in spherical aggregates and druses.
Twinning: Very common on {100} with {100} as composition plane, contact twins and fourlings.

Physical Properties:            Cleavage: On {010}, perfect. Hardness = 3.5                D(meas.) = 3.46–3.74
D(calc.) = 3.65

Optical Properties: Transparent to translucent. Color: Pale rose-pink to dark rose-pink;
rose-pink in transmitted light, may be color zoned. Luster: Vitreous.
Optical Class: Biaxial (+). Pleochroism: X = pale rose-pink to dark rose-pink; Y = paler
rose-pink; Z = palest rose-pink. Orientation: X = b to X ∧ c ∼0◦ –4◦ ; Y = b to Y ∧ c = 12◦ –20◦ ;
Z ∧ c –70◦ to 91◦ . Dispersion: r < v, weak to strong. α = 1.694–1.725 β = 1.704–1.730
γ = 1.719–1.735 2V(meas.) = 60◦ –75◦

Polymorphism & Series:              Dimorphous with roselite-beta; forms a series with wendwilsonite.

Mineral Group:          Roselite group.

Occurrence:        A rare secondary mineral in cobalt-bearing hydrothermal mineral deposits.

Association:       Talmessite (Bou Azzer, Morocco).

Distribution: In Germany, from the Rappold and Daniel mines, Schneeberg, Saxony; at the
Friedrich Christian mine, Wildschapbachtal, and the Anton mine, Heubachtal, near Schiltach,
Black Forest. From Bou Azzer, Morocco. In the Talmessi mine, 35 km west of Anarak, Iran. From
Huercal Overa, Almerı́a Province, and at Molvizar, Granada Province, Spain.

Name: To honor Gustav Rose (1798–1873), Professor of Mineralogy, University of Berlin,
Berlin, Germany.
