Polymorphism & Series:              Forms three series: with andradite; with hibschite and katoite; and
with uvarovite.

Mineral Group:          Garnet group.

Occurrence: In contact and regionally metamorphosed calcareous rocks, or rocks which have
undergone calcium metasomatism; in some schists and serpentinites.

Association: Calcite, dolomite, epidote, clinozoisite, wollastonite, scapolite, vesuvianite,
diopside, tremolite, quartz.
