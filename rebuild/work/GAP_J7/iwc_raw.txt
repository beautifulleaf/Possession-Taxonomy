The following is a complete list of Italian quality-wine denominations as
of July 2025. The list consists of:
78 Denominazioni di Origine Controllata e Garantita (DOCG) and
332 Denominazioni di Origine Controllata (DOC)
= 410 Denominazioni di Origine Protetta (DOP)
The list is organized by region, with DOCGs appearing first followed by the
region’s DOCs, both in alphabetical order.
