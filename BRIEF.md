# Possession taxonomy — working brief

Agreed with the owner on 2026-10-17.

## Scope
- **Fresh domain.** The old Possessions taxonomy is not used or consulted.
- **In:** every kind of object a person can own and keep, including plants (houseplants, bonsai, garden plants), property (houses, land, structures, boats and vehicles as dwellings), digital goods (game items, domains, software licences, NFTs and other tokens), consumables kept or collected rather than used up (wine cellars, perfume collections, cigars), money as collectible objects (coins, banknotes), and adult objects (clinical, descriptive register).
- **Groceries are in (owner, 2026-10-17):** food and drink bought as ingredients and raw or minimally processed products, judged by the quality of the ingredient (for example high-quality meat: breed, cut, grade, origin, age). Dishes and prepared foods stay with the Culinary taxonomy.
- **Software and media (owner, 2026-10-17):** types of software are in, by what the software does (for example a password manager), however it is licensed or delivered. Media, games and digital content are in only as objects: carriers, formats and files. Literary, game, music and film/TV genres, and the internet as entertainment, belong to separate taxonomies; they never divide categories here.
- **Subscriptions and streaming are in (owner, 2026-10-17):** paid subscriptions and memberships are purchases that give enjoyment or quality of life. They are divided by the kind of access they give, never by content genre.
- **Out:** animals of any kind (pets, livestock, aquarium fish). Their equipment and housing are in (an aquarium tank, a saddle, a dog bed). Bank balances and other non-object financial positions are out.
- **Level of generality (owner, 2026-10-17):** a kind is a generic type of object someone has one of. Specific product models, cuts and iconic designs are named forms under a kind. Individual unique items (a particular card, a particular painting) are out.
- **Depth:** areas with very deep variety must be captured in depth — for example clothing, including the brands of different subcultures and scenes.
- **Quality:** some areas have clear tiers of quality set by brand or specification (for example many PC components); others are personal preference. The structure must allow both.

## Method (owner, 2026-10-17)
- **Stage 1:** a preliminary top-level category pass first. **Stage 2:** each category is researched separately to find the best structure for it, and the owner approves each structure. Brand handling, tiers, order axis and depth are decided per category in stage 2, not in advance.
- **Shared contract across all categories:** one home per object; kinds are generic types; modifiers (region, price, era, motive, scene, tier) stay off the tree as tags or views; every added name has an attested source; a maximum depth even where level names differ; data in tables with generated pages; resumable sessions.
- Cross-domain edges: Possession files the object as owned and kept, and groceries as ingredients bought, graded by quality; Culinary files dishes and what is eaten or drunk as a dish; Pleasure files the practice.

## Owner decisions on edge calls (2026-10-17)
- Pet food and animal feed are in, with animal equipment.
- Vitamins, dietary supplements and protein powders are in (filed under health unless the owner says otherwise).
- Tickets for a single event or journey, and one-off services, are out: the enjoyment is the experience, not the ticket. A kept ticket stub is in as a keepsake paper.
- Cryptocurrency holdings are out, like a bank balance; NFTs are in.
- Online services and accounts are in or out by **utility vs entertainment, not by price**: utility services (email, cloud storage, messaging, password vaults, maps and similar) are in whether free or paid; the internet as entertainment (social feeds, free video and content platforms) belongs to a separate taxonomy. Paid entertainment subscriptions are in as purchases.
- Grocery products that the Culinary taxonomy also holds (cheese, cured meats, fruit, tea, coffee, wine) are described here as bought, with their quality grade, and link to the Culinary kind.
- Cannabis and nicotine products are in, with tobacco.
- Baby and child goods have no category of their own; each goes to its functional home.

## Owner decisions on stage 2 (2026-10-17)
- **Stage-2 answers delegated to Claude:** "Your judgment for the answers." Claude answers the shared-convention, framework and per-category questions of framework/structure/STAGE2_OWNER_QUESTIONS.md and STAGE2_SUMMARY.md. The answers are in framework/structure/decisions/ (D1 shared and framework, D2 categories A–E, D3 categories F–J), each marked as delegated. The owner may overturn any of them; until then they bind like owner decisions.
- **No separate sourcing pass:** the owner questioned the need for sourcing passes compared with the Culinary taxonomy. Claims marked † in the stage-2 memos are attested when kinds are harvested in stage 3, as in the Culinary taxonomy. A decision that rests on an unverified fact is marked "provisional until attested".
- **Depth and breadth are the point of the research** (owner): "The research in this taxonomy will primarily be about depth and breadth". Examples given: under puzzles, the Japanese puzzle-box makers; fashion with its subcultures and brands; fragrances. So maker ledgers, lines and named forms have no per-category cap: they are seeded from attested lists and grow as far as the attested evidence goes. Makers, artisans, houses, workshops, perfumers and schools are recorded in ledgers off the tree, not as extra kinds. Scene and subculture tags are used wherever communities exist.
- **One source proves existence; no two-source rule** (owner): unlike Culinary and Pleasure, where research checked that a thing existed, here "if something is able to be bought it is real", and needing two sources "would diminish real, one of a kind items". A kind, named form, line or maker enters on one fetched page showing it is or was made or sold (a maker's or shop page, a catalogue, an auction or marketplace listing, a museum or trade record). A second source or a second region is never a condition of inclusion. Region or source counts may still help decide the level (kind against named form) or the weight of a tier scheme, but never whether an item is in. Individual objects (one particular painting or watch) stay out, as above.
- **Use existing catalogues first** (owner): "depending on category there will possibly be sources that already exist that we can utilize rather than searching blindly". Stage 3 harvests each category from the catalogues, databases, registries and community wikis that already list its makers, lines and named forms (for example perfume databases, record and game databases, coin and stamp catalogues, collector registries). Open web search fills the gaps. A source census per category comes before harvesting.
- **Board game genres belong to a future Interactive Entertainment taxonomy** (owner), together with video game genres and the internet as entertainment. Here a board, card or tabletop game is filed as an object (its physical form, publisher, edition and components). Game genres, mechanics and themes (worker placement, deck-building, eurogame against ameritrash) are never a level, kind or scene here. At most they are a link field to that future taxonomy.
