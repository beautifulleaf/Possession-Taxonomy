BATCH COVREF_A1. REFERENCE DISTRIBUTION OF THE WORLD'S CLOTHING BRANDS BY COUNTRY (web allowed; no git). Owner principle (BRIEF.md, "Coverage proportional to the world's distribution"): each category's brand coverage per country is compared with an outside reference of how that category's brands are spread across the world, built from fetched sources. Your job is to collect the raw proxy figures; the orchestrator combines them (tools/coverage_ref.py).
Web rules (CLAUDE.md): only WebSearch/WebFetch or mcp__Parallel_Auth__web_search / mcp__Parallel_Auth__web_fetch (allow_live_fetch on); never curl, wget or Python fetching, archives or caches; never build or guess a URL: fetch only URLs from a search result or a fetched page's links. A search excerpt may locate a figure but never attests it: every value you write must be visible on a page you fetched yourself.
RESUME RULE: append rows as you go; if your output exists, skip (country, proxy) pairs already written and continue. Log fetches in verify/work/COVREF_A1/fetched.tsv (url, fetch_status, what it gave). Helper files only in verify/work/COVREF_A1/.

Collect as many countries as possible (at least the 60 largest economies, and every country a source covers) for each proxy, from one consistent source family per proxy where possible:
P1 market — apparel (clothing) market size by country, in USD, one recent year (e.g. Statista Market Insights "Apparel" country pages, Euromonitor or other market-research pages that state a country's apparel market revenue, or a fetched table that ranks countries by apparel market). Prefer one source family for all countries; note the year.
P2 trademarks — trademark filings in Nice class 25 (clothing, footwear, headgear) by country of origin, or failing that by filing office (a national office's class-25 count; WIPO IP Statistics, WIPO World Intellectual Property Indicators, EUIPO, USPTO, CNIPA, national office annual reports). If class 25 by country cannot be found for many countries, record total trademark filing activity by origin (WIPO "trademark filing activity by origin", class counts all classes) as P2b, and say so.
P3 wikipedia — the number of pages in each country's subcategory of English Wikipedia "Category:Clothing brands by country" (and, as P3b, "Category:Clothing companies by country" or "...by nationality"): the category page shows each subcategory with its page count. Fetch the category page(s) and record every country listed.
P4 firms — number of enterprises in apparel manufacturing or apparel brands per country where a fetched statistical page states it (Eurostat NACE C14 enterprises, US Census NAICS 315, UNIDO, national statistics). Optional, as many as found.

Output verify/out/coverage_proxies_A1.tsv with columns: country (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2), proxy (P1, P2, P2b, P3, P3b, P4), value (a number, no thousands separators), unit, year, source (the fetched URL), fetch_status (full or partial), notes (≤ 20 words; e.g. "by office, not origin").
Report (under 250 words): per proxy, the source used, number of countries, the year, and known biases (e.g. trademark-volume inflation in some offices, English-Wikipedia bias), plus what you tried and failed. Working directory: /home/user/Possession-Taxonomy. Never run git.
