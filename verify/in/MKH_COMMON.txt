STAGE 3 MAKER HARVEST BY REGION (STAGE3_PLAN step 2b; owner principle BRIEF.md "Coverage proportional to the world's distribution"; R-80, R-98 c). Web allowed; no git.
Web rules (CLAUDE.md): only WebSearch/WebFetch (the Parallel tools are out of credit); never curl, wget or Python fetching, archives or caches; never build or guess a URL: fetch only URLs from a search result or a fetched page's links (follow pagination links a page shows, never construct them). A search excerpt may locate a maker but never attests it; every row needs a page you fetched yourself.
RESUME RULE: append rows as you go; if an output exists, read it, skip ids already written and continue. Log fetches in verify/work/<BATCH>/fetched.tsv (url, fetch_status full/partial/failed, what it gave). Helper files only in verify/work/<BATCH>/.
Job: every maker (brand, manufacturer, producer, publisher or studio) of category <CODE> based in the countries of your task, big and small, active or defunct (status when stated). The category's tree is verify/out/TREE_<CODE>.md and its kinds are the rows of data/kinds.tsv whose code is <CODE>. The reference share of each country is in verify/out/coverage_reference_<CODE>.tsv: spend effort in proportion to it, and do not stop at the famous names.
Sources, best first, searching in each country's own languages as well as English: national industry or trade association member directories; trade-fair exhibitor lists that give the exhibitor's country; national-language Wikipedia categories and lists of companies or brands of this category; retailer and marketplace brand A–Z directories of the country; government export-promotion directories; national trade press lists. The hints in your task name some.
Rules:
- country = where the maker is based (HQ, as the page states or clearly implies; a national association's member list or a "brands from X" list states it). Makers based elsewhere stay out of your batch.
- Before creating a maker_id, search data/makers.tsv by name and obvious variants, and grep verify/out/MKH_<CODE>_*_makers.tsv (parallel batches); reuse an existing id (write the row again with country filled; the writer merges). A same-name maker of a different trade (a clothing brand, say) is a homonym: give yours a distinguishing id (M-<name>-<trade word>).
- maker_id = M- plus the name in lower case, ASCII letters and digits only, words joined by "-". maker_class from framework/structure/CONVENTIONS_v1.md C1.
Outputs (TSV, tab-separated, header row exactly as given; no tabs or line breaks in fields; several sources joined with " | ", one fetch_status per source in the same order; fetch_status full or partial only):
- verify/out/<BATCH>_makers.tsv: the makers columns of verify/in/HV_COMMON.txt (maker_id, name, native_name, maker_class, supply_role, country, place, founded, ended, status, parent_id, licensor_id, authority_id, school_ids, extension, notes, sources, fetch_status). notes ≤ 20 words.
- verify/out/<BATCH>_maker_kinds.tsv: offer_id, maker_id, kind_id, notes, sources, fetch_status — the <CODE> kinds a fetched page shows the maker makes or sells; offer_id = MK-<maker_id without M->-<kind_id>. A maker whose page shows only the category, not a kind, gets no row here: the loader ties it at category level (R-76).
- optional verify/out/<BATCH>_named_forms.tsv (HV_COMMON columns, named_form_id NF-<kind_id>-K<nnn>) when a maker page names a model or product line inside one kind.
Report (under 250 words): makers per country, the sources that served best and those that failed, and any generic type of this category you saw that the tree lacks ("missing kind?" with URL).
