## 1. Named entries come from a canon, never a selection
Every kind that has named entries (`x`) states:
- `nl`: what one named entry is, in one plain sentence ("One entry is a soft-drink brand sold under its own name; diet, zero-sugar and flavour variants are versions.").
- `sel`: which fetched reference(s) the list follows and any scope cut. Include EVERY entry of that reference that falls in scope: big complete lists are preferred to a picked subset. Where no whole-space reference exists, say so and name the overviews used.
- `xg` and each entry's `g` when sections help (the field's own axis: country, owner, generation, region). Order within a section by the axis, then year, then name.
- Prominence is a badge: `role` = "origin" (first of its kind or lineage, per a fetched page) or "landmark" (named by a fetched page as best-selling, defining or most influential). At most about one entry in five.
- Versions (`v`) are what changes within the entry: revisions, generations, flavours, sizes that the reference treats as variants. Each {"n","y","d"}.
- No loose models: every former model (`m`) becomes an entry, a version of one, or is removed as out of scope (say which in the report). Ranges (`r`) only for a maker's range that spans many entries.
- Kinds that are commodities (no canon, no brands worth naming) keep no entries: the kind is the end.

## 2. The market layer (where the market is the point: groceries first)
Market facts sit on the entries, and the department gets a short sourced market summary. The page shows the same entries a second way, by market.
- On every branded entry: `own` (current owner company, exact name), `tier` (one of: "Global group", "National champion", "Independent", "Craft & indie", "Own label"), `cty` (home country of the brand), and `own_y` (year the current owner acquired it, if a fetched page gives it).
  - Global group: brand owned by one of the multinational groups that dominate the category worldwide.
  - National champion: a brand that leads or is iconic in its home market, whoever owns it now (Irn-Bru, Inca Kola, Thums Up); `own` still records the owner.
  - Independent: an established brand owned by a company that is not a global group.
  - Craft & indie: small-batch, artisanal or new-wave brands named by a fetched craft or indie overview.
- On the department: `ms` = list of market facts, each {"mkt":"US carbonated soft drinks","who":"The Coca-Cola Company","share":"44.6 %","yr":2024,"s":"<fetched url>","note":"volume or value, as the page says"}. Only figures a fetched page states, with the year. No figure is better than an unsourced one.
- Coverage: the global groups' complete brand portfolios in the category (from each group's own brand page or a fetched list), national champions for the largest markets with every world region represented (aim for about 20 markets), and independents and craft from published lists or overviews.
- Brand register: add every new brand owner or brand used as `mk`/`own` to rebuild/out/<CODE>_brands.json with country and founding year only if a fetched page gives them.
