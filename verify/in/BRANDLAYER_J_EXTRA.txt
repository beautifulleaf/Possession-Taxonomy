EXTRA FOR ALL J (groceries) CATEGORIES — owner decisions 2026-10-31 (BRIEF.md):
- What to find: (a) premium, craft and high-quality producers ("a specific luxury / high end / craft brand fetch ... obviously not any given label at the dollar store"); (b) culturally relevant everyday brands, even when not luxury ("Coca Cola isn't 'Luxury' but it's universal ... cultural relevance in general"); (c) the market layer (owner groups and their brands, national champions, independents and craft), from rankings and market reports with year.
- Awards: use every year findable, not just the latest ("Every year for awards"). Juried competitions with blind tasting are quality; reader or public votes are standing.
- Retailers and shops (specialist grocers, butchers, fishmongers, cheesemongers, delicatessens, online craft stores) are acceptable as sources and as sellers (role "seller"); their brand lists prove existence only.
- Protected names (PDO/PGI/GI, appellations, DOC/DOCG, AOC) are designations: a region with a protected name counts as renowned (DESIGNATION AS RENOWN); producers named on a consortium's or register's member list are cultural standing.
- Diversity: push regional discovery hard — every cuisine's producers, not only Western Europe and North America.
- Owner pastes may arrive for big award tables (Great Taste, World Cheese Awards, IWSC, Decanter, SFWSC, World Beer Cup, International Chocolate Awards, Cup of Excellence, NYIOOC, Good Food Awards — verify/PASTE_PLAN.md): check verify/retail/ for any pasted file for your category and use it as a fetched page.
- Recipes and dishes belong to the Culinary taxonomy; packaged prepared foods are J5.
