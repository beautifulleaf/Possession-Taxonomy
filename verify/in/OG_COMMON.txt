(2) VARIETIES: named cultivars, club cultivars, breeds and strains that shoppers meet on labels and in shops: national variety lists and plant variety rights registers (CPVO, USDA GRIN and PVP, national lists, UPOV member offices), breed societies and FAO DAD-IS breed pages, university extension and variety-trial pages, growers' and nurseries' variety pages, Wikipedia lists of cultivars or breeds. A certification (organic, MSC, free range as a label) is not a grade unless a law or standard sets it as a ladder or class (EU egg farming codes 0–3 are a classification scale; write them).
