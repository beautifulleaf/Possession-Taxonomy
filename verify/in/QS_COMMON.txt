How signals are stored:
- A scheme (award, rating, test, list) is one row in schemes: scheme_id SCH-<owner short>-<name>, lower-case ASCII joined by "-"; scale_class one of: ii (third-party measured score or rank), iii (editorial, jury or community class); steps = the levels best first joined by " > " (e.g. Super Gold > Gold > Silver > Bronze); direction higher-better or lower-better; test_version (year or test protocol where results change by edition); stale_after (a date: for yearly awards the edition year + 3 years, YYYY-12-31; for lab tests the test date + 2 years); kinds_served = kind ids joined by " | ".
- A result is one tier_entries row: tier_entry_id TE-<scheme short>-<subject short>-<edition>; subject_id and subject_type (named_form, maker or line; kind only where your task says the rated subject is a species from a source and method); value = the level or score as published; rank when published; direction as the scheme; scope = what the entry covers (product name as entered, size, origin and method for a species rating); test_version and date (edition date or year); stale_after as the scheme.
- A certification measured on the object (80 PLUS, Cybenetics) is a standard (standards: standard_id STD-<name>, name, authority, steps best first " > ") and a standard_values row per certified product (sv_id SV-<standard>-<subject>, subject_id = named form id, standard_id, value = the level).
- The rated product: if it is already a named form, use its id. Otherwise add it as a named form under the right kind (named_form_id NF-<kind_id>-<BATCH>-<n>, three digits; name as entered; maker_bound yes; maker_id) and its maker (makers row: maker_id M-<name slug>, name, maker_class, supply_role, country only if the page states it, notes ≤ 15 words) plus a maker_kinds row (offer_id MK-<BATCH>-<n>, maker_id, kind_id). For groceries this is the R-115 b exception: a producer named by a quality source enters. Never add a product whose kind is not in the categories your task names; count those skips.
- An attribute your task names (season and the like) is one attributes row: attr_id AT-<subject>-<attribute>-<n>, subject_id, subject_type (kind, named_form or variety), attribute, value, scope, notes.
