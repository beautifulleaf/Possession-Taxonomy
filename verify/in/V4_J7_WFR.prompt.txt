Scope: wine of France, including Champagne and French sweet and fortified wines (vin doux naturel). Wine is place-first: named entry = an appellation (protected designation of origin or geographical indication for wine, or a national wine region/AVA in countries without such a system), complete from the official or reference list (EU register eAmbrosia via its pages or Wikipedia lists of AOC/DOC/DO/DOP/PDO wines; US TTB AVA list; national lists elsewhere). Versions = classified producers or vineyards where a classification exists (Bordeaux 1855, Saint-Émilion, Graves, Burgundy grands crus, Champagne grand cru villages, VDP Grosse Lage, Barolo/Barbaresco MGAs, Port and Madeira styles), each with its rank in `d` and the top rank as `role`=landmark on the appellation only when a fetched page says so. Colour and style (red, white, rosé, sparkling, sweet, fortified) and permitted grapes go in `tt` per appellation. Departments: one per country group in your scope; shelves: regions; kinds: sub-regions or the appellation tier the country uses (e.g. "Bordeaux: Left Bank appellations"); entries = appellations. Producers outside classifications are not entries (they belong in the brand register only if already there). Remove the old grape-based kinds of your scope via renames to the new kind that holds the most of their wines; the grape-variety register is a separate part (GRAPES).
