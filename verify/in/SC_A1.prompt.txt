BATCH SC_A1. SCENE CENSUS FOR CLOTHING (web allowed; no git). Owner decision (BRIEF.md, "Owner decisions on navigation"): clothing is navigated scene first: scene clusters → the brands in each → their signature pieces. This batch builds the scene vocabulary; brand membership is harvested next, per cluster.
Web rules (CLAUDE.md): only WebSearch/WebFetch or mcp__Parallel_Auth__web_search / mcp__Parallel_Auth__web_fetch (allow_live_fetch on); never curl, wget or Python fetching, archives or caches; never guessed URLs. A search excerpt may locate a scene but never defines it: every scene row needs a page you fetched that describes it as a style, scene or subculture.
RESUME RULE: append rows as you go; if an output exists, continue after its last row. Log fetches in verify/work/SC_A1/fetched.tsv (url, fetch_status, what it gave). Helper files only in verify/work/SC_A1/.
Read first: BRIEF.md (owner rules: depth and breadth, no caps, one fetched page proves existence, game/music/film genres are never divisions here: a music scene counts only as a clothing scene with its own dress code and brands, e.g. punk, goth, hip-hop fashion); framework/structure/CONVENTIONS_v1.md C8; framework/structure/A1.md (section 1 and 2 sources: the MFA wiki guides, Subculture Wiki, Aesthetics Wiki, Heddels, DenimHunters, Lolibrary, the Japanese and Korean and Chinese scene sources, disco guides); data/source_catalogues.tsv rows for A1.
Task: build the scene vocabulary for clothing, worldwide, as wide as the fetched sources allow. Seed list (each needs its own fetched definition; add every further scene the sources name): workwear and Americana heritage, Japanese denim and amekaji, ivy and trad, preppy, classic tailoring and sartorial, British countryside, rockabilly and greaser, mod, skinhead and terrace casual, punk, goth, emo and scene, grunge, metal, hip-hop, streetwear, skate, surf, techwear, gorpcore and outdoor, athleisure, quiet luxury and old money, luxury houses, avant-garde and archive fashion (Japanese and Antwerp designers), minimalism, Lolita (classic, gothic, sweet), Ura-Hara, gyaru, Harajuku decora, fairy kei, visual kei, mori, Korean street fashion, guochao and hanfu revival, y2k, cottagecore, dark academia, western wear, military surplus, cycling and motorcycle (rider) fashion, burner and festival, traditional dress worldwide where shoppers buy it as a style.
Clusters: group scenes into about 8 to 15 clusters a shopper would recognise (e.g. Heritage & workwear; Tailoring & classic; Street & skate; Outdoor & technical; Japanese street fashion; Alternative & music subcultures; Luxury & designer; Avant-garde & archive; Revival & retro; Online aesthetics; Traditional & regional dress). Use the sources' own groupings where they exist.
Outputs:
- verify/out/SC_A1_scenes.tsv: scene_id, name, native_name, cluster, parent_id, domains, description, definition_source, sources, fetch_status. scene_id = SC- plus the name in lower case ASCII joined by "-"; parent_id for a sub-scene (classic Lolita under Lolita); domains "A1" (add A2, A3, A4 where the source says the scene has its own footwear, jewellery or bags); description ≤ 25 words in your own words; definition_source = the fetched URL that defines it (also first in sources); fetch_status per source (full, partial).
- verify/out/SC_A1_brand_sources.tsv: scene_id, url, kind_of_source (scene guide, shop scene collection, community wiki list, brand directory), approx_brands, fetched (yes/no), notes: the pages that list the brands of each scene, for the membership harvest.
Report (under 250 words): counts of scenes and clusters, scenes you could not define from a fetched page, and the strongest brand-list sources.
