## Regions (4)

- **South Asia (Pakistan, Gujrat-Gujranwala fan cluster)**: electric fans: ~98% of Pakistan's fan output from Gujrat and Gujranwala, 70+ year industry, ~550 makers, exported worldwide. Brands: Royal Fans, GFC Fans, Khurshid (Khursheed) Fans, Super Asia, Tamoor Fans, Shaheen Fans, Al-Ahmad, Starco. [src](https://tdap.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Fan-Brochure.pdf)
- **Southeast Asia (Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand)**: local electric fan makers as household names: Hatari (Thailand, largest fan share), Cosmos and Miyako (Indonesia), Senko (Vietnam). Brands: Cosmos, Miyako, Maspion, Hatari, Senko. [src](https://ecatalog.sinarmasland.com/article/gaya-hidup-hobi/cosmos-vs-miyako-4-perbandingan-menarik-dua-merek-ini)
- **South Asia (India, desert/evaporative air coolers)**: desert and personal air coolers, a mass Indian category; Symphony is the world's largest air-cooler company. Brands: Symphony, Havells, Voltas, Bajaj Electricals, Kenstar, Orient Electric, Crompton. [src](https://www.reportprime.com/air-cooler-r18911/company)
- **Britain (paraffin heaters, mid-20th century)**: portable paraffin room heaters, a domestic icon until the 1970s; rival fuels Aladdin Pink and Esso Blue. Brands: Aladdin (Blue Flame heaters), Valor. [src](https://scottishshale.co.uk/stories/paraffin-heaters)
