## Regions (8)
- Mallorca (Spain): hydraulic cement tile (baldosa hidráulica) tradition; myriad Mallorcan makers supplied Catalonia and Spain in the 1930s (Huguet) - https://www.remodelista.com/posts/huguet-mallorca-tiles
- Morocco (Marrakech, Tangier): handmade encaustic cement tile (carreaux de ciment) and zellige (Popham Design, Mosaic Factory) - https://www.owlandvine.co.uk/blog/the-best-tile-suppliers-and-products-for-every-style-and-budget
- Sweden (Jönköping, Tidaholm): safety-match industry; Swedish Match makes national match brands worldwide (Solstickan, Swan Vestas, Fiat Lux, Redheads, Tres Estrellas, Quinas) - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Match
- Kenya: cement (Bamburi, Blue Triangle, Simba) and East African paint industry (Bamburi Cement, Blue Triangle, Simba Cement, Nyumba Cement, Crown Paints Kenya) - https://constructionkenya.com/5190/biggest-cement-firms-kenya/
- Nabeul (Tunisia): hand-painted faience tiles, Andalusian tin-glaze tradition; 350+ workshops (Kharraz, Kedidi, Ben Sedrine) - https://archive.aramcoworld.com/issue/200302/tunisia.s.center.of.ceramics.htm
- Santa Catarina (Brazil): Criciúma / Cocal do Sul ceramic tile cluster (Eliane) - https://www.eliane.com/institucional?lang=en
- Kishū / Wakayama (Japan): binchōtan white charcoal from ubame oak; Minabe produces more than any other town (Wakayama Prefectural Charcoal Cooperative, Nakasho Shoten) - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binch%C5%8Dtan
- China: ceramic tile mass production: three Chinese groups in the world top ten (Ceramic World Review 2024) (Marco Polo, Dongpeng, Monalisa, New Pearl) - https://ceramicworldweb.com/en/economics-and-markets/worlds-top-ceramic-tile-manufacturers-2024
