# B1 Furniture & Storage, BRANDLAYER notes

Status: partial. brand_led = true (furniture, mattresses, nursery furniture, outdoor furniture are strongly brand-led; spare parts and anonymous carpentry are not).

Result: 307 new brands (110 premium, 197 cultural), 40 sources, 16 regions.

What worked
- Wikipedia category trees (English country subcategories, de/fr/it/nl/pt/sv/no/tr/pl/ja/zh category pages): cultural layer.
- French EPV label list (data.economie.gouv.fr): the fetched list has no per-company sector, so only names with seat/sofa/bedding/upholsterer/cabinetmaker words were taken (19). More furniture EPV firms are likely hidden under neutral names.
- Compasso d'Oro 2024/2025/2026, Red Dot seating ranking 2010-2014: premium by design award.
- Stiftung Warentest mattress test via a news report (Breckle, F.A.N., Ravensberger): affordable quality, independent test.
- Specialist retailer directories (Carolina Furniture, Daniel House, Japanese furniture shops, Sleepworks, Comosum), DENMARK-DESIGN manufacturer directory.

Blocked or weak
- Parallel_Auth fetch returned 402 (no credit); only the plain Parallel fetch was used.
- Which? mattress brand table, Wirecutter office chair/sofa picks, Consumer Reports and test.de returned no full text (paywalled or script-rendered). Their best-buy brand lists are therefore NOT in the register: worth a retry with Firecrawl if wanted.
- Royal Warrant Holders directory not enumerated (only the Hypnos entry page seen). Italian Marchi Storici, Red Dot Best of the Best product search, iF, German Design Award, Good Design Award and Interior Innovation Award winner lists were not fetched (search only found news items), so award coverage is incomplete (only Red Dot seating ranking 2010-2014 and three Compasso d'Oro editions).
- Sweeps: kind sweep covers only the sections with a logged query; sections marked "not searched separately" (specialist tables, racks/hooks/hangers, screens, plant stands, spare parts) were not searched. Regions Central Asia, North Africa and Sub-Saharan Africa and Latin America produced no brand that a fetched independent page names (Bonobo Ghana, Jomo Furniture, Etel, Tidelli, Luteca, Asghar, Levantiques appeared only in search excerpts or their own pages and were left out).
- Caveats: some Wikipedia-category names are corporate groups or retailers-with-own-brand (e.g. Heal's, Roche Bobois, Murphy Door, California Closets); kitchen makers (Bulthaup, Poggenpohl, SieMatic, Boffi, Snaidero, Scavolini, Cucine Lube, Arclinea) and lighting firms were deliberately left out as outside B1. Chinese listed groups (zh.wikipedia) were noted but not added except four from the English category.
- Parallel fetch results do not save to a file for small pages, so names were typed into scripts from the raw returned text (large results were parsed from the saved JSON).

Entries flagged "pending_retail": true are names that also appear in a pending retailer harvest.
