# VERIFY G1 Vehicles (BRANDLAYER)

Checked 2008 brands: harvest 1,925 minus 78 audit drops and 97 merged rows (now aliases), plus 61 register-name merge rows, 100 homonym rows, 24 cross-tag rows (23 audit + Rába), 70 audit additions and 3 MOVES rows (Blaupunkt, Sugino, Smittybilt). Not shown: 1 (Hashim's Beypore uru yard). By tier: cultural 1512, listed 446, quality 49, seller 1. Homonyms 111, cross_tags 26, aliases 98.

Regions: 15 (10 with renown, 5 without).

## Method
- The Wikipedia car, motorcycle, bicycle and Coachbuilder lists and TheFramebuilders were checked by script against the harvester's raw saves (G1_tmp/cars.txt, moto.txt, bike.txt, coach.txt, fb.txt) and the audit's row locator (AUDIT_G1_tmp/locate.json). Every other harvest page, all audit-addition pages and all region pages were re-fetched raw (Parallel web_fetch, full_content) into VERIFY_G1_tmp/pages/, and every name was confirmed by script (VERIFY_G1_tmp/build.py, lib.py, regions.json).
- The country field comes from AUDIT_G1_tmp/country_fix.json, never from the list's Active/Former or A-Z columns. USA/RO are normalised to United States/Romania; navbox 'References' values are gone with their dropped rows.
- Excerpt-mode audit pages were all re-fetched raw. NPR's raw fetch is truncated, so its syndicated full copy on VPM (src2) was used, and it names Barkat Ali and Hassan Autos. The Hindu (Beypore) returns only its title, so Hashim's yard is shown false; the Beypore renown comes from Wikipedia Uru (boat). One guessed URL (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_Motors) returned a 404 and was not used.
