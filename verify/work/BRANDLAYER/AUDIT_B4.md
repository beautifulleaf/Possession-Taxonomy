## Notes

Accuracy: 25/25 sample names found on their src pages (fetched raw); 0 wrong. Caveats: Laguna B shown as 'Laguna~B'; Müller Kleinkunst and Herrnhuter Sterne appear in machine-translated form on nutcrackerland ('Müller cabaret', 'Herrnhut stars'); 14 of 25 rest on Wikipedia categories/templates or a consumer agarbatti listicle, which prove existence only, not quality.
