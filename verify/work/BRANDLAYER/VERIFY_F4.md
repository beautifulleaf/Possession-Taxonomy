Rule used for renown: an independent page's statement, or an official designation of the place-named product (national traditional-craft designation, GI registration, national ICH listing), which is counted as renown and marked as a designation. A maker's own page never counts. A brand's 'region' is set wherever a region entry names it and that entry's src or src2 page shows the name, renown or not.
