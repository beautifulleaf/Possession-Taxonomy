## Additions: 36 brands, 9 regions

- **Neiliansheng** (China, cultural): Beijing time-honored cloth-shoe maker founded 1853, made Qing court boots; its qiancengdi cloth-shoe craft on China's national intangible cultural heritage list (2008) [src](https://www.chinatoday.com.cn/ctenglish/2018/cs/201803/t20180308_800120841.html)
- **Nanyang** (Thailand, cultural): leading Thai canvas shoe and ChangDao flip-flop maker since 1953, 300m+ pairs; declared 'The Pride of Thai' by the Thai government [src](https://nanyang.co.th/home-eng)
- **Biti's** (Vietnam, cultural): 'The Pride of a Vietnamese Footwear Brand': leading Vietnamese footwear maker since 1982 (Biti's Hunter sneakers) [src](https://vietnam.vnanet.vn/english/tin-tuc/bitis-the-pride-of-a-vietnamese-footwear-brand-129644.html)
- **Wojas** (Poland, cultural): maker's page: 'one of the largest and most respected Polish footwear manufacturers', Nowy Targ (Podhale), ~180 stores; supplies uniformed services [src](https://www.wojas.eu/en_EE/company/about-us)
- **Servis** (Pakistan, cultural): Servis shoes: opened Pakistan's first organized shoe factory (Gujrat, 1959); overtook Bata Pakistan in sales by 2007; brands Servis, Cheetah, Ndure [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Service_Industries_Limited)
- **Kaptaan Chappal (ChaCha Noor Din)** (Pakistan, cultural): Peshawar chappal maker Nooruddin Shinwari; his chappal for Imran Khan became the widely known 'Kaptaan Chappal', earlier made Peshawari chappals for Benazir Bhutto [src](https://kaptaanchappal.net/story-of-kaptaan-chappal)
- **Paez** (Argentina, cultural): La Nación: Argentine brand that turned the alpargata into an aspirational shoe ('no existía una marca referente'), compared to Havaianas; 550,000 pairs a year [src](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/economia/paez-se-reinventa-para-seguir-dando-batalla-con-sus-alpargatas-nid1923110/)
- **Bosi** (Colombia, cultural): Colombian leather shoe maker founded 1975, 160+ stores; page links Superindustria naming Bosi a 'marca notoria' (2020); 'orgullosamente colombiana' [src](https://www.larepublica.co/empresas/entrevista-con-la-gerente-general-de-bosi-clemencia-velez-4244283)
- **Vélez** (Colombia, cultural): Colombian leather house; shoes and boots 'hecha a mano, uno a uno, por artesanos expertos' (maker's own claim) [src](https://www.velez.com.co/)
- **Samello** (Brazil, cultural): Franca (SP) men's shoe brand, 'uma das marcas mais reconhecidas no segmento de calçados e acessórios de couro' (maker's claim) [src](https://www.samello.com.br/index.php?information_id=2&route=information%2Finformation)
- **Zapateria (Sta. Ana family)** (Philippines, premium): Marikina shoe brand and hub run by the Sta. Ana cobbler family, making shoes since 1887 for five generations [src](https://www.spot.ph/newsfeatures/culture/108781/zapateria-hub-marikina-interview-feature-a4373-20240501-lfrm)
- **Cayucas** (Mexico, cultural): huaraches handwoven by artisans in Sahuayo, Michoacán, 'a town known for generations of huarache-making' [src](https://www.cayucashoes.com/)
- **Kineya Tabi** (Japan, cultural): tabi maker established 1929 in Gyoda, 'a leading center for the Tabi industry'; traditional methods, also tabi running shoes (Muteki) [src](https://www.kineyatabi.com/)
- **Marugo** (Japan, cultural): jika-tabi maker since 1919 (Kurashiki); integrated jika-tabi production; 'Kurashiki long history company' award 2020 [src](https://www.marugo.ne.jp/en)
- **Tsujiya Honten (辻屋本店)** (Japan, premium): Asakusa wasō hakimono specialist founded 1912; resident artisans fit geta/zōri/setta hanao to each foot; all hand-made from selected makers [src](https://getaya.jp/about/)
- **Gakuya (ガクヤ)** (Japan, cultural): tabi maker founded 1922 in Gyoda ('足袋の行田か、行田の足袋か'); still domestic production [src](https://gakuya.co.jp/gakuya)
- **Boot Black (Columbus)** (Japan, premium): Shoegazing shoe-care guide: Japanese premium range by Columbus, 'when the Japanese do something, they often do it properly'; Columbus 'the biggest shoe care company in Japan' [src](https://shoegazing.com/2021/07/30/guide-shoe-care-brands)

## Notes

Accuracy: all 25 sampled names were on their source pages. 1 is wrong (EG Cappelli: the Skoaktiebolaget menu lists it under TIES; it makes Neapolitan ties, not shoes). The sample came 15/25 from The World of Shoes A-Z and 5/25 from The International Man A-Z. Their claims are only 'listed in directory', which is true but shows no quality or cultural standing. The International Man A-Z extraction (134 names) is the weakest block: it has truncation artefacts (London, The London), duplicates and misspellings (Luchese, Lowa Europe/Lowa Usa), retailers (Styledrops, Diane B., Talbots), licences (Steve Harvey) and a non-footwear house (Pineider). Review the whole block, not just the 12 drops listed. The World of Shoes 'dept' field is generic: Farfalla and Broadland are slipper makers but are labelled welted/bespoke shoes. Country is blank on all directory rows. Omissions: the harvest is very strong on Western premium and heritage makers. Almost every expert-level European, US, Japanese-welted and Australian name I tested was already known. Gaps were (1) shoe care: no polish or care brand beyond Saphir and Paul Brunngard; Boot Black, Columbus, Collonil, Famaco, Tarrago, Siegol, Burgol, Turms, Brift-H and others were added from the Shoegazing guide. (2) Everyday national icons outside Western Europe and the US: Neiliansheng, Nanyang, Biti's, Compass, Servis, Wojas, Bosi, Velez, Paez and Samello were added. (3) Traditional makers: Kineya, Marugo and Gakuya (tabi), Tsujiya Honten (geta and zori), Kaptaan Chappal (Peshawari), Melissinos (Greek sandals), Rondini (Tropezienne), Sandgrens and Sven (clogs), Cayucas (huarache) and Zapateria (Marikina) were added. Regions added: Peshawar, Marikina, Sahuayo, Gyoda, Kurashiki, Athens, Saint-Tropez, Kermanshah/Senejan giveh, Aba/Ariaria. Still not found with a fetched page naming a maker: Moroccan babouche (Fez/Marrakech, only shop listings), Serbian opanak makers, Russian valenki (Kukmor has only a shop page; Ralf Ringer went bankrupt in 2024, no attestation fetched), Central Asian boots, Turkish yemeni makers, Korean brands (SCMP Suecomma Bonnie page came back empty), Kolhapuri and jutti named makers, and Franca (Brazil) and Leon (Mexico) as regions with a renown page.
