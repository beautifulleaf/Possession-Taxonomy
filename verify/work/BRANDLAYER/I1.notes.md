# I1 Software - BRANDLAYER notes
brand_led: true (software makers/products are brands), games excluded per owner ruling (E1/E5).
Method: Wikipedia year tables (Apple Design Awards 1997-2025) and ~45 Wikipedia software categories (cultural), AV-TEST 2024/2025 and AV-Comparatives 2025 (premium), Google Play Best of 2023-25, PCMag roundups (only intro excerpts returned).
Register covers makers (Adobe, Kaspersky...) but few product brands; products whose maker is already registered were skipped. Product-level names (Obsidian, KeePass, REAPER-type) recorded only where the product is itself the brand.
Register-size caveat: many names are products of known makers; the merge should map them (e.g. Cakewalk->BandLab, Soundtrap->Spotify, Skylum Luminar).
Homonyms: Things (Cultured Code) vs Things Inc (not recorded); Reunion, Warp, Notion (Notion Labs vs Notion music software not recorded), Bear (Shiny Frog) qualified.
