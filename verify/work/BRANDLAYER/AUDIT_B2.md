## Regions

- Kerala (Alleppey/Alappuzha), India: Coir door mats and mattings woven from coconut fibre; Alleppey is the centre of the Indian coir industry (Aspinwall, William Goodacre & Sons, The Alleppey Company)
- Bengal (Bangladesh and West Bengal): Nakshi kantha embroidered quilts and throws (Aarong, Kumudini Handicrafts, Karika, Arshi, Sasha, House of Wandering Silk)
- Caucasus (Azerbaijan, Armenia): Caucasian hand-knotted pile and flatweave carpets (Azerbaijani carpet UNESCO-listed 2010) (Azerkhalcha, Megerian Carpet, Artsakh Carpets, Tufenkian)
- Venice and Lyon: Historic silk furnishing-fabric weaving on hand looms (velvets, damasks, lampas) (Tessitura Luigi Bevilacqua, Rubelli, Fortuny, Prelle, Tassinari & Chatel)
- Isfahan and Fars, Iran: Fine Isfahan workshop carpets and Ghashghai/Luri gabbehs (Seirafian, Zollanvari, Hossein Rezvani)
- Lithuania: Linen weaving (bed, table, kitchen linen) (Siulas, Linas)
- Nara, Japan: Nara-sarashi ramie and kaya mosquito-net cloth used for fukin kitchen cloths (Nakagawa Masashichi Shoten)
- Kyrgyzstan (Naryn, Kochkor): Shyrdak and ala-kiyiz felt carpets (UNESCO urgent safeguarding 2012) (Altyn Kol)
- Mali (Segou, Djenne): Bogolan mudcloth bed covers, throws and wall hangings (Ndomo, Tanti Bogolan Womens Association)
