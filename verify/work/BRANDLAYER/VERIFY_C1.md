## Tier rules applied
- cultural: Wikipedia category members (en Knife manufacturing companies, Machine tool builders, Striking tool manufacturers; de Werkzeughersteller); list or navbox rows that link the brand's own article (List of tool manufacturers, the Hand tool manufacturers navbox on Sutton Tools); own articles (Tramontina, Kirloskar Brothers, Megger, Kyeyang Electric, Werner Co., Wacker Neuson, Delta Plus, Sutton Tools); designated-craft producer lists (Echigo Sanjo Blacksmith Group, Echigo Yoita Uchihamono Union); Superbrands Indonesia (Tekiro).
- quality: PTIA winners/finalists; EPV state label; HKJ (lygte-info) high-end multimeter test; Stiftung Warentest via Galaxus; Outdoor Life hands-on test; ACHR News Dealer Design Awards (Testo); certified Traditional Craftsmen on the Takenaka Carpentry Tools Museum Miki flyer; CENS trade press stating standards tests or awards (Proxene, Redai, Allprofessional); BBC feature (Wood Tools Ltd, excerpt only); A Concord Carpenter hands-on review (Tsunoda).
- listed: rows on the List of tool manufacturers without own article (brands in the third column, e.g. Nicholson, Cleco, Belzer) and the five unlinked Solingen makers (Hubertus, Linder, Herder, Martor, Eickhorn, Puma); retailer and distributor lists; ToolGuyd reader comments; Ronix, Zhongdi and CNPP listicles; market research (IMARC, Daxue); forums; own sites; ALI member directory (6 ladder makers re-sourced there).
