RTINGS monitor rows: KOORUI and AOC tagged premium are likely budget picks (not re-checked); HyperX dept 'monitors' wrong.
NOT CONSUMER-FACING Wikipedia rows (listed in drop): Tyan, Seventeam, Delta Electronics, Supermicro, Mylex, Gumstix, VIA, Winbond, Micron, Innodisk, Infortrend, Optiarc, Foxconn, Star Micronics, Graphtec, Mutoh, Emdoor, Korea Data Systems, Elektronorgtechnica, Norsk Data, and industrial 3D printing (Desktop Metal, Markforged, Carbon, EnvisionTEC, Voxeljet, Massivit, Solidscape, Mcor).
