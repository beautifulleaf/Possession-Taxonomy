## Add regions
- **Jena (Thuringia, Germany)**: precision optical instruments and optical glass: Carl Zeiss, Ernst Abbe and Otto Schott's 'Zeiss Works', 'the first enterprise in Germany and probably in the world for manufacturing high-precision optical instruments' (Carl Zeiss, Schott). https://rotarynewsonline.org/jena-germanys-city-of-glass/
- **Heerbrugg (St. Gallen Rhine Valley, Switzerland)**: surveying and optical instruments: Heinrich Wild's workshop for precision mechanics and optics founded 1921, became world-renowned Leica Geosystems; Kern & Co in Aarau linked (Wild Heerbrugg, Kern & Co.). https://leica-geosystems.com/about-us/news-room/customer-magazine/reporter-89/100-years-of-innovation-in-heerbrugg
- **Rathenow (Brandenburg, Germany)**: 'Stadt der Optik': cradle of the German optical industry from Duncker's 1801 Optische Industrie-Anstalt; Emil Busch products of worldwide repute; 163 optical firms by 1896, 200 by 1926; Rathenower Optische Werke (ROW) from 1948 (J.H.A. Duncker (Optische Industrie-Anstalt), Emil Busch AG (Rathenower Optische Werke)). https://www.rathenow.de/fileadmin/rathenow-de/Tourismus/PDF/2023/Rathenow_Imagebroschuere_Download.pdf
- **Ambala Cantonment (Haryana, India)**: India's scientific-instrument cluster: over 2,000 units making laboratory glassware, educational instruments and scientific apparatus (microscope parts, models), turnover over Rs 3,000 crore, exporting worldwide; no individual maker named on the page (no maker named). https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/ambala-scientific-industrys-survival-at-stake-due-to-cheaper-chinese-imports
