## Accuracy
25 harvested brands sampled with random.seed(7); each src fetched raw (Parallel web_fetch, full_content). **0/25 wrong**: all names shown on their page and claims supported. Brokis/SkLO/Feiss etc. checked on Lumens only (Lightology half of the claim not rechecked).

## Drops (7)
- Aputure: film/photo LED lights: studio and photo lights are D4 by the B3 test
- Theben: German maker of time switches, motion detectors and building controls; on Lampenwelt only for sensors, not a lighting brand
- Eutrac: track/rail systems for luminaires: built-in track is G3
- Thomas Research Products: LED drivers and surge protectors (B2B components), no consumer lighting footprint
- Tridonic: control gear/LED drivers for luminaire makers (B2B components), not a consumer lighting brand
- Lighting Ever: Amazon budget label (OutdoorGearLab budget pick), no cultural footprint
- ED Ellen DeGeneres: celebrity licensing label produced by Generation Lighting (already listed); no separate maker identity - consider merging rather than listing
