## Groups (for the edition-publisher owner decision)
Every row carries "group". "edition publisher" = publishers and publisher-galleries (all Hanga houses, Editions-named houses, Peter Blum Edition, Krakow Witkin, Marlborough Graphics, Paragon, Two Palms...). "workshop / studio" = collaborative printshops that print and publish (Crown Point, Tamarind, Mixografia, Harlan & Weaver...), plus ceramics, glass, tapestry, lacquer and figurine makers. "art centre / co-op" = Aboriginal art centres, Inuit co-ops, Jacmel co-ops, the Palekh association. "dealer / gallery" = IFPDA/AIPAD/IVPDA dealers and other sellers. Printshops that also publish were split by name and IFPDA wording, so that split is a judgement call.
