## Notes

Accuracy: 25 sampled (random.seed(7); random.sample(brands,25)); raw pages fetched with Parallel web_fetch full_content. 24/25 names shown on their src page and the 'listed' claim holds; 1 wrong: Brown Street Clock Company is NOT on en 'List of United States clock companies' (82 entries), it is on en Category:Clock manufacturing companies of the United States, so the src must be changed. Systemic weakness: about 540 of 631 harvested brands rest only on a list/category row ('Listed in ...'), which proves existence, not quality or cultural standing (owner test). Sampled rows such as Marc Ecko, Kidper, RSW and Just & Son are 'supported' only in that weak sense. Recommend a second evidence pass or a filter for list-only names before publishing; the drop list gives the clearest cases (budget/electronics labels, smartwatch start-ups, a fashion licensor, a bike-computer maker). Omissions found: 67 brands and 5 regions, all on raw fetched pages that state quality, cultural standing or regional renown. Main gaps were (a) newer independents (independentwatchmaking.com profiles, F.P.Journe Young Talent winners, Biver, Simon Brette, Garrick, RGM, Marco Lang); (b) regional and national icons outside Europe (Jaipur Watch Co., Bangalore Watch Co., Allwyn, Ajanta, Romanson, Nacar, Quantum, Beik+Moll, Amir, A-rtisan, Curnon, Oan.Gio, Matoa, Paceracer, Statera, LN Watches); (c) clock traditions (Saint-Nicolas-d'Aliermont: Bayard, Vaucanson, Denis Frères; Haut-Jura Comtoise: Odo, Romanet; Mora; Bornholm; Japy known; Schatz 400-day; Lemnos, Copal, Matthew Norman); (d) accessories (Rapport, SwissKubik, Staib, RIOS1931, Bonetto Cinturini, Uncle Seiko, Dagaz, Timesavers, Cousins); (e) famous sellers (Beyer, Wako, The Hour Glass, Ethos, Ahmed Seddiqi & Sons). Thin areas retried: interval timers/hourglass (only retail pages: Ichendorf 'Times', Hightide, Polspotten; no renowned maker attested, not added); Mexico (only SIAR fair, no Mexican maker found); Portugal, Greece, Philippines, Uzbekistan, Iran: nothing found; Vienna regulators: only single-item dealer listings (Anton Pohl, Happacher, Bachner), not added; Jaz (France) only resale listings, not added; Warmink (Dutch Zaanse clocks) only dealer listings, not added; Strasser & Rohde only shop/auction pages, not added; Vaucher and Kenissi movement makers: no attesting page fetched. Duplicates avoided: Delhi Watch Company (= 'Delhi Watch Company (DWC)'), Kundo (= Kieninger & Obergfell), Japy Frères already known. Harvest also has near-duplicates to merge: 'Dubey Schaldenbrand'/'Dubey & Schaldenbrand', 'Grovana'/'Grovana Watch', 'Sea-Gull'/'Tianjin Seagull Watch Group', 'ALB Watches'/'A.L.B', 'International Watch Company (IWC)' vs IWC.
