Tier fixes (not counted as errors because the 'says' text is honest): all 13 Tucson rows and all 4 meteorite.com rows carry layer 'premium' on existence-only pages: set tier 'listed'. The 83 AAPS rows carry layer 'premium': per the coordinator ruling they are 'cultural' (ethics-bound professional association), not premium. Get Stuffed rests only on its own page: 'listed'. Coober Pedy Dale Price Opals: existence only, 'listed'.
LABEL ARCHIVE CAVEAT: all 16 copied names checked against the raw index text and against the Label Archive inventory PDF; all are real firms, not persons. Two need fixing: 'Bayerisches Petrefacten und Mineralien' is truncated (page: 'Bayerisches Petrefacten und Mineralien Comptoir'); 'Mineral Cabinet, The' is the index's inverted form (use 'The Mineral Cabinet', New Providence NJ). Most are historic or closed firms: mark status historic. The archive only proves a firm left labels; it supports historic presence, not quality.
HOMONYMS: Carolina Biological Supply Co. is not register 'Carolina' (A2 work boots): homonym:true. Ward's Natural Science Establishment and NASCO have no register clash. Pala International (gem and mineral dealer) is not in the register at all; if A3 adds it, cross_tag. Mineral Kingdom of Woodmere must keep its qualifier (distinct from the Houston dealer Mineral Kingdom). Fabre Minerals is unrelated to Marius Fabre and Gael Fabre. Heritage Auctions (F2) is the same company: cross_tag F6 (added as a cross_tag row). Fine Minerals International appeared only in a harvest region list, never as a brand row: now added.
CITES / LEGALITY NOTES NEEDED: Antonino De Simone and the Torre del Greco coral region (Corallium; Pacific precious corals are on CITES Appendix III); Deyrolle and Get Stuffed (taxidermy, shells, coral); historic trophy houses already in the register (Rowland Ward, Van Ingen & Van Ingen, Tocher and Tocher, Edward Gerrard & Sons: tiger, big-game, ivory: Appendix I taxa, antique-ivory rules); Skulls Unlimited, Valley Anatomical Preparations, The Evolution Store, Wunderkammer Zurich (skulls, skeletons, taxidermy); The Bug House, BugsDirect-type insect sellers, Mushi-sha (birdwing butterflies Ornithoptera/Troides, Teinopalpus, Bhutanitis: Appendix I/II); shell sellers Conchology Inc. and Femorale (Tridacnidae giant clams, queen conch, Nautilidae, black and stony corals: Appendix II). Fossil ivory (mammoth) is not CITES but is banned by several US states (AAPS's own 'Fossil Ivory Ban' page): add a state-law note to any AAPS dealer selling it. Minerals, meteorites, fossils and amber are not CITES, but national export and heritage laws apply (Morocco, China, Brazil, Mongolia dinosaur material).
IMCA: the harvest said the IMCA member list is login-restricted. It is public (imcax.com memberlist, 513 members, persons with country; per-member pages sometimes give a website). It confirms Geoffrey Notkin (member since 2006, Aerolite Meteorites, AAPS row) and Bruno Fectay (since 2010, La Memoire de la Terre, AAPS row); many Moroccan and Australian members. Firm names are rarely given, so it is weak for new brands (only Treasure Coast Meteorite Co., member since 2026: not added).
