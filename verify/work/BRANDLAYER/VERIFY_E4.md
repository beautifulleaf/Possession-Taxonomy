## Tier rules applied

- Wikipedia category members: cultural (own article).
- ABS Master Bladesmith directory: quality (juried rating). Names cleaned of directory suffixes ("- Retired ABS Instructor", "-Retired", "MS RETIRED", "PHD"). Country left blank for all 117: the directory pages state no country. Names R–Z (Bill Sowell and 18 others) now point to directory page 2 (?ps&pn=2&limit=100), where they appear.
- ja.wikipedia 日本刀: Living National Treasures and the Imperial Household Artist are cultural (official heritage designation); Masamune Prize winners (Sumitani, Amata, Ōsumi, Gassan Sadakazu II, Kawachi) are quality.
- NBTHK Gendai Toshoku-ten 2024 catalogue (juried national exhibition, mukansa and award sections): quality. Smiths are matched by mei and real name as the catalogue prints them (e.g. 上林 勇二 for Kanbayashi Tsunehira, 森國 利文 for Morikuni Kiyohiro).
- Seller pages are listed: Everest Forge blog, Optics Trade list, Outfit4Events, the HEMA directory, KNB, chessnok.kz, the Samurai Museum shop and the antique-dealer glossary. Precision Rifle Blog surveys raise Kahles, Schmidt & Bender and Leupold & Stevens to quality (src2).
- Swordis (seller blog) names are tiered from src2: ja.wikipedia (Sumitani, Amata) or the NBTHK catalogue (Yoshihara, Gassan Sadatoshi, Ōno Yoshimitsu).
- Seller tier: traditional-craft workshops named in the press as makers or sellers (Ona Blades, Leo Rivera shop, Sor Arunyig, Shen Guanglong Sword Shop, Al Farsi khanjar workshops).
- Standing claims with no article, award or craft statement are listed: "Italian legends" (F.A.I.R., Rizzini, Zoli), "renowned houses" of Toledo (Marto, Artesanía Tradicional Toledana), "great names" of Liège (Bury, Defourny, Dumoulin), and Shooting Sportsman link lists (William Powell, A.A. Brown, McKay Brown, E.J. Churchill). SABRE is listed because its source is sponsored content.
