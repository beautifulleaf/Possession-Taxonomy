- Books of Wonder: ABAA member but a children's bookshop: F1 (books), not F5. Another 7 rows carry a cultural tier that rests only on directories (Rare Book Hub x3, IBSS x4). Counting those, 10/25 have a tier or category problem. Systemic: the ABAA and ILAB rows (cultural) are fine; Rare Book Hub, IBSS and FFPP rows should be 'listed'. Lineco has only its own PAT claims (existence). Giesecke+Devrient should be re-sourced to the Wikipedia Smart card article ('First smart card manufactured by Giesecke & Devrient in 1979'), which supports F5; its HSP credit is for banknotes (F3). Cross-tags rather than new rows: VingCard (index B8; keycard inventor, en wiki), Secol (index F1, the same archival-sleeve maker), Jostens and Herff Jones (index A3; also diplomas, not verified here), Vistaprint (I1; business cards).
