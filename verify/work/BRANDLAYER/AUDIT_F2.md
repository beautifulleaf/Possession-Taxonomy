# AUDIT F2 Art: brand layer cold audit (2026-10-31)

**Accuracy:** 0 wrong out of 25 checked (random.seed(7)). All 25 names appear on their src pages, and every claim is supported. The sample came from only six list pages (SVA, Desart, IFPDA, IACA, myhanga, AACHWA), and the whole harvest rests on those six.

**Drops (3, all duplicates):**
- Birriliburu Artists / Tjukurba Gallery: same as Tjukurba Gallery
- Tjarlirli Art: same as Tjarlirli and Kaltukatjara Art
- SOLO Impression: same as Solo Impression Inc.

**Review flags:**
- Krakow Witkin, Peter Blum and Marlborough Graphics are general galleries that appear on the SVA list. They stay only if they publish editions.
- Several Hanga houses no longer exist, so their works are second-hand only.
- Two Palms: the index has an unrelated A1 brand with the same name.
- Department 56 is only in B4; it needs an F2 tag.
- Taller de Gráfica Popular now also rests on peoplesgdarchive.org, not just the Met essay.

**Blind spots that remain:**
- No named makers found for: Ethiopian painting workshops, Isfahan miniature ateliers, Kalighat and Gond cooperatives, Pichwai workshops, Korean ceramic studios, Shona sculpture (Tengenenge), East Asian calligraphy and seal makers
