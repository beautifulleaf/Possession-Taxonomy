## Regions

- **United Kingdom**: BBC monitor tradition: the LS3/5A, licensed by the BBC to a line of British makers (Rogers first, ~60,000 pairs 1975-93) (https://www.markhennessy.co.uk/rogers/ls35a.htm): Rogers, Audiomaster, Chartwell, Spendor, Goodmans, Harbeth, KEF, Falcon Acoustics, Stirling Broadcast, Graham Audio
- **Denmark**: loudspeakers and drive units: 'more loudspeaker manufacturers per person than any other country' (Stereophile) (https://www.stereophile.com/content/visit-dali-factory): Audio Technology, Peerless, Vifa, Scan-Speak, Bang & Olufsen, DALI, Dynaudio, Gamut, Gryphon Audio Designs, Jamo, Lyngdorf Audio, Peak-Consult, Raidho Acoustics, Steinway Lyngdorf
- **Japan**: valve amplification: Luxman's SQ-38 valve amplifiers became an icon 'particularly in Japanese jazz cafés' (https://hifihalloffame.com/business/luxman): Luxman
- **Poland**: loudspeakers and valve electronics; Pylon is 'the largest Central European manufacturer of loudspeakers' (https://highfidelity.pl/@main-1215&lang=en): Pylon Audio
- **Italy**: historic radio and TV brands; Phonola (1931) is 'one of the oldest brands in the sector, especially in radios' (https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phonola): Phonola, Brionvega, Mivar, Sèleco
