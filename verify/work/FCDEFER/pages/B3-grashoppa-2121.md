# Gräshoppa Floor Lamp
Gräshoppa Floor Lamp by Greta Grossman for Gubi.
The Gräshoppa floor lamp was designed in 1947 by Greta Magnusson Grossman, a Swedish born designer who was one of the first-ever female to graduate from the famous Stockholm Konstfak School of industrial design.
