# Cognac Brands: Great Houses & Hidden Gems
380+ cognac brands, ranked by our community and curated by our team
The great houses: Hennessy (World's largest, Since 1765, Fins Bois blends); Rémy Martin (Fine Champagne, Since 1724); Martell (Oldest house, 1715, Borderies heart); Courvoisier (Since 1828, Jarnac quay); Camus (Family-owned, Borderies champion, Since 1863); Hine (Since 1763); Otard (Château de Cognac, Since 1795); Delamain (Old Grande Champagne, Since 1824); Hardy (Old blends, Négociant); Bisquit & Dubouché (Since 1819); Ferrand (Grande Champagne); Frapin (Single estate, Grande Champagne, Growers since 1270).
Hidden gems: Navarre, Prunier (Founded 1700), Grosperrin, Audry, Tesseron, Vallein Tercinier, Pasquet (Organic), Jean Fillioux, Paul Giraud, François Voyer, Château de Montifaud, Mauxion Selection. Also: Dolmen de Pierre Folle, Choloux, Les Ormeaux, Louis Bouron; Raymond Ragnaud, Park, Lheraud, Peyrat, Leopold Gourmel, Bourgoin, Bache Gabrielsen, Guy Pinard, Meukow.
The ‘Big Four’: Hennessy, Remy Martin, Courvoisier, and Martell ... Between them they account for an incredible 90% of the worldwide market.
Camus, the largest family-owned house, follows close behind.
