# AVRO by Martinelli Luce wins the Compasso D’oro International Award
**Martinelli Luce** won the **Compasso d’Oro ADI International Award**, the prestigious international Italian design award established in 2014, for its **Avro** pendant lamp. ... had the theme of the event as its guiding thread: “Designing Future Society for Our Lives”.
Inside the Italian Pavilion at Expo, the **Avro** suspension lamp, designed by **Studio Natural** for Martinelli Luce, won the award in the Design for Lighting category.
The suspension is designed for private and public environments, with an electrical socket integrated into the lower diffuser ... The integrated dimmable LED offers diffused and adjustable light
