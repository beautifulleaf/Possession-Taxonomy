# String Furniture 1949-Now
Behind this timeless Scandinavian design were architects, Nisse and Kajsa Strinning.
In 1949, one of Sweden’s largest publishers, Bonniers Folkbibliotek, launched a design competition. The challenge was to make an affordable, wall-mounted, expandable bookshelf with at least two metres of shelf space
Without hesitation, Nisse and Kajsa submitted their design….and won first prize. ... In less than a year, they’d sold 40,000.
1954 String® was awarded the gold medal at the Milan Triennale.
1952 String® shelves were chosen to decorate the newly built UN headquarters in New York.
1979 String® became part of Nationalmuseum's permanent collection
Fast forward to November 2004, when Peter Erlandsson and Pär Josefsson – two young entrepreneurs and design enthusiasts – tracked down String®’s original creators and convinced them to go back into business.
String® is now sold in 48 countries around the world.
