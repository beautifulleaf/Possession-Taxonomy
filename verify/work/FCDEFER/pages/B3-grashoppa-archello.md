# Gräshoppa floor lamp GM1
The Gräshoppa floor lamp GM1 by Gubi embodies a stylish timelessness. The three-leg floor lamp GM1 of 1947 is one of the most important works of the Swedish-American designer Greta Magnusson Grossman.
The Danish company Gubi produces the Gräshoppa floor lamp after original designs of Greta Magnusson Grossman. The floor lamp is available in different colours.
