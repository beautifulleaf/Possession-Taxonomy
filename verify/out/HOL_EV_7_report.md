# HOL_EV_7 report (E1 E2 E3 E4)

Evidence: verify/out/HOL_EV_7_evidence.tsv (35 rows); fetch log verify/work/HOL_EV_7/fetched.tsv. Several big retailers (Decathlon, SportScheck, Xebio, Cabela's, Sweetwater, Reverb, Walmart, eBay) returned 403 or bot pages, and the WebFetch domain check was often rate-limited. So retailer-menu evidence is thin for E2 and E4; no community source for E2, E3, E4.

**E1 Toys & Games.** Ladder: material or kind (plastic, diecast, wood, brick; board, card, puzzle, doll) > subject or theme > scale/age/skill. Maker-owned and homeschool shops lead with brand or publisher; enthusiast shops lead with type then scale and brand. Quality: board games by luck vs skill, weight, length, interaction, replayability; kits by skill grade, scale, fit (Tamiya). Trusted signals: Spiel des Jahres (Kinder/Kenner), safety marks (EN71, ASTM F963, CE). Genres lead for games; makers lead for kits. Surprising: Warhammer buyers choose faction by look and lore, and warn against the meta.

**E2 Sports, Fitness & Recreation.** Ladder: sport > product type > brand; some shops lead with user (women/men/kids) then sport. Quality is use-specific: bikes by flywheel; shoes by lab cushioning data; boots by comfort, support, traction. Signals: UIAA Safety Label and CE for climbing gear; independent lab scores (RunRepeat, OutdoorGearLab); ISO 20957 for fitness machines (search only). Sport leads, not maker. Surprising: certification is a gate, and second-hand safety gear is discouraged.

**E3 Musical Instruments & Music Gear.** Ladder: instrument family > named body design (Strat, Les Paul, Telecaster) > pickups, strings, scale > brand; studio/stage/DJ gear are parallel top-level shelves. Quality: build and pickups, played in hand; synths by sound and genre, voice count; interfaces by noise and dynamic range. Few formal grades; maker tiers are by price (about EUR 100 / 300 / 1000). Genre and body design lead guitars; makers lead pianos.

**E4 Weapons & Armour.** Ladder: use or quarry > type (bow: recurve, compound; rifle: action, caliber) > maker. Quality: steel and tang for knives; draw weight and fit for bows; NIJ level for armour. Trusted grades: NIJ II, IIIA, III, IV; NBTHK Hozon, Tokubetsu Hozon, Juyo for Japanese swords; no body for HEMA swords, only community reputation. Surprising: antique and modern swords are separate shelves, and NIJ is called "a floor, not a ceiling".
