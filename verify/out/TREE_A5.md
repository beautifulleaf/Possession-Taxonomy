# A5 Clocks & Watches: decided tree

Category: A5 Clocks & Watches (domain A, Worn and Carried). Membership test (placement key step 16): a timepiece, a watch or clock whose main function is to show or measure time, of any kind, including stopwatches, hourglasses and timers made as timepieces, and every sundial (R-28). Structure (A-23, Candidate A): sub-family (how the timepiece is carried or placed in use, with a closing parts sub-family exempt from that axis, C5d), then leaf (wristwatches by the defining complication or certified function, tested on the object; clocks by placement or mounting; no leaf for carried watches, timing instruments and parts), then kind (the generic type buyers compare on quality or maker). Each object gets one kind. Movement and power (A-22), design family (dress, pilot, field, military, racing, sailing, sports or tough, jewellery), display, skeleton, gem setting, case material and size, certification (COSC, Geneva Seal, METAS and maker seals as standards), standard_met (ISO 6425, MIL-W-46374), origin_label (Swiss Made), maker, tier, era, region, user and condition are attributes, tags or views, never kinds (section 14: watch families and watch worlds are the design_family attribute and maker_class values, not scenes; the watch-family groups of the navigation proposal are display only and do not change placement). A kind marked † needs a fetched page for its name before it enters the data.

Ids (R-58): A5.n is a sub-family and A5.n.m a leaf; a kind is A5.n.m.kk, or A5.n.kk where a sub-family has no leaf.

## A5.1 Wristwatches
Leaf axis: the defining complication or certified function, tested on the object, applied in the order Dive; Tourbillon and grande complication; Striking; Calendar and astronomical; Chronograph; Multi-zone; Time-only (A-23).

### A5.1.1 Time-only & simple-date watches
- A5.1.1.01 Time-only watch: analogue wristwatch with two or three hands, with or without a date window, and none of the defining functions of the other leaves.
- A5.1.1.02 Digital watch: wristwatch whose time display is digital; stopwatch and alarm modes standard on the form do not move it.
- A5.1.1.03 One-hand watch: wristwatch that shows the time with a single hand.
- A5.1.1.04 Jumping-hours watch: wristwatch that shows the hour as a numeral that jumps in a window.

### A5.1.2 Dive watches
- A5.1.2.01 Diver's watch: wristwatch with a unidirectional timing bezel and at least 100 m water resistance, or conforming to ISO 6425.

### A5.1.3 Chronograph watches
- A5.1.3.01 Chronograph: wristwatch with a chronograph mechanism that starts, stops and resets an elapsed-time hand or register.
- A5.1.3.02 Flyback chronograph: chronograph that can be reset and restarted with one push while running.
- A5.1.3.03 Split-seconds chronograph (double chronograph, rattrapante): chronograph with a second seconds hand that can be stopped separately to time intermediate intervals.

### A5.1.4 Multi-zone watches
- A5.1.4.01 GMT watch: wristwatch with an extra hand or display showing a second time zone, usually on a 24-hour scale.
- A5.1.4.02 World-time watch: wristwatch that shows the time in all world time zones at once, with a city ring.

### A5.1.5 Calendar & astronomical watches
- A5.1.5.01 Moon-phase watch: wristwatch with a moon-phase display.
- A5.1.5.02 Annual-calendar watch: wristwatch whose calendar corrects itself for months of 30 and 31 days and needs setting once a year.
- A5.1.5.03 Perpetual-calendar watch: wristwatch whose calendar corrects itself for month lengths and leap years.

### A5.1.6 Striking watches
- A5.1.6.01 Alarm watch: wristwatch with a mechanical alarm that sounds at a set time.
- A5.1.6.02 Minute-repeater watch: wristwatch that strikes the hours, quarters and minutes on demand.

### A5.1.7 Tourbillon & grande-complication watches
- A5.1.7.01 Tourbillon watch: wristwatch whose escapement turns in a rotating cage (tourbillon).
- A5.1.7.02 Grande-complication watch: wristwatch with at least one timing, one astronomical and one striking complication.

## A5.2 Carried watches
Leaf axis: none: 4 kinds, so the leaf level is skipped (C5b); carried on the person, not on the wrist; kinds by case style.

- A5.2.01 Open-face pocket watch: pocket watch whose crystal has no protective cover.
- A5.2.02 Hunter-case pocket watch: pocket watch with a hinged metal lid that covers the crystal.
- A5.2.03 Demi-hunter pocket watch: hunter-case pocket watch whose lid has a window or glazed centre through which the hands can be read.
- A5.2.04 Coin watch: watch built into a case made from or shaped as a coin.

## A5.3 Clocks
Leaf axis: placement or mounting (floor, table or shelf, wall, portable, building-mounted); the body differs by mount (C12a).

### A5.3.1 Floor-standing clocks
- A5.3.1.01 Longcase clock (grandfather clock): tall floor-standing clock with the pendulum and weights in a long case.

### A5.3.2 Table & shelf clocks
- A5.3.2.01 Mantel clock: clock made to stand on a mantelpiece or shelf.
- A5.3.2.02 Bracket clock: portable spring-driven table clock with a carrying handle, made to stand on a table or wall bracket.
- A5.3.2.03 Carriage clock: small spring-driven travelling clock in a glazed metal case with a top handle.
- A5.3.2.04 Desk clock: small clock made to stand on a desk.
- A5.3.2.05 Alarm clock: table or bedside clock that sounds at a set time.
- A5.3.2.06 Digital clock: table or shelf clock with a digital time display.
- A5.3.2.07 Skeleton clock: clock whose open frame shows the movement with no case around it (A-25).

### A5.3.3 Wall clocks
- A5.3.3.01 Wall clock: wall-mounted clock with no distinctive case form of the kinds below.
- A5.3.3.02 Dial clock: wall clock with a large dial in a round case, with or without a drop case below.
- A5.3.3.03 Wall regulator: precision wall clock with a long pendulum in a tall glazed case.
- A5.3.3.04 Cuckoo clock: wall clock whose striking sounds a cuckoo call, with a moving bird.
- A5.3.3.05 Lantern clock: weight-driven wall clock in a brass lantern-shaped case with a bell on top.
- A5.3.3.06 Tavern clock: large wall clock with a big painted dial and a trunk below, made for public rooms.

### A5.3.4 Portable & marine clocks
- A5.3.4.01 Travel clock: small clock made to be carried on journeys, often folding or in a case.
- A5.3.4.02 Marine chronometer: precision timekeeper made to keep time at sea for navigation.

### A5.3.5 Building-mounted clocks
- A5.3.5.01 Tower or turret clock: clock fixed to a building, with a movement driving one or more exterior dials. †

## A5.4 Timing instruments
Leaf axis: none: 7 kinds, so the leaf level is skipped (C5b); stand-alone or hand-held instruments that time an interval, record time or keep time without being a room clock.

- A5.4.01 Stopwatch: hand-held instrument that measures an elapsed interval.
- A5.4.02 Hourglass: timepiece that measures an interval by sand running between two glass bulbs.
- A5.4.03 Sundial: instrument that shows the time by the sun's shadow on a scale, portable, pocket, wall, horizontal or equatorial.
- A5.4.04 Garden sundial: sundial made to stand or hang in a garden or park, including those sold as garden ornaments, with a working gnomon. †
- A5.4.05 Game clock (chess clock): pair of linked clocks that times each player's moves in a game.
- A5.4.06 Time clock (attendance clock): clock that records the times workers arrive and leave.
- A5.4.07 Atomic clock: laboratory time standard regulated by an atomic resonance.

## A5.5 Parts and accessories made for one watch or clock
Leaf axis: none yet: 8 kinds, so the leaf level is skipped (C5b, CV-16); the stated axis for later use is the object served (watch; clock), CV-17. Exempt from the level-1 axis (C5d, S3).

- A5.5.01 Watch strap: band of leather, textile, rubber or other flexible material made to hold a watch on the wrist.
- A5.5.02 Watch bracelet: metal link band made to hold a watch on the wrist.
- A5.5.03 Watch winder: powered stand that holds one automatic watch and keeps it wound.
- A5.5.04 Watch dial: dial made to be fitted to a watch.
- A5.5.05 Watch movement: movement (calibre) made to be fitted in a watch.
- A5.5.06 Watch crystal: glass, sapphire or plastic crystal made to cover a watch dial.
- A5.5.07 Watch case: case made to house a watch movement.
- A5.5.08 Watch chain: chain (Albert chain, fob chain) made to attach a pocket watch.

## Precedence inside A5

- The kind of a wristwatch is found by its defining complication or certified function, tested on the object, never by marketing lineage, design family or occasion; pilot, field, military, racing, sailing, dress and sports or tough are design_family values, not kinds.
- A wristwatch with more than one defining function goes to the first it has, in this order: Dive (A5.1.2); Tourbillon and grande complication (A5.1.7); Striking (A5.1.6); Calendar and astronomical (A5.1.5); Chronograph (A5.1.3); Multi-zone (A5.1.4); Time-only (A5.1.1). A watch with an annual calendar and a second time zone (the Sky-Dweller) is A5.1.5.02.
- A display that is the form of the watch beats a function: a digital watch with stopwatch and alarm modes is A5.1.1.02; one-hand and jumping-hours watches are A5.1.1.03 and A5.1.1.04.
- A pocket watch is a pocket watch (A5.2) whatever its complication, because the carry axis comes first. A trench watch or wristlet (a pocket-watch case adapted to the wrist) is a historical named form of A5.1.1.01.
- A skeleton watch is a watch with the attribute skeleton; a skeleton clock is A5.3.2.07 (A-25). Longcase and grandfather clock are one kind (A5.3.1.01). Historical-only clock styles (banjo, cartel, schoolhouse, railway) are non-maker named forms of their nearest kind (A5.3.3.01 or A5.3.3.02) unless a fetched seller gives the style its own category (A-1); regional styles (Vienna regulator, Black Forest cuckoo) are named forms with a region tag.
- A watch whose main function is to tell time is A5 whatever its case metal, gems or price (a diamond-set watch carries gem_set); an ornament with a decorative dial that cannot be read as a timepiece is A3.
- A watch chain, Albert chain or fob chain made to attach a pocket watch is A5.5.08 (S3, K-20); a decorative fob, seal or charm worn without a watch is A3. A watch strap follows its watch (A5.5.01); "wristlet" is also an A4 word, with two targets in the alias table.
- A smartwatch, hybrid smartwatch, fitness tracker or GPS sports watch is D2; a watch that only syncs time by Bluetooth or radio stays A5. A watch whose main display is the time is A5 whatever sensors it has (altimeter, barometer, compass); a device whose main function is sensing or navigation is D2, tested on the object's default display (K-124, D-14).
- A clock radio, smart display or voice-assistant clock is D3; a clock with radio-controlled time is A5.
- Every sundial is A5: a garden sundial is A5.4.04, a sundial made to read the time as an instrument is A5.4.03, and an astronomical teaching sundial is a named form of A5.4.04; a decorative dial with no working gnomon is B4. A sunrise lamp is B3.
- A kitchen timer is B5; an hourglass or sand timer made as a timepiece is A5.4.02.
- A box, roll or cabinet that holds several watches is B1; a winder or stand that holds one watch is A5.5.03. A grandfather clock is A5.3.1.01, not furniture.
- A watchmaker's tool is C1; a barometer or weather station is C6; a marine chronometer is A5.3.4.02 and a laboratory atomic clock A5.4.07 (step 16 before step 23).
- A metronome is E3. A chess or game clock is A5.4.05 (step 16 before step 25). An attendance clock is A5.4.06 (step 16 before step 31). A tower or turret clock fixed to a building is A5.3.5.01, not G2.
- A nurse's pulse watch made to monitor a patient is H2 (step 2 before step 16). A clock made as an award or trophy is F3; a commemorative clock is a clock.
- A diver's watch with ISO 6425 is A5.1.2.01, not E2 (step 16 before step 26); a sensing-led dive computer is outside A5.
