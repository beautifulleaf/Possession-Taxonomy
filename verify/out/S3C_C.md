# S3C_C source census, domain C (C1–C8)

Table: `verify/out/S3C_C.tsv` (80 rows, 10 per category, best first). Every kept URL was fetched in this batch; the few notes that say "not fetched" name a second page seen only in a search result. The uncut table (103 rows, including 23 sources fetched but cut to hold the 10-row limit) is `verify/work/S3C_C/S3C_C.full.tsv`.

- **C1 Tools & Workshop Equipment.** Anchor: Wikipedia "List of tool manufacturers" (maker, HQ, brands, products), then Pro Tool Reviews "who owns" for parent links; VintageMachinery.org and DATAMP for historic machine and tool makers. Gaps: no open register of hand-tool makers outside Japanese chisels; the pre-1900 US list (about 14,000 toolmakers) is a paid CD; welding, generator and ladder makers have only vendor or B2B pages (ESAB, DirectIndustry); battery-platform membership lists (CAS, Power for All, AMPShare) are in downloads, not on pages.
- **C2 Garden & Grounds.** Anchor: GIMA member directory (200+ UK/EU garden suppliers, 17 pages) plus the Old Lawnmower Club directory for mower makers and models. Gaps: no catalogue of secateur, irrigation, planter or pool-equipment makers; no named-form list for garden hand tools; OPEI's member list is a PDF found only through a forum attachment; Chinese and Japanese lists are blog or ranking pages.
- **C3 Plants & Living Cultures.** Anchor: ISHS Directory of ICRAs (route to every cultivar register), then RHS Orchid Register, AHS Daylily Database (100,000+ cultivars) and the CPVO Variety Finder / UPOV PLUTO for protected varieties. Gaps: PLUTO result display is capped; HelpMeFind detail needs a login; no register for houseplants beyond aroids, for succulents, or for household cultures (sourdough, kombucha, kefir, mushroom spawn), where only retailer pages exist; WDCM CCINFO covers laboratory strains.
- **C4 Craft & Art Supplies.** Anchor: Wikipedia "List of sewing machine brands" and "List of scale model kit manufacturers" for machine and kit makers, Artist Pigments for paint lines and named colours, Jackson's and Blick brand indexes for the long tail. Gaps: no maker list for fabric, notions, beads or leather; Ravelry's yarn database needs a login; kiln makers have only retailer pages; model-paint colour lists are in PDF charts.
- **C5 Office & Stationery.** Anchor: The Typewriter Database (1,073 brands, 3,466 models) and The Fountain Pen Database (500+ brands, 50,000+ inks); ja.wikipedia Category 日本の事務用品メーカー (132 makers) for Japan. Gaps: no catalogue of pencil, notebook or paper makers; modern office machines (shredders, laminators, label makers) have only retailer brand filters; the two pen-database counts disagree on the page.
- **C6 Optical & Scientific Instruments.** Anchor: AllBinos (3,200 binoculars, about 90 makers) and Wikipedia "Category: Telescope manufacturers" (66); historic makers from the Scientific Instrument Commission database list, microscopist.net and the Science Museum Group. Gaps: no current microscope maker list in English; weather-instrument makers and science-kit makers have no register (kinds list only); lab glassware is a single maker group's brand page (DWK).
- **C7 Farm & Forestry Equipment.** Anchor: TractorData.com (280 tractor makers with model lists), Wikipedia current and former tractor lists, and the German Wikipedia "Liste von Landmaschinenherstellern" for implement makers; Nebraska Tractor Test reports for tested models. Gaps: only one small forestry maker list (20 makers); no catalogue of implements, harvesters, or livestock-handling makers; CEMA's page gives no member company list; trade-association member lists (VDMA, AEM, JAMMA) not found as open pages.
- **C8 Building Materials & Hardware.** Anchor: NBS Source (1,200+ makers, 30,000+ products) with Uniclass for kinds; Wikipedia "List of battery sizes" and "Battery nomenclature" for named battery forms; Kenzai-Navi, Baulinks and Batiweb for Japanese, German and French makers. Gaps: no maker list for fuels, lighters, plumbing or electrical supplies; fastener makers only through a B2B directory; paint and adhesive makers have no list of their own; nothing found for Chinese building-material makers.

No category lacked a usable catalogue. Weakest: C3 household cultures, C4 fabrics and beads, C7 forestry and implements, C8 fuels and plumbing.
