# B3 Lighting: decided tree

Category: B3 Lighting (domain B, Home). Membership test: made to give light: lamps, light fittings, bulbs (including smart bulbs), lanterns and torches, sunrise lamps; stage lighting is here, and so are head torches and dive lights, light strips sold to light a space, emergency lights and the parts made for one lamp or fitting.

Structure: sub-family (the role the object has in lighting), then leaf (where it mounts, stands or is carried, or the shape and cap form of a light source; none in the parts sub-family), then kind (the generic type buyers compare). Each object gets one kind. Technology, fuel, power source, connectivity, emergency use, output class, cap code and filament style are attributes and never kinds or leaves. A kind marked † is provisional, named only by the framework key or a decision and needs a fetched page before it enters the data.

Ids (R-58): B3.n is a sub-family and B3.n.m a leaf; a kind is B3.n.m.kk, or B3.n.kk where a sub-family has no leaf.

## B3.1 Fixed fittings

Leaf axis: where the fitting mounts (ceiling, wall, recessed or track, ground or post, rigged for a performance). Placement is a level here because each fitting is made only for its mount and its body differs.

### B3.1.1 Ceiling-mounted
Fittings fixed to or hung from the ceiling.
- B3.1.1.01 Flush ceiling light: Fitting fixed directly against the ceiling that lights the room from a shade or diffuser close to it.
- B3.1.1.02 Pendant light: Fitting hung from the ceiling on a cord, chain or rod, with the light source and shade below the ceiling.
- B3.1.1.03 Chandelier: Branched or tiered hanging fitting that carries several light sources, lit by electricity, gas, oil or candles.
- B3.1.1.04 Panel light: Flat, thin ceiling fitting that gives an even sheet of light, surface-mounted or set in a grid.

### B3.1.2 Wall-mounted
Fittings fixed to a wall.
- B3.1.2.01 Wall light (sconce): Fitting fixed to a wall that lights the room, a wall or a passage, whatever its source or fuel.
- B3.1.2.02 Mirror light: Wall light made to light the face at a mirror (the lighted mirror itself is not a lamp kind).
- B3.1.2.03 Wall-mounted lantern light: Fixed fitting in the form of a lantern, bracketed to a wall or post, not made to be carried.

### B3.1.3 Recessed, spot and track
Fittings set into a ceiling or carried on a rail, usually giving directed light.
- B3.1.3.01 Recessed downlight: Fitting sunk into a ceiling so that only its trim shows, casting light downward.
- B3.1.3.02 Spot fitting: Directional fitting with a swivelling head, fixed to a ceiling or a track head (the bulb of the same name is a spot bulb).
- B3.1.3.03 Track or rail lighting system: Powered rail or cable that carries movable heads along its length, sold as a system.

### B3.1.4 Ground-mounted and post
Outdoor fittings set on the ground, a post or a mast; a wall, post or ground bracket is an installation attribute.
- B3.1.4.01 Bollard or path light: Low outdoor fitting standing on the ground to light a path or edge; the power source, solar included, is an attribute.
- B3.1.4.02 Post or mast light: Tall outdoor fitting on a post or mast that lights a yard, drive or area.
- B3.1.4.03 Ground-spike light: Outdoor fitting pushed into the soil on a spike, to light a plant, tree or wall from below.
- B3.1.4.04 Plinth light: Small outdoor or step fitting set into a surface or low base to light a step, edge or wall.
- B3.1.4.05 Floodlight: Wide-beam outdoor or area fitting fixed on a wall, post or ground bracket, including the light-only security floodlight and the searchlight.

### B3.1.5 Rigged and stage
Fittings placed on a truss, bar or stand for a performance or event; professional market.
- B3.1.5.01 Stage lighting fixture: Fixture rigged on a truss, bar or stand to light a stage or event; fixture types stay named forms until a catalogue compares them separately. †

## B3.2 Standing lamps

Leaf axis: where the lamp stands (table or desk, floor). A lamp that plugs in or stands in place stays here whatever its power source.

### B3.2.1 Table and desk
Lamps that stand on a table, desk, shelf or bedside, or plug in at socket height.
- B3.2.1.01 Table lamp: Lamp with a base and a fixed shade or head that stands on a table; oil, gas and candle table lamps are this kind with a fuel tag.
- B3.2.1.02 Desk lamp: Lamp with an adjustable arm or head made for task light, standing or clamped; a clip-on desk lamp is this kind with a clamp installation attribute.
- B3.2.1.03 Night light: Small low-output lamp that plugs in or stands to give dim light overnight; baby use is a user tag.
- B3.2.1.04 Sunrise lamp: Bedside lamp that brightens gradually to wake the sleeper, with or without a radio. †

### B3.2.2 Floor-standing
Lamps that stand on the floor.
- B3.2.2.01 Floor lamp: Tall lamp on a base that stands on the floor beside furniture, with a shade or head near head height.
- B3.2.2.02 Floor uplighter: Floor-standing lamp whose light is thrown upward to the ceiling.

## B3.3 Carried and worn lights

Leaf axis: how the light is carried or used (hand, head, lantern, work light). Power source and setting (camping) are attributes.

### B3.3.1 Hand torches
Lights held in the hand and aimed.
- B3.3.1.01 Handheld flashlight: Hand torch from pocket size to duty size; output class, cell, beam and water or depth rating (a dive light included) are attributes, and EDC and tactical are scene tags.
- B3.3.1.02 Penlight: Slim pen-shaped hand light for close work or inspection.
- B3.3.1.03 Keychain light: Very small light made to hang from a key ring.
- B3.3.1.04 Clip-on light: Small light with a clip made to attach to a cap, pocket, strap or bag.
- B3.3.1.05 Handheld searchlight: Large-head or pistol-grip hand spotlight, a form apart from the cylindrical torch.

### B3.3.2 Head-worn
Lights worn on the head.
- B3.3.2.01 Headlamp: Light worn on the head on a band or cap mount, also called a head torch; running, ultralight and caving versions are named forms or an attribute.

### B3.3.3 Lanterns (carried)
Enclosed lights with a handle or hanger, made to be carried as a light, whatever the setting.
- B3.3.3.01 Electric lantern: Carried lantern lit by a battery, rechargeable cell or solar panel; the power source is an attribute.
- B3.3.3.02 Mantle fuel lantern: Carried lantern in which a gas or liquid fuel heats a mantle to give light (propane, liquid fuel, dual fuel).
- B3.3.3.03 Hurricane lantern: Carried lantern with a wick, a fuel reservoir and a tubular glass globe, burning oil.
- B3.3.3.04 Candle lantern: Enclosed lantern with a handle that holds a candle, made to be carried.
- B3.3.3.05 Paper lantern [chochin]: Collapsible or rigid paper-covered lantern made to be carried as a light; one made to hang as decoration is not a lamp kind. †

### B3.3.4 Work and inspection lights
Portable lights moved from job to job.
- B3.3.4.01 Clamp work light: Portable work light with a guard and hook, clamped or hung at the job, sold as work or commercial lighting.
- B3.3.4.02 Trouble (inspection) light: Portable corded or cordless light on a hook or handle for inspecting dark spaces such as engine bays and crawl spaces.

## B3.4 Light sources

Leaf axis: shape and cap form (general bulbs, reflector and spot and capsule bulbs, forms with their own holder or ballast, strips), in the order of the bulb shape codes. Technology, cap code, filament style, connectivity and colour are attributes.

### B3.4.1 General bulbs (screw and bayonet caps)
Bulbs of the common shapes in screw or bayonet caps.
- B3.4.1.01 General-service bulb: Pear-shaped (A-shape, GLS) bulb for ordinary lamps and fittings; filament style is an attribute and a smart bulb is this kind with a connectivity attribute.
- B3.4.1.02 Candle bulb: Slim bulb in the shape of a candle flame, for chandeliers and small fittings.
- B3.4.1.03 Globe bulb: Round, ball-shaped bulb.

### B3.4.2 Reflector, spot and capsule bulbs
Bulbs that direct light with a built-in reflector, or small capsules for low-voltage fittings.
- B3.4.2.01 Reflector bulb: Bulb with a built-in reflector that throws the light forward (R, BR and PAR shapes).
- B3.4.2.02 Spot bulb: Small directional bulb for spot fittings, with pin or twist-lock caps (GU10, GU5.3 and MR16 types); the fitting of the same name is a spot fitting.
- B3.4.2.03 Capsule bulb: Very small bulb with a pin cap, for low-voltage lamps and small fittings (G4 and G9 types).

### B3.4.3 Forms that need their own holder or ballast
Light sources that cannot be swapped for another bulb because they need their own holder or ballast.
- B3.4.3.01 Linear tube lamp: Long straight tube light source with its own cap and holder; fluorescent, LED and halogen versions are one kind and technology is an attribute.
- B3.4.3.02 Compact fluorescent lamp: Folded or coiled fluorescent source, of the pin-base types that need their own holder. †
- B3.4.3.03 High-intensity discharge lamp: Discharge source that needs a ballast and cannot be swapped for another source. †

### B3.4.4 Strips
Flexible lengths of light.
- B3.4.4.01 Strip light: Flexible strip of light sources sold to light a space (under-cabinet, cove); technology is an attribute.

## B3.5 Parts and accessories made for one lamp or fitting

No leaf (closing parts sub-family, leaf = null). Leaf axis, held as the attribute fits: the object served.

- B3.5.01 Lamp shade: Shade made to be fitted to a lamp or fitting.
- B3.5.02 Lamp base: Base or stand sold alone to carry a lamp.
- B3.5.03 Lamp cord set: Cord with a plug and switch or socket made for a lamp; a pendant cord set is this kind with fits set to pendant.
- B3.5.04 Lamp holder or socket: Holder or socket that carries a light source in a lamp or fitting.
- B3.5.05 Chandelier crystal or glass diffuser: Crystal drop or glass diffuser made to be fitted to a chandelier or fitting.

## Precedence inside B3

- Fittings and lamps are filed by placement, never by the word in the name or the power source. A plug-in or cordless lamp that stands or plugs in place stays a standing lamp (B3.2) and is not a carried light; a lamp that is carried or worn is B3.3 even when it is used in a fixed place such as a campsite.
- Dual-mount lamp. An object made to be used in two placements is filed under the first placement in its generic name: a "table/wall lamp" is a table lamp (B3.2.1.01). Where the same body is offered with different fixings (clamp, wall plate, stand), the fixing is an installation attribute and the kind does not change.
- Desk lamp against table lamp. A lamp with an adjustable arm or head made for task light is a desk lamp (B3.2.1.02), balanced-arm lamps on a base included; a lamp with a fixed shade on a base is a table lamp (B3.2.1.01). A desk lamp that clips on is still a desk lamp with the attribute installation = clamp. A clamp work light with a guard and hook, sold as work or commercial lighting, is the clamp work light (B3.3.4.01).
- Lanterns. A lantern is filed by mount or carry, never by the word. A fixed lantern fitting bracketed to a wall or post is the wall-mounted lantern light (B3.1.2.03). An enclosed lantern with a handle, made to be carried as a light, is one of the carried lantern kinds (B3.3.3.01 to B3.3.3.05), by what lights it: battery, rechargeable or solar is the electric lantern (B3.3.3.01); a mantle heated by gas or liquid fuel is the mantle fuel lantern (B3.3.3.02); a wick and tubular globe is the hurricane lantern (B3.3.3.03); a candle is the candle lantern (B3.3.3.04). A paper lantern made to be carried as a light is B3.3.3.05, and if it fails the two-region test at harvest it becomes a named form under the candle lantern (B3.3.3.04), tagged Japan. A lantern made to stand or hang as decoration is not in B3.
- Spotlight is a homonym. The directional fitting on a ceiling or track head is the spot fitting (B3.1.3.02); the small directional bulb with a GU10 or MR16 cap is the spot bulb (B3.4.2.02).
- Non-electric lamps are not kinds of their own. An oil, gas or candle lamp is filed under the placement kind it matches (table lamp B3.2.1.01, floor lamp B3.2.2.01, wall light B3.1.2.01, chandelier B3.1.1.03, or a carried lantern in B3.3.3) with a fuel tag; an emergency or power-cut light is the same kind with an emergency attribute.
- Torches. A dive light is the handheld flashlight (B3.3.1.01) with a water or depth rating attribute, and the pocket-to-duty hand torch is one kind whatever its output class; a pen-shaped light is the penlight (B3.3.1.02), a ring-mounted one the keychain light (B3.3.1.03), a clipped one the clip-on light (B3.3.1.04), and a large-head or pistol-grip hand spotlight the handheld searchlight (B3.3.1.05). A head torch and a headlamp are one kind (B3.3.2.01).
- Floodlight. A light-only floodlight, plug-in or mains, is the floodlight (B3.1.4.05); wall, post or ground bracket is an installation attribute, and the generated view lists it under every use. A night light is filed at B3.2.1.03 whether it plugs in or stands.
- Mirror light. A wall light made to light the face at a mirror is the mirror light (B3.1.2.02); any other wall light is B3.1.2.01.
- Sunrise lamp. A sunrise or wake-up lamp is the sunrise lamp (B3.2.1.04), even when it carries a radio.
- Stage lighting. A fixture rigged on a truss, bar or stand for a performance is the stage lighting fixture (B3.1.5.01); its fixture types stay named forms, and controllers, dimmers and truss are not entered as kinds until a fetched page shows them sold with stage lighting.
- Bulbs are filed by shape family; cap code, technology, filament style, connectivity and colour are attributes. A smart bulb is the general-service bulb (B3.4.1.01) or the shape kind it matches, with a connectivity attribute. A tube sold to replace a fluorescent tube is the linear tube lamp (B3.4.3.01) whatever its technology. The compact fluorescent lamp (B3.4.3.02) and the high-intensity discharge lamp (B3.4.3.03) are kinds only because they need their own holder or ballast; if a fetched source does not show that, the compact fluorescent lamp becomes a shape attribute of the general bulbs.
- Strips. A flexible light strip sold to light a space is the strip light (B3.4.4.01).
- Parts follow the lamp. A shade, base, cord set, holder or crystal made for one lamp or fitting is in B3.5; a lamp cord set, a pendant cord set included, is B3.5.03, and a lamp holder or socket is B3.5.04.
- Multi-use kinds go to the leaf whose rule defines them, else to the general leaf of the family that holds all their uses (night light B3.2.1.03, floodlight B3.1.4.05).
- Design families are not kinds. A lamp is filed by its form, whatever its ornament or designer: a ceiling shade that hangs is the pendant light (B3.1.1.02), and the free-standing floor and table forms of a design line are the floor lamp (B3.2.2.01) and the table lamp (B3.2.1.01).
