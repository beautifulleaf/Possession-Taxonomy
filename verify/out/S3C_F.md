# S3C_F source census: domain F (Culture, Records & Meaning)

Table: verify/out/S3C_F.tsv (95 rows: F1 27, F2 11, F3 14, F4 15, F5 12, F6 16). Every row URL was fetched in this batch (WebFetch or Parallel_Auth web_fetch). Search excerpts were used only to locate pages. Sites that blocked fetch tools (Discogs, Bedetheque, Artsy, NGA Gemini, RKD, Blu-ray.com studios, LastDodo) are either reached through another page that did load or left out.

- **F1 Media & Publications.** Anchor: Grand Comics Database (publisher > brand group > series) for comics, IMSLP publisher table for printed music, Japanese Wikipedia bunko and light-novel label lists plus the KADOKAWA label directory for Japanese lines, Penguin Classics list and Criterion list as line templates, Cartovia and Stanfords for map publishers and series, Astroshop and George Glazer for globe makers. Gaps: no fetchable cross-publisher book imprint and collection index (Bedetheque 403, no German or French collection index found); no worldwide vinyl pressing-plant or tape-maker directory (only the dated 2009 US list); no magazine publisher index beyond one Fujisan page; no Chinese or Korean publisher list; blank cassette brand lines and film-print makers have no catalogue.
- **F2 Art.** Anchor: Getty AAT for kinds and techniques and ULAN for the artist authority; for makers Artfacts (artists, galleries), Artron (China, 93,094 artists) and Motokawa (Japan technique counts); for figurines the Lladro Database, the Hummel HUM-number PDF (1,983 items) and the Royal Doulton HN list. Gaps: no catalogue of print publishers and ateliers (Gemini, Tamarind, ULAE) and no foundry directory; no Meissen, Capodimonte, Nao or Herend model list; Korean and German artist registers not found; Artsy and RKD would not load.
- **F3 Coins, Stamps, Medals & Trading Cards.** Anchor: Numista (10,417 issuers, 4,905 mints) for coins, tokens and medals; Colnect (68 stamp catalogue systems, 1.57 million stamps, 203,574 coins) and StampWorld for stamps; TCDB (672,817 sets) with Cardboard Connection for card lines; LBMA gold list for refiners; Wikipedia List of mints. Gaps: no catalogue of trophy, award-plaque and regalia makers; no medallist or die-sinker directory; Beckett and Scott numbers sit behind login or licence; no online Japanese or Chinese stamp catalogue found (printed Japan catalogue only); medals and orders only reachable country by country.
- **F4 Religious & Ceremonial Objects.** Anchor: Aeclectic Tarot (2,000+ decks, by publisher) for tarot; METI and Traditional Crafts of Japan lists (17 butsudan and butsugu forms) for Japanese altar forms; Himalayan Art Resources for Tibetan ritual objects; AAT and Nomenclature for kinds. Gaps: no maker directories for Judaica silver, church goods, urns or caskets (casket makers found only in trade news); no Hindu pooja, Sikh, Shinto, Chinese folk-religion or Orthodox icon catalogue; relics and funerary goods reached only through retailers (Memorials.com counts, HECK candle makers).
- **F5 Documents & Personal Records.** Anchor: PRADO (EU identity and travel document models by country), EU driving licence models (195 listed), Colnect phonecards (904,212 cards, 834 manufacturers), Wikipedia list of public transport smart cards, Coxrail for certificates; SAA dictionary for record kinds. Gaps: no register outside Europe for IDs and licences; no catalogue of payment-card programmes or gift cards beyond Colnect sister catalogues; ticket, postcard-publisher and ephemera lists not found; album makers only via retailers (Hakuba, Goldbuch series at Fotoalbumshop, Walther at testsieger.de, Kaufland brand facet); no maker catalogues of archival boxes beyond Gaylord.
- **F6 Natural History & Specimens.** Anchor: IMA-CNMNC master list (about 6,200 species) with Mindat, then Meteoritical Bulletin Database for meteorite classes, MolluscaBase and Conchology for shells, Paleobiology Database for fossil taxa, GIA Gem Encyclopedia with GemSelect for gem species, Mineralogical Record Biographical Archive for dealers. Gaps: no register of gem trade names or varieties; no taxidermy-studio or specimen-preparer directory; no insect, butterfly or bone dealer catalogue; no Chinese-language specimen catalogue; dealer lists for shows (Tucson, Munich) not found.

Cross-cutting notes: Colnect, Numista, TCDB and Discogs are community databases whose licence terms need a check before bulk use (Colnect uses bot protection). Wikipedia lists are seeds only. Catalogue numbers (Michel, Yvert, Stanley Gibbons, Scott) are listed by Colnect but may have rights limits (F3 memo, section 6).
