# HOL_EV_10 report (I1, I2, I3)

Sources: 29 evidence rows from fetched pages (I1 11, I2 10, I3 8); log in verify/work/HOL_EV_10/fetched.tsv. Several big retailers (eBay, Kobo store, Office Depot, Best Buy, Audible, Amazon) refused or were never reachable, so retailer coverage is thin; no Parallel tools used. No I3 community source was found.

**I1 Software.** Shopper ladder: job category (security, office, photo, system, AI) > subtype (antivirus, firewall, password management) > product; platform, price model (trial, free, paid, open source) and product type are filters (heise, a German download library). Quality: for security software, independent lab scores (protection, performance, usability) and audits; for password managers, audit history, open source, encryption design. Signals: AV-TEST (Certified at 10 of 18, TOP PRODUCT at 17.5), AV-Comparatives (Approved, Top-Rated, Advanced+, Gold/Silver/Bronze), Cure53 and SOC 2 audits. Makers lead the quality ranking; the browse tree leads with job. Surprising: Steam users say its software browse is poor.

**I2 Digital media and items.** Ladder: audiobook and e-book stores lead with genre (BookWalker with format-and-readership groups such as manga and light novels), then filters for length, price, language, publisher, abridged; fonts lead with style and foundry. Quality: file ownership (DRM-free), allowance, catalogue, price. Signals: Audie Awards (27 categories, narration plus genre). Genre leads in shops, which the brief bars as a division; makers matter little. Surprising: ownership and who is paid rank above catalogue size.

**I3 Subscriptions.** Ladder: medium or use (films and series, sport, live TV) > provider > plan seat tier (individual, duo, family, student). Quality: has the content you want, then price, ad-free tier, picture quality, simultaneous streams, devices, term. Signals: Stiftung Warentest deliberately gives no grade or winner; Ofcom and survey awards for mobile networks. Providers lead. Surprising: the main test body refuses to rank; Japan shortlists by anime, K-drama and sport.
