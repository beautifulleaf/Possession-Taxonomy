# HOL_EV_11 report (J1 J2 J3 J4)

Caveats: web fetches were hit by 503/403 blocks (Waitrose, REWE, BigBasket, Specialty Produce) and domain-check rate limits; the Parallel tools were not used. Several retailer trees are therefore thin. Evidence rows: J1 8, J2 6, J3 12, J4 7. Fetch log: verify/work/HOL_EV_11/fetched.tsv.

**J1 Meat & Poultry.** Shopper ladder: species (beef, chicken, pork, lamb, turkey, game) > product form (fresh cut, sausage, bacon, deli meat, frozen) > cut or sausage/ham type. REWE's wurst range is about 89 named types, not maker-led; brand is a filter. Quality: marbling and maturity (USDA Prime/Choice/Select), Japanese A5 with BMS (lowest of four sub-scores), Ibérico colour labels (breed, diet, cure time), and production claims (grass-fed, pasture-raised, organic, humane). Buyers trust state or association grades and label colours. Species and type lead, never makers. Surprising: wagyu is not automatically top grade; Ibérico labels are one of the few official ham tiers. No community found.

**J2 Fish & Seafood.** Ladder: fish vs shellfish > species or form (fresh, frozen, smoked, caviar); wild vs farmed splits appear early. Quality: freshness (touch, sight, smell) comes first; then sustainability. Signals: MSC blue label, Seafood Watch tiers; "sushi/sashimi grade" has no standard. Caviar leads with sturgeon species, then egg size and salt level. Species and form lead. Surprising: no USDA-style grade exists for fish; the main buyer signals are sustainability NGOs. Retailer trees were mostly blocked.

**J3 Dairy, Eggs & Alternatives.** Ladder: dairy chilled and eggs as one department > cheese by firmness or make (fresh, soft, mould-ripened, semi-hard, hard, blue) > milk animal and ripening. Specialist cheese shops offer four routes: type, brand, taste, characteristic, with award winners as a collection. Quality: natural rind, paste, flavour length, single-farm provenance. Signals: PDO/AOP, World Cheese Awards, Great Taste, USDA egg grades and housing labels, Ethical Consumer ratings for plant milks. Cheese type leads, makers second; plant milks are chosen by base and use. Surprising: eggs are graded by housing system as much as by quality.

**J4 Fruit & Vegetables.** Ladder: fruit / vegetables (/ salad, herbs, mushrooms) > species > cultivar; brand and organic are filters. Quality: freshness cues per species (firmness, colour, weight for size, smell at stem), season, ripeness. Signals: UNECE/EU classes Extra, Class I, Class II, organic certification, RHS AGM. Species lead, cultivar is the unit for apples (Orange Pippin filters by taste, season, keeping, use). Community: heirloom seed savers identify by variety history. Surprising: veg boxes lead with season and box, not species.
