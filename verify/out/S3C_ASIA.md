# S3C_ASIA source census: Chinese, Korean, Indian and Southeast Asian catalogues

Table: verify/out/S3C_ASIA.tsv (48 rows, 25 categories: A1, A3, A5, B1, B5, B9, C1, C3, D1, D2, D3, D4, E1, E3, F1, F2, F3, F4, G1, H1, I1, I3, J5, J6, J7). Category names are from framework/CATEGORIES_v0_7.md (the live framework; the prompt named v0_6). Every row's page was fetched through the authenticated Parallel fetch tool in this batch. Where the fetched text was only navigation or an intro, the row says so in its notes. Japanese catalogues were left to the other S3C files.

Reading rules for the harvest:
- Chinese "top-10" sites (CNPP, ChinaPP, Maigoo) are pay-to-list rankings. They give brand, parent company, founding year and a link to a sub-ranking per product kind. Harvest names and companies, never the order.
- Namuwiki article pages open, but its category pages (분류:...) returned only the IP-block notice from this host, so no category list could be attested there.

## Per category: anchor source to harvest first, then gaps

- **A1 Clothing.** Anchor: KREAM brand index (Korean and English names, A-Z) and CNPP women's wear with its per-niche rankings (hanfu, jackets, outdoor, middle-aged). Gaps: Musinsa's global brand index fetched as a snap feed, not a brand list; Namuwiki fashion-brand category blocked; Indian ethnic wear brands (Myntra is already in S3C_A) and Southeast Asian labels, batik and baju kurung makers, school and work uniform makers have no catalogue found.
- **A3 Jewellery.** Anchor: Tanishq (38,876 items, 19 product kinds including haram, kada, maang tikka, nose pin, jhumka, 200 named collections, regional bridal communities). ChinaPP gives the ten mainland chain jewellers. Gaps: Chinese long tail beyond ten chains, Malabar, Kalyan and Joyalukkas catalogues (not fetched), Thai and Indonesian silver and gold makers, Korean jewellery makers, Tibetan and Himalayan worn items.
- **A5 Clocks & Watches.** Anchor: Xbiao (腕表之家), 74 brands and 90,890 watches with per-brand counts, including Beijing, Shanghai, Seagull, Fiyta, Peacock and Orient. Gaps: Indian watch makers (Titan, HMT, Sonata, Fastrack) were only seen in search excerpts, so no fetched Indian catalogue; Korean and Taiwanese brands; Chinese clock makers.
- **B1 Furniture & Storage.** Anchor: ChinaPP furniture top-10 and its roughly 60 sub-ranking links (luohan bed, kungfu tea table, jichimu furniture). Gaps: Nilkamal, Godrej Interio, Hanssem and Danawa furniture pages were found in search but not fetched; Southeast Asian rattan and teak makers; traditional Korean (hanok) and Indian furniture kinds.
- **B5 Kitchen & Dining.** Anchor: Hawkins catalogue (22 named series of Indian pressure cookers, cookware and idli stands) for lines; ChinaPP cookware top-10 with about 60 per-kind rankings for Chinese makers (Supor, ASD, CookerKing, Tonze, Joyoung). Gaps: Prestige, TTK and other Indian makers; Korean cookware (Happycall, Lock&Lock) and rice-cooker makers; Chinese tea ware and Southeast Asian kitchenware.
- **B9 Smoking & Vaping.** Anchor: Chinese Wikipedia cigarette brand list (manufacturer, origin and sales region per brand, covering China, Japan, Hong Kong) with Baiqiang's 20-brand Chinese list. Gaps: etmoc.com tobacco brand directory returned garbled text; Indonesian kretek, Indian bidi and Korean cigarette lists were not found as catalogues; Chinese e-cigarette brands; pipes, hookah and betel products.
- **C1 Tools & Workshop Equipment.** Anchor: Taiwan Hand Tool Manufacturers' Association member list (17 product groups, record IDs past 700). Gaps: mainland China and Korean tool brand lists (CNPP power tools is in S3C_C already); Indian tool makers; Vietnamese and Thai makers.
- **C3 Plants & Living Cultures.** Anchor: India's PPV&FR register (10,981 certificates per its home page) for cultivars, and China's plant new variety protection site for application batches. Both hold the names in attachments or PDFs, so harvesting needs those files. Gaps: China's ipr.mofcom.gov.cn catalogue is a frameset and could not be read; Korean seed-variety register not located; bonsai, orchid and ornamental cultivar lists for China and Japan-style registers.
- **D1 Computers & Components.** Anchor stays ZOL and Danawa from S3C_D; this batch adds Smartprix laptops (17,308 items, Indian market). Gaps: Chinese brand-level laptop and monitor lists beyond ZOL; Taiwanese component makers (no member directory fetched); Indian peripheral makers.
- **D2 Phones, Tablets & Personal Devices.** Anchor: 91mobiles brand index for India (Lava, Micromax-type local makers and Chinese brands sold there). Gaps: ZOL phone pages and Smartprix mobiles appeared in search but were not fetched; Korean phones are covered only through Samsung and LG on Danawa; Chinese feature-phone and kids-watch makers.
- **D3 Audio & Video.** Anchor: ZOL TV brand directory (174 brands, 8,532 products, long tail of Chinese white-label brands such as Xiaomi, Redmi, Thunderbird, VIDDA, Royalstar, Chigo). 91mobiles TV finder adds Indian names (VU, Onida, Kodak, Thomson). Gaps: Chinese HiFi, earphone and speaker brand lists; Korean audio makers; ZOL's separate "manu_list" page rendered no list.
- **D4 Cameras & Photography.** Anchor: Danawa lens search (32 makers in the filter, 851,917 listings; lens kinds and mounts) and 7Artisans as a model of Chinese third-party lens series naming. Gaps: camera-wiki's Chinese lens makers category (Viltrox, TTArtisan, Laowa, Meike, Yongnuo) could not be fetched without guessing its URL; ZOL's digital camera brand page did not render; Indian and Korean camera makers other than Samsung and Samyang.
- **E1 Toys & Games.** Anchor: ChinaPP blind-box top-10 with sibling rankings for art toys, plush and kids' toys; Namuwiki toy companies for Korean and Japanese makers; Channapatna shop for Indian craft toy kinds. Gaps: Hpoi and Pop Mart catalogues are in S3C_E; Korean board-game and character-goods makers; Indian craft toy clusters other than Channapatna (Kondapalli, Etikoppaka); Southeast Asian traditional toys.
- **E3 Musical Instruments & Music Gear.** Anchor: Samick product catalogue (Korea, licensed brands Seiler, Knabe, Kramer, Epiphone), Pearl River Piano brand ladder (China), Wikipedia's Indian musical instruments category (150 pages). Gaps: Cort, Young Chang and other Korean makers; Chinese folk-instrument makers (Dunhuang, Yangqin, guzheng) and guitar OEM firms; Indian instrument makers by town (Kolkata, Miraj, Tanjore); gamelan and Thai instrument makers.
- **F1 Media & Publications.** Anchor: Chinese Wikipedia PRC publisher list (ISBN 978-7 publisher code, city, type) and Namuwiki publisher lists by country. Gaps: Douban's publisher index showed only 11 entries in search; Indian publishers rest on Wikipedia categories (88 book publishers) not fetched; webtoon, manhwa and Chinese web-fiction platforms; film and music label lists for all three regions.
- **F2 Art.** Anchor: Saffronart artist directory (about 230 pages, Indian artists) beside Artron (China, already in S3C_F). Gaps: Korean artists (Namuwiki 한국의 미술가 appeared in search but not fetched); Southeast Asian artists; folk and tribal art traditions (Madhubani, Warli, minhwa).
- **F3 Coins, Stamps, Medals & Trading Cards.** Anchor: Youpiaowu PRC stamp catalogue (1,692 issues with issue codes). Gaps: Numista and Colnect China pages and chinesestamp.org (3,850+ items) appeared in search but were not fetched; Indian stamp issues (Wikipedia lists by period); Korean stamps and coins; Asian trading-card and idol-card catalogues.
- **F4 Religious & Ceremonial Objects.** Anchor: Gandhanra (Tibetan implement kinds with counts: tingsha, phurba, kartika, vajra), PujaZen (Hindu puja items) and the Chinese Wikipedia 法器 article (Buddhist and Taoist implements; fetched page gave only the navigation box). Gaps: Chinese and Korean Buddhist altar-ware makers; Taoist implement catalogue (daojiaowang.org page seen in search only); Shinto and Southeast Asian spirit-house and amulet makers; Baidu Baike 宗教用品 not fetched.
- **G1 Vehicles.** Anchor: Danawa auto brand index (about 20 domestic including micro-EV and bus makers, about 55 imported incl. BYD, Zeekr, Dongfeng, VinFast); CarWale (India) and Carlist.my (Malaysia, with Proton and Perodua counts). Gaps: Dongchedi stays the Chinese anchor from S3C_G; Autohome series pages not fetched; two-wheelers outside India; Indonesian, Thai and Vietnamese catalogues; three-wheelers and e-rickshaws.
- **H1 Personal Care & Beauty.** Anchor: Nykaa (1,900+ brands, India), Namuwiki cosmetics brands (21 country sections, Korea grouped under parent firms) and CNPP domestic cosmetics. Gaps: Olive Young and Hwahae are in S3C_H; Chinese NMPA cosmetics registry not found as a page; Southeast Asian (Sociolla) brand lists seen in search only; Ayurvedic and herbal brands; Chinese barbershop and nail brands.
- **I1 Software.** Anchor: Namuwiki software list (function tree with Korean titles, old revision) and Korean Wikipedia's 213 software companies. Gaps: Chinese download-site catalogues (ZOL, Huajun is in S3C_I) beyond what S3C_I holds; Indian app and software product lists found only as press about "Made in India" apps; Southeast Asian app stores; super-app mini-program lists.
- **I3 Subscriptions, Memberships & Online Services.** Anchor: Wikipedia's Over-the-top media services in India (at least 99 providers with parent and languages). Gaps: no Chinese or Korean subscription catalogue was found (only analyst articles on Tencent Video, iQiyi, Youku memberships); no source with plan and price tiers for any region; telecom, music and e-book memberships.
- **J5 Pantry Staples.** Anchor: RiceVarMap v3 (4,726 rice accessions, pinyin cultivar names plus Southeast and South Asian lines). Gaps: ricedata.cn (China national rice variety database) was named in a paper but could not be reached without a guessed URL; APEDA's basmati variety page returned 404 (45 notified varieties per a secondary article); Korean rural-development rice list not located; Indian spices, pulses and flour brands; Asian noodle, sauce and soy-product makers.
- **J6 Coffee, Tea & Cocoa.** Anchor: CNPP famous teas (named teas with their producer associations) and Maigoo's six-class Chinese tea page. Dooars-Terai Wikipedia maps Indian gardens to owners. Gaps: Darjeeling and Assam estate registers (Tea Board list not found); Taiwanese oolong, Japanese-style tea cultivars are in S3C_J; Sri Lankan, Vietnamese and Indonesian estates; Asian coffee (Vietnam, Sumatra, Yunnan) catalogues.
- **J7 Wine, Beer & Spirits.** Anchor: Korean Wikipedia soju page (regional makers and brands), Maigoo baijiu top-10 with about 30 further named brands, and Wikipedia's Beer in Asia. Gaps: Namuwiki soju maker template and traditional liquor pages found in search but not fetched; full baijiu brand list (Maigoo's list_1355) not fetched; makgeolli, Indian whisky (only press rankings found), Thai, Vietnamese and Philippine spirits; Chinese wine (Ningxia) producers.
