## New kinds

Ids are assigned at build under R-58. Both kinds are provisional.

- **"Printed advertisement or handbill"**, leaf F5.3e. Only a buying guide (AbeBooks) names the type. A seller page must show it sold as its own category; otherwise it becomes a named form of broadside.
- **"Opal (cut)"**, F6.5 coloured gemstones. It shares species_id with the F6.1 Opal kind (F-4 placement test). Gem Rock Auctions leads its menu with opals.
