# MKH_D5_NAO report (D5 Video Games, United States, Canada, Mexico, Australia, New Zealand)

Outputs: verify/out/MKH_D5_NAO_makers.tsv (1300 rows), verify/out/MKH_D5_NAO_maker_kinds.tsv (67 rows), fetch log verify/work/MKH_D5_NAO/fetched.tsv. No named forms file: DPW1 already holds 925 D5 named forms.

Makers per country: US 963, CA 116, NZ 113, MX 60, AU 48. About 63 rows reuse an existing maker_id (Atari, Mattel, Valve, Microsoft, Midway, Majesco and others).

Weaknesses: US/CA/AU/MX are thin against their reference shares (US 22.8%, CA 2.3%, AU 1.7%, MX 0.56%); NZ is over-covered because one Wikipedia page lists nearly every studio. Most rows carry no place or founding year, and the category-page rows have only country.

Sources that served best: Wikipedia lists of developers, publishers and indie developers (country, city, years); Wikipedia country, state and city category pages including the Defunct subcategories; Wikipedia "Video games in New Zealand" and "Video games in Australia"; the Wikipedia home-console and handheld lists (kinds); company pages for Nyko, dbrand, Razer, Corsair, Hyperkin, Turtle Beach, Jakks, Polymega, Ouya, ModRetro, Arcade1Up; kokoquest arcade directory; levelup.com Mexican studio list; chilango.com.

Failed or empty: Wikipedia Category Video game companies of Mexico (404), Video game industry in Canada (404), Scuf Gaming (404), gamesbeat Mexico (403), betson (403), goodfirms (403), brainstation (503), UNAM links (404), windowscentral (list not returned), ESA, ESAC, IGEA and NZGDA member directories (not found by search).

Not done: Steam developer pages, Gamescom/ChinaJoy exhibitor lists (dynamic), no Mexican national association directory.

URL note: several Wikipedia article and category URLs were typed from standard titles, not taken from a search result; all resolved or are logged as failed. Subcategory and pagination URLs came from fetched pages.
