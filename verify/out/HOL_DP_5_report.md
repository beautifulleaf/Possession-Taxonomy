## C5 Office & Stationery (not built)
**Way in:** type of object. Cult Pens, Pulp Addiction, Muji and Goulet start there; brands sit in a separate menu. **Ladder:** what you write with or on > which type > what makes the best one. That last step is nib, filling and material for pens, and weight, coating and binding for paper. Models then follow by price band and house. It has 8 level-1 groups, pens first. **Quality:** a nib that writes at once, then nib material (steel < 14k < 18k), then craft. Paper: coating, bleed-through, then gsm (thin coated Tomoe River is prized). **Signals:** no certification body; hallmarks, community favourites and shop tiers stand in. **Maker role:** owner, confirmed. Proposed, provisional: a scoped way_in role for fountain pens, ink, notebooks and planners, plus a tab for pen and paper houses. **Flag:** paper stock needs a register or lines treatment.

## C6 Optical & Scientific Instruments (built)
**Way in:** instrument type, then optical design. Astroshop and High Point both work this way. Use (what you want to observe) is the second filter. **Quality:**
- Telescopes: aperture, then glass class (achromat < ED < APO), then the mount.
- Binoculars: pollutant-free first, as Warentest requires, then optics, then mechanics.
- Microscopes: objective class, metal build, condenser.

**Signals:** Stiftung Warentest, Outdoor Life with the USAF chart, BirdForum "alpha" (disputed), and DIN and JIS standards as attributes. **Role:** owner, confirmed. Beside the main tree go an alpha tier tab, a brand index and use views. **Flags:**
- Dobsonian: decide whether it becomes a kind under C12(a).
- Astrograph: recheck.
- Missing kinds: finder, Barlow lens, eyepiece filter, objectives and eyepieces.
- C6.3.2 is split into five display groups.

## C8 Building Materials & Hardware (not built)
**Way in:** product type. Tooled-Up, Howarth and Tile Outlets lead with it; brand is a filter. Room and use are project views (Choice, Victorian Plumbing). **Ladder:** what you are building with > product type, by application > which grade or class fits. **Quality:**
- Timber: strength class C16 < C24, then treatment.
- Tiles: water absorption, PEI 0–5, slip rating R10+ or DCOF 0.42+.
- Paint: hiding and scrub classes, then emissions, then gloss.
- Fixings: material and property class.

**Signals:** Warentest (40/30/20/10 weighting), Choice, EN 13300, and PEI and DCOF. FSC and PEFC are sourcing marks, not grades. EN 338, ANSI A326.3 and DIN 51130 are named from memory, to attest. **Role:** none, confirmed; a product a test names enters as a named form. **Flags:**
- Paint kinds missing: woodwork paint, primer, spray paint.
- Other kinds: dowel pins and packers.
- Plumbing, electrical, adhesives, batteries and fuels had no HOL evidence: provisional on the memo.
