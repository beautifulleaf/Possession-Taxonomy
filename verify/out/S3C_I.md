# S3C_I source census, domain I (Software, Digital & Subscriptions)

Table: verify/out/S3C_I.tsv (49 rows: I1 14, I2 20, I3 15). Rows with a catalogue that WebFetch could not open (AlternativeTo, TAAFT, Huajun, CryptoSlam, Morisawa, ICANNWiki) were read through the Parallel_Auth tools and say so in the notes.

**I1 Software.** Anchor: AlternativeTo (about 197K products, function category plus licence and platform facets, only reachable through Parallel fetch); second pass through the Wikipedia "Lists of software" category (257 list pages with makers) and the per-region libraries Vector (JP, counts per category), CHIP and heise (DE), Huajun (CN). Operating systems: DistroWatch. AI tools: There's An AI For That (14,536, login for the full list). Security software with tiers: AV-TEST. Gaps: no open catalogue of commercial desktop software by maker for Mac and Windows (Capterra returned 403 and G2 gives categories only); no Korean or French library confirmed (Clubic and Softpedia were not fetched); Homebrew, Chocolatey, winget and the Microsoft Store were not reached; mobile app stores are known only through category lists.

**I2 Digital Media & Items.** Anchor depends on the leaf: fonts, Fonts In Use (typeface, foundry and designer indexes) with Morisawa for Japanese and Wikipedia for classes; NFTs, CryptoSlam (10,000 collections) then CoinGecko (1,951); domain names, IANA Root Zone Database then the Public Suffix List; file formats, PRONOM (over 2,000) with the Library of Congress Recommended Formats Statement; in-game items, cs.trade (16,400 CS2 items) and fnbr.co (21 Fortnite types). Gaps: no open catalogue of e-book titles by publisher or of digital film and music titles (retailer catalogues are huge and mostly not browsable); Google Fonts, MyFonts, Adobe Fonts, Steam Community Market, Roblox, BookWalker, Kobo, Audible and Aozora Bunko were searched but not fetched (429, 402, 403 or no URL found); the owner's own photographs and videos have no catalogue at all, only format registries; Chinese and Korean sources for digital goods are missing.

**I3 Subscriptions, Memberships & Online Services.** Anchor: Wikipedia "List of streaming media services" (parent company and subscribers per service), with the Wikipedia comparison tables for music, webmail, file hosting and messaging for utilities. Connectivity: Wikipedia European mobile operator list (ownership, MCC/MNC), the UK MVNO list, ARCEP's French MVNO list, Check24 (DE) and Uswitch (UK). Subscription boxes: FindSubscriptionBoxes; Japan: Subsc. Venues and passes: ROAM (526 reciprocal museums), Wikipedia health club chains, Eurail/Interrail. Gaps: no catalogue of game subscriptions or of news and magazine services (Readly, PressReader) was fetched; no meal-kit or wine-club directory; no national museum, heritage or zoo membership directory beyond ROAM; Chinese streaming memberships and Korean services have no usable fetched catalogue (the Korean comparison page returned empty); US and Indian connectivity plans are not covered beyond the S2 list; JustWatch's per-country provider list could not be reached.

No category lacks a usable catalogue, but I1 and I3 have no maker-level list for the commercial (non-open) long tail, and I2 has no title-level list for e-books, music and film.
