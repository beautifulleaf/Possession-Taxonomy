# D4 Cameras & Photography: decided tree

Category: D4 Cameras & Photography (domain D, Electronics). Membership test (placement key CATEGORIES_v0_7, step 24, fourth branch; K-151, K-156): a camera (camcorders, action and 360 cameras, and wildlife trail cameras included) or equipment for taking and making pictures, powered or not: lenses, flashes and photo lighting, ring lights, tripods, film, photographic paper, darkroom equipment, and drones made for filming; together with the parts and accessories made for one camera or lens (S3: camera bags, straps, batteries and their chargers, camera-specific cables, lens filters, hoods, mount adapters, cages). Not here: webcams, scanners (film scanners included), printers, memory cards, card readers, standard USB and HDMI cables and generic chargers (D1); smartphones and gimbals, tripod clamps, selfie sticks and clip-on lenses made only for phones (D2, K-5); slide and film projectors (D3); room lamps (B3) and decorative lights (B4); security, doorbell and baby cameras and their recorders (B8, K-14); pet-only cameras (B10, K-103); binoculars, telescopes and cameras made only to fit a telescope (C6, K-15); developed negatives, slides and prints (F5; editioned, signed or stamped prints F2) and photo files (I2); toy cameras that cannot take a picture and hobby or camera-less toy drones (E1, K-97); dashcams and other cameras made to be fitted to a vehicle (G1, K-6); general backpacks (A4); studio backdrops (B2) and studio furniture (B1, K-17); copy and printer paper (C5, K-158). Structure (Candidate A, decided D-19, D-20, D-21, CV-16, CV-17; memo sections 3, 4b and 10): sub-family (role in the picture-making chain: capture, optics, light, support, sensitised media, processing; the closing parts sub-family D4.7 is exempt from this axis, C5(d)), then leaf (the axis stated per sub-family: recording technology in D4.1, what the object does with light in D4.3, process stage in D4.6; D4.2, D4.4, D4.5 and D4.7 hold about 12 kinds or fewer and store `leaf = null`, C5(b), so their kind ids carry no leaf part), then kind (the generic type buyers compare: body and viewing system within the recording technology for cameras, optical design for lenses, source type for lights and shape for modifiers, process for film). Each object gets one kind. Lens mounts, flash shoes, light-modifier mounts, film formats, instant-film formats and camera-battery platforms are rows of the C13 compatibility ledger, never kinds or levels; sensor format (medium format and Micro Four Thirds included), format coverage, focal class, maximum aperture, ISO speed, enlarger type, chemical type and paper process are attributes; maker, era, price band, condition, user, setting (wildlife, underwater, studio, travel) and scene (vlogging, film revival, lo-fi, cine) are modifiers, tags or views, never kinds (memo sections 4b, 5, 6, 7; section 14). Model, series, stock and iconic design are named forms (tripod-head types, softbox shapes and the C-stand are non-maker named forms); a digital back for view and medium-format cameras is a candidate kind only after attestation and has no id. Maker role: owner (maker_roles.tsv, provisional: camera and lens systems carry the maker; body and lens models carry the maker, and identity makers such as Leica, Hasselblad, Polaroid, Kodak, Ilford and Lomography show through their lines and named forms). Display groups (navigation proposal, display only, never moving a home; D4 is not built, so no group ids are issued): level 1 shows the seven sub-families as Cameras (D4.1); Lenses and optical attachments (D4.2); Lighting (D4.3); Tripods, gimbals and support (D4.4); Film and instant film (D4.5); Darkroom and processing (D4.6); Camera accessories made for one camera or lens (D4.7); level 2 shows the memo leaves; a page intro asks whether a phone is enough and cross-links to D2 smartphones (section 14, display only, not a kind); side views: By system (lens mount and film format, generated from C13 rows, loaded with the tree; section 14) and a brand tab. Quality markers (display, judged on named forms, never stored on a kind): cameras by sensor size (medium format, full frame, APS-C, Micro Four Thirds, 1-inch, 1/2.3-inch), pixel size over megapixel count, lens quality and lens availability in the system, stabilisation, autofocus and speed, video features, build, weather sealing and long-term support; lenses by sharpness, distortion, vignetting, transmission (T-stop), chromatic aberration, maximum aperture and build; film by process and speed (ISO) with grain and latitude; lighting by power, recycle time and colour accuracy; support by load capacity and stability (lighting, support and film markers provisional on the memo, section 14). A kind marked † is named only by the key, a decision or a reference work, or rests on a claim not yet shown on a fetched page, and needs a fetched page in the stage-3 harvest before it enters the data (memo section 10 attestation list); its id is allocated here in memo order.

Ids (R-58): D4.n is a sub-family and D4.n.m a leaf; a kind is D4.n.m.kk, or D4.n.kk where a sub-family has no leaf.

## D4.1 Cameras

Leaf axis: recording technology (what the image is recorded on), admitted as a kind-level split by C10(b) (D-3): digital, film, instant.

### D4.1.1 Digital cameras

Kinds by body and viewing system: mirrorless before DSLR before rangefinder before compact, then the video-first and special bodies. Sensor format is an attribute (D-19).

- D4.1.1.01 Mirrorless interchangeable-lens camera: digital stills and video body with exchangeable lenses and no reflex mirror; any sensor size, medium format included.
- D4.1.1.02 Digital single-lens reflex camera: digital body with exchangeable lenses and a mirror-and-prism optical viewfinder.
- D4.1.1.03 Digital rangefinder camera: digital body with a rangefinder focusing and framing window and exchangeable lenses.
- D4.1.1.04 Compact fixed-lens camera: digital stills camera with a lens that cannot be removed, built small for pocket or carry use.
- D4.1.1.05 Bridge or superzoom camera: digital stills camera with a fixed long-range zoom lens in a larger, SLR-like body.
- D4.1.1.06 Camcorder: handheld camera built mainly to record video for long takes, with a fixed or built-in zoom lens.
- D4.1.1.07 Digital cinema camera: professional motion-picture camera body for cinema or high-end video, with cine mount and cine connectors.
- D4.1.1.08 Action camera: small rugged camera made to be mounted on a person, helmet, bike or vehicle for video.
- D4.1.1.09 360-degree camera: camera with two or more lenses that records a full spherical view.
- D4.1.1.10 Pocket gimbal camera: small handheld device with a camera fixed to a built-in motorised stabilising gimbal. †
- D4.1.1.11 Trail camera: camera in a weatherproof housing that is left outdoors and triggered by motion, mainly for wildlife (K-156; wildlife is a setting tag). †
- D4.1.1.12 Photo booth: cabinet or stand that combines a camera, light and printer to take pictures of guests (D4-O1). †
- D4.1.1.13 Camera drone: unmanned aircraft made for filming, carrying a stabilised camera (K-97). †

### D4.1.2 Film cameras

Kinds by viewing and body system: SLR, rangefinder, viewfinder, TLR, view, box or folding, single-use, pinhole. Film format (135, 120, sheet) is a compatibility attribute.

- D4.1.2.01 Film single-lens reflex camera: film body with a mirror-and-prism viewfinder that shows the picture through the taking lens.
- D4.1.2.02 Film rangefinder camera: film body with a rangefinder window for focusing and framing.
- D4.1.2.03 Viewfinder or point-and-shoot film camera: film camera with a separate viewfinder and simple controls, including plastic lo-fi cameras that take real pictures.
- D4.1.2.04 Twin-lens reflex camera: film body with two lenses, one for the waist-level viewing screen and one for taking.
- D4.1.2.05 View camera: large-format camera with bellows, ground-glass back and lens movements.
- D4.1.2.06 Box or folding camera: simple box-shaped camera, or one with a folding bellows body, for roll film.
- D4.1.2.07 Single-use camera [disposable camera]: pre-loaded film camera sold to be used once and sent for processing.
- D4.1.2.08 Pinhole camera: camera whose lens is a small aperture with no glass.

### D4.1.3 Instant cameras

Kinds by film type. Instax and Polaroid formats are instant-film-format entries in the compatibility ledger.

- D4.1.3.01 Integral-film instant camera: camera that makes a self-developing print on integral instant film.
- D4.1.3.02 Peel-apart instant camera: camera that uses pack film with a separate negative and positive that are peeled apart.
- D4.1.3.03 Hybrid instant camera: camera with a digital sensor and screen that also prints on instant film. †

## D4.2 Lenses and optical attachments

No leaf (`leaf = null`, 13 kinds). Kinds by optical design: general-purpose (prime, zoom), special-purpose designs, cine lens, then optical attachments and adapters. Mount is a compatibility entry; format coverage and focal class are attributes.

- D4.2.01 Prime lens: lens with one fixed focal length.
- D4.2.02 Zoom lens: lens with a variable focal length.
- D4.2.03 Macro lens: lens built to focus close and reproduce small subjects at high magnification.
- D4.2.04 Fisheye lens: ultra-wide lens with a very strong curved (non-rectilinear) projection.
- D4.2.05 Tilt-shift lens: lens whose optics tilt or shift to control perspective and the plane of focus.
- D4.2.06 Anamorphic lens: lens that squeezes the image horizontally for later de-squeezing to a wider frame.
- D4.2.07 Special-effect lens: lens made to give a deliberate effect such as soft focus, swirl or selective blur.
- D4.2.08 Cine lens: lens built for motion-picture work, marked in T-stops, with geared rings and a cine mount.
- D4.2.09 Teleconverter: optical unit fitted between camera and lens that lengthens the focal length.
- D4.2.10 Close-up lens: simple glass element fitted in front of a lens to focus closer. †
- D4.2.11 Lens filter: glass or resin disc or plate that fits a lens front to alter the light reaching it.
- D4.2.12 Mount adapter: ring with no optics that fits a lens of one mount to a body of another.
- D4.2.13 Focal reducer: mount adapter with optics that shorten the focal length and widen the aperture while changing mounts.

## D4.3 Light

Leaf axis: what the object does with light (makes a flash; gives continuous light; shapes light; holds the source or the studio backdrop).

### D4.3.1 Flash

Kinds by portability.

- D4.3.1.01 Speedlight (flashgun): small battery flash that mounts on the camera hot shoe or a stand.
- D4.3.1.02 Studio monolight (strobe head): self-contained studio flash head with its own power supply and modelling lamp.
- D4.3.1.03 Studio flash pack and head: studio flash whose power pack is separate from one or more flash heads. †

### D4.3.2 Continuous light

Kinds by source type; power is an attribute.

- D4.3.2.01 LED panel: flat panel of LEDs giving constant light.
- D4.3.2.02 COB LED light: single chip-on-board LED source, usually in a reflector or with a modifier mount.
- D4.3.2.03 Fresnel light: constant light with a stepped Fresnel lens that focuses the beam.
- D4.3.2.04 Ring light: circular light source made to surround the lens or the subject's face. †

### D4.3.3 Light modifiers

Kinds by shape, ordered soft to hard; the modifier mount (Bowens, Profoto) is a compatibility entry.

- D4.3.3.01 Softbox: fabric box with a diffusion front that softens a light placed behind it (strip box and octabox are non-maker named forms).
- D4.3.3.02 Umbrella: umbrella-shaped reflector or shoot-through diffuser for a light.
- D4.3.3.03 Reflector dish: metal bowl that directs light from a flash head.
- D4.3.3.04 Portable reflector or diffuser: collapsible disc or panel that bounces or diffuses light.
- D4.3.3.05 Beauty dish: shallow dish with a central deflector that gives a crisp, wrapped light.
- D4.3.3.06 Snoot: tube that narrows a light to a small circle.
- D4.3.3.07 Grid: honeycomb or egg-crate that narrows and straightens a beam.
- D4.3.3.08 Barn doors: hinged flaps fitted to a light to cut and shape the beam.
- D4.3.3.09 Gobo: pattern or shape cutout placed in a beam to project a shadow or pattern.
- D4.3.3.10 Colour gel: coloured or correction sheet fitted over a light.

### D4.3.4 Light stands and mounts

- D4.3.4.01 Light stand: adjustable stand that carries a light or modifier (the C-stand is a non-maker named form).
- D4.3.4.02 Boom arm: counterweighted arm that carries a light overhead.
- D4.3.4.03 Backdrop stand: stand and crossbar that hold a studio backdrop (R-1; the backdrop itself is B2). †

## D4.4 Support and stabilisation

No leaf (`leaf = null`, 9 kinds). Material, height and load capacity are attributes; head types (ball, pan-and-tilt, fluid, gimbal, panoramic) are non-maker named forms of the tripod head.

- D4.4.01 Tripod: three-legged stand for a camera.
- D4.4.02 Tabletop tripod: very short tripod for a table or surface.
- D4.4.03 Tripod head: separate head (ball, pan-and-tilt, fluid, gimbal, panoramic) that mounts on a tripod or monopod.
- D4.4.04 Quick-release system: plate, L-bracket or collar that lets a camera or lens click on and off a support.
- D4.4.05 Monopod: single-leg support.
- D4.4.06 Handheld gimbal stabiliser: motorised handheld stabiliser for a camera.
- D4.4.07 Camera rig: shoulder or frame system that carries a camera with accessories.
- D4.4.08 Camera cage: frame that fits around a camera body and gives it mounting points.
- D4.4.09 Camera mount and clamp: clamp or bracket that fixes a camera to a pole, rail, window or vehicle.

## D4.5 Film and sensitised media

No leaf (`leaf = null`, 6 kinds). Kinds by process (the chemistry the film is made for). Film format (135, 120, sheet sizes, motion-picture gauges, instant formats) is a compatibility attribute; the stock is the named form.

- D4.5.01 Black-and-white negative film: panchromatic negative film processed in black-and-white chemistry.
- D4.5.02 Colour negative film (C-41): still-photography colour negative film processed in the C-41 process.
- D4.5.03 Colour negative film (ECN-2, motion picture): cinema colour negative stock processed in the ECN-2 process. †
- D4.5.04 Colour reversal film (E-6): slide film processed in the E-6 process to a positive.
- D4.5.05 Integral instant film: self-developing film in a frame that develops in the print itself.
- D4.5.06 Peel-apart instant film: instant film pack whose negative and positive are separated after development.

## D4.6 Darkroom and processing

Leaf axis: the stage of the darkroom process the object serves, with materials used across stages in the last leaf (C14(c)).

### D4.6.1 Film processing

- D4.6.1.01 Developing tank: light-tight tank in which film is processed.
- D4.6.1.02 Developing reel: spiral reel that holds film in a tank.
- D4.6.1.03 Changing bag: light-proof bag for loading film into a tank.

### D4.6.2 Enlarging and exposure

- D4.6.2.01 Enlarger: darkroom projector that exposes a negative onto paper (condenser, diffusion or colour dichroic: `enlarger_type` attribute).
- D4.6.2.02 Enlarging lens: lens made to project a negative in an enlarger.
- D4.6.2.03 Enlarging easel: frame that holds paper flat under an enlarger.
- D4.6.2.04 Focus finder: magnifier used to focus the projected grain.
- D4.6.2.05 Darkroom timer: timer that controls the enlarger or safelight.
- D4.6.2.06 Enlarger filter: multigrade or colour filter for the enlarger.

### D4.6.3 Print processing

- D4.6.3.01 Developing tray: shallow tray for print chemistry.
- D4.6.3.02 Safelight: darkroom lamp that does not fog paper.
- D4.6.3.03 Print dryer: equipment for drying prints.

### D4.6.4 Darkroom materials

- D4.6.4.01 Photographic chemistry: developer, stop, fixer and related chemistry (`chemical_type` attribute).
- D4.6.4.02 Photographic printing paper: light-sensitive paper for prints (K-156; `paper_process`: silver-gelatin fibre or resin-coated, chromogenic, inkjet photo paper).

## D4.7 Parts and accessories made for one camera or lens

No leaf (`leaf = null`, 13 kinds); exempt from the role axis (C5(d), CV-17). Kind axis: the accessory type, by the object served (camera or lens).

- D4.7.01 Camera bag or case: bag, backpack or case made to carry a camera and lenses.
- D4.7.02 Camera strap: strap or sling that carries a camera.
- D4.7.03 Camera battery: rechargeable battery made for a camera.
- D4.7.04 Camera battery charger: charger made for a camera battery.
- D4.7.05 Battery grip: grip that adds a second battery and controls to a camera body.
- D4.7.06 Viewfinder or eyepiece: viewfinder, eyepiece or magnifier fitted to a camera.
- D4.7.07 Remote shutter release: cable or wireless release for a camera, including camera-specific cables.
- D4.7.08 Light meter (exposure and flash): handheld meter that measures light for exposure.
- D4.7.09 Camera cleaning kit: kit for cleaning a lens or sensor.
- D4.7.10 Underwater housing: waterproof case made for a camera.
- D4.7.11 Lens hood: shade that fits the front of a lens.
- D4.7.12 Lens cap: cover for the front or rear of a lens.
- D4.7.13 Telescope camera adapter (T-ring): T-ring or adapter that fits a camera to a telescope.

## Precedence inside D4

- A hybrid camera that records stills and video, such as a vlog-oriented interchangeable-lens body, goes by its body class (D4.1.1.01), not by the video bias; vlogging is a tag.
- Sensor format makes no kind: a medium-format digital body with interchangeable lenses and no mirror is D4.1.1.01, and full-frame against APS-C is an attribute (D-19). A digital back has no kind until attested.
- A speed booster or focal reducer is both a mount adapter and an optical converter: it goes to D4.2.13, not D4.2.12, with both mounts recorded as compatibility relations.
- A camera cage with handles supports the camera for shooting, so it is D4.4.08, not D4.7.
- An instant-film camera is the camera kind (D4.1.3.01 to D4.1.3.03); the film made for it is D4.5.05 or D4.5.06, and both point to the same instant-film-format row. A camera that is both a digital camera and an instant printer is D4.1.3.03.
- A tripod or gimbal made for cameras that also takes a phone stays in D4.4; a universal tripod is D4.4.01. One made only for phones is D2 (K-5).
- A camera made to be left outdoors and triggered by motion is D4.1.1.11, whatever the setting; wildlife is a setting, not a kind.
- A drone made for filming is D4.1.1.13; a hobby RC or FPV drone and a camera-less toy drone are E1 (K-97).
- A booth that holds the camera, light and printer in one cabinet is D4.1.1.12, not furniture.
- A camera made for general video and only mounted on a vehicle is an action camera (D4.1.1.08), and its vehicle mount or clamp is D4.4.09; a dashcam is G1 (K-6).
- A four-by-five lens for a view camera is a prime lens (D4.2.01) with `format_coverage = large format`; a lens made to project a negative in a darkroom enlarger is D4.6.2.02, not D4.2.
- A plastic film camera that takes real pictures (Holga, Lomo) is D4.1.2.03; a toy camera that cannot take a picture is E1. A cinema stock is D4.5.03, not a separate cine-film kind.
- The stand and crossbar that hold a studio backdrop are D4.3.4.03; the studio light stand is D4.3.4.01; the backdrop itself is B2 (K-17, R-1).
- A backpack made with fitted dividers to carry a camera and lenses is D4.7.01; a general backpack with a padded sleeve is A4. A T-ring that fits a camera to a telescope is D4.7.13.
- Photographic printing paper of any process, inkjet photo paper included, is D4.6.4.02. Unexposed film and unused paper are D4; developed negatives, slides and prints are F5 (editioned, signed or stamped prints F2).
- Tripod-head types (ball, fluid, gimbal) are D4.4.03 as one kind; "gimbal" also names the handheld stabiliser (D4.4.06), told apart by whether it mounts on a tripod.
- A camera battery, its charger, a camera strap, a lens filter and a camera-specific remote cable are D4 (D4.7.03, D4.7.04, D4.7.02, D4.2.11, D4.7.07); a memory card, card reader, standard USB or HDMI cable and generic charger are D1.
