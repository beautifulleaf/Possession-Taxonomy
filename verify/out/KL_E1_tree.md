# E1 Toys & Games: decided tree (kind-level test)

Category: made for play, or for a game of skill or chance played at a table, on a board or floor, or with cards, dice, tiles or tokens. Place each object in exactly one kind. Levels: sub-family, then leaf (where there is one), then kind. Named forms (lines, series, published titles) sit below a kind and are not part of this test. Scale, kit-or-built, material, age, franchise, sales format (blind box, prize, booster) and the like are attributes, never kinds.

## E1.1 Dolls, figures & soft toys
Leaf axis: the form of the figure played with or displayed.

Dolls
- E1-K01 Fashion doll: a styled doll with a slim adult-like or teen body, made to be dressed and posed.
- E1-K02 Baby doll: a doll made to look like an infant or toddler, for nurturing play.
- E1-K03 Ball-jointed doll: a doll whose limbs and torso are joined by ball joints on elastic or string, made for posing and customising.
- E1-K04 Bisque or porcelain doll: a doll with head and body cast as one in porcelain or bisque.

Figures (scale is a required attribute; prize channel and kit-or-built are attributes)
- E1-K05 Articulated action figure: a figure with many joints made to be posed and played with or displayed in varied poses.
- E1-K06 Static pre-posed figure: a figure fixed in one pose, in any scale including statue scale, sold finished or as an unpainted kit to assemble and paint; includes prize figures and loose miniatures sold outside a game-system box.
- E1-K07 Chibi or super-deformed figure: a figure with an oversized head and small body, usually with swappable faces or parts.
- E1-K08 Designer or art-toy figure: a figure made as a collectible art object, often vinyl, from a designer's or brand's own character, often blind-packed.
- E1-K09 Toy animal or toy soldier figure: a small moulded figure of an animal or soldier made for pretend play.
- E1-K10 Bobblehead: a figure with a head on a spring or pin so that it nods.

Soft toys
- E1-K11 Plush toy: a stuffed fabric toy; includes plush pendants with clip or chain and figures with a stuffed fabric body.
- E1-K12 Puppet: a figure worked by the hand, rods or strings to act out play.

Dolls' houses & playsets
- E1-K13 Dolls' house: a house or building made at doll scale to be furnished and played in.
- E1-K14 Figure-and-scene playset: small figures sold with a scene, building or set of accessories for pretend play.

## E1.2 Construction toys
No leaf. Kinds by system class (the way the pieces join).
- E1-K15 Interlocking brick set: a set of plastic bricks or blocks that clip together by studs or tubes.
- E1-K16 Metal construction set: a set of perforated metal strips and plates joined by nuts and bolts.
- E1-K17 Wooden construction set: a set of wooden blocks, rods or boards for building.
- E1-K18 Marble-run construction set: a set of parts that join into a track or tower for rolling balls.
- E1-K111 Toy robot: a robot made as a toy for play.
- E1-K112 Coding toy: a toy made for play that teaches or plays programming by commands.

## E1.3 Scale models & model railways
Leaf axis: the object modelled at scale, by its form (two leaves). Kit or built, subject, material and scale are attributes.

Model vehicles & machines (cars, aircraft, ships, armour, mecha)
- E1-K19 Assembly scale model: a model of a vehicle or machine in plastic, resin or card, sold as a kit to assemble or pre-built.
- E1-K20 Die-cast scale model: a metal model of a vehicle at any scale, from 1:64 pocket cars to 1:18 display models.

Model railways (scale and gauge are required attributes)
- E1-K22 Model locomotive: a powered model railway engine.
- E1-K23 Model rolling stock: an unpowered model railway wagon, coach or carriage.
- E1-K24 Model railway track: rails and points for a model railway.
- E1-K25 Model railway train set: a starter set of locomotive, stock and track sold together.
- E1-K26 Model railway scenery kit: buildings, scenery or lineside accessories for a model railway.

## E1.4 Play vehicles & ride-ons
No leaf. Play vehicles other than die-cast scale models, plus ride-ons.
- E1-K28 Push-along or pull-back toy vehicle: a plastic or wooden vehicle the child pushes or that runs by pull-back or friction.
- E1-K29 Wooden toy railway: a wooden toy train with wooden track.
- E1-K30 Radio-controlled vehicle: a car, boat, aircraft or hobby drone controlled by radio, sold as an RC toy or hobby model; includes combustion-engined hobby cars and camera-less toy drones.
- E1-K31 Slot-car racing set: a toy car racing set with grooved track and hand controllers.
- E1-K32 Ride-on car: a child-sized car pushed, pedalled or powered, that the child sits in.
- E1-K33 Balance bike: a child's bike without pedals, pushed along with the feet.
- E1-K34 Children's tricycle: a three-wheeled pedalled cycle for a young child.

## E1.5 Games
Leaf axis: the component set owned. Mechanism, theme, audience, player count and playing time are never kinds. Every published title is a named form of its kind.

Board games
- E1-K35 Boxed board game: a published game on a board or in a box with its own pieces.
- E1-K36 Chess set: a board and pieces for chess.
- E1-K37 Go set: a board and stones for go.
- E1-K38 Shogi set: a board and pieces for shogi.
- E1-K39 Backgammon set: a board with checkers and dice for backgammon.
- E1-K40 Reversi set: a board and two-sided discs for reversi (Othello is a named form).
- E1-K41 Draughts set: a board and discs for draughts (checkers).

Card games
- E1-K42 Standard playing-card deck: a deck of regional-pattern playing cards (French-suited, German-suited, tarock and similar), sold for card play; size is an attribute.
- E1-K43 Proprietary card game: a published card game with its own special cards.
- E1-K44 Flower-card deck: a 48-card Japanese or Korean flower-card deck (hanafuda).

Tile and dice games
- E1-K45 Domino set: a set of domino tiles.
- E1-K46 Mahjong tile set: a set of mahjong tiles.
- E1-K47 Proprietary tile game: a published game played with its own tiles.
- E1-K48 Proprietary dice game: a published game played mainly with its own dice.
- E1-K49 Dice set: a set of polyhedral or ordinary dice sold as a set.

Trading card games
- E1-K50 Trading-card-game sealed product: a sealed pack, box, tin or ready-made deck of a trading card game.
- E1-K51 Trading-card-game single card: a single card of a trading card game, as a kind (a particular card is an individual item and out).

Miniatures games (sold in a game-system box)
- E1-K52 Miniatures-game starter set: a box with miniatures, rules and accessories to start a miniatures game.
- E1-K53 Miniatures-game army or unit box: a box of miniatures making up a unit or army for a miniatures game.

Table, cabinet & dexterity games (no screen)
- E1-K54 Table football: a table with rods of players for a game of football.
- E1-K55 Air hockey table: a table with an air cushion for a puck game.
- E1-K56 Pinball machine: a coin-operated or home machine with flippers and a ball.
- E1-K57 Claw crane, redemption game or other coin-operated game without a screen.
- E1-K58 Carrom board: a square board with pockets, played by flicking discs.
- E1-K59 Crokinole board: a round board with a central hole, played by flicking discs.
- E1-K60 Dexterity game: a game of physical skill with its own pieces (stacking, balancing, flicking).

Casino & gaming equipment
- E1-K61 Poker chip set: chips sold as a set for poker or casino play.
- E1-K62 Roulette wheel: a roulette wheel for casino or home play.
- E1-K63 Gaming table: a table made for poker, blackjack, craps or baccarat.
- E1-K64 Card shoe or shuffler: a dealing shoe or card shuffler.
- E1-K65 Prize wheel: a spinning wheel for prizes or chance.
- E1-K66 Raffle drum: a rotating drum for drawing raffle tickets.
- E1-K67 Bingo set: bingo cards, balls, a cage or a caller sold for bingo.
- E1-K68 Slot machine: a casino or skill-stop slot machine, whatever display it uses.

## E1.6 Puzzles
No leaf. Piece count, format and material are attributes.
- E1-K69 Jigsaw puzzle: a flat picture cut into interlocking pieces.
- E1-K70 Three-dimensional jigsaw puzzle: interlocking pieces that assemble into a solid shape.
- E1-K71 Mechanical puzzle: a puzzle solved by manipulating its parts, including twisty, interlocking and disentanglement puzzles and puzzle boxes.

## E1.9 Parts and accessories made for one toy or game
No leaf; exempt from the sub-family axis. A part made for one kind of object follows that object and goes here.
- E1-K102 Doll clothes and shoes: clothes or shoes made for dolls.
- E1-K103 Dolls' pram: a pram or pushchair for dolls.
- E1-K104 Card sleeve: a protective sleeve for individual playing or trading cards.
- E1-K105 Deck box: a box made to hold a deck of cards.
- E1-K107 Poker-chip case: a case made to hold poker chips.
- E1-K108 Casino table layout: a printed cloth layout for a casino game table.
- E1-K109 Model-railway controller: a hand or digital controller for a model railway.
- E1-K110 Replacement game pieces and game accessories: spare or add-on pieces made for a game, including game money sold for one game.

## In-category precedence sentences
1. Games are filed by the physical component set the owner holds (a board with pieces, a standard deck, a domino set, dice, a sealed trading-card product, a miniatures starter box, a wheel or table), never by mechanism, theme, audience, player count or playing time.
2. Chess, go, shogi, backgammon, reversi and draughts sets are their own kinds; every other published boxed game is a boxed board game.
3. Hot Wheels, Matchbox and every other die-cast car are one kind (die-cast scale model) at every scale; there is no separate die-cast toy vehicle kind.
4. A figure kit (an unpainted figure to assemble and paint) is a static pre-posed figure; a model of a vehicle or machine sold as a kit is an assembly scale model. Kit or built does not change the kind.
5. Miniatures sold in a game-system box are miniatures games; loose miniatures sold outside a game-system box are static pre-posed figures.
6. A plush pendant with a clip or chain is a plush toy; a figure pendant is filed by the figure kind of its form; a figure with a stuffed fabric body is a plush toy even if its face is vinyl.
7. A trading-card-game pack, box, tin or ready-made deck is sealed product; a single card is a single card; a card sleeve or deck box follows the cards into the parts sub-family; a binder holding several cards is outside E1 and not a kind here.
8. A pack sold for card play (tarock, French tarot, German-suited) is a standard playing-card deck; a flower-card deck is its own kind.
9. A spirit board made as a board game is a boxed board game.
10. Model-railway track is a model railway kind, not a part; a part made for one toy or game (doll clothes, sleeves, deck boxes, chip cases, table layouts, railway controllers, a display container for one figure) goes to the parts sub-family.
11. A slot-car racing set is its own kind; a wooden toy railway with its wooden track is its own kind; a radio-controlled vehicle is its own kind whatever it models.
12. A toy robot or coding toy made for play is E1.2; a toy that is a torch or nightlight is a toy light.
13. Pinball machines, claw cranes and redemption games without a screen are E1.5 table, cabinet and dexterity games; casino slot machines are gaming equipment.
14. A swing set (frame with seats) and a swing seat sold alone are two kinds; a garden or play trampoline and a paddling pool for play are play equipment.
15. A toy made to imitate a sport, music or weapon object belongs here when it cannot do the real object's job to that object's own standard.
