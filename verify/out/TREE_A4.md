# A4 Bags & Accessories: decided tree

Category: A4 Bags & Accessories (domain A, Worn & Carried). Membership test (key step 17, fourth branch): a bag or luggage of any kind (handbags, backpacks and rucksacks, suitcases, duffels, dry bags, garment bags, tote and shopping bags), wallet or purse, umbrella, sunglasses or other non-corrective eyewear, hand fan, keyring, fashion cane, baby carrier or sling, shopping trolley, worn on the body or carried on the person or in hand as a personal accessory; holsters (K-84), hydration packs sold with their bag (K-161), chalk bags and quivers (K-137) and luggage scales are A4. A case made for one object at a time follows that object (S3); a case made for the small items of one task follows the task (pencil case C5, toiletry bag H1, tool bag C1, smoking cases B9); insulated lunch and cool bags are B5, furoshiki B2, powered hand-held fans B7, standing umbrellas B1. Structure: sub-family (what the object is made to do for the owner; the closing sub-family, parts and accessories made for one bag, is exempt from this axis, C5(d)), then kind (silhouette or format: the generic type a buyer names and compares). The leaf level is not used anywhere in A4: every sub-family stores `leaf = null` (C5(b); A4.1 keeps 14 kinds, "about 12" being a guide, A-21). Each object gets one kind. Maker, price band, size (with its standard), size class, shell construction and material, leather grade, rim structure, frame material, laptop size, capacity, scene, region, user and era are attributes, tags and views, never kinds. Display only (proposals.tsv, DPH flag 3): level-1 groups follow the sub-families, with A4.9 and A4.10 shown together as "Small carried accessories and bag parts"; inside the bag tab, use groups ("Everyday and school", "Travel", "Outdoor") list kinds at level 2 without changing their home (C14). A kind marked † is named only by the key, a review or a decision, or rests on a claim not yet shown on a fetched page, and needs a fetched page before it enters the data.

Ids (R-58): A4.n is a sub-family and A4.n.m a leaf; a kind is A4.n.m.kk, or A4.n.kk where a sub-family has no leaf.

## A4.1 Handbags & shoulder bags

No leaf (leaf = null). Kind axis: silhouette. Size ("mini") is an attribute; "shopper" is an alias of the tote.

- A4.1.01 Clutch bag: small handle-less bag carried in the hand or under the arm.
- A4.1.02 Minaudière: small rigid, often ornamented evening case carried in the hand.
- A4.1.03 Wristlet bag: small bag with a wrist strap and its own interior compartment (homonym with A4.5.12).
- A4.1.04 Tote bag: open-topped bag with two parallel handles and an unstructured body.
- A4.1.05 Satchel: structured bag with a flap front, usually with top handles and a shoulder strap.
- A4.1.06 Top-handle bag: structured bag carried mainly by one or two short handles on top.
- A4.1.07 Shoulder bag: bag on a single strap worn on one shoulder.
- A4.1.08 Crossbody bag: bag with a soft body and no front flap, on a strap worn across the body.
- A4.1.09 Messenger bag: crossbody bag with a full front flap and a wide gusset.
- A4.1.10 Sling bag: single-strap pack worn on the back or chest.
- A4.1.11 Hobo bag: slouchy crescent-shaped bag on a single shoulder strap.
- A4.1.12 Bucket bag: bucket-shaped bag with a round or oval base, closed by a drawstring or open top.
- A4.1.13 Baguette bag: small, long, narrow bag carried close under the arm on a short strap.
- A4.1.14 Saddle bag: bag shaped like a rider's saddlebag, with a curved body and front flap.

## A4.2 Backpacks & body-worn packs

No leaf (leaf = null). Kind axis: use type (the designed function buyers compare); capacity and laptop size are attributes.

- A4.2.01 Daypack: general backpack sized for one day's personal items.
- A4.2.02 Laptop backpack: backpack built around a padded laptop compartment.
- A4.2.03 Hiking backpack: backpack with a load-bearing harness and hip belt for walking on trails.
- A4.2.04 Carry-on travel backpack: backpack sized to airline cabin limits that opens like a suitcase.
- A4.2.05 Belt bag (bum bag): small bag worn on a belt round the waist or across the chest.
- A4.2.06 Hydration pack: pack sold with its bag that carries a drink reservoir with a drinking tube. †
- A4.2.07 Chalk bag: small pouch worn at the waist to hold climbing chalk. †
- A4.2.08 Quiver: case worn on the body to carry arrows. †
- A4.2.09 School satchel (rigid school backpack): backpack with a rigid or reinforced box-shaped body and frame, a padded back system and reflective panels, made for a schoolchild to carry books and sold as its own category. †

## A4.3 Luggage & travel bags

No leaf (leaf = null). Kind axis: form of the travel container. `shell_construction` (hardside, softside) and `size_class` (with the airline-band standard named) are attributes of every kind here, never kinds.

- A4.3.01 Suitcase: rigid or semi-rigid case with a lid for carrying clothes on a journey, usually on wheels with a telescoping handle.
- A4.3.02 Travel trunk: large box-shaped case made to travel with its owner.
- A4.3.03 Wheeled duffel: duffel bag on a wheeled frame with a telescoping handle.
- A4.3.04 Duffel bag: soft, roughly cylindrical bag with a long top opening and carry handles.
- A4.3.05 Garment bag: bag that carries clothes on hangers, folded once or hung.
- A4.3.06 Dry bag: waterproof roll-top bag that keeps its contents dry. †

## A4.4 Work & document cases

No leaf (leaf = null). Kind axis: case format.

- A4.4.01 Briefcase: flat case with a handle for carrying papers and work items.
- A4.4.02 Attaché case: hard-sided, box-shaped briefcase with a hinged lid.
- A4.4.03 Pilot case: deep, box-shaped hard case with a top opening and a handle.

## A4.5 Wallets & small leather goods

No leaf (leaf = null). Kind axis: format. Leather grade and exotic skins are attributes.

- A4.5.01 Bifold wallet: wallet folded once, with card slots and a note section.
- A4.5.02 Trifold wallet: wallet folded twice into three panels.
- A4.5.03 Long (continental) wallet: long flat wallet that holds notes unfolded.
- A4.5.04 Zip-around wallet: wallet closed by a zip running round three sides.
- A4.5.05 Cardholder: slim holder with card slots and no note section.
- A4.5.06 Money clip: clip that holds folded notes, sometimes with card slots.
- A4.5.07 Coin purse: small pouch or purse for coins.
- A4.5.08 Key pouch: small pouch holding keys, usually on an inner ring or hooks.
- A4.5.09 Passport cover: cover or wallet made to hold a passport.
- A4.5.10 Wallet on chain: small bag-like wallet carried on a shoulder or cross-body chain (homonym with A4.5.11).
- A4.5.11 Chain wallet: wallet fixed by a chain to a belt loop.
- A4.5.12 Wristlet wallet: wallet with a wrist strap, card slots inside and no separate interior compartment (homonym with A4.1.03).

## A4.6 Eyewear, non-corrective

No leaf (leaf = null). Kind axis: frame shape, sharing its names with the H2 frames, which are filed by correction (A-17, K-34); `corrective: no`. A frame with plano lenses sold as fashion is the same kind. Rim structure, frame material, lens filter category and polarisation are attributes.

- A4.6.01 Aviator: teardrop-shaped frame with a double or triple bridge.
- A4.6.02 Wayfarer-style: trapezoidal frame, wider at the top, usually in thick plastic.
- A4.6.03 Browline: frame with a bold upper rim and a thin or rimless lower part.
- A4.6.04 Round: frame with circular lenses.
- A4.6.05 Cat-eye: frame that sweeps upward at the outer top corners.
- A4.6.06 Square: frame with square or rectangular lenses.
- A4.6.07 Geometric (hexagonal): frame with angular, many-sided lenses.

## A4.7 Umbrellas & hand fans

No leaf (leaf = null). Kind axis: mechanism or form; automatic opening is an attribute.

- A4.7.01 Compact (folding) umbrella: umbrella whose shaft and ribs collapse to a short length.
- A4.7.02 Stick umbrella: umbrella with a fixed full-length shaft, often with a crook handle.
- A4.7.03 Golf umbrella: large stick umbrella with a wide canopy.
- A4.7.04 Bubble umbrella: umbrella with a deep dome-shaped clear canopy.
- A4.7.05 Folding fan: hand fan of ribs and leaf that folds closed.
- A4.7.06 Rigid fan: hand fan with a fixed flat leaf on a handle.

## A4.8 Baby carriers

No leaf (leaf = null). Kind axis: wearing method.

- A4.8.01 Soft structured baby carrier: buckled carrier with a padded panel, shoulder straps and waistband.
- A4.8.02 Woven wrap: long length of woven, non-stretch fabric tied round the wearer to hold a baby.
- A4.8.03 Stretchy wrap: long length of stretch fabric tied round the wearer to hold a baby.
- A4.8.04 Ring sling: length of fabric threaded through two rings and worn over one shoulder.
- A4.8.05 Mei tai: panel carrier with four straps that are tied rather than buckled.
- A4.8.06 Hip seat: waist belt with a seat on which a child sits on the wearer's hip.

## A4.9 Small carried accessories

No leaf (leaf = null). Kind axis: object.

- A4.9.01 Keyring: ring or clip that holds keys and is carried on the person.
- A4.9.02 Fashion cane: cane carried as a dress accessory, not as a mobility aid. †
- A4.9.03 Shopping trolley: wheeled bag on a frame with a handle, pulled to carry shopping. †
- A4.9.04 Luggage scale: hand-held scale for weighing a packed bag. †
- A4.9.05 Holster: case worn on the body to carry a handgun. †

## A4.10 Parts and accessories made for one bag

No leaf (leaf = null). Kind axis: part type; the object served is the bag (S3; the closing sub-family, exempt from the level-1 axis, C5(d), CV-17).

- A4.10.01 Bag charm: decorative pendant made to hang on a bag, sold apart from keyrings. †
- A4.10.02 Bag strap: separate shoulder or crossbody strap made to attach to a bag. †
- A4.10.03 Bag organiser insert: insert with pockets and dividers placed inside a bag to arrange its contents. †

## Precedence inside A4

- Shoulder bag, crossbody bag, messenger bag and sling bag are told apart by strap and body, not by the seller's word: one strap on one shoulder is A4.1.07; a strap across the body with a soft body and no front flap is A4.1.08; a crossbody bag with a full front flap and a wide gusset is A4.1.09; a single-strap pack worn on the back or chest is A4.1.10. †
- A shopper is A4.1.04 tote bag (an alias). A sports bag or Boston bag is A4.3.04 duffel bag (aliases); a duffel on a wheeled frame with a telescoping handle is A4.3.03.
- A laptop bag is not a kind: it is filed by its form (A4.1.09 messenger bag, A4.4.01 briefcase or a backpack in A4.2), with `laptop_size` as an attribute; a backpack built around a laptop compartment is A4.2.02.
- A wristlet with card slots inside and no separate interior compartment is A4.5.12 wristlet wallet; otherwise it is A4.1.03 wristlet bag (one homonym entry).
- A wallet fixed to a belt-loop chain is A4.5.11 chain wallet; a small bag-like object carried on a shoulder or cross-body chain is A4.5.10 wallet on chain (one homonym entry).
- A named form that fits two kinds goes under the kind its maker uses on a fetched maker page; where the maker gives none, under the first retail category on a fetched maker page (A-18).
- A convertible bag is filed by the form its maker names first; the other forms are aliases. A kind that serves two uses keeps one home where its defining form is: the carry-on travel backpack is A4.2.04 and a generated view lists it under luggage (C14).
- Cabin, carry-on and check-in are `size_class` values of A4.3.01 suitcase, and hardside and softside are `shell_construction` values of every A4.3 kind; neither makes a kind. The travel trunk A4.3.02 is a kind because its form differs; a chest or trunk made to stand in a room as storage is B1.
- A soft backpack sold for school is A4.2.01 daypack with use and user tags; only a rigid or reinforced box-shaped school backpack sold as its own category is A4.2.09; a soft-bodied school bag in satchel form stays under A4.1.05 satchel. Items of a school set keep their own homes (pencil case C5).
- A diaper bag is a bag filed by its form (A4.1.04 tote bag, a backpack in A4.2) with `user: baby` as a tag.
- A belt bag, bum bag, waist pack or chest rig is a bag (A4.2.05 or the pack it is); a garment of vest form with pockets is A1.
- A bag charm (A4.10.01) hangs on a bag and is a part made for one bag; a keyring (A4.9.01) holds keys. A plush or figure pendant with a clip or chain is E1.
- Eyewear in A4.6 is filed by frame shape only when it does not correct vision; the same shape with correction is H2. A frame with plano lenses sold as fashion is the same A4.6 kind as the sunglasses of that shape.
- A hand fan is A4.7.05 or A4.7.06; a powered hand-held or neck fan is B7. An umbrella carried in the hand is A4.7; a standing beach or patio umbrella is B1. A golf umbrella is A4.7.03, not a golf bag.
- A fashion cane carried as a dress accessory is A4.9.02; a cane made to support a person with difficulty walking, and a white cane, are H2. †
- A hydration pack sold with its bag is A4.2.06; a loose drink bladder or bottle is B5. A bag made for one piece or pair of sport equipment follows it (E2); chalk bags (A4.2.07) and quivers (A4.2.08) stay in A4.
- Rare silhouettes are non-maker named forms under the nearest A4.1 kind, unless a fetched source compares one as a kind of its own (A-20).
