# D1 Computers & Components: decided tree

Category: D1 Computers & Components (domain D, Electronics). Membership test: a desktop, laptop or convertible computer, its components, peripherals (keyboards, mice, monitors, printers, scanners and multifunction printers, webcams, speech microphones, drawing tablets, 3D printers, barcode scanners; computer speakers are D3), storage, networking, uninterruptible power supplies, point-of-sale terminals, card readers and cash registers, hardware wallets and security keys, cables with standard connectors and adapters made to connect electronic devices, generic chargers (USB, GaN, Qi), loose electronic components and maker boards. A device whose screen and keyboard can separate is D2 if sold as a tablet with an optional keyboard, and D1 if the keyboard comes with it. Structure: sub-family (role in a computing setup), then leaf (the axis stated per sub-family; skipped in D1.5, D1.6, D1.7 and D1.9), then kind (the physical class buyers compare inside). Compatibility codes, quality tiers, makers and named forms are not kinds.

Ids (R-58): D1.n is a sub-family and D1.n.m a leaf; a kind is D1.n.m.kk, or D1.n.kk where a sub-family has no leaf.

## D1.1 Complete computers

Leaf axis: form of the machine (desktop, portable, kit). The historic classes carry an era tag.

### D1.1.1 Desktop computers

- D1.1.1.01 Tower desktop PC: complete desktop computer in a tower case, with the parts fitted.
- D1.1.1.02 Mini PC: complete desktop computer in a small box, sold as a ready machine.
- D1.1.1.03 All-in-one PC: complete computer with the screen and the computer in one body.
- D1.1.1.04 Keyboard computer: computer built into its keyboard (historic class).

### D1.1.2 Portable computers

- D1.1.2.01 Laptop: portable computer with its screen and keyboard in one folding body.
- D1.1.2.02 Pocket computer: programmable pocket computer (historic class; provisional until attested). †

### D1.1.3 Kits

- D1.1.3.01 Barebone kit: case, board and power supply sold as the body of a machine, to which the buyer adds the rest.
- D1.1.3.02 Trainer kit: kit sold to teach computing (historic class; provisional until attested). †

## D1.2 Internal components

Leaf axis: part function (the shop category). Compatibility codes (socket, chipset, DDR and PCIe generation, M.2 length, board, case and PSU form factor) are attributes, not kinds.

### D1.2.1 Processors

- D1.2.1.01 Processor: central processing chip (CPU) of a computer.

### D1.2.2 Motherboards

- D1.2.2.01 Motherboard [mainboard, Mainboard]: main circuit board of a computer, sold alone.
- D1.2.2.02 Board with CPU fitted: one object sold with the CPU soldered or fitted to the board.

### D1.2.3 Memory

- D1.2.3.01 DIMM memory module: full-size memory module for desktop computers.
- D1.2.3.02 SODIMM memory module: small-outline memory module for notebooks and small machines.

### D1.2.4 Graphics

- D1.2.4.01 Graphics card [video card; グラフィックボード; ビデオカード]: expansion card that renders images for a display or computes in place of a CPU, with or without display outputs.
- D1.2.4.02 External GPU enclosure: housing that holds a graphics card outside the computer and connects to it. †

### D1.2.5 Power

- D1.2.5.01 PC power supply unit: power supply fitted in a computer case to feed its parts.

### D1.2.6 Enclosures

- D1.2.6.01 PC case: enclosure that holds a computer's board, drives and cards.

### D1.2.7 Cooling

- D1.2.7.01 Air CPU cooler: heatsink with a fan that cools a processor by air.
- D1.2.7.02 All-in-one liquid cooler: sealed pump, radiator and block unit that cools a processor by liquid.
- D1.2.7.03 Custom-loop parts: separate parts (blocks, radiators, pumps, tubing) for building a liquid cooling loop.
- D1.2.7.04 Case fan: fan fitted to a computer case to move air.
- D1.2.7.05 Thermal paste: paste applied between a chip and its cooler.
- D1.2.7.06 M.2 heatsink: heat spreader fitted to an M.2 drive.
- D1.2.7.07 Fan controller: unit that sets the speed of case fans.

### D1.2.8 Expansion cards

- D1.2.8.01 Sound card: card fitted inside the computer that handles audio (internal only; an external or USB one is D3).
- D1.2.8.02 Network adapter card: card fitted inside the computer that adds a network connection.
- D1.2.8.03 Video capture card: card fitted inside the computer that records a video signal.
- D1.2.8.04 I/O expansion card: card fitted inside the computer that adds ports.

### D1.2.9 Internal cables

- D1.2.9.01 Internal PC cable: cable that connects parts inside a computer case, including the PCIe riser cable by default.

## D1.3 Storage and media

Leaf axis: how the storage is attached or carried (fixed drive, carried medium, housing or reader, disc drive, network storage).

### D1.3.1 Drives

- D1.3.1.01 Internal SSD: solid-state drive fitted inside a computer, of any interface or size (M.2 or 2.5-inch).
- D1.3.1.02 Internal HDD: spinning-disk drive fitted inside a computer.
- D1.3.1.03 External SSD: solid-state drive in its own case, connected from outside.
- D1.3.1.04 External HDD: spinning-disk drive in its own case, connected from outside.

### D1.3.2 Portable media

- D1.3.2.01 USB flash drive: small flash storage device that plugs into a USB port.
- D1.3.2.02 Memory card: flash card of a standard format (SD, microSD, CompactFlash, CFexpress); a proprietary console-only card is D5.

### D1.3.3 Enclosures and readers

- D1.3.3.01 Drive enclosure: housing that turns a bare drive into an external drive.
- D1.3.3.02 Memory-card reader: device that reads memory cards and connects to a computer. †

### D1.3.4 Optical and legacy

- D1.3.4.01 Optical drive: drive that reads or writes discs.
- D1.3.4.02 Floppy disk drive: drive that reads or writes floppy disks.

### D1.3.5 Network storage

- D1.3.5.01 NAS: network-attached storage device that holds drives and serves files over a network (a recorder sold only to run security cameras is B8).

## D1.4 Input, display and imaging

Leaf axis: device family (what the user handles).

### D1.4.1 Keyboards

- D1.4.1.01 Keyboard: key-based input device (size class is an attribute).
- D1.4.1.02 Numeric keypad: separate input device with number keys.
- D1.4.1.03 Keyboard kit: case, plate and circuit board sold to be built into a keyboard. †

### D1.4.2 Keyboard switches

- D1.4.2.01 Linear keyboard switch: loose keyboard switch with a smooth press and no bump or click.
- D1.4.2.02 Tactile keyboard switch: loose keyboard switch with a felt bump and no click.
- D1.4.2.03 Clicky keyboard switch: loose keyboard switch with a felt bump and an audible click.

### D1.4.3 Pointing

- D1.4.3.01 Mouse: hand-held pointing device moved over a surface.
- D1.4.3.02 Trackball: pointing device operated by a fixed ball.
- D1.4.3.03 Pen tablet: drawing tablet used with a pen, with or without a built-in screen (a pen display is a pen tablet with a screen).

### D1.4.4 Display

- D1.4.4.01 Monitor: display sold for use with a computer.

### D1.4.5 Printing and scanning

- D1.4.5.01 Printer: machine that prints from a computer. †
- D1.4.5.02 Scanner: machine that scans documents or images into a computer. †
- D1.4.5.03 Multifunction printer: machine that prints and scans (and copies) for use with a computer. †
- D1.4.5.04 3D printer: machine that builds objects by additive manufacturing. †
- D1.4.5.05 Barcode scanner: device that reads barcodes. †

### D1.4.6 Capture and switching

- D1.4.6.01 Webcam: camera made for use with a computer.
- D1.4.6.02 Video capture device: external device that records a video signal into a computer (connection is an attribute). †
- D1.4.6.03 Speech microphone: microphone made for speech, calls or streaming. †
- D1.4.6.04 KVM switch: switch that shares one keyboard, display and mouse between several computers.

## D1.5 Network and power

No leaf.

- D1.5.01 Wireless router: network device that routes traffic and provides wireless access.
- D1.5.02 Access point: network device that adds wireless access to a wired network.
- D1.5.03 Network switch: network device that connects wired devices on a local network.
- D1.5.04 UPS: uninterruptible power supply that keeps equipment running through a mains failure (a portable power station is B7).

## D1.6 Cables, adapters and hubs

No leaf.

- D1.6.01 USB cable: cable with USB connectors.
- D1.6.02 HDMI cable: cable with HDMI connectors.
- D1.6.03 LAN cable: network cable with standard patch connectors.
- D1.6.04 Audio cable: audio cable with standard connectors. †
- D1.6.05 Instrument cable: cable with standard connectors for connecting an instrument. †
- D1.6.06 Microphone cable: cable with standard connectors for connecting a microphone. †
- D1.6.07 MIDI cable: cable with MIDI connectors. †
- D1.6.08 Adapter or hub: device that converts one standard connector to another or splits one port into several.
- D1.6.09 Docking station: base that carries power delivery and several outputs for a laptop, which an adapter or hub does not. †
- D1.6.10 Generic charger: charger for many devices (USB, GaN or Qi); a charger made for one device follows it. †

## D1.7 Transaction and authentication hardware

No leaf.

- D1.7.01 Point-of-sale terminal: terminal at which a sale is rung up and paid. †
- D1.7.02 Payment card reader: device that reads payment cards. †
- D1.7.03 Cash register: till that records sales and holds cash. †
- D1.7.04 Hardware wallet: device that holds cryptocurrency keys. †
- D1.7.05 Security key: device used to prove identity when signing in. †

## D1.8 Embedded, maker and loose components

Leaf axis: component family as the distributor catalogue groups it (provisional until the Mouser harvest; loose components are carried to the Mouser class level).

### D1.8.1 Maker boards and kits

- D1.8.1.01 Single-board computer: board that runs an operating system (a board that does is this kind even with a microcontroller beside it).
- D1.8.1.02 Microcontroller board: board that runs one program on a microcontroller.
- D1.8.1.03 Electronics component kit: component kit without an experiment programme. †

### D1.8.2 Passive components

- D1.8.2.01 Capacitor: passive component that stores charge.
- D1.8.2.02 Resistor: passive component that resists current.
- D1.8.2.03 Inductor: passive component that stores energy in a magnetic field. †

### D1.8.3 Semiconductors

- D1.8.3.01 Microcontroller chip: loose integrated circuit that holds a processor, memory and ports.
- D1.8.3.02 MOSFET: loose transistor used as a switch or amplifier. †

### D1.8.4 Electromechanical and interconnect

- D1.8.4.01 Relay: loose switch operated by an electromagnet. †
- D1.8.4.02 Component connector: loose connector for circuit boards and wiring. †

### D1.8.5 Sensors, RF and identification

- D1.8.5.01 Sensor: loose component that senses a physical quantity.
- D1.8.5.02 Blank RFID tag or card: unissued RFID tag or card (an issued card is F5). †

## D1.9 Parts and accessories made for one computer or peripheral

No leaf. Leaf axis exempt (object served).

- D1.9.01 Ink cartridge: ink supply made for one printer type. †
- D1.9.02 Toner cartridge: toner supply made for one printer type. †
- D1.9.03 3D-printer filament: filament made for 3D printers. †
- D1.9.04 Laptop AC adapter: power adapter made for one laptop.
- D1.9.05 Laptop battery: battery made for one laptop.
- D1.9.06 Laptop sleeve or case: sleeve or case made to hold or protect one laptop.
- D1.9.07 Monitor arm or stand: arm, bracket or stand made to hold one screen. †
- D1.9.08 Laptop cooler: pad or stand with fans that cools a laptop.
- D1.9.09 Keycap set: set of keycaps for a keyboard. †
- D1.9.10 Keyboard plate: plate that holds switches in a keyboard. †

## Precedence inside D1

- Form factor is a kind boundary only where buyers never compare across it and one cannot replace the other in the same slot (desktop DIMM D1.2.3.01 against SODIMM D1.2.3.02); it is an attribute where buyers compare across it inside one kind (ATX and SFX supply in D1.2.5.01, ATX and Mini-ITX board in D1.2.2.01, case size classes in D1.2.6.01, keyboard size classes in D1.4.1.01).
- Compatibility codes (socket, chipset, DDR and PCIe generation, M.2 length) are attributes. An M.2 NVMe drive and a 2.5-inch SATA drive are both an internal SSD (D1.3.1.01); an internal SSD and an internal HDD (D1.3.1.02) are separate kinds, as are air (D1.2.7.01) and all-in-one liquid (D1.2.7.02) coolers.
- A board sold with its CPU soldered or fitted is a board with CPU fitted (D1.2.2.02). A CPU and a board sold together as a pair are a mixed set filed by the item of greatest value (a processor D1.2.1.01 or a motherboard D1.2.2.01) with a bundle variant.
- A compute accelerator card with no display output is a graphics card (D1.2.4.01) with zero display outputs.
- A barebone kit is filed in Kits (D1.1.3.01) because it is sold as the machine's body, not as a part.
- A NAS is storage (D1.3.5.01), not network equipment (D1.5); a general network drive is D1, and a recorder sold only to run security cameras is B8.
- A board that runs an operating system is a single-board computer (D1.8.1.01), even with a microcontroller beside the processor; a board that runs one program is a microcontroller board (D1.8.1.02).
- A pen display is a pen tablet (D1.4.3.03) with a screen; an electronic writing tablet is D2.
- Keyboard switches sold loose are filed by feel (linear D1.4.2.01, tactile D1.4.2.02, clicky D1.4.2.03); a keyboard with switches fitted is a keyboard (D1.4.1.01), and a case, plate and circuit board sold to be built into a keyboard are a keyboard kit (D1.4.1.03).
- A keycap set (D1.9.09), keyboard plate (D1.9.10) and keyboard stabiliser (D1.9.11) are parts made for keyboards and are filed in D1.9, not in D1.4.
- A sound card is internal only (D1.2.8.01); an external or USB sound card, audio interface or converter is D3.
- A microphone made for speech, calls or streaming is a speech microphone (D1.4.6.03); one made for music is E3, and one sold for both is filed by the maker's stated first use.
- A cable with standard connectors (USB D1.6.01, HDMI D1.6.02, LAN D1.6.03, audio D1.6.04, instrument D1.6.05, microphone D1.6.06, MIDI D1.6.07) is D1.6 whatever device it serves; patch cables are filed by their connector family. A cable with a proprietary connector made for one device follows that device. A cable that connects parts inside a computer case is an internal PC cable (D1.2.9.01), and the PCIe riser cable has this home until a fetched seller page confirms it.
- A generic charger (D1.6.10) serves many devices; a laptop's own AC adapter (D1.9.04) or battery (D1.9.05) is a part made for one computer (D1.9); a charger made for one phone or a power bank is D2, a charger for standard-size cells is C8, and a cordless-platform charger is C1.
- A bracket, arm or mount made to hold one screen follows the screen: a monitor arm or stand (D1.9.07). Cases, bags and covers are parts (D1.9.06) only when made to hold, carry or protect one object at a time; furniture, racks and shelves are never parts.
- Ink and toner cartridges (D1.9.01, D1.9.02) and 3D-printer filament (D1.9.03) made for one printer type are D1.9; paper is C5.
- Printers, scanners and multifunction printers (D1.4.5.01 to D1.4.5.03) are D1 whatever the connection of a given model; copiers, shredders, laminators, label makers and label printers are C5.
- A memory card of a standard format (D1.3.2.02) or a USB flash drive (D1.3.2.01) is D1 storage whatever device it is sold for; a proprietary console-only card is a D5 part.
- A blank RFID tag or card (D1.8.5.02) is D1; an issued payment, access or transit card is F5, but a hardware wallet (D1.7.04) or security key (D1.7.05) is D1.
- Component kits without an experiment programme are D1 (D1.8.1.03); experiment kits are C6 and play robots and coding toys E1. Electronics instruments, soldering irons, multimeters and bench power supplies are C1.
- A UPS is D1 (D1.5.04); home batteries, inverters, solar equipment and portable power stations are B7.
- A programmable pocket computer is D1 (D1.1.2.02); a PDA or electronic organiser is D2. A handheld gaming PC is D5 with a PC operating-system attribute. A computer whose keyboard comes with it is D1 (laptop D1.1.2.01); a tablet sold with an optional keyboard is D2.
