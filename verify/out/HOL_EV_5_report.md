## C5 Office & Stationery (9 rows)
Ladder: instrument or paper type (pens, pencils, ink, journals, diaries, stationery) > pen sub-type or user tier (starter, gold nib, flex) > brand as a separate menu or filter. Specialist shops add price bands. Quality: for pens, nib smoothness and feel, filling system, material and craft; for paper, gsm (90-100 sweet spot), coating, bleed-through, binding that lies flat. Signals: no certification body; only shop sales rank, hallmarks and community brand lists. Makers lead in community talk (Rhodia, Midori, Tomoe River, Lamy, TWSBI, Pilot); type leads in navigation. Surprise: paper is judged by measurable specs, pens by feel.

## C6 Optical & Scientific Instruments (9 rows)
Ladder: type (refractor, reflector, compound, stereo, roof or Porro prism) > maker > use or field of application > aperture, mount, price. Quality: aperture first for telescopes; objective lens quality for microscopes; optical performance then mechanics for binoculars. Signals: Stiftung Warentest (pollutants first, optics weighted heavily), Outdoor Life scorecard with USAF chart, DIN-compatible objectives, achromat/apochromat. Informal "alpha" brand tier (Swarovski, Zeiss, Leica) seen only in a search excerpt, not a fetched page. Types lead; makers matter at the top end. Surprise: a German consumer test fails binoculars on chemical pollutants before optics.

## C7 Farm & Forestry Equipment (7 rows)
Ladder: machine type (tractor, attachment, chainsaw) > size or power class > maker > power, hours, year. Chainsaws use maker-owned tiers (homeowner, farm and ranch, professional). Quality: PTO horsepower and fit to implements, dealer service and parts, reliability. Signals: Nebraska Tractor Test (OECD) data; German KWF mark (seen as mentioned in a trade test, KWF site not reached). Makers lead in community talk; use and size lead in guides. Surprise: dealer proximity ranks beside test data.

## C8 Building Materials & Hardware (6 rows)
Ladder: material or product type (timber, fixings, paint, tiles) > dimensions, grade or finish > brand as a filter. Guides lead with room and use. Quality: numeric specs (C16/C24 strength class, PEI 0-5, DCOF 0.42+, R10+ slip, water absorption, paint hiding class and wet-abrasion class, gloss). Signals: Stiftung Warentest paint test (40% paint properties, 30% application, 20% health, 10% labelling), Choice lab tests, FSC and PEFC as sourcing marks separate from grade. Types and specs lead, never makers. Surprise: certification is an attribute, not a way in.
