# S3C_E1P: puzzles in E1 (source census)

Table: verify/out/S3C_E1P.tsv (20 sources, code E1). Eight are Japanese-language (Karakuri Creation Group, the yosegi cooperative list, Hakone Maruyama Bussan, ja.wikipedia x2, Hanayama, Torito, JIGSAW CLUB). One French shop is included; a second French shop (cassetete-boutique.com) was fetched but left out of the table.

**E1.6 Puzzles, by sub-area**

- **Mechanical puzzles, kinds (burrs, interlocking, packing, disentanglement, sequential, twisty):** anchor is Rob's Puzzle Page classification table, which maps Slocum (10 classes), Hordern-Dalgety (14) and Hoffmann. Use the Slocum digital collection and the Puzzle Museum catalogue for named forms and old makers. Gap: no catalogue gives burr, packing or disentanglement forms as a tidy named list; Rob's pages are narrative.
- **Twisty puzzles:** anchor is the TwistyPuzzles.com Museum (about 13,000 items, shape and mechanism facets, manufacturer and inventor facets). Speedsolving Wiki gives 30 cube makers. Gap: Japanese and Korean twisty makers (Maru, Okamoto-type hand makers) are thinly covered.
- **Puzzle designers and craftsmen:** anchor is the Nob Yoshigahara Design Competition year pages (about 65 entries a year since 2001, each with designer, materials, classification). Puzzle Master Canada's brand facet adds retail brands and designers. Gaps: IPP exchange history is login-only (401); the baxterweb archive search returned nothing when fetched; PuzzleWorld's designer pages were not opened.
- **Puzzle boxes (himitsu-bako, karakuri boxes):** anchor is the Karakuri Creation Group site (12 craftsmen, listed by craftsman) together with the Odawara-Hakone yosegi cooperative member list (15 workshops). Hakone Maruyama Bussan shows the naming scheme (steps, sun size, pattern). Gaps: the Karakuri product list is script-loaded and was not read; no register of independent box makers outside the cooperative (Oka Craft appeared in search only and was not fetched).
- **Jigsaw and 3D jigsaw:** anchor is JIGSAW CLUB (Japan) for Japanese makers and 3D types (球体, クリスタル, キャスト, クムクム), with Wikipedia (ja) for the seven makers. Wikipedia (en) lists 20 Western makers (Ravensburger, Clementoni, Trefl, Piatnik and others). Gap: no maker-and-line catalogue for European jigsaws beyond retailer facets; antique makers appear only in the Puzzle Museum.
- **Cast and metal puzzles:** anchor is the Hanayama huzzle line-up (6 levels) with Torito's full series list. Gap: other metal-puzzle makers (Metal Puzzles UK, Eureka) have no list of their own.
- **Collector registries, exchanges, museums:** IPP (puzzleparty.org) is invitation-only. The Slocum collection (Lilly Library) and Puzzle Museum are the open records. The Strong National Museum of Play (already in S2_E1_sources.tsv) was not re-fetched.
- **Specialist shops:** Japan (Torito, JIGSAW CLUB, Hakone Maruyama), Europe (Mr Casse-Tête, FR), US/Canada (Puzzle Master, Cubicdissection), Australia (Mr Puzzle archive). Not covered: Germany (searched, nothing fetched), Korea and China puzzle shops.

**Notes for the harvest.** Board-game genres and mechanics are not used; only puzzle types by physical mechanism. Cubicdissection (cubicdissection.com, fetched), Puzzle Box World (US, fetched) and the Mechanical Puzzle Database at thepreservationnetwork.org (89 entries, 30 brands, fetched) were left out of the table to stay within 20 rows; they are usable as extras. The Japan Jigsaw Puzzle Association page (worldjigsawpuzzle.org) lists contest events only.
