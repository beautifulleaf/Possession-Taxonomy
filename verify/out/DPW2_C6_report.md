## Task 3: consumer scope

Laboratory-only instruments that a person can buy and keep are **in C6**:
- Scope is ownability (BRIEF; S2; key step 1).
- C6's test is designed function: step 23 names laboratory instruments, and its note puts the spectroscope in C6.
- Market (professional, institutional) is a modifier, never a divider (key modifiers list; K-62).
- The owner-only maker role (R-108, R-109) only governs makers: no regional maker harvests, and makers appear only through named forms and lines. "Suppliers sell to institutions" justifies that role; it is not a kind-scope test.
- A benchtop spectrometer or a used electron microscope is therefore a C6 kind, tagged `market: laboratory`.

The **observatory dome** is in scope but not in C6:
- Step 4 precedes step 23, so an observatory building is G2, filed whole.
- A dome sold alone is an envelope product, C8 by the G2 note.
- A cover made for one telescope is a C6 part (S3).
- A key sentence is proposed for the OWNER QUEUE.

**Existing C6 kinds that fail: none.** All can be privately owned. These are attested on airport or dealer pages but sold used to individuals: windsock, fume hood, clean bench, biosafety cabinet, ULT freezer, autoclave. A ducted hood is installed, and installation is a modifier (C-18, R-28).
