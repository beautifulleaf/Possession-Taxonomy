# HOL_DP_2 report (B1 B2 B3 B4 B5)

Stage-2 proposals from evidence batch HOL_EV_2 (6 to 13 fetched sources per category) and the memos. Outputs: `HOL_DP_2_proposals.tsv` (5 rows) and `HOL_DP_2_groups.tsv`. Only B3 has a tree in data/, so it is the only category with groups: 7 level-1 groups and 19 level-2 groups, which cover all 53 B3 kinds once each. B1, B2, B4 and B5 map their display groups to memo leaves in `level2_note`. No kind ids change. In all five categories the main way in is the type of object, and country is never used.

**B1 Furniture & Storage.** People choose the piece family first, then the type and size. Room is a second door, as a view. What makes the best: construction from the inside out (frame, then suspension, then foam density, then fabric Martindale class). For case furniture: solid wood, then joinery, then finish. For mattresses: construction type, then firmness. Maker role stays owner, with a "Design houses & designers" tab. Flags: check whether the futon mattress is the same kind as the floor mattress; outdoor-only forms such as the sun lounger have no kind; albums sit badly in furniture, so they get their own display group.

**B2 Soft Furnishings.** People choose by item type, using 8 display groups (bath mat and shower curtain are shown under Towels & bath). What makes the best: fibre and weave rather than thread count; fill power and tog; for rugs, material, knot density and weave tradition. Signals: OEKO-TEX, GOTS, GoodWeave, Martindale and pilling grades. Maker role stays owner, with a handmade-rug tab by weave and tradition. Flags: new kind candidate "chair pad"; the city/village/tribal tier needs a classification row in the scale registry. *Provisional:* the rug tab, because no community source was fetched.

**B3 Lighting.** This confirms R-104: people choose by placement and role. There are 7 groups; outdoor lights are gathered from two leaves for display only. Bulbs open on the cap (C13 rows still need to be built), then shape. What makes the best: CRI, lumens and colour temperature; for torches the FL1 figures, then build, tint and service. Maker role stays owner, with tabs for design houses and torch makers. No new kinds. *Provisional:* the bulb ladder, because the bulb-guide fetch failed.

**B4 Decor.** People choose by decor item type, using the ten memo sub-families as groups; seasonal decorations are shown first in season. What makes the best differs by group: candles by throw and clean burn; mirrors by glass and sealed backing; vases by material, finish and maker. Maker role stays owner, with a tab for makers and creators. Flags: the sundial is still in B4.7, but R-28 moved it to A5; clocks and wall art are sold in decor departments, but clocks are A5 and wall art has no home; the windsock line against C6 needs a sentence. *Provisional:* the quality signals, because the evidence is thin (one retailer's tier list and one set of reviews).

**B5 Kitchen & Dining.** People choose by product family as shops sell it. The 12 display groups are laid over the stage-of-work sub-families, which stay as the placement index. What makes the best: for cookware, heat evenness, release and coating durability (Consumer Reports); for knives, forged full tang, then steel hardness, then the maker's line; for appliances, the technology tier (conventional < micom < IH < pressure IH), then capacity. Maker role stays owner, with a makers & lines tab and a vintage cast-iron view. Flags: new kind candidates "saute pan" and "cookware set" (K-1); large appliances get their own display group. *Provisional:* US specialist navigation, because those pages were blocked.
