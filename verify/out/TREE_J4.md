# J4 Fruit & Vegetables: decided tree

Category: J4 Fruit & Vegetables (domain J, Groceries & Cellar). Membership test: fruit, vegetables, herbs, edible fungi and seaweeds, fresh or frozen, whole, cut or washed, but not dressed, sauced, fried or coated. Nuts, seeds, pulses, dried and preserved produce are J5. A kind is the species or commodity, the level at which grade standards and trade codes are written; cultivars and commercial types are named forms, and storage state (fresh, chilled-cut, frozen), form, size, grade, organic and origin are attributes, never kinds.

Ids (R-58): J4.n is a sub-family and J4.n.m a leaf; a kind is J4.n.m.kk, or J4.n.kk where a sub-family has no leaf.

Sub-family axis: class of produce as sold (fruit; vegetables; herbs; fungi and other edible organisms). A mix is a final kind "mixed <class>" at the end of the lowest level that holds all its parts: leaf = null (leaf 0) in a sub-family with leaves, and sub-family 0 for a mix across sub-families (J4.0.01).

## J4.1 Fruit

Leaf axis: crop group (botanical-culinary group as shops and standards use it).

### J4.1.1 Pome fruit

- J4.1.1.01 Apple: fresh or frozen apple, eaten or cooked, sold as the fruit.
- J4.1.1.02 Pear: fresh or frozen European-type pear.
- J4.1.1.03 Asian pear [nashi]: fresh round, crisp-fleshed Asian pear, a different fruit from the European pear.

### J4.1.2 Stone fruit

- J4.1.2.01 Peach: fresh or frozen peach.
- J4.1.2.02 Nectarine: fresh or frozen smooth-skinned nectarine.
- J4.1.2.03 Plum: fresh or frozen plum.
- J4.1.2.04 Cherry: fresh or frozen cherry, filed with stone fruit.

### J4.1.3 Citrus

- J4.1.3.01 Orange: fresh sweet orange.
- J4.1.3.02 Blood orange: red-fleshed sweet orange.
- J4.1.3.03 Clementine: small seedless, easy-peel mandarin type sold as clementine.
- J4.1.3.04 Satsuma [mikan]: loose-skinned, seedless mandarin type sold as satsuma or mikan.
- J4.1.3.05 Lemon: fresh lemon.
- J4.1.3.06 Grapefruit: fresh grapefruit.
- J4.1.3.07 Pomelo: fresh pomelo (pummelo), the large thick-skinned citrus.

### J4.1.4 Berries and small fruit

- J4.1.4.01 Strawberry: fresh or frozen strawberry.
- J4.1.4.02 Blueberry: fresh or frozen blueberry.
- J4.1.4.03 Mixed berries: final kind of the leaf: a mix of berries or small fruit in which no one kind is half or more of the weight, such as a forest-fruit mix.

### J4.1.5 Grapes and vine fruit

- J4.1.5.01 Grape: fresh table grape, sold to eat.
- J4.1.5.02 Kiwifruit: fresh kiwifruit.

### J4.1.6 Melons

- J4.1.6.01 Melon: fresh melon other than watermelon, sold whole or cut.
- J4.1.6.02 Watermelon: fresh watermelon, sold whole or cut.

### J4.1.7 Tropical and subtropical fruit

- J4.1.7.01 Banana: fresh eating banana.
- J4.1.7.02 Mango: fresh or frozen mango.
- J4.1.7.03 Pineapple: fresh pineapple, whole or cut.
- J4.1.7.04 Plantain: starchy cooking banana, green or ripe, filed with tropical fruit; the cooking use is an attribute. †

### J4.1.8 Other temperate and dryland fruit

### J4.1.0 Mixed fruit (leaf = null)

- J4.1.0.01 Mixed fruit: final kind of the sub-family: a mix of fruit from several crop groups in which no one kind is half or more of the weight, such as a smoothie mix.

## Precedence inside J4

- Fruit or vegetable is decided by sweetness as sold, not by botany: tomato (J4.2.5.01), sweet pepper (J4.2.5.02), chilli (J4.2.5.03), aubergine (J4.2.5.04), avocado (J4.2.5.05), cucumber (J4.2.5.06), courgette and squash (J4.2.5.07), sweetcorn (J4.2.5.08) and fresh olive (J4.2.5.09) are Vegetables > Fruiting vegetables. Sweet fruit as sold is Fruit; cherry (J4.1.2.04) is Stone fruit and plantain (J4.1.7.04) is Tropical and subtropical fruit.
- Sweet pepper (J4.2.5.02) and chilli (J4.2.5.03) are two kinds; heat is a measured attribute, not a type. Citrus is filed one step finer than the group, by the heading a shop or standard gives (J4.1.3.01 to J4.1.3.07); mushrooms go by market type (J4.4.01 to J4.4.03).
- Herbs (J4.3) are leafy or soft-stemmed plants sold in bunches or packs to flavour food. Rocket is a leaf vegetable (J4.2.1.04); ginger is a root, tuber or rhizome (J4.2.3.04); bean sprouts and cut microgreens are Stems and shoots (J4.2.7.03); sweetcorn is a fruiting vegetable (J4.2.5.08); seaweed sold fresh or frozen is in Fungi and other edible organisms (J4.4.05).
- Storage state (fresh, chilled-cut, frozen), form (whole, cut, washed, prepacked), grade, size, organic, origin and ripeness are attributes: a frozen or cut produce is the same kind as the fresh one. Cultivars, commercial types and a designation that binds one kind are named forms of their kind, never kinds.
- A mix of kinds is filed under the kind that makes up half or more of its weight (a berry bag that is over half blueberry is blueberry, J4.1.4.02). Otherwise it is the final kind "mixed <class>" at the end of the lowest level that holds all its parts: a mix inside one leaf ends that leaf (mixed berries, J4.1.4.03; mixed salad leaves, J4.2.1.05); a mix across the leaves of one sub-family ends the sub-family with leaf = null (mixed fruit, J4.1.0.01; mixed vegetables, J4.2.0.01); a mix of fruit and vegetables with no half-weight kind ends J4 (J4.0.01).
- A mix across categories (a tray with dip, a hamper) is filed by its item of greatest value and is OUT if that item is a prepared food; a basket kept afterwards is B1.
- Fresh olives are J4 (J4.2.5.09) and cured table olives are J5; every nut, chestnut and coconut, tender coconut included, is J5.
