# B10 Animal Equipment: decided tree

Category: B10 Animal Equipment (domain B). Membership test: made for keeping, feeding, housing, carrying, training or caring for animals, kept or wild: hutches, coops and kennels, tack, carriers, beds, feeders, nest boxes, aquariums, beekeeping equipment, pet toys, pet food and animal feed of every kind (including bird seed, hay and frozen feeder animals; live feeders are out), grooming and care equipment, veterinary medicines and treatments, animal identification microchips. Structure: sub-family (the animal group the object is made for, a written exception to Appendix A, K-102; then "Several animals" for objects made for two or more groups; then a closing parts sub-family), then leaf (the function the object serves for that animal, from one controlled vocabulary, used in this order: feed and treats; feeding equipment; housing and containment; habitat life-support; habitat furnishing; beds and furniture; carrying a rider or load; steering and restraint; worn by the animal; keeper's tools; produce handling; carrying and travel; care and hygiene; health and veterinary; toys and training; identification and tracking), then kind (the generic type of object a buyer can own and compare). A leaf name never joins two functions. A kind marked † is named by the key or a decision and awaits attestation at the harvest.

Ids (R-58): B10.n is a sub-family and B10.n.m a leaf; a kind is B10.n.m.kk, or B10.n.kk where a sub-family has no leaf.

## Precedence inside B10

- An object made for two or more animal groups is filed once in B10.11 Several animals; an object whose uses all lie in one animal group is filed in that group (a dog collar sold for puppies and adult dogs is B10.1.4.01); the number of groups is not a test beyond "more than one". Hay, sold for horses and small mammals alike, is B10.11.1.01.
- Parts follow the whole: hive bodies and frames, saddle girths and aquarium filter media are made for one B10 object and are in B10.12; aquarium filters, heaters and lights are compared by type and made for aquariums in general and stay equipment kinds in B10.5.3. The saddle pad stays in B10.4.1.03.
- A set of one kind is that kind; a mixed set is filed by its item of greatest value, with its contents listed; a kit is a kind only when sold, standardised and compared as one object, so an aquarium starter kit is filed as the aquarium (B10.5.2.01).
- Saddles are kinds by riding tradition (B10.4.1.01 English saddle, B10.4.1.02 Western saddle); the discipline is a named form.
- Feed for dogs and cats is a kind by form (B10.1.1.01 to B10.1.1.05); the life stage is an attribute. Feed for poultry and livestock is a kind by life stage (B10.10.1.01 starter, B10.10.1.02 grower, B10.10.1.03 layer).
- Hive systems (Langstroth, National, Dadant, Zander, AZ) are platform values, and box depth and frame count are size attributes; a hive is a kind by design (B10.9.2.01 to B10.9.2.04).
- Cat litter is B10.2.3.01; honey extraction and bottling gear is B10.9.4 with beekeeping equipment; dog-sport equipment and riding gear for the horse are B10 (B10.1.6.02, B10.4).
- A lamp, heater or thermostat made to be fitted to one aquarium or vivarium is B10 (B10.5.3, B10.6.3); the animal sub-family follows the animal the appliance is made for.
- An empty vivarium or terrarium sold as an enclosure is B10.6.2.01; a wild-bird feeder, bath or nest box is B10.8; a gate, pen or camera sold only for pets is B10.11.3 or B10.11.4.
