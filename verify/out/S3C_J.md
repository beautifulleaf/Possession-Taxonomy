# S3C_J source census, domain J (Groceries & Cellar)

Table: `verify/out/S3C_J.tsv` (99 rows). Every kept source was fetched at least once in this batch; counts are as the pages state, and some come from search excerpts of the same page (marked in the row notes). No category lacks a usable catalogue, but several have thin maker coverage (see gaps).

- **J1 Meat & Poultry.** Anchor: the INAO product search CSV (5,252 French GI/Label Rouge products) together with the Italian salumi DOP/IGP list (21 + 23) for named cured forms; for brands use the Japanese brand-beef lists (ja.wikipedia, Meats Town, brand-meat.com pork and beef indexes). Cuts: AHDB Meat Purchasing Guide (700+), USDA IMPS, Interbev posters. Breeds: Wikipedia cattle and pig breed lists. Gaps: no maker or brand catalogue for Western fresh meat, bacon, ham or sausage producers (Open Food Facts is the only route); no Korean, Spanish, German or Chinese brand lists beyond the Chinese GI list; poultry and game have no dedicated catalogue.
- **J2 Fish & Seafood.** Anchor: the EU commercial designations tool (market names per member state), then AllTinned (8,510 tins, 915 brands) for the tinned-fish maker ledger. Species depth: FishBase, FAO ASFIS (13,420 items), and the Japanese 市場魚貝類図鑑 (2,500+ species). Gaps: no catalogue of caviar or roe houses (only the CITES labelling standard); no catalogue of smoked-fish, frozen-fish or live-shellfish makers; fish-sauce and surimi makers are outside any list found.
- **J3 Dairy, Eggs & Alternatives.** Anchor: Cheese.com (2,046 cheeses, 74 countries) cross-checked with cheese-database.com (about 1,890, type taxonomy) and the eAmbrosia/INAO GI registers for protected cheeses and butters. Makers: Wikipedia cheesemakers and US dairy company lists only. Infant formula: Formula Atlas (111 formulas, 50 brands, affiliate-funded). Gaps: no catalogue for yoghurt, butter, milk or cream brands outside Open Food Facts; no plant-milk brand directory; eggs have standards (EU codes, USDA grades) but no brand list; no non-US dairy company list.
- **J4 Fruit & Vegetables.** Anchor: the EU Plant Variety Portal (vegetable and fruit registers, with breeders) for named cultivars, plus the Japanese MAFF variety register (query form) and Orange Pippin for tree fruit. Encyclopedias: Specialty Produce, UC Riverside citrus collection, Know the Pepper. Classes: UNECE FFV standards and IFPS PLU codes (1,500+). Gaps: UPOV PLUTO is login or paid; no catalogue of fresh-produce brands and growers (Zespri, Dole-type); seaweed and edible fungi have only the PLU mushroom list from S2; frozen-vegetable brands only via Open Food Facts.
- **J5 Pantry Staples.** Anchor: Open Food Facts for brands and lines across the category, with Wikipedia lists for named forms (pasta, noodles, sauces, hot sauces, cereals, brand-name food products) and the INAO CSV for GI oils, honey, mustard, salt, lentils. Specialist: Olive Oil Times Official Guide (10,000+ brands, 40 countries), Hotsauce.com brands (120+), Japan Grain Inspection Association rice ranking. Gaps: no catalogue of spice, salt, flour, sugar, honey, vinegar, tinned-goods or tofu/meat-alternative makers; no Japanese or Chinese sauce-brand lists (soy sauce brands need the JAS bodies from S2); rice and bean cultivars have no register comparable to J4; the Heat hot sauce database is only a map.
- **J6 Coffee, Tea & Cocoa.** Anchor: Steepster (91,152 teas, 8,892 companies) and RateTea for tea makers, Coffee Review (about 10,000 reviews) and Wikipedia coffee companies for roasters, World Coffee Research for coffee varieties, NARO plus Wikipedia List of Chinese teas for tea cultivars and named teas. Cocoa: Wikipedia bean-to-bar manufacturers and Open Food Facts only. Gaps: no cocoa or chocolate product catalogue (bars by maker); no complete roaster directory (the Coffee Roaster Database is tiny); Cup of Excellence results sit on a separate site not fetched; mate and kava have only Yerbatica and retailer pages; Indian estate lists exist only as Tea Board PDFs.
- **J7 Wine, Beer & Spirits.** Anchor: eAmbrosia (wine, spirit and beer GIs) with the INAO CSV for French appellations; for brands and bottlings use Whiskybase (2,903 distilleries, 295,511 whiskies), the TTB COLA Public Registry (all approved US labels), Open Brewery DB (11,959 breweries, bulk download), Untappd and sakedata.jp (7,497 sake brands). Agave Matchmaker gives NOM codes per tequila brand. Gaps: no open wine-producer catalogue (Vivino, Wine-Searcher and CellarTracker were not searched or fetched in this batch; their openness is untested); RateBeer closed on 1 February 2025; no open rum, gin, baijiu, soju, cognac or brandy house database beyond uneven Wikipedia lists; mead and cider have BJCP styles and the Wikipedia cider brand list only; TTB COLA has no bulk download.
