### What conformance changed

- C1: First level rebuilt as 12 sub-families by the work done (not hand/power/machine); `drive` and `mount` became attributes; Japanese kinds renamed by form; nailers, jacks, pumps and earth-movers housed (memo s.11, F1-F3, X2).
- C2: Cutting leaves re-split by the thing cut, not power; snow, spreader, roller, supports and sprayer given homes; tier moved off kinds; platforms moved to the compatibility ledger; parts sub-family C2.10 added (s.11, F7-F9).
- C3: Leaf axis restated as horticultural group with a written test per leaf; strawberry to fruit, lavender to shrubs; rose one kind with class attribute; spawn one kind; turf and moss housed (s.11, F12-F15).
- C4: Added C4.9 General craft tools & adhesives and C4.10 parts; spinning, weaving and printmaking placed by role; artist/student grade moved from kinds to tier entries on lines (s.11, F18-F19).
- C5: Eraser moved to a new correcting leaf; occasion-named leaf renamed by form; boards, fastening and correction kinds added; pen tier withdrawn as price band; parts sub-family C5.5 added (s.11, F23-F24).
- C6: Weather station made one kind with sensor attributes (home/professional dropped); magnifiers by lens holder, not use; parts sub-family C6.5 added; tier to entries; telescope platform claim held back (s.11, F28-F31).
- C7: Tractor kinds recast as designs for a task with size as attributes; `carrier` attribute merges self-propelled and mounted variants; shearing, slaughter, poultry and ground-shaping leaves and parts sub-family added (s.11, F34-F36).
- C8: Sub-family axis restated as "supply family"; unsourced finish and hardware kinds added with daggers; battery size-class rule; LPG cylinder merged; paint kinds by surface; fencing leaf added (s.11, F40-F42).
