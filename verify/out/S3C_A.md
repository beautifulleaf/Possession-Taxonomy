# S3C_A source census, domain A (Worn & Carried)

Table: verify/out/S3C_A.tsv (71 rows: A1 23, A2 15, A3 10, A4 12, A5 11). Every row's page was fetched in this batch (Wikipedia, ZOZOTOWN, Chrono24, WatchBase, GOAT, Heritage Auctions, ABC-MART and others, some through the Parallel fetch tool because plain fetch returned 403). Counts are as each page states them, or marked as my tally.

- **A1 Clothing.** Anchor: ZOZOTOWN brand directory (11,633 brands with item counts), then Farfetch and Zalando A-Z lists for premium tiers; Wikipedia clothing-brand category tree for founded, country and parent. Scene anchors: Football Kit Archive (5,494 kit brands), Lolibrary, Heddels with DenimHunters (Japanese denim), SHARP and Virginia Tech (helmets), Myntra (Indian garment forms). Gaps no catalogue covers: workwear, uniform and school-uniform makers, techwear and streetwear labels (Grailed designer page is script-rendered; Musinsa would not load), kimono, hanbok, ao dai and African dress makers, costume and cosplay makers, PPE other than helmets (respirators, hi-vis, ear defenders), baby clothing, and named forms per maker beyond Heddels.
- **A2 Footwear.** Anchor: Wikipedia Shoe brands plus Shoe companies by country (US 176, UK 62, Italy 57) for makers; ABC-MART for the Japanese type tree and model series; Assocalzaturifici (about 500 Italian makers) for contract and craft makers. Named forms and variants: Sneaker History (321 models), GOAT, Kicks and Grips, Wikipedia shoe styles and boots lists, Red Wing style numbers. Gaps: Northampton and other craft-maker directories, Asian, Latin American and African makers, traditional footwear (geta, clogs, sandals by region), orthopaedic and barefoot makers; Zappos and Sneaker Database lists did not render.
- **A3 Jewellery.** Anchor: Wikipedia List of jewellery designers (about 300) with Fashion Press Japan (about 310 brands) and the Heritage Auctions maker's mark database for historic and costume makers (count unconfirmed). Kinds: Wikipedia List of jewellery types; lines: Cartier collections, Chow Tai Fook series; tiers and creators: 1stDibs. Gaps: India and Middle East gold houses (Tanishq blocked), Chinese long tail, costume-jewellery makers beyond marks, independent makers (Etsy not fetched), body-jewellery makers beyond 20 verified, assay-office sponsor registers (not fetched), amulet and religious worn items.
- **A4 Bags & Accessories.** Anchor: Wikipedia luggage brands and manufacturers (54 and 51) plus Bags (fashion) (95, mixes named models and types); Sabae lists (Megane Museum 80 makers, Japan Glasses Factory a few hundred) for eyewear; Toyooka Kaban for bag makers; A to Z Dex (309 handbag brands) as a seed to be re-attested. Gaps: handbag models and lines (no catalogue beyond Wikipedia and brand sites), wallets and small leather goods makers, keyrings, fashion canes, baby carriers and slings (nothing found), umbrellas beyond 15 heritage makers, Italian, German and Chinese bag districts, sunglasses makers outside Japan.
- **A5 Clocks & Watches.** Anchor: WatchBase (42,292 watches, 3,561 calibers, reference level) for lines and variants; Chrono24 brand A-Z for breadth including obscure brands; Wikipedia List of Swiss watch manufacturers (about 170, with founder and owner) and Category:Watch brands (160) for the maker ledger; AHCI (63 names across statuses) for independents. Kinds and clocks: NAWCC Index, Collectors Weekly, Selling Antiques. Gaps: clock makers (only NAWCC and Collectors Weekly, American-heavy), Japanese, Chinese and Russian mass brands (Kakaku gives about 39 men's brands only), microbrand directory (only Wikipedia concept page), smart-adjacent hybrids, hourglass, sundial and timer makers, Fondation Haute Horlogerie brand page did not render.

No category lacks a usable catalogue. Weakest on makers' named forms: A3 and A4 (Wikipedia and brand sites only).
