# C4 Craft & Art Supplies: decided tree

Category: C4 Craft & Art Supplies (domain C, Making, Growing & Work). Membership test (CATEGORIES_v0_7, placement key step 28, with K-169 to K-172, K-191 and R-31): made for picture-making, textile, fibre, paper, bead, clay and model crafts, candle and soap making, resin, glass and mosaic, basketry, floristry and wood crafts: sewing and embroidery machines, knitting and needlework tools, yarn, fabric, leather and tanned hides as material, notions sold loose (never parts under S3), paints, inks, pastels, charcoal, coloured pencils, crayons and markers made for making pictures, brushes, canvases, easels and sketchbooks, model-making paints, glues and tools, beading and jewellery-making tools (jeweller's saws, jewellery torches and soldering kits), leatherworking tools, loose and drilled beads, craft feathers, fleece and shell assortments, scrapbooking and paper-craft supplies (decorative stamps, washi tape, origami paper), pottery tools, wheels, slab rollers, pugmills and kilns, cutting plotters and heat presses, pyrography pens and craft wood blanks. Not here: pens, pencils and markers made first for writing, lettering or calligraphy, or for both writing and drawing, office stamps, desk glue and household scissors (C5); tools for shaping wood, metal and stone or for electronics work, general soldering equipment, carving and whittling knives and gouges, knife-making kilns and workshop compressors (C1); paint for walls, woodwork and furniture, building adhesives and building timber (C8); drawing tablets and 3D printers (D1); scale models and model kits, built or not, and play kits (E1); finished pictures, sculptures, hangings and mosaics (F2); paper patterns and colouring books (F1), PDF patterns (I2); strung jewellery (A3); undrilled pearls, unset gems and identified single shells (F6); finished candles and dried flowers (B4); craft storage, the craft table and art-studio furniture (B1); makeup brushes (H1). Structure (Candidate A, decided; memo sections 3, 4b, 10 and 14): sub-family by the craft the object is for (C4.1 to C4.14), then the multi-use sub-family C4.15 for objects made or sold for several crafts (C14(c); leaf axis: operation) and the closing parts sub-family C4.16 (CV-17, C5(d); leaf axis: object served); then leaf by role in the craft (material, support, applicator or tool, machine, fitting), null in C4.6, C4.8 and C4.9 to C4.14 (C5(b), CV-16); then kind by the axis each leaf states (medium, stitch job, cloth name, product form, tannage, body class, chemistry, material or operation). Each object gets one kind. Grade (artist or student, a tier entry on the maker's range), lightfastness class, pigment code, format and size, yarn weight (CYC), ply, fibre, fabric GSM and width, leather grain grade and weight, clay cone, kiln `fuel`, `loading` and `chamber_size`, machine `control`, `duty` and `market`, `drive`, `mount`, brush hair and shape, wood species, `kit_contents`, skill level, user (kids is a user tag), era, scene and price band are attributes, tags, tier entries or views, never kinds. A maker's range inside one kind is a maker-bound named form and a range spanning kinds is a lines row; colour and part codes are variants. Maker role (maker_roles.tsv, provisional): owner for the category (craft supplies are chosen by medium, makers through named products); way_in for yarn (scope C4.3 yarn and fibre kinds: knitters choose by yarn company, Ravelry evidence, R-116). Pending kinds: the memo marks 41 kinds † (no fetched page behind them; section 10 attestation list) and section 14 adds three kit kinds, provisional until a harvested seller page shows the kit type sold as a class, also marked †; their ids are allocated here in memo order, each line ending in †, and a † kind enters the data only when a fetched page attests it. Sub-families C4.10, C4.12 and C4.13 and leaf C4.16.2 hold only † kinds and are provisional with them; C4.14 Wood & pyrography is provisional until attested (R-31); the brush choice level (C4.1.3.01) is provisional until a brush page with shape and hair facets is fetched (C-16). Display groups (navigation proposal, display only, never moving a home; no group ids issued): Art & painting (C4.1); Sewing & quilting (C4.2); Knitting, crochet & needlecraft (C4.3); Leathercraft (C4.4); Jewellery & beading (C4.5); Papercraft & card making (C4.6); Pottery & clay (C4.7); Model-making (C4.8); Candle, soap & resin (C4.9, C4.10); Glass & mosaic (C4.11); Basketry, floristry & woodcraft (C4.12, C4.13, C4.14); Craft tools, cutters & glue (C4.15); Machine parts & accessories (C4.16). Quality markers (display, judged on the named form or range, never on the kind): artist colours by lightfastness (ASTM I to V, I and II as artist quality), pigment load and tint strength, single-pigment colours (Colour Index code), artist against student range, and opacity, staining and granulation by need; yarn by fibre content, weight matched to the pattern, colour and texture, and care; sewing machines by build, motor and stability, stitch quality and tension, features for the use and skill, and brand reliability and servicing; clay by firing range (cone); leather by tannage, grain grade and weight; beads by shape consistency of the maker's line.

Ids (R-58): C4.n is a sub-family and C4.n.m a leaf; a kind is C4.n.m.kk, or C4.n.kk where a sub-family has no leaf.

## C4.1 Picture-making
Leaf axis: role in the craft (material, support, applicator or studio equipment). Printmaking is placed by role inside these leaves; the section-14 kit kinds stand at leaf 0. Display group: Art & painting.

### C4.1.1 Colours & drawing media
Kind axis: medium. Grade sits on the maker's range (tier entry), never on the kind; single colours are attributes or variants.
- C4.1.1.01 Watercolour paint: transparent water-soluble paint in tubes or pans made for painting pictures.
- C4.1.1.02 Acrylic paint: water-based paint with an acrylic polymer binder made for painting pictures.
- C4.1.1.03 Oil paint: artists' paint with pigment bound in drying oil. †
- C4.1.1.04 Gouache: opaque water-based paint made for painting pictures. †
- C4.1.1.05 Art ink: liquid ink made for drawing and painting pictures.
- C4.1.1.06 Printing ink: ink made for relief and other hand printmaking.
- C4.1.1.07 Paint medium (acrylic medium, gesso, primer): medium, ground or primer mixed with or laid under artists' paint.
- C4.1.1.08 Coloured pencil: pencil with a coloured core made for drawing.
- C4.1.1.09 Pastel: stick of pigment and binder (soft, oil or hard pastel) made for drawing and painting.
- C4.1.1.10 Drawing pen and marker: pen, fineliner or marker whose maker's first-named use is drawing, comics or painting (K-170).

Kind axis: form of support.

- C4.1.2.02 Watercolour paper pad: pad or block of paper made for watercolour painting.

### C4.1.3 Applicators & studio equipment
Kind axis: operation.
- C4.1.3.01 Artist brush: brush made for painting pictures; hair and shape are attributes (brush choice level provisional, C-16).
- C4.1.3.02 Easel: stand that holds a canvas or board while it is painted (K-172). †
- C4.1.3.03 Relief-cutting tool (lino and wood): hand cutter made for carving lino or wood blocks for printing. †
- C4.1.3.04 Brayer: hand roller made for rolling ink onto a printing block. †

### C4.1.0 Kits (leaf = null, section 14)

## C4.2 Sewing & fabric

- C4.2.1.01 Sewing machine (lockstitch): machine that sews seams with a lockstitch.

- C4.2.2.04 Felt: non-woven matted cloth sold as craft or sewing material.

### C4.2.4 Thread & notions

## Precedence inside C4

- Made for one craft or several (C14(c), K-171): a tool or material made for one craft goes to that craft's sub-family; one made or sold for several crafts goes to C4.15. A cutting tool, adhesive, stamp or ink pad is filed by the material or work it is made for: dressmaking shears C4.2.5.02, craft scissors C4.15.1.01, general household scissors C5; decorative stamps and their ink pads C4.6.04 and C4.6.05, office stamps and pads C5; washi tape C4.6.06 and origami paper C4.6.07; glue made for paper craft, fabric, leather, beads, clay or model-making is C4 (craft glue C4.15.3.01), desk glue sticks and tape are C5 and wood and construction adhesives C8.
- Technique rule (sewing against needlecraft): constructing with fabric and thread, machine embroidery included, is C4.2 (embroidery machine C4.2.1.03); hand stitching and working with yarn or floss (knitting, crochet, embroidery, cross stitch, needlepoint) is C4.3 with its cloth (C4.3.1.05) and hoops (C4.3.2.04).
- Thread: sewing thread is C4.2.4.01 and embroidery floss C4.3.1.03 by technique and use, with a generated "all thread" view.
- Spinning, weaving and printmaking are placed by role: wheels, spindles and looms in C4.3 (C4.3.3.02, C4.3.2.05, C4.3.3.03), printing ink with the inks (C4.1.1.06) and the relief-cutting tool, brayer and press with the applicators and studio equipment (C4.1.3.03 to C4.1.3.05); generated views list "printmaking" and "spinning and weaving".
- Writing against drawing (K-170): a pen, marker or ink is filed by the maker's first-named use: drawing, comics or painting C4 (drawing pen and marker C4.1.1.10, art ink C4.1.1.05); handwriting, lettering, calligraphy and shodo, or both named, C5. A sketchbook is C4.1.2.03 and a blank notebook C5.
- Cutting plotters and heat presses (K-172): a Cricut-type cutting machine is C4.15.1.05 and a heat press C4.15.2.01, not D1; a drawing tablet is D1.
- Kilns: a kiln made for firing clay is C4.7.2.05; a kiln made only for fusing glass is C4.11.04; a furnace for heat-treating metal (knife-making kiln) is C1. Pottery wheels, slab rollers and pugmills are C4.7.2.01 to C4.7.2.03 (K-172).
- Polymer and air-dry clay are clay body classes in C4.7 (C4.7.1.06, C4.7.1.05), not model-making materials.
- Airbrush and its compressor: the airbrush is made for picture-making and model-making, so it is C4.15.4.01; a compressor made to drive an airbrush follows the airbrush (C4.16.2.01, S3); a general workshop compressor is C1. Sewing-machine needles, feet and bobbins are parts of a C4 machine (C4.16.1).
- Notions sold loose (zips C4.2.4.02, buttons C4.2.4.03) are never parts under S3; they stay C4.
- Beads, stones and shells (K-77, K-78): a pearl or gem bead drilled for stringing is C4 (C4.5.1.04, C4.5.1.03), strung A3, undrilled F6; a gem simulant is C4.5.1.05 or A3 by form; an unsorted shell assortment sold for crafts is C4.5.1.06, an identified single shell F6.
- Jewellery soldering (C4-O2, K-171): soldering, brazing and annealing torches and kits made for jewellery work are C4.5.3.01; general soldering irons, stations and equipment are C1.
- Paint (K-172): paint made for pictures, models and crafts is C4 (C4.1.1, C4.8.01 to C4.8.03, fabric paint C4.2.3.02, craft paint C4.15.5.01); paint for walls, woodwork and furniture, chalk and furniture paint included, is C8. Textile dye is C4.2.3.01 (B6-O1); textile care and waterproofing sprays are B6.
- Brushes: a brush made for painting a picture or applying glaze is C4.1.3.01; a makeup brush H1, a cleaning brush B6, a wire or decorating brush C1, a calligraphy brush C5.
- Easel (K-172, K-190): the easel is C4.1.3.02; art-studio furniture, craft storage and the craft table are B1.
- Scrapbooks (K-9): a blank scrapbook album is C4.6.02; an empty multi-object album B1; a filled scrapbook F5.
- Craft kits (K-1, section 14): a set of one kind is that kind; a kit sold, standardised and compared as one object is a kind (paint-by-numbers kit C4.1.0.01, diamond-painting kit C4.1.0.02, needlecraft kit C4.3.0.01); every other mixed kit (jewellery, candle, soap, mosaic) is filed by its item of greatest value with `kit_contents` listed; a kit made for play that cannot do the real job is E1 (C15). A paper sewing or knitting pattern is F1 and a PDF pattern I2.
- Wood crafts (R-31): a pyrography pen, craft wood blank or shape, or turning blank sold for crafts is C4.14 (C4.14.03, C4.14.01, C4.14.02); carving, whittling and chip-carving knives and gouges are C1; timber sold as building or joinery stock is C8.
