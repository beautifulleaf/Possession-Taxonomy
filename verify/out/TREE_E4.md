# E4 Weapons & Armour: decided tree

Category: E4 Weapons & Armour (domain E, Play, Sport & Music). Membership test (placement key step 10, with K-83 to K-88, K-163, K-173 and R-16(a)): a weapon made for fighting, self-defence or hunting, by striking, cutting, shooting, firing, spraying or electric shock (firearms, air guns, bows and crossbows, swords, fighting and hunting knives including butterfly and automatic knives, martial-arts weapons, pepper spray, stun guns and projectile electroshock weapons), including firearms, air guns, bows and crossbows made for target shooting; armour made to protect against such weapons; ammunition, with bullets, primers and powder sold for loading; and the parts and fittings made for one of these objects (S3: magazines, bowstrings, scabbards, loose sword fittings, loose armour pieces, rifle scopes and weapon-mounted lights, a case made to carry one firearm or bow). Not here: blunted or padded implements for a combat sport and their protective gear, airsoft and paintball markers, archery targets, arm guards and finger tabs, boomerangs, distress flare pistols and flares, spearguns and fishing tackle, decoys, blinds, game calls and clay throwers (E2); toy and foam replicas (E1); decorative replicas that cannot injure and deactivated ordnance (B4); EDC and other locking folders, pocket knives, multi-tools, axes for wood, reloading presses and dies (C1); machetes and billhooks (C2); kitchen knives (B5); holsters, belt pouches and quivers (A4); ear defenders, shooting glasses and hazard helmets (A1); safes, pest-killing tools and traps and personal attack alarms (B8); racks and cabinets (B1); binoculars and spotting optics (C6); hand-held flashlights (B3); cleaning products (B6); livestock stunners (C7); bombs, grenades, mines, missiles and artillery (OUT). Structure (Candidate A, decided, E-5; memo sections 3, 4b, 10, 12 and 14): sub-family by how the object acts on a target or protects against it (propellant, compressed gas, stored muscle energy, blade, shaft or impact, non-lethal agent, protection, projectile; E4.1 to E4.9), then the parts sub-family E4.10 (C5(d), exempt from that axis; leaf axis: weapon class served), then leaf by the axis each sub-family states (E4.5, E4.7 and E4.9 have no leaf, `leaf = null`, C5(b)), then kind (action, powerplant, blade style or tradition-named type, at the level where buyers compare quality or makers), then named form (model, line, platform, lettered typology, standard cartridge or iconic design, uncapped, `maker_bound` flagged; not listed here). Legal class (C17 ledger), use, discipline and scene (C8 tags), region and era, school (standing school table), protection level (with the standard and edition named), `function_class` (battle-ready, working replica), user, material, calibre or gauge, capacity, barrel length, `ignition`, `piston_type`, `deployment` (fixed, folding, automatic), hilt length, `blade_shape`, `gut_hook`, `steel` and `hardness_hrc` (section 14 E4-4 to E4-6), condition, brand, tier and price band are attributes, tags, ledger rows or views, never kinds or leaves; standard cartridges are non-maker named forms and C13 rows, and a maker's load is a variant (E-7, E-6); certificate grades of individual blades are never stored (C16). Maker role (maker_roles.tsv, provisional; section 14): way_in for E4.4 Swords, E4.5 Knives and daggers, leaf E4.6.1 Polearms, the historical armour kinds plate helmet E4.8.1.01, gauntlet, arm and leg armour E4.8.3.01 to E4.8.3.03, plate armour harness E4.8.4.01 and shield E4.8.5.01, and leaf E4.10.3 Fittings for blades (tsuba makers via the school table); owner for E4.1, E4.2, E4.3, E4.7, E4.9, the modern ballistic armour (E4.8.1.02, E4.8.2.01 to E4.8.2.04, chosen by model and NIJ level) and the other E4.10 leaves. Pending kinds: the memo marks 30 kinds † (no fetched page behind them; section 10 attestation list); their ids are allocated here in memo order, each line ending in †, and a † kind enters the data only when a fetched page attests it. Leaf E4.8.3 holds only † kinds and is provisional with them. Provisional levels: the kind level of the regional-word kinds katana, tachi, wakizashi, tantō, naginata, karambit, tonfa, nunchaku, kubotan, bō staff and grip ornament (menuki) is provisional until fetched sellers in two regions give each its own category (R-19, CV-27); ballistic helmet E4.8.1.02 is provisional until attested (E-23); the PCP and CO2 air-gun kinds (E4.2.1.01, E4.2.1.03, E4.2.2.01, E4.2.2.02) are provisional until a fetched source ranks the powerplants separately (C10(b)); CO2 launcher E4.7.04 may merge with pepper-ball launcher E4.7.03 when attested. E4.9 stands at 12 kinds, the limit for `leaf = null`; growth at the harvest adds a leaf by role. Display groups (navigation proposal, display only, never moving a home; no group ids issued): Firearms (E4.1); Air & gas guns (E4.2); Bows, crossbows & arrows (E4.3); Swords (E4.4); Knives & daggers (E4.5, shown by blade style); Polearms, axes & impact weapons (E4.6); Self-defence devices (E4.7); Body armour, historical armour & shields (E4.8); Ammunition & reloading (E4.9); Parts, optics & fittings (E4.10). Cross-links without moving homes: arrow E4.9.08, crossbow bolt E4.9.09, bowstring E4.10.2.01 and arrow rest E4.10.2.02 are also listed in the Bows group, and air-gun pellet E4.9.07 in the Air guns group; Swords and Armour carry an antique / modern-made toggle from era tags, with `nihonto_period` as a filter beneath it; a use view (hunting, target and competition, self-defence, HEMA and re-enactment, collecting, martial arts) reads C8 use tags. Quality markers (display, judged on the named form, line or maker, never on the kind): knives by steel (edge retention, toughness, hardness HRC, corrosion resistance, through the steel reference table) and the maker's heat treatment above the steel name, tang (full, then half, then rat-tail), blade shape and length for the job, handle material, balance and fit in hand; bows by intended use, then fit and draw length, then a draw weight the archer can hold, handedness and budget; hunting rifles by shooter build and recoil, quarry size, action, weight and balance, calibre and stock fit, with maker accuracy guarantees as claims only; body armour by the threat level faced (the NIJ level is a floor, shown first, both editions' labels held), then mobility and wear time, materials and construction, plate curvature, weight and fit; Japanese swords by a verifiable licensed smith, clear attribution, genuine hamon, polish and tang, with NBTHK and NBSK papers as grading-scale registry rows only; HEMA swords by maker reputation, customisation, lead time and repair. Quality orders are provisional (US-heavy, vendor-written evidence, section 14 E4-9).

Ids (R-58): E4.n is a sub-family and E4.n.m a leaf; a kind is E4.n.m.kk, or E4.n.kk where a sub-family has no leaf.

## E4.1 Firearms
Leaf axis: form class of the whole firearm (pistol, revolver, rifle, shotgun, combination gun, blank-firing pistol). Kinds by action within the form, from the simplest action to self-loading; `ignition` (flintlock, percussion, cartridge), calibre, capacity and barrel length are attributes. Display group: Firearms.

### E4.1.1 Pistols
- E4.1.1.01 Semi-automatic pistol: handgun with the chamber in the barrel that reloads itself from a magazine after each shot.

### E4.1.2 Revolvers
- E4.1.2.01 Double-action revolver: handgun with a revolving cylinder whose trigger pull both cocks the hammer and fires.
- E4.1.2.02 Single-action revolver: handgun with a revolving cylinder whose hammer is cocked by hand before each shot; a percussion revolver is this kind with `ignition` = percussion.

### E4.1.3 Rifles
- E4.1.3.01 Bolt-action rifle: rifled long gun loaded and cycled by a hand-operated rotating or straight-pull bolt.
- E4.1.3.02 Lever-action rifle: rifled long gun cycled by a lever below the receiver.
- E4.1.3.03 Semi-automatic rifle: rifled long gun that reloads itself from a magazine after each shot.
- E4.1.3.04 Single-shot rifle: rifled long gun that holds one cartridge and is reloaded by hand after each shot.
- E4.1.3.05 Muzzle-loading rifle: rifled long gun loaded with powder and projectile from the muzzle (E-21).

### E4.1.4 Shotguns
- E4.1.4.01 Over-under shotgun [Bockdoppelflinte]: smoothbore long gun with two barrels stacked one above the other.
- E4.1.4.02 Side-by-side shotgun: smoothbore long gun with two barrels placed beside each other.
- E4.1.4.03 Pump-action shotgun: smoothbore long gun cycled by sliding the fore-end back and forth.
- E4.1.4.04 Semi-automatic shotgun [Selbstladeflinte]: smoothbore long gun that reloads itself after each shot.
- E4.1.4.05 Muzzle-loading shotgun: smoothbore long gun loaded with powder and shot from the muzzle (E-21). †

### E4.1.5 Combination guns
- E4.1.5.01 Drilling or rifle-shotgun combination gun [Kombinierte Waffen]: long gun with at least one rifled and one smoothbore barrel in one action.

### E4.1.6 Blank-firing pistols
- E4.1.6.01 Blank-firing pistol [Schreckschusswaffe]: pistol that fires only blank, irritant or signal cartridges, made for self-defence or deterrence.

## E4.2 Air and gas guns
Leaf axis: form class (rifle, pistol). Powerplant is a kind only where it changes the body or needs new fittings (C10): a reservoir and filling equipment for PCP, a capsule housing for CO2; spring and gas piston are one kind with `piston_type` an attribute. Display group: Air & gas guns.

### E4.2.1 Air rifles
- E4.2.1.01 PCP air rifle: air rifle fired from an on-board reservoir pre-charged with compressed air.
- E4.2.1.02 Piston air rifle: air rifle that compresses its charge with a spring or gas piston at each cocking stroke; `piston_type` is an attribute.
- E4.2.1.03 CO2 air rifle: air rifle powered by carbon dioxide from a capsule or cylinder.

### E4.2.2 Air pistols
- E4.2.2.01 CO2 air pistol: air pistol powered by carbon dioxide from a capsule.
- E4.2.2.02 PCP air pistol: air pistol fired from an on-board reservoir pre-charged with compressed air.

## E4.3 Bows and crossbows
Leaf axis: bow (hand-drawn), crossbow (stock-mounted) or other hand-held launcher (K-84). Draw weight, draw length, let-off and handedness are attributes; Olympic, 3D and bowhunting are disciplines (use tags). Display group: Bows, crossbows & arrows.

### E4.3.1 Bows
- E4.3.1.01 Recurve bow: hand-drawn bow whose limb tips curve away from the archer.
- E4.3.1.02 Compound bow: hand-drawn bow whose limbs are bent by a system of cams and cables that gives let-off at full draw.
- E4.3.1.03 Longbow: tall hand-drawn bow with straight, narrow limbs.

### E4.3.2 Crossbows
- E4.3.2.01 Recurve crossbow: crossbow with recurved limbs mounted across a stock and released by a trigger.
- E4.3.2.02 Compound crossbow: crossbow whose limbs work through cams and cables.

### E4.3.3 Other hand-held launchers
- E4.3.3.01 Slingshot: hand-held fork with elastic bands that launches a small projectile; E1 when made as a toy (K-84).
- E4.3.3.02 Blowgun: tube through which a dart is propelled by the breath; E1 when made as a toy (K-84). †

## E4.4 Swords
Leaf axis: blade profile (straight double-edged; single-edged; thrusting). Hilt length (one-handed, hand-and-a-half, two-handed) and `function_class` are attributes; region and era are tags; school is a school-table link. Regional names (jian, dao, liuyedao, dadao, shamshir, kilij, talwar) and lettered typologies (Oakeshott types, blade-construction names) are named forms. Display group: Swords.

### E4.4.1 Straight double-edged
- E4.4.1.01 Arming sword: one-handed straight double-edged sword with a cross guard. †
- E4.4.1.02 Longsword: straight double-edged sword with a hilt long enough for one or two hands.
- E4.4.1.03 Greatsword: large straight double-edged sword wielded with two hands.

### E4.4.2 Single-edged
- E4.4.2.01 Sabre: single-edged, usually curved cavalry sword; national and regional patterns are named forms. †
- E4.4.2.02 Cutlass: short, broad single-edged sword with a basket or cup guard.
- E4.4.2.03 Katana (Japanese long sword): curved single-edged Japanese long sword worn edge up through the sash.
- E4.4.2.04 Tachi (Japanese long sword): curved single-edged Japanese long sword slung edge down from the belt.
- E4.4.2.05 Wakizashi (Japanese short sword): single-edged Japanese short sword, the shorter blade of the daishō pair.
- E4.4.2.06 Tantō (Japanese dagger-length sword): single-edged Japanese blade under one shaku, a sword kind by tradition.

### E4.4.3 Thrusting
- E4.4.3.01 Rapier: long, slender sword with a complex hilt made mainly for the thrust. †
- E4.4.3.02 Smallsword: light, short thrusting sword with a small guard.

## E4.5 Knives and daggers
No leaf (`leaf = null`). Kinds by blade style; `deployment` (fixed, folding, automatic) and `blade_shape` are attributes, except where the opening mechanism is the form (butterfly, automatic). Display group: Knives & daggers (shown by blade style).
- E4.5.01 Dagger: short double-edged thrusting blade with a hilt.
- E4.5.02 Push dagger: short blade set at right angles to a T-handle gripped in the fist.
- E4.5.03 Bowie or fighting knife: large fixed-blade knife made for fighting, typically with a clip point and guard. †
- E4.5.04 Hunting knife: knife made for dressing and skinning game; skinning and caping knives are non-maker named forms (section 14 E4-5).
- E4.5.05 Karambit: knife with a curved, claw-like blade and a finger ring, fixed or folding.
- E4.5.06 Butterfly knife: folding knife whose two handles rotate around the tang to cover or expose the blade.
- E4.5.07 Automatic knife: knife whose blade opens by a spring at the press of a button or switch, side-opening or out-the-front.
- E4.5.08 Throwing knife: balanced knife made to be thrown at a target.
- E4.5.09 Bayonet: blade made to fix to the muzzle of a firearm.
- E4.5.10 Shuriken: hand-thrown star or spike with sharpened points; E1 when made as a toy (K-84). †

## E4.6 Polearms, axes and impact weapons
Leaf axis: form class by handling (polearm; axe; staff; impact; flexible), following the long, short and flexible classes of the Chinese apparatus lists. Display group: Polearms, axes & impact weapons.

### E4.6.1 Polearms
- E4.6.1.01 Spear: shaft with a pointed head made for thrusting or throwing; yari and qiang are named forms.
- E4.6.1.02 Halberd: polearm combining an axe blade, a spike and a hook on one head.
- E4.6.1.03 Naginata: Japanese polearm with a long curved single-edged blade.
- E4.6.1.04 Glaive: polearm with a single-edged blade on the end of the shaft. †

### E4.6.2 Axes
- E4.6.2.01 Battle axe: axe made for fighting, not for cutting wood.
- E4.6.2.02 Tomahawk: light one-handed axe made for fighting or throwing. †

### E4.6.3 Staffs
- E4.6.3.01 Bō staff: long wooden staff used as a weapon. †
- E4.6.3.02 Gun staff [棍]: Chinese long staff weapon of the apparatus lists.

### E4.6.4 Impact weapons
- E4.6.4.01 Mace: club with a heavy head, often flanged or spiked.
- E4.6.4.02 War hammer: hafted weapon with a hammer head, often backed by a spike.
- E4.6.4.03 Baton: rigid or telescoping club made for striking.
- E4.6.4.04 Tonfa: baton with a side handle set at right angles to the shaft. †
- E4.6.4.05 Nunchaku: two short sticks joined by a cord or chain.
- E4.6.4.06 Kubotan: short rod held in the fist to strike or apply pressure. †

### E4.6.5 Flexible weapons [软兵器]
- E4.6.5.01 Flail: striking head joined to a handle by a chain.
- E4.6.5.02 Rope dart: dart or blade on the end of a long rope, swung and cast.
- E4.6.5.03 Chain whip: jointed steel segments ending in a dart, swung as a whip.
- E4.6.5.04 Three-section staff: three staff sections joined end to end by chains or cords. †

## Precedence inside E4

- One home per object: a named form, lines row or variant is a ledger row under one kind and never a second home; legal class, use, discipline, scene, region, era and school never divide the tree, and the use, nihonto and Chinese-weapons views are generated from tags.
- A tantō is a sword kind by tradition (E4.4.2.06) and never a knife, whatever its length; katana, tachi and wakizashi (E4.4.2.03 to E4.4.2.05) are kinds because the length class names a different carried object and mounting set (the daishō pair); their kind level is provisional until sellers in two regions give each its own category (R-19, CV-27).
- A regional sword name is a named form under the generic kind with a region tag: jian under arming sword (E4.4.1.01), dao, liuyedao, dadao, shamshir, kilij and talwar under sabre (E4.4.2.01); the Zweihänder and claymore are named forms of greatsword (E4.4.1.03) and the Oakeshott types named forms of longsword (E4.4.1.02) (E-4, C11).
- Muzzle-loading is a kind for long guns only (E4.1.3.05, E4.1.4.05), where it changes the loading form; `ignition` is otherwise an attribute, so a percussion revolver is a single-action revolver (E4.1.2.02) (E-21). A combination gun with rifle and shotgun barrels is E4.1.5.01, neither a rifle nor a shotgun.
- A spring-piston and a gas-piston air rifle are one kind, piston air rifle (E4.2.1.02), with `piston_type` an attribute; PCP and CO2 are kinds because the powerplant changes the body (C10).
- A knife sold both fixed and folding has one kind, with `deployment` an attribute (karambit E4.5.05); the butterfly knife (E4.5.06) and automatic knife (E4.5.07) are kinds because the opening mechanism is their form. A bayonet is E4.5.09 by form. Skinning and caping knives are named forms of hunting knife (E4.5.04); the gut hook is the attribute `gut_hook` (section 14 E4-5).
- An EDC or other locking folder is C1 unless its blade is a weapon kind (karambit E4.5.05, push dagger E4.5.02, butterfly E4.5.06, automatic E4.5.07); a bushcraft or survival knife is C1 unless the maker sells it as a fighting or hunting knife (E4.5.03, E4.5.04), and a camping or survival use tag goes on an E4 knife only where the maker's page says so (K-83, section 14 E4-1, E4-2).
- A baton and a tonfa are impact weapons (E4.6.4.03, E4.6.4.04) by form, not self-defence devices (E4-O3); an axe made for fighting or throwing is a battle axe or tomahawk (E4.6.2.01, E4.6.2.02), and one made for cutting wood is C1 (K-173).
- Arrows (E4.9.08) and crossbow bolts (E4.9.09) are ammunition, not E4.3 (E4-O1); bullets, primers and powder sold for loading are E4.9.10 to E4.9.12 and black powder E4.9.06, not C8 fuel (K-86, E4-O2); calibre is never a kind: the standard cartridge is a named form of its cartridge class (E-7).
- A slingshot, blowgun or shuriken (E4.3.3.01, E4.3.3.02, E4.5.10) is E4 unless made as a toy, then E1 (K-84).
- A plate carrier is armour in its own right (E4.8.2.04), not a part; ballistic and fragmentation helmets are one kind (E4.8.1.02) with `protection_level` an attribute (E-23); loose armour pieces are E4.8.3 and loose sword fittings (scabbard, sword guard with tsuba, menuki) are E4.10.3 under S3 (K-86); an antique sword or parade helmet is filed by its form (S5).
- A part or fitting made for one weapon class follows it to its E4.10 leaf; a fitting that serves several weapon classes (rifle scope E4.10.4.01, weapon light E4.10.4.02, bipod E4.10.4.03, shooting rest E4.10.4.04) is E4.10.4 (C14(b)); a case made to carry one firearm or bow follows it into E4 under S3.
- A reproduction is E4 when battle-ready (`function_class`), E2 when blunted for sport (HEMA feders and synthetics, section 14 E4-3), B4 when decorative and E1 when made of foam (K-86); blunt or unsharpened wushu implements are E2, decorative ones B4, sharpened ones E4 (K-87).
- Boundaries that bound these kinds: a blank-firing pistol (E4.1.6.01) made for self-defence or deterrence is E4 and one made to start races E2; airsoft and paintball markers are E2 and a pellet-firing air gun E4; archery targets, arm guards, finger tabs, decoys, blinds, game calls and clay throwers are E2 (K-163); holsters, belt pouches and quivers are A4 (K-84, K-137); ear defenders, shooting glasses and helmets against other hazards are A1, a one-sport helmet E2 (K-125); binoculars and spotting optics are C6 and laser rangefinders E2 (K-148), while a scope or red-dot sight made to mount on a weapon is E4; a hand-held flashlight is B3 (K-162); gun safes and pest-killing tools are B8, racks and cabinets B1, personal attack alarms B8 (K-88); reloading presses and dies are C1; cleaning products are B6; livestock stunners are C7; bombs, grenades, mines, missiles and artillery are OUT except deactivated display pieces (B4) (K-85).
