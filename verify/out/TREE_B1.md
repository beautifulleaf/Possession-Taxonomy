# B1 Furniture & Storage: decided tree

Category: B1 Furniture & Storage (domain B, Home). Membership test (placement key step 30, branch 5): made to sit, lie, work at, display, or store and organise things on or in, indoor or outdoor, or to hold a small child in place: seating, tables, beds and mattresses, bean bags, cots, high chairs and playpens, low tray-tables such as the soban, cabinets and fitted cabinetry, shelving, wardrobes, racks (wine, shoe, coat, magazine), hooks, hangers, boxes, baskets, shoe bags, chests and trunks, plant stands, display stands, counters and shop fittings, movable partitions, folding and camping furniture, pop-up gazebos, lap desks, rolling carts; also empty albums, binders, stock books and trays made to hold several collected objects (K-9). Not here: tool boxes, tool storage, workbenches and sawhorses for tool work (C1); safes and gun cabinets (B8); stair gates and guards fitted to the home (B8); mattress toppers, pads, protectors, cushions, bedding and sofa or chair covers (B2); mirrors, vases, frames and decorations (B4); food storage, dish racks and wine fridges (B5); laundry baskets (B6); baths, basins and vanity units with a built-in basin (B7); carried smoking cases and travel humidors (B9); pet beds, cat trees and animal housing (B10); planters, pots and cachepots (C2); blank scrapbook albums (C4); desk-top trays and paper binders (C5); flat packing boxes (C8); camping sleeping bags and mats, and pool, snooker and table-tennis tables (E2); play structures and toy furniture (E1); medical, hospital and treatment furniture (H2), barber and massage chairs (H1); coffins, altars and shrines (F4); filled photo albums (F5); a built gazebo (G2). Structure (Candidate A, decided B-1; memo sections 3 and 10): sub-family by primary use (sit; lie or be held in place; work at; store in furniture; hold and organise loose items; show, divide or shelter), closed by a parts sub-family exempt from that axis (CV-17); leaf by an axis stated per sub-family; B1.6 and B1.7 have no leaf; kind at the use type where buyers compare, one step deeper for mattresses (construction; B-12). Convertibles are filed by the state they are sold in (B-2). Room, style, period, region, material, setting (indoor, outdoor, garden, camping), user age, market, installation (fitted or freestanding), power source, size and bed-size name, seat count, configuration (corner, L-shaped), with storage, unit of sale (set), Martindale rubs or fabric class, firmness (with its scale named) and finish are attributes, tags or generated views, never kinds; furniture suites and garden sets are filed by their pieces (K-1). A kind marked † is named by the key, a decision or the DPH pass without a fetched page in the memo; its id is fixed and it is attested in the stage-3 harvest. Maker role: owner (maker_roles.tsv, provisional): models and design classics carry the maker; design houses are a side tab. Display groups (navigation proposal, display only, no id changes): Sofas & armchairs (B1.1.1, B1.1.2, B1.1.6); Chairs, stools & benches (B1.1.3, B1.1.4, B1.1.5); Beds & mattresses (B1.2); Tables & desks (B1.3); Storage furniture (B1.4); Boxes, racks & organisers (B1.5.1, B1.5.2); Albums & collection holders (B1.5.3, shown outside the furniture groups); Screens, stands & shelters (B1.6); Replacement parts (B1.7). Quality markers (navigation proposal; attributes, never kinds): for upholstered seating frame, suspension, cushion filling, Martindale class, stitching and frame warranty; for case furniture and tables material, joinery, sturdiness and finish; for mattresses construction type (a choice, not a ladder), firmness, support and durability; for design classics licence status on maker or line, designer and period.

Ids (R-58): B1.n is a sub-family and B1.n.m a leaf; a kind is B1.n.m.kk, or B1.n.kk where a sub-family has no leaf.

## B1.1 Seating
Leaf axis: the form of seat; kinds canonical form first, then by structural complexity.

### B1.1.1 Sofas & settees
Display group: Sofas & armchairs.
- B1.1.1.01 Sofa: an upholstered seat with back and arms for two or more people.
- B1.1.1.02 Sectional sofa: a sofa made of joined sections that can form a corner or L shape (corner sofa is an alias; configuration is an attribute).
- B1.1.1.03 Sofa bed: a sofa sold as a seat that unfolds or pulls out into a bed with a hidden mattress (sleeper sofa).
- B1.1.1.04 Futon sofa: a convertible frame whose seat cushion is the mattress, folding flat into a bed (the US "futon"), sold with or without its mattress.

### B1.1.2 Armchairs & lounge chairs
Display group: Sofas & armchairs.
- B1.1.2.01 Armchair: an upholstered single seat with arms and back.
- B1.1.2.02 Hanging chair: a single seat suspended from a ceiling or a stand.
- B1.1.2.03 Recliner: an armchair whose back tilts and footrest rises, manually or by motor.
- B1.1.2.04 Rocking chair: a chair on curved rockers or a glider base that rocks.
- B1.1.2.05 Chaise longue: an upholstered indoor seat long enough to support the legs.
- B1.1.2.06 Sun lounger: an outdoor reclining seat with an adjustable backrest, often on wheels, made to lie on in the sun. †

### B1.1.3 Dining & side chairs
Display group: Chairs, stools & benches.
- B1.1.3.01 Dining chair: an upright chair with a back, made for a dining or side table.
- B1.1.3.02 Folding chair: a chair that folds flat for storage or carrying.
- B1.1.3.03 High chair: a raised chair with a tray or harness that holds a small child at table height.

### B1.1.4 Stools, benches & footrests
Display group: Chairs, stools & benches.
- B1.1.4.01 Bar stool: a tall seat without arms for a bar or counter.
- B1.1.4.02 Bench: a long seat for several people, with or without a back (with storage is an attribute).
- B1.1.4.03 Ottoman: a padded seat or footrest without back or arms (pouf; with storage is an attribute).

### B1.1.5 Work & task seating
Display group: Chairs, stools & benches.
- B1.1.5.01 Office chair: an adjustable seat made for desk work, usually swivelling on castors.

### B1.1.6 Floor & soft seating
Display group: Sofas & armchairs.
- B1.1.6.01 Bean bag chair: a soft seat of a fabric bag filled with loose beads that takes the sitter's shape.
- B1.1.6.02 Floor chair: a legless seat with a back that rests directly on the floor.

## B1.2 Sleeping & lying
Leaf axis: what is lain on or in (bed or cot, mattress, bed base); the playpen sits here because it holds a small child in place.

### B1.2.1 Beds & cots
Display group: Beds & mattresses.
- B1.2.1.01 Platform bed: a bed frame with a solid or slatted platform that takes a mattress without a separate base.
- B1.2.1.02 Bunk bed: two or more beds stacked one above the other on one frame.
- B1.2.1.03 Daybed: a bed sold as a bed that also serves as a seat, with a back and sides.
- B1.2.1.04 Four-poster bed: a bed with a tall post at each corner, often carrying a canopy.
- B1.2.1.05 Baby cot (crib): a barred bed made for an infant or toddler.
- B1.2.1.06 Camp bed: a light folding bed on a frame, used for camping or guests (US "cot").
- B1.2.1.07 Playpen (play yard): a freestanding enclosure that holds a small child in place to play or sleep. †

### B1.2.2 Mattresses (by construction)
Display group: Beds & mattresses.
- B1.2.2.01 Innerspring mattress: a mattress whose support core is a unit of metal springs.
- B1.2.2.02 Foam mattress: a mattress whose core is polyurethane, memory or other foam.
- B1.2.2.03 Hybrid mattress: a mattress with a spring core under substantial foam or latex comfort layers.
- B1.2.2.04 Latex mattress: a mattress whose core is natural or synthetic latex.
- B1.2.2.05 Airbed: a mattress whose support is an inflatable air chamber, sold as a bed for a room.
- B1.2.2.06 Floor or futon mattress [shiki-futon]: a thin mattress that folds or rolls away, used on the floor or on a futon sofa frame. †

### B1.2.3 Bed bases
Display group: Beds & mattresses.
- B1.2.3.01 Slatted bed base: a frame of sprung or flat slats that supports a mattress.
- B1.2.3.02 Divan base: an upholstered box base on which a mattress sits, often with drawers.

## B1.3 Tables & work surfaces
Leaf axis: how the surface is used (formality of use: dining, occasional, desk, folding or floor, mobile).

### B1.3.1 Dining & kitchen tables
Display group: Tables & desks.
- B1.3.1.01 Dining table: a table made for eating meals at, seated.
- B1.3.1.02 Gateleg table: a table with hinged leaves held up by legs that swing out like gates.

### B1.3.2 Occasional tables
Display group: Tables & desks.
- B1.3.2.01 Coffee table: a low table set in front of a sofa.
- B1.3.2.02 Side table: a small table set beside a seat or bed (accent or end table).
- B1.3.2.03 Console table: a narrow table set against a wall.
- B1.3.2.04 Tray table: a small low table whose top is a tray, often removable or folding.

### B1.3.3 Desks & work tables
Display group: Tables & desks.
- B1.3.3.01 Desk: a table made to work, write or study at, often with drawers.
- B1.3.3.02 Sit-stand desk: a desk whose height adjusts between sitting and standing (drive is an attribute).
- B1.3.3.03 Dressing table: a table with drawers and often a mirror, made to sit at while dressing or grooming.
- B1.3.3.04 Lap desk: a small portable surface made to rest on the lap. †

### B1.3.4 Folding & floor tables
Display group: Tables & desks.
- B1.3.4.01 Folding table: a table whose legs or top fold for storage or carrying.
- B1.3.4.02 Heated low table [kotatsu]: a low floor table with a built-in heater beneath, covered by a quilt.

### B1.3.5 Carts & islands
Display group: Tables & desks.
- B1.3.5.01 Rolling cart: a small wheeled unit of shelves or baskets.
- B1.3.5.02 Kitchen island: a freestanding or fitted work-surface unit standing clear of the kitchen walls.
- B1.3.5.03 Bar cart: a wheeled cart made to hold and serve drinks.

## B1.4 Storage furniture
Leaf axis: the case form (drawers, doors, open shelves, chest); installation, fitted or freestanding, is an attribute; kinds ordered by number of closed compartments.

### B1.4.1 Chests of drawers & bedside
Display group: Storage furniture.
- B1.4.1.01 Chest of drawers (dresser): a case of stacked drawers for clothes or linen.
- B1.4.1.02 Nightstand: a small bedside cabinet or table with a drawer or shelf.

### B1.4.2 Cupboards & cabinets
Display group: Storage furniture.
- B1.4.2.01 Sideboard: a long low cabinet with doors and drawers for dining-room storage (buffet, credenza).
- B1.4.2.02 Display cabinet: a cabinet with glass doors or sides for showing what it holds.
- B1.4.2.03 TV unit: a low cabinet made to hold a television and media equipment.
- B1.4.2.04 Filing cabinet: a cabinet of deep drawers for hanging files and papers.
- B1.4.2.05 Locker: a tall narrow lockable cupboard, often in banks, for personal storage.
- B1.4.2.06 Kitchen cabinet: a base, wall or tall cupboard made for kitchen storage, fitted or freestanding.
- B1.4.2.07 Bathroom vanity cabinet: a bathroom cabinet made to carry a basin, sold without the basin.
- B1.4.2.08 Humidor (standing): a cabinet that stands in a room and keeps cigars at a set humidity. †

### B1.4.3 Wardrobes & closets
Display group: Storage furniture.
- B1.4.3.01 Wardrobe (armoire): a tall cupboard with a hanging rail for clothes, freestanding or built in.

### B1.4.4 Shelving
Display group: Storage furniture.
- B1.4.4.01 Bookcase: a freestanding unit of open shelves.
- B1.4.4.02 Wall shelf: a shelf made only to be fixed to a wall.
- B1.4.4.03 Modular shelving system: a system of uprights, shelves and units that combine and extend within one maker's system.

### B1.4.5 Chests & trunks
Display group: Storage furniture.
- B1.4.5.01 Blanket chest (hope chest): a lidded box chest for linen and blankets.
- B1.4.5.02 Trunk: a large strong lidded chest, kept as storage.

## B1.5 Containers & organisers
Leaf axis: how it holds (closed container; open rack or hook; pages, pockets or compartments); kinds ordered open to closed.

### B1.5.1 Boxes, bins, baskets & bags
Display group: Boxes, racks & organisers.
- B1.5.1.01 Storage box: a lidded or open box or bin made to hold several objects in a home.
- B1.5.1.02 Storage basket: a woven or wire basket made to hold several objects.
- B1.5.1.03 Shoe bag: a hanging or zipped bag with pockets made to store shoes. †

### B1.5.2 Racks, hooks & hangers
Display group: Boxes, racks & organisers.
- B1.5.2.01 Wine rack: a free-standing or wall rack that holds wine bottles lying down.
- B1.5.2.02 Shoe rack: an open rack of tiers that holds pairs of shoes.
- B1.5.2.03 Magazine rack: a rack or holder for magazines and newspapers.
- B1.5.2.04 Wall hook: a hook or row of hooks fixed to a wall or door for hanging things.
- B1.5.2.05 Coat rack: a free-standing stand or wall rail of hooks for coats and hats. †
- B1.5.2.06 Clothes hanger: a shaped frame from which a garment hangs on a rail. †

### B1.5.3 Albums & collection holders
Made to hold several collected objects in pages, pockets or compartments; B1 when empty (K-9). Display group: Albums & collection holders (shown outside the furniture groups).
- B1.5.3.01 Photo album (empty): a bound or binder album made to hold photographic prints, sold empty (page mechanism is an attribute). †
- B1.5.3.02 Film album: an album of sleeves made to hold negatives and slides. †
- B1.5.3.03 Stamp album: an album with printed or blank pages made to hold postage stamps. †
- B1.5.3.04 Stock book: a book of strip pockets in which stamps are slotted and held loose. †
- B1.5.3.05 Coin album: an album or folder of pages with holes or pockets made to hold coins. †
- B1.5.3.06 Card binder: a binder of pocket pages made to hold trading cards and similar cards. †
- B1.5.3.07 Collection tray: a tray of compartments made to hold coins, stamps or specimens. †

## B1.6 Display, division & shelter
No leaf (`leaf = null`); axis: show a thing, divide a space, or give shelter.
Display group: Screens, stands & shelters.
- B1.6.01 Room divider: a freestanding partition or open shelving unit that divides a room.
- B1.6.02 Folding screen: a freestanding screen of hinged panels that folds.
- B1.6.03 Display stand: a stand made to show objects or goods. †
- B1.6.04 Plant stand: a stand that raises one or more plant pots. †
- B1.6.05 Shop counter and fitting: a counter, gondola or shelving fitting made for a shop. †
- B1.6.06 Pop-up gazebo: a portable frame-and-canopy shelter that opens and folds without being built. †

## B1.7 Parts and accessories made for one furniture object
Exempt from the level-1 axis (CV-17); no leaf (`leaf = null`); axis: the object served, ordered bed, table, shelving; each kind carries a `fits` link to the kind it serves.
Display group: Replacement parts.
- B1.7.01 Bed slats: loose slats made for one bed frame.
- B1.7.02 Bed guard rail: a rail made to be fitted to one bed to keep a child from rolling out. †
- B1.7.03 Table leg: a leg sold to be fitted to a table top. †
- B1.7.04 Replacement shelf: a shelf made for one shelving or cabinet model. †

## Precedence inside B1

- A convertible is filed by the state it is sold in: a sofa bed is B1.1.1.03 and a futon sofa B1.1.1.04, also when sold with its mattress; a daybed is B1.2.1.03; a storage bench or storage ottoman is B1.1.4.02 or B1.1.4.03 with the attribute "with storage"; a chest sold as storage stays B1.4.5.01; there is no multi-use sub-family (B-2).
- A corner or L-shaped sofa is B1.1.1.02 with its `configuration` attribute; "corner sofa" is an alias.
- A sun lounger made for outdoor use is B1.1.2.06; the upholstered indoor chaise longue is B1.1.2.05.
- A garden furniture set, garden lounger set, dining set or bedroom suite is filed by its pieces, each as its own kind, with `unit_of_sale` = set; there are no suite or set kinds (K-1). Garden and outdoor furniture is a setting view, not a branch.
- A bunk bed with a desk beneath is B1.2.1.02 with the desk as an attribute.
- A mattress and its base are B1 (B1.2.2, B1.2.3); a mattress topper, pad or protector, pillow, duvet, loose seat cushion or sofa cushion sold separately is B2. A cushioned seat sold as a piece (ottoman, pouf, bean bag) is B1.1.4.03 or B1.1.6.01.
- Mattresses are kinds by construction (B1.2.2.01 to B1.2.2.05); pocket spring, cold foam, gel foam and viscoelastic are attributes of those kinds; firmness and size are attributes (B-12).
- A thin mattress that folds or rolls away, used on the floor or on a futon sofa frame, is B1.2.2.06 ("futon mattress" and "shiki-futon" are aliases); the kake-futon quilt over it and the kotatsu cover are B2 (B2-O2).
- An inflatable mattress sold as a bed for a room is B1.2.2.05; an inflatable or foam pad sold for camping or outdoor sleeping is E2. Camp beds (B1.2.1.06), camp chairs and folding tables are B1; sleeping bags and mats are E2.
- A freestanding play yard or playpen is B1.2.1.07; a gate or guard fitted to the home is B8 (R-8, B8-O1). A child bed guard rail is fitted to a bed, not the home, and is B1.7.02.
- Loose bed slats made for one bed frame are B1.7.01; a complete slatted base is B1.2.3.01.
- Sofa and chair covers are B2.4 kinds, also when made to fit one sofa model, never B1 parts (B2-O1); positioning cushions are B2 (K-82).
- The nightstand is storage furniture (B1.4.1.02), not a table (B-13).
- Highboy, lowboy, chiffonier and commode are non-maker named forms of B1.4.1.01; kaidan and isho tansu are named forms of B1.4.1.01, mizuya tansu of B1.4.2.06 and kuruma tansu of B1.4.5.01 (region Japan); "tansu" is not a kind and is a homonym with those three targets. Zaisu is a named form of B1.1.6.02 and soban a named form of B1.3.2.04 (region tag). Chesterfield is a named form of B1.1.1.01 and Windsor chair of B1.1.3.01 (B-13).
- A kotatsu, korsi or any table with a built-in heater is B1.3.4.02; its cover is B2; a bath, basin or vanity basin is B7. A washbasin sold built into its cabinet is B7; a vanity cabinet sold without a basin is B1.4.2.07 (K-184).
- Fitted kitchen cabinets and built-in wardrobes are B1.4.2.06 and B1.4.3.01 with `installation` = fitted (B-13).
- A wall shelf (B1.4.4.02) is a kind apart from the bookcase (B1.4.4.01) because its body is made only for the wall.
- A safe, gun cabinet or lockable security cabinet made to protect valuables or firearms is B8; a locker (B1.4.2.05), filing cabinet (B1.4.2.04) or lockable cupboard made to store things is B1 (B-33).
- A humidor, tobacco jar or cabinet that stands in a room is B1 (the humidor B1.4.2.08); a cigarette case, cigar case, travel humidor or tobacco pouch is B9; rolling trays are B9, stash jars and boxes are B1 (K-164, K-167).
- A tool box, tool chest or tool storage is C1; a household chest, cabinet or shelving unit is B1 whatever it holds (S3). A workbench, sawhorse or trestle made for tool work is C1; a desk or craft table is B1.3.3.01; art-studio, studio and lab furniture are B1; easels are C4 and studio backdrops B2 (K-190, C1-O1, K-172, K-17, C-18).
- A dining table, dining chair, bar stool, kitchen cabinet, pantry unit, island or trolley is B1; food storage containers, bread bins and canisters are B5; a dish rack is B5, a wine rack B1.5.2.01, a wine fridge B5 (B-33).
- A laundry basket or hamper is B6; a storage basket (B1.5.1.02), shoe bag (B1.5.1.03) or box (B1.5.1.01) is B1. A festival or decorative basket kept for storage is B1.5.1.02; a decoration is B4. The basket or box kept from a hamper is B1.
- Mirrors, vases, picture frames and ornaments are B4 even when they stand on furniture; a dressing table (B1.3.3.03) or console table (B1.3.2.03) is B1. Bookends and jewellery boxes are B1; a tray made to serve is B5, one made to hold small items on a surface is B1 (K-191).
- Garden furniture, loungers and outdoor storage boxes are B1; planters and pots are C2; a plant stand is B1.6.04 and a cachepot C2 (K-179). A standing beach or patio umbrella is B1 (K-133). A pop-up gazebo is B1.6.06; a built gazebo is G2.
- A pool, snooker, table-tennis or darts table is E2; a card, poker or general game table is B1; play structures and toy furniture sold as toys are E1; a child-size chair, table or bed sold as furniture stays B1 (user age is a modifier). Piano benches and racks are B1 (K-145).
- A hospital bed, examination table and dentist chair are H2; barber and massage chairs are H1; an ordinary bed or recliner made in an adapted form is B1; a bed, mattress or chair made to treat or manage a condition is H2; furniture made to restrain or suspend is H3 (K-190, K-39, K-82). A recliner with added vibration is B1.1.2.03.
- A dog bed, cat tree or pet cage is B10; a coffin, casket, altar or shrine is F4; a hope chest, blanket chest or trunk is B1.4.5.01 or B1.4.5.02.
- Luggage, rucksacks and bags that travel with a person are A4; a storage bag or tote that stays in a home is B1 only when it holds several objects (S3), otherwise it follows the object.
- A free-standing rack is B1; a roof box or rack made to be fitted to one vehicle is G1 (K-7). A stand with shelves or storage is B1; a bracket, arm or mount made to hold one screen or speaker follows it (K-8). A case or stand made to hold one object and travel or stand with it is a part of that object (S3).
- An album, binder, stock book, box or tray made to hold several collected objects is B1.5.3 when empty; a filled photo album or scrapbook is F5; a blank scrapbook album is C4; a document binder made for papers and desk-top trays and holders are C5 (K-9, C-17). How the pages hold the objects (slip-in or pocket, ring-binder, post-bound, stick-in, accordion, box) is the `page_mechanism` attribute of B1.5.3.01, not a kind; the construction-record album is a named form of B1.5.3.01. Storage boxes are B1.5.1.01; cardboard boxes sold flat for packing or moving are C8 (K-193).
- Picnic baskets, fitting rooms and shop fittings stay where the key puts them: baskets B1.5.1.02, counters and shop fittings B1.6.05 (B-14).
- Room divider (B1.6.01) against folding screen (B1.6.02) is decided by hinged panels or not (provisional until a page shows both).
