**Output:** 261 named forms, 12 lines, 180 makers in `verify/out/HV_E4_1_*.tsv`. Nine makers reuse existing ids (Benchmade, Boker, Spyderco, Castille Armory, Fabri Armorum, Kingston Arms, Krieger Armory, Landsknecht Emporium, Regenyei). Same-name rows already in data/makers.tsv are other entities (Taurus, Diana, Citadel, Excalibur, Albion), so I gave new ids. E4.1 to E4.3 makers appear only as owners of named forms. E4.4 and E4.5 makers are every maker the pages show, including the KnifeCenter fixed-blade filter brands (partial; custom makers marked artisan from the name only). No countries unless a page states them.

**Catalogues:** Kult of Athena category pages served E4.4 and E4.5 very well. Pyramyd AIR, Lancaster Archery, Sportsman's Warehouse and BladeHQ served well (BladeHQ, KnifeCenter, Walmart and Lancaster sub-pages needed the Parallel fetch after 403). IMFDB browser is mostly fictional (not used). Modern Firearms and Wikipedia brands list were partial. Plink Exchange gave brands and models without kinds. No crossbow models recorded (pages give brand only). Federschwert trainers (E2), licensed fiction swords and collaboration items were skipped.
