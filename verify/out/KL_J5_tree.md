# J5 Pantry Staples: decided tree (kind-level test)

Category J5 holds groceries kept in the pantry, filed once, by what the product is when bought. Place each object at one kind (the id of the most specific line below). Pack size, brand, packaging medium (dried, tinned, jarred, pouch), milling, roast, salt, firm/soft, organic and grade are attributes of a kind, not kinds. A cultivar, cut, protected name or maker's line is a named form under its kind.

Ids: J5.n is a sub-family, J5.n.m a leaf, J5.n.m.kk a kind. A leaf marked "no kinds decided" holds no kind yet; do not use it as an answer if a listed kind fits.

## J5.1 Rice, grains & flours
Leaf axis, an ordered key, first yes: (1) milled to flour or meal: Flours & meals; (2) a rice grain, whole or flattened: Rice; (3) oats, or a cereal made to eat at breakfast: Oats & breakfast cereals; (4) otherwise: Other grains, pseudo-cereals & starch pearls.

### J5.1.1 Rice
Kinds are grain types and cultivar groups as sold. Milling (white, brown, parboiled) and age are attributes. Kind key, first yes: flattened; glutinous; red or black bran; basmati group; jasmine group; sold as risotto rice; sold as paella rice; long-grain; otherwise medium- and short-grain.
- J5.1.1.01 Long-grain rice: slender non-aromatic rice grains that stay separate when cooked.
- J5.1.1.02 Basmati rice: the long, aromatic, aged grain of the basmati group.
- J5.1.1.03 Jasmine rice: the long, soft, fragrant grain of the jasmine group.
- J5.1.1.04 Medium- and short-grain rice: rounder rice that cooks tender and clings, including sushi and pudding rice and the Indian table rices.
- J5.1.1.05 Risotto rice: rice sold for risotto because it gives up starch (arborio, carnaroli types).
- J5.1.1.06 Paella rice: rice sold for paella because it absorbs a lot of stock (bomba type).
- J5.1.1.07 Glutinous (sticky) rice: rice that turns sticky and translucent when cooked.
- J5.1.1.08 Red and black rice: rice with a red or black bran layer left on.
- J5.1.1.09 Flattened rice (poha): rice grains steamed and rolled flat.

### J5.1.2 Other grains, pseudo-cereals & starch pearls
- J5.1.2.01 Millet: small whole millet grains (finger, foxtail, kodo and others).
- J5.1.2.02 Quinoa: the seed of the quinoa plant sold as a grain.
- J5.1.2.03 Wild rice: the long dark grain of the aquatic grass Zizania, not a rice plant.
- J5.1.2.04 Tapioca pearls (sabudana): dried pearls of cassava starch.

### J5.1.3 Oats & breakfast cereals
No kinds decided.

### J5.1.4 Flours & meals
Kinds are by grain, and for wheat by use class. Type numbers (405, 550, 1150) are attributes.
- J5.1.4.01 Plain / all-purpose wheat flour: refined soft-wheat flour for general baking and cooking.
- J5.1.4.02 Bread and pizza wheat flour: stronger wheat flour for yeast doughs.
- J5.1.4.03 Wholemeal wheat flour: wheat flour milled with the whole grain.
- J5.1.4.04 Rye flour: flour milled from rye.
- J5.1.4.05 Spelt flour: flour milled from spelt.
- J5.1.4.06 Whole-wheat atta: stone- or chakki-ground whole-wheat flour for South Asian flatbreads.
- J5.1.4.07 Semolina (sooji, rava): coarse granular milled wheat (durum) endosperm.
- J5.1.4.08 Chickpea flour (besan): flour milled from chickpeas.
- J5.1.4.09 Rice flour: flour milled from rice.

## J5.2 Pasta & noodles
Leaf axis is the dough base, an ordered key, first yes: (1) potato dough: Gnocchi; (2) buckwheat in the dough: Buckwheat noodles; (3) egg dough: Egg pasta & egg noodles; (4) rice flour or a pure starch (mung bean, potato): Rice & starch noodles; (5) durum wheat semolina, including whole-wheat durum: Durum wheat pasta; (6) soft wheat flour: Soft-wheat noodles; (7) any other flour: Other-flour pasta & noodles. Fresh or dried is an attribute. Shapes within a family (spaghetti, penne, fusilli) are named forms of the family kind; a maker line is a named form with its cut recorded.

### J5.2.1 Durum wheat pasta
- J5.2.1.01 Long-strand pasta: spaghetti, bucatini and other long cut strands or tubes.
- J5.2.1.02 Tube pasta: penne, rigatoni, macaroni, manicotti and similar hollow cuts.
- J5.2.1.03 Short and twisted pasta: fusilli, farfalle, conchiglie and similar short shapes.
- J5.2.1.04 Soup pasta (pastina): very small shapes for soups.

### J5.2.2 Egg pasta & egg noodles
- J5.2.2.01 Egg pasta, ribbons and nests: pasta made with egg in the dough, cut into ribbons or coiled nests (tagliatelle, fettuccine).

### J5.2.3 Soft-wheat noodles
An instant noodle brick (dry, with or without a flavour sachet) is a noodle of its kind; a cup or pot of noodles ready to add water to is a prepared meal and is not in J5.
- J5.2.3.01 Udon: thick Japanese wheat noodles.
- J5.2.3.02 Somen: very thin Japanese wheat noodles.
- J5.2.3.03 Ramen noodles: Chinese-style alkaline wheat noodles, fresh, dried or in an instant brick.

### J5.2.4 Buckwheat noodles
- J5.2.4.01 Soba: Japanese noodles made with buckwheat flour; the buckwheat share is an attribute.

### J5.2.5 Rice & starch noodles
- J5.2.5.01 Glass noodles (harusame): transparent noodles made from mung bean, potato or similar starch.

### J5.2.6 Other-flour pasta & noodles
No kinds decided.

### J5.2.7 Gnocchi
- J5.2.7.01 Potato gnocchi: small dumplings of potato dough.

## J5.3 Pulses
Leaf axis is the species group. Whole or split (dal) and dried or tinned are attributes: a dried and a tinned pulse of one species are one kind. Pulses cooked plain in water or brine stay here; cooked in a seasoned sauce they are prepared food and not in J5.

### J5.3.1 Beans
- J5.3.1.01 Red kidney beans (rajma): the dark red kidney-shaped Phaseolus bean.
- J5.3.1.02 Small red beans: small red Phaseolus beans (heirloom red beans).
- J5.3.1.03 Lima beans: flat, pale, broad lima (butter) beans.
- J5.3.1.04 Mung beans: small green mung beans, whole or split.
- J5.3.1.05 Black gram (urad): black or split white gram, whole or split.

### J5.3.2 Lentils
- J5.3.2.01 Lentils (masoor): lentils of any colour, whole or split.

### J5.3.3 Peas
No kinds decided.

### J5.3.4 Chickpeas & other pulses
- J5.3.4.01 Chickpeas: kabuli or desi chickpeas.
- J5.3.4.02 Pigeon peas (toor, arhar): pigeon peas, usually split as dal.

## J5.4 Nuts & seeds
Leaf axis is the plant group. Form (in shell, shelled, blanched, flaked, ground), roasting and salting are attributes, so ground almonds are almonds. Every nut, chestnut and coconut is here. Nuts coated in chocolate, sugar or batter are not in J5.

### J5.4.1 Tree nuts
- J5.4.1.01 Almonds: almonds in any form.
- J5.4.1.02 Walnuts: walnuts in any form.
- J5.4.1.03 Pecans: pecans in any form.
- J5.4.1.04 Hazelnuts (filberts): hazelnuts in any form.
- J5.4.1.05 Cashews: cashew nuts in any form.
- J5.4.1.06 Chestnuts: chestnuts, fresh, roasted or peeled.

### J5.4.2 Coconut
- J5.4.2.01 Desiccated coconut: dried shredded or grated coconut flesh.
- J5.4.2.02 Coconut milk and cream: the pressed milk or cream of coconut flesh sold in a tin or carton for cooking.

### J5.4.3 Peanuts
- J5.4.3.01 Peanuts: peanuts in any form.

### J5.4.4 Seeds
- J5.4.4.01 Hemp seeds: shelled or whole hemp seeds sold as food.

### J5.4.0 Final kind, no leaf
A mix of several kinds from one category is filed under the kind that makes up half or more of its weight; otherwise it is a final kind "mixed" at the end of the lowest level that holds all its parts.
- J5.4.0.01 Mixed nuts: a mix of several nuts where no one nut makes up half or more of the weight.

## J5.5 Oils, fats & vinegars
Leaf axis is the kind of product: plant oil, solid fat, vinegar or cooking liquid.

### J5.5.1 Plant oils
Legal category (extra virgin, refined) is an attribute.
- J5.5.1.01 Olive oil: oil pressed from olives, all legal categories together.
- J5.5.1.02 Sesame (gingelly) oil: oil pressed from sesame seed.
- J5.5.1.03 Mustard oil: oil pressed from mustard seed.
- J5.5.1.04 Rapeseed oil: oil pressed from rapeseed.
- J5.5.1.05 Sunflower oil: oil pressed from sunflower seed.

### J5.5.2 Animal & solid cooking fats
- J5.5.2.01 Lard and dripping: rendered pork or beef fat.
- J5.5.2.02 Margarine and plant-only fat spreads: spreadable fat made from plant oils and water, with no butter.

### J5.5.3 Vinegars & cooking liquids
Age class and protected name are attributes or named forms.
- J5.5.3.01 Wine vinegar: vinegar of wine.
- J5.5.3.02 Balsamic vinegar: vinegar of cooked grape must, including the protected Modena types.
- J5.5.3.03 Rice vinegar: vinegar of rice.
- J5.5.3.04 Malt vinegar: vinegar of malted barley.
- J5.5.3.05 Cider vinegar: vinegar of apple cider.
- J5.5.3.06 Mirin: sweet rice seasoning (hon-mirin and mirin-style seasoning) sold for cooking.
- J5.5.3.07 Cooking wine: wine made or sold for cooking.
- J5.5.3.08 Cooking sake: sake made or sold for cooking.

## J5.6 Spices, herbs & salt
Leaf axis is the form of the seasoning: single spice, dried herb, blend, salt. A dried chilli, whole, flaked or ground, sold to season food is a spice.

### J5.6.1 Single spices
Origin, species variant, cut and grade are named forms or attributes.
- J5.6.1.01 Saffron: dried stigmas of the saffron crocus.
- J5.6.1.02 Cinnamon: cinnamon or cassia bark.
- J5.6.1.03 Dried chilli and paprika: dried chillies and paprika, whole, flaked or ground.
- J5.6.1.04 Cumin (jeera): cumin seed.

### J5.6.2 Dried herbs
No kinds decided.

### J5.6.3 Spice blends & seasoning mixes
A blend sold under a blend name is a kind of its own.
- J5.6.3.01 Garam masala: the North Indian ground spice blend.

### J5.6.4 Salt
Brands are maker-bound named forms.
- J5.6.4.01 Table salt: fine, free-flowing salt.
- J5.6.4.02 Kosher salt: coarse, light-crystal cooking salt.
- J5.6.4.03 Flake salt: thin hollow or pyramid flakes used to finish dishes.
- J5.6.4.04 Fleur de sel: hand-skimmed fine sea salt crust.

## J5.8 Spreads, pastes & dips

### J5.8.1 Sweet spreads
- J5.8.1.01 Jam and fruit spreads: fruit cooked with sugar to a spread.
- J5.8.1.02 Hazelnut-chocolate spread: a sweet spread based on hazelnuts and chocolate or cocoa.

## J5.9 Sauces, condiments & stocks
Leaf axis is the product type as standards and shelves class it, an ordered key, first yes: (1) a soy sauce: Soy sauces; (2) another seasoning made by fermenting beans, grain or fish: Other fermented seasonings; (3) a stock or bouillon in any form: Stocks & bouillon; (4) a paste used as a cooking ingredient: Cooking & seasoning pastes; (5) a sauce to cook with or pour over pasta: Cooking & pasta sauces; (6) otherwise: Table sauces, condiments & dressings.

### J5.9.1 Soy sauces
Grade (Tokkyu, Jokyu, Hyojun) is an attribute; the brewery is a maker.
- J5.9.1.01 Koikuchi soy sauce: the common dark Japanese soy sauce of soy and wheat.
- J5.9.1.02 Usukuchi soy sauce: light-coloured, saltier Japanese soy sauce.
- J5.9.1.03 Tamari soy sauce: soy sauce made with little or no wheat.
- J5.9.1.04 Saishikomi soy sauce: twice-brewed soy sauce.
- J5.9.1.05 Shiro soy sauce: pale soy sauce made mostly from wheat.

### J5.9.2 Other fermented seasonings
- J5.9.2.01 Fermented soybean paste: miso, doenjang and similar pastes.
- J5.9.2.02 Fermented chilli paste: gochujang and similar fermented chilli pastes.
- J5.9.2.03 Broad-bean chilli paste: doubanjiang.
- J5.9.2.04 Fish sauce: the liquid of salted, fermented fish.

### J5.9.3 Cooking & pasta sauces
- J5.9.3.01 Tomato pasta sauces: tomato-based sauces for pasta, with or without meat.
- J5.9.3.02 Oyster sauce and hoisin sauce: thick dark Chinese cooking sauces.

### J5.9.4 Table sauces, condiments & dressings
A cooked relish or chutney of several ingredients is here.
- J5.9.4.01 Ketchup: tomato ketchup.
- J5.9.4.02 Mayonnaise: egg and oil emulsion.
- J5.9.4.03 Mustard: prepared mustard.
- J5.9.4.04 Horseradish: prepared horseradish.
- J5.9.4.05 Salad dressing: pourable dressings.
- J5.9.4.06 Chilli oil: oil infused with chilli, with or without solids.
- J5.9.4.07 Bitters sold as a flavouring by the dash: concentrated bitter flavouring used a few drops at a time (a bitter liqueur sold to drink by the glass is not in J5).

### J5.9.5 Stocks & bouillon
- J5.9.5.01 Stock cubes and bouillon: cubes, powders, pastes and liquids for making stock.

### J5.9.6 Cooking & seasoning pastes
No kinds decided.

## J5.10 Preserved fruit & vegetables
Fruit, vegetables, edible fungi and seaweed kept by a process. Leaf axis is the preserving process; the kind is the crop or product as preserved. A cultivar or protected designation is a named form; the pack medium is an attribute.

### J5.10.1 Dried
A dried vegetable is one sold to rehydrate and cook as a vegetable.
- J5.10.1.01 Dried mushrooms: dried edible fungi.
- J5.10.1.02 Dried seaweed: dried edible seaweed.

### J5.10.2 Tinned & jarred
Heat-processed in the pack, in water, brine, juice or syrup, or concentrated.
- J5.10.2.01 Whole peeled tomatoes: whole peeled tomatoes in the pack.
- J5.10.2.02 Chopped tomatoes: chopped tomatoes in the pack.
- J5.10.2.03 Passata: sieved raw-pressed tomato.
- J5.10.2.04 Tomato purée and paste: concentrated tomato purée or paste.
- J5.10.2.05 Tinned sweetcorn: sweetcorn kernels in the pack.

### J5.10.3 Pickled in vinegar
- J5.10.3.01 Gherkins (cornichons): small cucumbers pickled in vinegar.
- J5.10.3.02 Pickled peppers: peppers pickled in vinegar.

### J5.10.4 Salted, brined & fermented

- J5.10.4.01 Table olives: cured olives.
- J5.10.4.02 Sauerkraut: salt-fermented shredded cabbage.
- J5.10.4.03 Kimchi: salt- and chilli-fermented vegetables, usually cabbage.

### J5.11.1 Baking & fermenting aids

- J5.11.1.01 Baking powder: chemical raising agent.
- J5.11.1.02 Dried yeast: dried baker's yeast.
- J5.11.1.03 Malt extract: concentrated malt wort, liquid or dry, for brewing or baking.
- J5.11.1.04 Hops: hops for brewing.
- J5.11.1.05 Brewing yeast: yeast for home brewing and winemaking.

### J5.11.2 Flavourings, extracts & colourings
- J5.11.2.01 Vanilla and baking flavourings: vanilla extract, paste, essences and other flavourings for baking.

### J5.11.3 Setting agents, starches & thickeners
- J5.11.3.01 Gelatine and setting agents: gelatine, agar and similar.

### J5.11.4 Baking chocolate, doughs & mixes
Cocoa powder, cocoa butter and cocoa paste are not in J5.

- J5.11.4.02 Unbaked pastry: unbaked dough or pastry sheets.
- J5.11.4.03 Cake and baking mixes: dry mixes to which only liquid, egg or fat is added before baking.

## J5.12 Tofu, tempeh & meat alternatives
Leaf axis is how the protein food is made, an ordered key, first yes: (1) texturised or formed to imitate a meat product: Plant-based meat analogues; (2) washed from wheat dough: Seitan & wheat gluten; (3) a whole bean fermented: Tempeh & fermented beans; (4) set as a curd: Tofu & bean-curd products. Firmness and format are attributes.

### J5.12.1 Tofu & bean-curd products
- J5.12.1.01 Tofu: soybean curd set from soy milk.
- J5.12.1.02 Fermented bean curd: tofu cubes fermented in brine.

### J5.12.2 Tempeh & fermented beans
- J5.12.2.01 Tempeh: whole beans cultured into a firm cake.
- J5.12.2.02 Natto: whole soybeans fermented to a sticky mass.

### J5.12.3 Seitan & wheat gluten
- J5.12.3.01 Seitan: wheat gluten prepared as a meat-like protein food.

### J5.12.4 Plant-based meat analogues
A sausage, patty, ball or other shaped product made mainly of plant protein, seasoned but not coated, filled or sauced, is here. Coated (breaded or battered), filled (in pastry, as a dumpling or as a stuffed dish) or sauced products are prepared food and not in J5.
- J5.12.4.01 Plant-based burgers and sausages: formed plant-protein patties and sausages imitating meat.

## Precedence inside J5

1. A fruit, vegetable, edible fungus or seaweed sold dried, tinned, jarred, pickled, fermented or in oil is J5.10, in the leaf of its preserving process, with the crop as the kind. Except: pulses are J5.3 in any state (dried or tinned is a pack attribute); nuts, seeds and coconut are J5.4; a dried chilli or dried herb sold to season is J5.6; fruit cooked with sugar to a spread (jam) is J5.8; a concentrated purée of one crop is J5.10, not a J5.9 paste.
2. A dried chilli, whole, flaked or ground, sold to season food is a spice (J5.6); a dried vegetable (J5.10) is one sold to rehydrate and cook as a vegetable. A fermented soy or grain paste used to season (miso, doenjang, gochujang, doubanjiang) is J5.9; a fermented bean eaten as a protein body (natto, tempeh) is J5.12; a vegetable kept by fermenting (kimchi, sauerkraut) is J5.10.
3. A mix of kinds from one category is filed under the kind that makes up half or more of its weight. Otherwise it is a final kind "mixed <class>" at the end of the lowest level that holds all its parts (mixed nuts: J5.4, no leaf). A spice blend sold under a blend name (garam masala) is a kind of its own. Roasted and salted nuts are J5.4 with roast and salt as attributes.
4. A preserve of one main crop, pickled, brined, fermented or in oil, is J5.10; a cooked relish or chutney of several ingredients is J5.9 table condiments.
5. A spread, paste or dip eaten with other food (rillettes, liver pâté in a jar or tub, potted shrimp, taramasalata, hummus, guacamole, tzatziki, pesto) is J5.8, whatever it is made from.
6. A stock cube, bouillon paste, fish sauce, shrimp paste, oyster sauce or XO-type sauce is J5.9.
7. Margarine and plant-only fat spreads sold for cooking or spreading are J5.5 cooking fats.
8. Mirin, cooking wine, cooking sake and bitters sold as a flavouring are J5 even when they contain alcohol: the first three are J5.5.3, bitters by the dash J5.9.4. Extracts are J5.11.2.
9. Baking chocolate, couverture and chocolate chips are J5.11; cocoa powder, cocoa butter and cocoa paste are not.
10. A mix to which only liquid is added is J5.11 if the mix is for baking. A grocery is filed in J5 once, by what it is when bought.
11. A sausage, patty, ball or other shaped product made mainly of a plant protein, seasoned but not coated, filled or sauced, is J5.12.
12. A dry noodle brick is J5.2; a noodle cup or pot is a prepared meal, not J5. Rice and pulses cooked plain in water or brine are J5; cooked in a seasoned sauce they are not.
