# C5 Office & Stationery: decided tree

Category: C5 Office & Stationery (domain C, Making, Growing & Work). Membership test (CATEGORIES_v0_7, placement key step 31, with K-132, K-158, K-167, K-170, K-171, K-191, K-193, C-17, C5-O1 and C6-O2): made for writing, paper and desk work: pens, pencils, highlighters, paper goods, notebooks, diaries and planners, calendars, greeting cards, stickers, gift wrap, desk accessories and organisers, letter trays and filing supplies, rulers and postal scales, calculators, office machines whose main function is not printing from or scanning to a computer (copiers, shredders, laminators, label makers, binding machines), connected or not; also pens, nibs, brushes and inks whose maker's first-named use is handwriting, lettering, calligraphy or shodo, or both writing and drawing; copy and printer paper; envelopes, padded envelopes and mailing tubes for letters and post; pencil cases; general household scissors, office stamps and their ink pads, glue sticks and desk tape; drawing compasses, abacuses and slide rules; label makers and label printers of any connection; digital pens that write in ink on paper; and the parts made for one of these objects (ink, cartridges, converters, nibs, refills, leads, covers, refill books, index tabs, staples, label tape). Not here: printers, scanners, multifunction printers, cash registers and printer ink (D1); electronic organisers, e-ink and electronic writing tablets, and a stylus made for one tablet (D2); photographic paper (D4); pens, markers, inks, brushes and nibs whose maker names drawing, comics or painting only, coloured pencils, sketchbooks, blank scrapbook albums, washi tape, origami paper, decorative stamps and their ink pads, and craft and model glue (C4); written diaries, filled notebooks, kept letters, used greeting cards and picture postcards (F5); collectible sticker series (F3); desks, filing cabinets, shelves, free-standing racks, storage boxes and bookends (B1); lamps (B3); frames and ornaments (B4); parcel tape, bubble wrap, stretch film, void fill, flat cardboard boxes and building adhesives (C8); scissors-class tools for other work (C1); lab scales and science rulers (C6); navigation compasses (E2). Structure (Candidate A, decided, C-8; memo sections 3, 4b, 10 and 14): sub-family by the role the object plays in writing and desk work (C5.1 to C5.4), then the closing parts sub-family C5.5 (C5(d), CV-17; leaf axis: object served); then leaf by the axis each sub-family states (marking or unmarking mechanism; what the sheets are made to be; the job done at the desk; what the device does; object served); then kind (the generic type a person owns; calculators by capability class). Every sub-family has leaves, so every kind id has four parts. Each object gets one kind. Filling system, nib size, shape and material, body material, converter included, capped or retractable, tip size and type, ink colour and properties, lead grade and diameter, `paper_size` with its standard (ISO 216, JIS), paper weight, `paper_stock` (a lines row, section 14), ruling, binding, cover type, planner layout, dated or undated, calendar `format`, `drive`, `connection`, `recording` (digital pen), `reusable` (reusable notebook), cut type, sheet capacity, board mount (C12), `production_status`, `decoration_technique`, `pen_size`, brand, era, material, region, scene, user, setting, market, edition and price band are attributes, tags, tier entries, variants or views, never kinds. Retailer collections (Luxury, Japanese, Demonstrator, Pocket, Vintage-inspired, Maki-e) and shop experience tiers are platform_tags, tags or attributes, never kinds; a limited or special edition is a variant (C3). Models and lines (LAMY safari, Hobonichi Techo, TRAVELER'S notebook, Pilot Iroshizuku, soroban as the one non-maker named form) are named forms, not kinds. Maker role (maker_roles.tsv, provisional): owner for the category (stationery models carry the maker); way_in for fountain pens, fountain-pen ink, notebooks, planners and paper stocks (R-116 c, DPH_C, R-119), scoped to fountain pen C5.1.1.01 and dip pen C5.1.1.02, bottled ink C5.5.1.01 and cartridges C5.5.1.02, notebook C5.2.1.01, composition book C5.2.1.02, refillable notebook system C5.2.1.03, diary / planner C5.2.2.01 and the planner and notebook parts C5.5.5.01 to C5.5.5.03; paper stocks are lines rows, not kinds; country never divides the ledger. Pending kinds: the memo marks 21 kinds † (no fetched page behind them; section 10 attestation list) and section 14 adds one, label tape cassette, provisional until attested, also marked †; their ids are allocated here in memo order, each line ending in †, and a † kind enters the data only when a fetched page attests it. Leaf C5.1.4 and leaves C5.5.4, C5.5.6 and C5.5.7 hold only † kinds and are provisional with them. Provisional levels (C11, C10): the sticky-note calendar (himekuri) C5.2.2.03 and name seal (hanko) C5.3.3.07 become non-maker named forms under calendar and rubber stamp if no fetched seller in two regions gives each its own category; the digital pen and the reusable notebook stay attributes unless a fetched comparison ranks them apart. Display groups (navigation proposal, display only, never moving a home; no group ids issued): Pens (C5.1.1 to C5.1.4); Pencils, erasers and correction (C5.1.5, C5.1.6); Ink, refills and pen parts (C5.5.1 to C5.5.4); Notebooks and journals (C5.2.1, C5.5.5); Diaries, planners and calendars (C5.2.2); Paper, cards and post (C5.2.3, C5.2.4); Desk, filing and office supplies (C5.3, C5.5.6); Calculators and office machines (C5.4; C5.5.7 For label makers is placed here by this tree, since the proposal predates the leaf). Quality markers (display, judged on the named form or line, never on the kind): fountain pens by a nib that writes at once with steady flow, nib material (steel, then 14k, then 18k gold), nib widths and shapes offered, filling system and capacity (cartridge or converter, then piston or vacuum), body material and craft (resin, ebonite or celluloid, hallmarked silver, urushi, maki-e), weight and balance, and long production history; paper by coated or calendered surface, resistance to bleed-through and show-through, dry time, shading and sheen shown, weight in gsm, and a binding that lies flat (sewn, then glued, then wire); ink by saturation, shading, sheen, flow and water resistance; calculators by capability class (basic, printing, scientific, graphing); shredders by cut type (strip, cross, micro); desk and filing items by build and preference only.

Ids (R-58): C5.n is a sub-family and C5.n.m a leaf; a kind is C5.n.m.kk, or C5.n.kk where a sub-family has no leaf.

## C5.1 Writing, marking & correcting instruments
Leaf axis: the marking or unmarking mechanism (liquid ink through a nib; viscous or gel ink through a ball; ink through a fibre, felt or brush tip; ink carried from a separate stone or well by a hair brush; solid core; removing, covering or sharpening). Kinds in order of increasing specialisation. Pens, nibs, brushes and inks are filed by the maker's first-named use (K-170): handwriting, lettering, calligraphy and shodo, or both writing and drawing, C5; drawing, comics or painting only, C4.

### C5.1.1 Nib pens
Liquid ink through a nib. Display group: Pens.
- C5.1.1.01 Fountain pen [万年筆]: pen that feeds liquid ink from a reservoir to a split nib; filling system, nib size, shape and material and body material are attributes.
- C5.1.1.02 Dip pen (nib holder and nibs): holder with loose nibs dipped in a separate bottle of ink; a dip pen or nib whose maker names comics or drawing only is C4.

### C5.1.2 Ball and gel pens
Viscous or gel ink through a ball. Display group: Pens.
- C5.1.2.01 Ballpoint pen [ボールペン]: pen that rolls viscous oil-based ink onto paper through a ball tip; a digital pen that writes in ink and records it carries `recording`.
- C5.1.2.02 Gel pen: ball-tip pen with water-based gel ink.
- C5.1.2.03 Rollerball pen: ball-tip pen with liquid water-based ink; a recording digital pen of this type carries `recording`.
- C5.1.2.04 Multi pen: one barrel holding several ball or gel refills (and sometimes a pencil) selected in turn.

### C5.1.3 Porous-tip pens and markers
Ink through a fibre, felt or brush tip. Display group: Pens.
- C5.1.3.01 Felt-tip pen (fineliner): fine fibre-tip pen made for writing, or for writing and drawing both.
- C5.1.3.02 Marker pen: broad fibre or felt-tip pen for marking and labelling.
- C5.1.3.03 Highlighter [蛍光ペン]: broad-tip pen with translucent fluorescent ink for marking text.
- C5.1.3.04 Brush pen: pen with a flexible brush tip whose maker names handwriting, lettering or calligraphy first or both (a fudepen is here); drawing-only brush pens are C4.

### C5.1.4 Calligraphy brush & ink
Ink ground or poured from a separate stone or well and carried by a hair brush; shodo is C5 (K-170). Display group: Pens.
- C5.1.4.01 Calligraphy brush (shodo brush): hair brush made for East Asian calligraphy. †
- C5.1.4.02 Ink stick: solid stick of soot and binder ground on an inkstone to make calligraphy ink. †
- C5.1.4.03 Inkstone: stone slab with a well on which an ink stick is ground with water. †
- C5.1.4.04 Calligraphy set: brush, ink and inkstone sold and compared as one kit (S3a). †

### C5.1.5 Pencils
Solid core, graphite; wooden then mechanical. Lead grade and diameter are attributes. Display group: Pencils, erasers and correction.
- C5.1.5.01 Wooden pencil (wood-cased): graphite core cased in wood, sharpened to use.
- C5.1.5.02 Mechanical pencil [シャープペンシル]: refillable holder that advances a thin lead by a mechanism.

### C5.1.6 Erasers, correction & sharpeners
Removing, covering or sharpening: undoes or maintains a mark; fitted to no single object, so not in C5.5. Display group: Pencils, erasers and correction.
- C5.1.6.01 Eraser: block of rubber or plastic that rubs out pencil or ink marks.
- C5.1.6.02 Correction tape: dispenser that lays a dry white film over a written mark. †
- C5.1.6.03 Correction fluid: quick-drying white liquid brushed or penned over a written mark. †
- C5.1.6.04 Pencil sharpener: device that sharpens a wooden pencil; `drive` (manual, electric) is an attribute of the one kind.

## C5.2 Paper goods
Leaf axis: what the sheets are made to be (a bound book for notes; a time-ruled record; a loose sheet, a pad or a cover for posting sheets; a printed or decorated sheet). Tie-break: a bound book ruled by date is a diary or planner (it keeps time). Kinds simplest binding or form first. A blank paper good is C5; once written in it is a record in F5.

### C5.2.1 Bound books
A bound book for notes: sewn, glued, spiral or ring-bound. Display group: Notebooks and journals.
- C5.2.1.01 Notebook: bound book of blank, lined, dot or graph sheets for notes ("journal" is an alias); size, ruling, binding, paper stock and `reusable` are attributes.
- C5.2.1.02 Composition book: sewn notebook with a stiff marbled-style board cover and lined pages.
- C5.2.1.03 Refillable notebook system: cover plus refill books sold together as one system (TRAVELER'S notebook is a named form); covers and refills sold separately are C5.5.5.

### C5.2.2 Diaries, planners & calendars
A time-ruled record. Display group: Diaries, planners and calendars.
- C5.2.2.01 Diary / planner [手帳, techo]: bound book ruled by date or for dates (daily, weekly, monthly), dated or undated; Hobonichi Techo is a named form.
- C5.2.2.02 Calendar: printed sheets of the days, weeks or months of a year; `format` (wall, desk, pocket) is an attribute.
- C5.2.2.03 Sticky-note calendar [ひめくり, himekuri]: tear-off or sticky-note day calendar, one sheet per day (level provisional, C11).

### C5.2.3 Loose paper, pads & envelopes
A loose sheet, a pad, or a cover for posting sheets (envelope or tube). Display group: Paper, cards and post.
- C5.2.3.01 Loose-leaf paper: punched loose sheets made for a binder.
- C5.2.3.02 Memo pad: glued or bound pad of tear-off sheets for short notes.
- C5.2.3.03 Sticky notes: pad of small sheets with a repositionable adhesive strip ("adhesive notes" in Australia).
- C5.2.3.04 Letter-writing paper set: writing sheets sold with matching envelopes for letters ("letter set").
- C5.2.3.05 Copy and printer paper: plain cut sheets sold by the ream for copiers and printers (K-158; photographic paper is D4). †
- C5.2.3.06 Envelope: folded paper cover for posting a letter or document (K-193).
- C5.2.3.07 Padded envelope: envelope lined with bubble or paper padding for posting small items (K-193). †
- C5.2.3.08 Mailing tube: rigid tube for posting rolled documents (K-193). †

### C5.2.4 Printed & decorated paper
A sheet printed or decorated at the factory; defined by form, not by occasion. Display group: Paper, cards and post.
- C5.2.4.01 Greeting card: folded printed card with envelope, sold blank inside or with a message, to be written and sent. †
- C5.2.4.02 Gift wrap: printed or decorated paper sold for wrapping presents. †
- C5.2.4.03 Sticker sheet: sheet of printed self-adhesive stickers sold as stationery (a collectible sticker series is F3).

## C5.3 Desk accessories & filing
Leaf axis: the job done at the desk (hold things, display on a board, handle sheets, fasten or stick, file, measure). Kinds commonest desk use first. Display group: Desk, filing and office supplies.

### C5.3.1 Desk organisers
Hold things on the desk; desk-top trays and holders are C5, free-standing racks B1 (C-17).
- C5.3.1.01 Pen cup: open pot that stands pens and pencils upright on a desk.
- C5.3.1.02 Pencil case: pouch, box or roll that holds several pens and pencils (K-132; a one-pen sleeve is C5.5.4.01).
- C5.3.1.03 Document tray (letter tray): flat open tray, often stackable, that holds papers on a desk.
- C5.3.1.04 Magazine holder (desktop magazine file): upright open-sided box that holds magazines or files on a desk or shelf.
- C5.3.1.05 Desk pad / blotter: large mat or blotting pad laid on the desk top to write on.

### C5.3.2 Boards
Display or write on a board; mount (wall, stand) is an installation attribute (C12).
- C5.3.2.01 Noticeboard: cork or fabric board to which notices are pinned.
- C5.3.2.02 Whiteboard: smooth board written on with dry-erase markers. †

### C5.3.3 Paper-handling tools
Act on sheets.
- C5.3.3.01 Stapler [ホチキス]: device that drives a wire staple through sheets to join them; `drive` is an attribute.
- C5.3.3.02 Hole punch: device that cuts filing holes in sheets.
- C5.3.3.03 Paper trimmer: guillotine or rotary cutter that trims sheets straight.
- C5.3.3.04 Scissors (household and office): general-purpose scissors for paper and household use (K-171).
- C5.3.3.05 Rubber stamp (office): hand stamp with a rubber die for dates, words or marks on office papers (decorative stamps are C4).
- C5.3.3.06 Stamp ink pad (office): inked pad for office stamps (ink pads for decorative stamps are C4).
- C5.3.3.07 Name seal [印鑑, hanko]: personal seal stamped as a signature (region Japan; level provisional, C11).

### C5.3.4 Fastening & sticking paper
Join, clip or stick sheets.
- C5.3.4.01 Paper clip: bent wire loop that clips sheets together. †
- C5.3.4.02 Bulldog clip: sprung metal clip that grips a thick stack of sheets. †
- C5.3.4.03 Drawing pin: short pin with a flat head pressed into a board to hold paper. †
- C5.3.4.04 Glue stick: solid glue in a twist-up tube for sticking paper (K-171). †
- C5.3.4.05 Desk tape (sticky tape): narrow transparent adhesive tape for paper and desk use (K-171). †
- C5.3.4.06 Tape dispenser: weighted holder with a cutting edge for desk tape.

### C5.3.5 Filing
Hold sheets together or apart (step 31 filing supplies, kept in C5).
- C5.3.5.01 Ring binder: stiff cover with a ring mechanism that holds punched sheets.
- C5.3.5.02 Lever arch file: binder with a lever-operated two-ring arch mechanism for large volumes of sheets.
- C5.3.5.03 Box file: lidded box-shaped file with a spring clip that holds loose papers.

### C5.3.6 Measuring and geometry
Size or weight of paper work.
- C5.3.6.01 Ruler: straight strip with a graduated scale for measuring and ruling lines.
- C5.3.6.02 Geometry set (compasses, protractor): case of drawing compass, protractor, set squares and ruler sold as one set.
- C5.3.6.03 Drawing compass: two-legged instrument that draws circles and arcs (a navigation compass is E2).
- C5.3.6.04 Postal scale: small scale for weighing letters and parcels for postage (K-167). †

## C5.4 Calculators & office machines
Leaf axis: what the device does (calculate; act on paper or labels). Kinds by spec ladder, then historical forms. Display group: Calculators and office machines.

### C5.4.1 Calculators
Calculating devices, class by capability; the class is the kind.
- C5.4.1.01 Basic desktop calculator: calculator for the four operations and percentages, with a display.
- C5.4.1.02 Printing calculator: desktop calculator that prints its figures on a paper roll.
- C5.4.1.03 Scientific calculator: calculator with trigonometric, logarithmic and statistical functions.
- C5.4.1.04 Graphing calculator: programmable calculator that plots functions on a graphic display.
- C5.4.1.05 Financial calculator: calculator with time-value-of-money and other finance functions.
- C5.4.1.06 Abacus [そろばん, soroban]: frame of beads on rods moved by hand to calculate (soroban is a non-maker named form).
- C5.4.1.07 Slide rule: pair of sliding logarithmic scales for multiplying and dividing (C-17). †

### C5.4.2 Document machines
Machines that act on paper or labels; C5 whether connected or not (step 31).
- C5.4.2.01 Shredder: machine that cuts paper into strips or particles; cut type is an attribute.
- C5.4.2.02 Laminator: machine that seals a sheet between plastic films by heat or pressure.
- C5.4.2.03 Binding machine: machine that punches and binds sheets with combs, wires or covers.
- C5.4.2.04 Label maker (label printer of any connection): device that prints adhesive labels on tape; `connection` (standalone, computer, phone) is an attribute (K-158).
- C5.4.2.05 Typewriter: machine that prints characters on paper as keys are struck; `drive` (manual, electric) is an attribute.

## C5.5 Parts and accessories made for one object
Leaf axis: object served (rule S3; consumables used up in one object, such as ink, refills and lead, are parts). Exempt from the sub-family axis (C5(d), CV-17). Kinds: the parts of the commonest object served first.

### C5.5.1 For fountain pens
Display group: Ink, refills and pen parts.
- C5.5.1.01 Bottled fountain-pen ink: bottled ink made for fountain pens; dye or pigment, wetness, saturation, shading, sheen, water resistance and colour are attributes.
- C5.5.1.02 Ink cartridge: sealed ink cartridge that fits a fountain pen's cartridge standard.
- C5.5.1.03 Converter: refillable piston or squeeze reservoir that fits in place of a cartridge.
- C5.5.1.04 Replacement nib: nib or nib unit sold to fit a fountain pen.

### C5.5.2 For ball, gel and rollerball pens
Display group: Ink, refills and pen parts.
- C5.5.2.01 Pen refill: ink refill made to fit a ballpoint, gel, rollerball or multi pen.

### C5.5.3 For mechanical pencils
Display group: Ink, refills and pen parts.
- C5.5.3.01 Pencil lead refill: tube of leads made for mechanical pencils; grade and diameter are attributes.

### C5.5.4 For one pen at a time
Display group: Ink, refills and pen parts.
- C5.5.4.01 Single-pen sleeve: sleeve or case that holds one pen (K-132). †

### C5.5.5 For planners and refillable notebooks
Display group: Notebooks and journals.
- C5.5.5.01 Planner or notebook cover: cover made for one planner or notebook system, sold separately.
- C5.5.5.02 Refill notebook: refill book made for one cover or notebook system.
- C5.5.5.03 Index tabs (for a planner system): tabs made to fit one planner system.

### C5.5.6 For staplers
Display group: Desk, filing and office supplies.
- C5.5.6.01 Staple: wire staples in strips made to fit a stapler. †

### C5.5.7 For label makers
Added by section 14 (DPH). Display group: Calculators and office machines (assigned by this tree).
- C5.5.7.01 Label tape cassette: tape cartridge made to fit one label-maker system (Brother TZe, DYMO D1, DYMO LetraTag and Epson LK are C13 cartridge-standard rows; provisional until attested). †

## Precedence inside C5

- Bound book against diary (tie-break): a bound book ruled by date is a diary / planner C5.2.2.01 (designed function: it keeps time); an undated bound book is a notebook C5.2.1.01. The himekuri sticky-note calendar is C5.2.2.03 by designed function, not sticky notes C5.2.3.03; sticky notes are loose paper only, and the calendar C5.2.2.02 has one home with `format` as an attribute.
- System against parts (S3): a refillable notebook system as sold (cover plus refills) is C5.2.1.03; a cover, refill book or index tab sold separately for one planner or notebook system is C5.5.5.01 to C5.5.5.03. Hobonichi Techo is a named form of C5.2.2.01 and TRAVELER'S notebook of C5.2.1.03 (C-17).
- Cases: a sleeve or case made for one pen is part of the pen, C5.5.4.01; a case for the small items of one task follows the task, so a pencil case holding several pens is C5.3.1.02 (K-132); a toiletry or make-up bag is H1 and a tool bag C1.
- Erasers, correction tape, correction fluid and sharpeners (C5.1.6.01 to C5.1.6.04) are fitted to no single object, so they are kinds in C5.1, not parts in C5.5. Staples follow the stapler (C5.5.6.01); label tape follows the label maker (C5.5.7.01); fountain-pen ink, cartridges, converters and nibs follow the pen (C5.5.1); refills and leads follow their pen or pencil (C5.5.2.01, C5.5.3.01). Printer ink is D1.
- Writing against drawing (P1, K-170): a pen, nib, brush or ink is filed by the maker's first-named use: handwriting, lettering, calligraphy and shodo C5, including shodo brushes, ink sticks and inkstones (C5.1.4.01 to C5.1.4.04); drawing, illustration, comics and painting C4; both named, C5. So a brush pen C5.1.3.04, dip pen C5.1.1.02 or fineliner C5.1.3.01 is C5 only where its maker names writing first or both; a fudepen is C5.1.3.04; a maker that names no use leaves the product unplaced until a page is found. Fountain-pen ink is C5.5.1.01; drawing ink and sketchbooks are C4. Washi tape, origami paper and blank scrapbook albums are C4.
- Machines and devices (P2, K-158, C5-O1, K-156): a machine that mainly prints from or scans to a computer is D1; a shredder, laminator, binding machine, typewriter or calculator is C5 whether it has an app or not (C5.4); label makers and label printers of any connection are C5.4.2.04; copy and printer paper is C5.2.3.05 and a reusable paper notebook is C5.2.1.01 with `reusable`; a cash register is D1; an electronic organiser, e-ink tablet or electronic writing tablet is D2. A digital pen that writes in ink on paper and records it is C5.1.2.01 or C5.1.2.03 with `recording`; a stylus made for one tablet follows the tablet (S3); a universal capacitive stylus is unplaced (the placer asks). Photographic paper is D4.
- Blank against written (P3): a blank notebook, diary, calendar or greeting card is C5 (C5.2.1.01, C5.2.2.01, C5.2.2.02, C5.2.4.01); once written in, it is a personal record in F5; picture postcards, used or not, are F5.
- Stickers (P3b, K-121): a sticker sheet sold as stationery is C5.2.4.03; a collectible sticker series (Panini type) is F3.
- Furniture and holders (P4, C-17, K-191): furniture, cabinets and racks that stand in a room are B1 whatever they hold; desk-top trays and holders (pen cup C5.3.1.01, document tray C5.3.1.03, desktop magazine file C5.3.1.04) are C5 and free-standing racks B1; bookends are B1; a lamp is B3; a frame or ornament B4. A board made to write on or pin to is C5.3.2.01 or C5.3.2.02 whether it hangs or stands (mount is an attribute, C12). Ring binders, lever arch files and box files (C5.3.5.01 to C5.3.5.03) are filing supplies named in step 31 and stay C5; the S3b holder sentence (K-9) is read as covering albums and collection holders (open conflict, memo section 10 risk 1).
- Cutting tools, adhesives and stamps (P5, K-171): filed by the material or work they are made for: paper and desk work C5; fabric, yarn, leather, beads, clay, models, paper craft and picture-making C4; food B5; hair and body H1; plants C2; wood, metal, stone, building materials and electronics C1 or C8. Stapler C5.3.3.01, hole punch C5.3.3.02, trimmer C5.3.3.03, correction tape C5.1.6.02, glue stick C5.3.4.04, desk tape C5.3.4.05, tape dispenser C5.3.4.06 and general household scissors C5.3.3.04 are C5; office stamps C5.3.3.05 and their ink pads C5.3.3.06 are C5, decorative stamps and their pads C4; craft and model glue C4; building and repair fasteners and adhesives C8; a craft knife follows the work it is made for.
- Post and packing (P6, K-193): envelopes C5.2.3.06, padded envelopes C5.2.3.07 and mailing tubes C5.2.3.08 for letters and post are C5; parcel tape, bubble wrap, stretch film, void fill and cardboard boxes sold flat for packing or moving are C8; boxes made for storing things are B1.
- Measuring (P8, step 23 note, K-167, C6-O2): a ruler C5.3.6.01 and a postal scale C5.3.6.04 are C5; lab scales and rulers made for science are C6; a scale goes with its task (kitchen B5, bathroom H1, luggage A4); a drawing compass is C5.3.6.03 and a compass for navigation E2. Abacus C5.4.1.06 and slide rule C5.4.1.07 are C5 (C-17).
- Kits and sets (S3a, K-1): a set of like items is filed as the item; an unlike set by its item of greatest value; a kit sold and compared as one object is a kind (calligraphy set C5.1.4.04; geometry set C5.3.6.02).
- Multi-use check (C14): no kind is used in two leaves.
