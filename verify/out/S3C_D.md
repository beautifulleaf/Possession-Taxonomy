# S3C_D source census, domain D (Electronics)

One line per category: anchor source to harvest first, then gaps no catalogue covers. Rows are in S3C_D.tsv (99 rows). Fetch notes: TechPowerUp and HiFi Wiki could not be fetched (bot check; HTTP 526), so their entries rest on search excerpts and are marked "search only".

- **D1 Computers & Components** (22 rows). Anchor: Wikipedia "List of computer hardware manufacturers" (about 800 entries by part type) for the maker ledger, then Deskthority wiki (1,052 keyboards, 261 brand and 52 OEM categories, 94 switch brands), CPU-World chart (6000+ CPUs) and Kakaku/Tsukumo/ZOL for chipset, socket and Chinese-market makers. Gaps: cables and adapters, UPS, point-of-sale terminals, card readers, hardware wallets and security keys have no maker catalogue; monitors and mice rest on retailer trees only; GPU/SSD databases (TechPowerUp) unconfirmed; no line-level catalogue for printers or networking beyond maker lists.
- **D2 Phones, Tablets & Personal Devices** (17 rows). Anchor: GSMArena brand index (126 brands with device counts), then PhoneArena brands (adds Palm, Handspring, Danger and US carriers), Garmin catalogue for wearables, GPS and satellite messengers, MobileRead matrix and Wikipedia Kindle page for e-readers. Gaps: two-way radios, pagers, power banks, Bluetooth item trackers (AirTag, Tile), electronic dictionaries and translators, children's tablets and smart rings have no dedicated catalogue; tablets and smartwatches rely on retailer trees and PhoneArena/Geizhals.
- **D3 Audio & Video** (20 rows). Anchor: Radiomuseum.org (historic radios, radiograms, early TVs) and HiFi.fan (2,055 brands, 61 countries) for makers, Stereophile Recommended Components for tiers, ProjectorCentral for projectors. Gaps: HiFi Wiki (about 35,000 devices) not confirmed; no fetched catalogue for turntables or tonearms (Vinyl Engine seen only in search), AV receivers and soundbars, karaoke machines, smart-home hubs, or a general headphone database (Crinacle, AudioReviews, Headphone List cover IEMs and earphones only); TV models only via FlatpanelsHD (8 brands).
- **D4 Cameras & Photography** (21 rows). Anchor: Camera-wiki.org List of companies (about 1,000 companies) plus Lens makers category (86 pages) for makers; Wikipedia Lens mount (about 120 mounts) and film-format lists for the platform ledger; Wikipedia List of photographic films (41 brands) for film. Gaps: no fetched catalogue of lighting and light modifiers, tripods and heads, darkroom equipment or flash units beyond retailer trees (B&H, Foto-Erhardt); filmtypes.com film database seen only in search; instant film and disposable cameras covered only by retail pages; drones for filming rely on Kakaku.
- **D5 Video Games** (19 rows). Anchor: MobyGames platform list (347 platforms, 337,500 games) and IGDB API (platform families, generations) for the platform and game side; Wikipedia List of home video game consoles (1,000+) and handhelds for hardware; PriceCharting and ConsoleVariations for variants. Gaps: no catalogue of licensed or third-party accessory makers (HORI, PowerA, 8BitDo, Scuf); game-key cards and cartridge or disc formats only in narrative pages; Sega Retro, Nintendo and gametechwiki hardware pages were blocked on fetch; per-platform game lists exist (Wikipedia Lists of video games index) but were not counted; VR headsets rest on Reality Atlas (152 devices).

No category lacks a usable catalogue. The thinnest are D2 (two-way radios, power banks, trackers) and the D1 cables/security-key leaves.
