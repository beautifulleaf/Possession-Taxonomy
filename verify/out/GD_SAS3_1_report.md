- Not found: 51 brands (verify/out/GD_SAS3_1_notfound.tsv).
- Fetch log: verify/work/GD_SAS3_1/fetched.tsv. Every source URL in the rows is logged full or partial.
- Best attestations: brand shop pages (Ismail Farid, Hummingbirds, Bacha Party, Chinyere, Coco), the Zubaidas Bambies page, retailer pages (Nainpreet for Faiza Saqlain, Rania Zara for Farah Talib Aziz) and the Apparelsearch manufacturer directory (9 knitwear and garment makers).

- Failures: Newsline, Tribune, ProPakistani and Fibre2fashion returned 403 or 405 and British Council returned 503. Parallel tools were not used.
