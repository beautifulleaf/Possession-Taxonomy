# HV_F5_1 report (F5.1, F5.2, F5.3)

**Kinds:** 66 of 67 attested (verify/out/HV_F5_1_kinds.tsv). **Not attested:** F5.3.3.03 Home audio recording. I ran four searches, and fetched Etsy "old tape recording" and the Etsy home-recorded VHS page. They showed only blank or commercial tapes and recorders. Partial evidence only (no full page) for: divorce papers, cheque, postal order, certificate (achievement), ATM card, filled notebook, home videotape.

**Named forms:** 267 (HV_F5_1_named_forms.tsv): 254 transit farecards from the Wikipedia list, and 13 others (Taufschein, Geburts- und Taufschein, Marriage Fraktur, Indenture, Ausweis, Reward of merit card, Certificat d'études primaires, real photo postcard, carte de visite, Stevens bookmark, a Phoenix Stevensgraph book mark, Dollar Rewards Club card, Cingular SIM). **Makers:** 215 new (HV_F5_1_makers.tsv), mostly transit providers. No lines (lines.tsv has a header only). PRADO issuer designs were not recorded, since the tree puts them in the issuer-design register (F5-O1).

**Catalogues:** PRADO and the EU licence-model register served kinds F5.1.1.01 to .05 well (PRADO via Parallel after WebFetch 403). The Wikipedia transit list was the main source of named forms (the last 49,000 characters were not read). Colnect gave a category menu only. S2 album rows (Hakuba, Goldbuch, Walther, Gaylord) cover blank albums (B1/C4), so I did not use them. Fetchable sources were eBay and Etsy category pages and listings. plusmodels, napoleonexhibit and ABAA returned 403.
