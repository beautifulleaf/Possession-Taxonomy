# G1 Vehicles: decided tree

Category: G1 Vehicles (domain G, Vehicles & Property). Membership test (step 11): made mainly to transport people, animals or goods from place to place on land, water, rail, snow or air, however moved (engine, motor, muscle, wind, animal or tow), with its fitted parts and electronics (S3). Structure (Candidate A, G-1): sub-family (mode of travel: G1.1 Land, G1.2 Rail, G1.3 Watercraft, G1.4 Aircraft; G1.5 Parts and accessories made for one vehicle is the parts sub-family, exempt from the axis, C5(d), CV-17), then leaf (one stated axis per sub-family; Rail has no leaf, C5(b)), then kind (body style, discipline, class or type, the level where buyers compare quality or makers), then named form (the model line or class design, one level only, maker-bound or not, uncapped; not listed here). Legal and registration classes (kei, UNECE and EU L, M, N, O and O1 to O4, EPAC, speed pedelec, US e-bike classes 1 to 3, US RV Class A, B and C, FAA certification categories, light-sport and experimental) are C17 jurisdiction-ledger rows and never kinds (G-3, G-12); design standards (ISO 4210 and 8098, EN 15194, CE design category A to D) are `standard_met`. These are attributes and never kinds or leaves: `propulsion` (with a primary flag), `assist`, `rig`, `hull_count`, `body_substyle` (roadster, targa, crossover, coupé SUV, shooting brake, coupé utility; G-5), `geometry` (road and gravel gradients; G-7), `construction` (inflatable or rigid; G-8), `carrycot` (P14), `size_class` with its scheme (Autohome, EC segment, GB/T), `length_band`, seats and mass, user (child, adapted), region, era, price band, maker and scene; frame size is a size variant. Generation, model year, trim and engine are variants (G1-O1). Maker role: owner (maker_roles.tsv, provisional): makes sit on their models, with a make tab beside the type tree and no standalone maker list. Sub-family and leaf names are the memo's; the display groups of the navigation proposal (proposals.tsv level1_groups, memo section 14) are shown on each leaf as display only and never change a home or an id. Kinds are numbered in the memo's order: first the kinds of the worked tree (memo numbers 1 to 70), then the kinds the memo names in each leaf's "Not shown" list, in that list's order. A kind marked † is provisional and needs a fetched page before it enters the data (memo section 10 attestation list, section 14); the level of every regional-word kind is provisional until attested (R-19). The light-sport airplane has no id: it goes to the harvest as a "missing kind?" (section 14; R-96 c).

Ids (R-58): G1.n is a sub-family and G1.n.m a leaf; a kind is G1.n.m.kk, or G1.n.kk where a sub-family has no leaf.

## G1.1 Land vehicles

Leaf axis: the kind of vehicle by body and drive, as it is sold; the legal registration class, mass band and seat count are never the axis (C6, C17).

### G1.1.1 Cars
Kind axis: body style, in UNECE bodywork-code order AA to AF, then others. Display group: Cars.
- G1.1.1.01 Saloon (sedan): passenger car with a separate boot behind a fixed roof over two rows of seats.
- G1.1.1.02 Hatchback: passenger car whose rear door is a top-hinged tailgate opening onto the load space.
- G1.1.1.03 Estate (station wagon): passenger car with the roof carried back to the rear and a tailgate over a long load space.
- G1.1.1.04 Coupé: closed passenger car with a fixed roof, two doors and a sloping rear.
- G1.1.1.05 Convertible (cabriolet): passenger car whose roof folds or comes off.
- G1.1.1.06 SUV: passenger car with a tall body, raised ground clearance and often four-wheel drive.
- G1.1.1.07 MPV (minivan): passenger car with a tall one-box body and up to three rows of seats where the load space would be.
- G1.1.1.08 Microcar (quadricycle): light enclosed four-wheeled car below the ordinary car class, sold as its own category. †
- G1.1.1.09 Limousine: long-wheelbase chauffeur-driven car with a partition or extended rear passenger compartment.

### G1.1.2 Vans, trucks and buses
Kind axis: body; gross mass and seat count are attributes, and the UNECE class is a C17 row (G-9). Display group: Vans, trucks and buses.
- G1.1.2.01 Panel van: goods vehicle with an enclosed windowless load box behind the cab.
- G1.1.2.02 Window van (kombi): van with side windows and seats or space for passengers and goods. †
- G1.1.2.03 Pickup truck: light goods vehicle with a cab and an open load bed.
- G1.1.2.04 Chassis-cab with fitted body (rigid truck): truck built on one chassis with its body fitted (box, dropside, tipper).
- G1.1.2.05 Tractor unit: truck made to tow a semi-trailer on a fifth-wheel coupling.
- G1.1.2.06 Minibus: passenger vehicle with four or more rows of seats, below bus size.
- G1.1.2.07 Bus / coach: large passenger vehicle for many seated or standing passengers.
- G1.1.2.08 Special-purpose body vehicle: vehicle with a body made for one service, such as a fire engine, ambulance or hearse.

### G1.1.3 Motorcycles and other powered light vehicles
Kind axis: type. Display group: Motorcycles, scooters and powered light rides.
- G1.1.3.01 Standard (naked) motorcycle: general-purpose motorcycle with an upright riding position and little or no fairing.
- G1.1.3.02 Cruiser motorcycle: low-seated motorcycle with feet-forward controls and a laid-back riding position.
- G1.1.3.03 Sport motorcycle: motorcycle built for speed and handling, with a forward-leaning position and full fairing.
- G1.1.3.04 Touring motorcycle: large motorcycle made for long distances, with wind protection, luggage and passenger comfort.
- G1.1.3.05 Adventure / dual-sport motorcycle: motorcycle made for both road and unpaved riding, with long suspension travel.
- G1.1.3.06 Scooter: motor scooter with a step-through frame, floorboard and small wheels.
- G1.1.3.07 Electric kick scooter: stand-up scooter with an electric motor, made to carry a rider from place to place.
- G1.1.3.08 Moped: light low-powered two-wheeler with a small engine or motor and no working pedal drive.
- G1.1.3.09 Off-road motorcycle (enduro, motocross, trials): motorcycle made for off-road riding or off-road sport.
- G1.1.3.10 Trike: three-wheeled motorcycle.
- G1.1.3.11 Motorcycle with sidecar: motorcycle sold with a one-wheeled passenger car attached at the side.
- G1.1.3.12 Mini and pocket bike: scaled-down motorcycle with small wheels and a small engine.
- G1.1.3.13 Electric unicycle: one-wheeled self-balancing electric vehicle ridden standing.
- G1.1.3.14 Hoverboard (self-balancing board): two-wheeled self-balancing electric board ridden standing.
- G1.1.3.15 Powered skateboard: skateboard with an electric motor, made to carry a rider from place to place.

### G1.1.4 Off-road, snow and utility vehicles
Kind axis: terrain and seating. Display group: Off-road, snow and utility vehicles.
- G1.1.4.01 Quad (ATV): four-wheeled straddle-seat all-terrain vehicle with handlebars, off-road or road-registered.
- G1.1.4.02 Snowmobile: tracked vehicle with front skis made to travel over snow.
- G1.1.4.03 UTV (side-by-side): off-road utility vehicle with side-by-side seats and a steering wheel.
- G1.1.4.04 Golf cart: small low-speed vehicle made to carry players and their bags over a golf course.
- G1.1.4.05 Go-kart: small open single-seat four-wheeled vehicle with a low chassis.
- G1.1.4.06 Dune buggy: light open off-road car made for sand and rough ground.

### G1.1.5 Bicycles
Kind axis: discipline or use; a cycle made to be pedalled, with or without electric assist, is filed here by its discipline (P2). Display group: Bicycles and other cycles (with the "Electric bikes" shortcut view on `assist`).
- G1.1.5.01 Road bike: drop-bar bicycle made for speed on paved roads.
- G1.1.5.02 Gravel bike: drop-bar bicycle with wide tyre clearance made for mixed paved and unpaved roads (kept provisionally, section 14).
- G1.1.5.03 Cross-country mountain bike: light mountain bike with short suspension travel made for climbing and fast off-road riding.
- G1.1.5.04 Downhill mountain bike: mountain bike with long-travel full suspension made for steep descents.
- G1.1.5.05 BMX bike: small-wheeled single-speed bicycle made for racing, tricks and park riding.
- G1.1.5.06 City bike: upright everyday bicycle for town riding, usually with mudguards and a chain guard.
- G1.1.5.07 Folding bike: bicycle whose frame folds to a compact size for carrying or storage.
- G1.1.5.08 Mini-velo (small-wheel bicycle): non-folding bicycle with small wheels and a full-size frame fit. †
- G1.1.5.09 Cargo bike: bicycle built to carry loads or passengers on a platform, box or extended rear.
- G1.1.5.10 Trail mountain bike: mid-travel mountain bike made for all-round trail riding.
- G1.1.5.11 Enduro mountain bike: long-travel mountain bike made for timed descents and the climbs between them.
- G1.1.5.12 Dirt-jump mountain bike: small hardtail mountain bike made for jumps and pump tracks.
- G1.1.5.13 Cyclocross bike: drop-bar bicycle made for cyclocross racing on mixed off-road courses.
- G1.1.5.14 Track (fixed-gear) bike: fixed-gear bicycle without brakes made for velodrome racing.
- G1.1.5.15 Time-trial bike: aerodynamic road bicycle with aero bars made for racing against the clock.
- G1.1.5.16 Touring (trekking) bike: sturdy bicycle made to carry luggage on long rides.
- G1.1.5.17 Hybrid (cross) bike: flat-bar bicycle mixing road and mountain-bike features for general riding.
- G1.1.5.18 Fat bike: bicycle with oversized tyres made for snow and sand.

### G1.1.6 Other human-powered cycles
Kind axis: wheel and seat layout. Display group: Bicycles and other cycles.
- G1.1.6.01 Adult tricycle: three-wheeled pedal cycle made for an adult rider.
- G1.1.6.02 Recumbent bicycle: bicycle ridden in a reclined seat with the pedals in front.
- G1.1.6.03 Tandem: bicycle made for two or more riders pedalling one behind the other.

### G1.1.7 Trailers

- G1.1.7.01 Box (cargo) trailer: towed trailer with an open or closed box for goods.
- G1.1.7.02 Boat trailer: towed trailer made to carry, launch and recover a boat.
- G1.1.7.03 Horsebox (horse trailer): towed trailer made to carry horses.
- G1.1.7.04 Car transporter trailer: towed trailer made to carry one or more cars.

### G1.1.8 Caravans, motorhomes and campers
Kind axis: body, and whether towed or driven. Display group: Caravans, motorhomes and trailers.
- G1.1.8.01 Touring caravan (travel trailer): towed living trailer with a fixed body.
- G1.1.8.02 Folding camper (pop-up): towed camper whose walls and roof fold down for travel.
- G1.1.8.03 Fifth-wheel: towed living trailer whose raised front rides over a pickup bed on a fifth-wheel hitch.
- G1.1.8.04 Truck camper: living body made only to be carried in a pickup bed.
- G1.1.8.05 Campervan (van conversion): van converted for living and sleeping, keeping the van body.
- G1.1.8.06 Low-profile motorhome: coachbuilt motorhome with a low roofline and no over-cab bed.
- G1.1.8.07 Overcab (Luton) motorhome: coachbuilt motorhome with a sleeping area over the driving cab.
- G1.1.8.08 A-class (integrated) motorhome: motorhome whose body is built over a bare chassis with its own integrated cab.
- G1.1.8.09 Expandable-end trailer: towed living trailer with fold-out tent ends over a hard body.
- G1.1.8.10 Sport utility RV: towed or driven RV with a built-in garage for carrying vehicles or gear.

### G1.1.9 Animal-drawn vehicles

- G1.1.9.02 Horse-drawn wagon or cart: wheeled goods vehicle drawn by an animal.
- G1.1.9.03 Sleigh: runner-borne vehicle drawn by an animal or machine to carry people or goods over snow (P6).

- G1.1.10.02 Stroller (pushchair): wheeled seat for a baby or child pushed by hand, including a frame sold with carrycot and seat unit (P14). †

## Precedence inside G1

- P1 (powered boards). A board, skate or kick scooter with no motor is E2; a scooter or board with a motor, made to carry a rider from place to place, is G1 in the powered light vehicles leaf: electric kick scooter (G1.1.3.07), electric unicycle (G1.1.3.13), hoverboard (G1.1.3.14), powered skateboard (G1.1.3.15); an unpowered unicycle is E2; an eFoil is G1 in Watercraft by its mode of travel (G1.3.3.14).
- P2 (e-bikes). A cycle made to be pedalled, with or without electric assist of any power, speed or throttle, is filed in the bicycle leaf (G1.1.5) by its discipline, with `assist` as an attribute; its legal class (EPAC, speed pedelec L1e-B, US Class 1, 2 or 3) is a C17 row. A two-wheeler with no working pedal drive is a moped (G1.1.3.08), scooter (G1.1.3.06) or motorcycle.
- P3 (children's cycles). A balance bike or a children's tricycle is E1; a children's pedal bicycle with a working drive is a bicycle in G1.1.5 with `user = child`, never a kind of its own. The 435 mm saddle-height cut applies only to balance bikes and ride-on toys, never to pedal bicycles; an adult tricycle is G1.1.6.01.
- P4 (G2 against G1). Test the object as made: a hull with its own propulsion and steering made to navigate (narrowboat, Dutch barge, cruising or pontoon houseboat: G1.3.3.06), and a trailer, caravan or motorhome made for road towing or driving (G1.1.7, G1.1.8), are G1 however long they stay in one place and whether or not someone lives in them. A floating home or house barge without propulsion, a static caravan, lodge or park home, a park model and a tiny house on wheels are G2.
- P5 (trailers and utility vehicles). A trailer, tipper or wagon made only to carry goods or animals is G1 (G1.1.7, tipper trailer G1.1.7.05, horsebox G1.1.7.03); one that also loads, mixes, spreads or processes what it carries is C7. A quad (G1.1.4.01), side-by-side (G1.1.4.03) or other vehicle made mainly to carry people or goods is G1 even when it powers implements; tool-carrier tractors are C7.
- P6 (sledges). A sledge or sleigh drawn by an animal or machine to carry people or goods is G1 (sleigh, G1.1.9.03); one slid downhill for play or sport is E2.
- P7 (parts). A part, accessory or consumable made for one kind of vehicle follows it (G1.5); a product made for several kinds of object goes by what it does (tools C1, cleaning products B6, fuels C8). Vehicle lights, factory navigation and electronics made to be fitted to a vehicle (car audio, dashcam, mounted sat-nav, bike computer, OBD tracker) are G1.5 parts; a portable sat-nav is D2; a roof box or rack made to fit one vehicle is G1.5 and a free-standing rack is B1; tools made to service a vehicle are C1.
- P8 (worn gear). A helmet, jacket or pair of shoes worn by a rider is filed by step 17, never as a vehicle part (cycling and motorcycle helmets A1, riding boots A2); a bike lock or child car seat made for one vehicle is G1.5.1.
- P9 (models). A scale model, toy vehicle or pedal car is E1; a full-size vehicle kept for display is G1 under its kind, whatever the motive.
- P10 (baby transport). A carrier or sling carried on the body is A4; a wheeled pram (G1.1.10.01) or stroller (G1.1.10.02) is G1; an infant car seat is a G1.5.1 part (S3).
- P11 (racing and off-road sport machines). A motor vehicle made for racing, track or off-road sport (racing car, kart G1.1.4.05, enduro, motocross or trials motorcycle G1.1.3.09, dune buggy G1.1.4.06, drag car) is G1 under the kind of its body; racing is a use, not a form.
- P12 (mobility). A mobility scooter or wheelchair made to compensate for a disability is H2; an e-scooter (G1.1.3.07) or microcar (G1.1.1.08) for general use is G1; a car adapted for a wheelchair user is a car kind with `adapted = yes`.
- P13 (paddle craft and drive). A paddle craft keeps its hull kind (G1.3.1) whatever its drive (paddle, pedal or electric motor); the drive is the attribute `propulsion`.
- P14 (prams and strollers). A frame sold with both a carrycot and a seat unit is a stroller (G1.1.10.02) with `carrycot = yes`; a pram (G1.1.10.01) is a carriage whose only body is a lying carrycot. A wheeled pet stroller is G1 (G1.1.10.05). †
- P15 (vans, MPVs and minibuses). A passenger body with seats where the load space would be is an MPV (G1.1.1.07) when it has at most three rows of seats and a minibus (G1.1.2.06) when it has four or more; seat count itself stays an attribute. † (provisional until attested)
- Motorsailer (G-2). A sailing craft that also cruises under engine is a motorsailer (G1.3.2.06) in the sailing leaf, with `propulsion` = wind + combustion engine and a primary flag; it is never also a motor-craft kind.
- Campervan (C1, CV-3). A van converted for living is a campervan (G1.1.8.05) on function; the base van is recorded as `base_named_form_id` on the conversion's named form, never as a second home under panel van (G1.1.2.01) or window van (G1.1.2.02).
- Legal classes (G-3, G-12). Kei, UNECE and EU L, M, N, O, EPAC, US e-bike, US RV Class A, B, C, FAA and light-sport classes never make a kind: a kei car goes under its body (hatchback G1.1.1.02, panel van G1.1.2.01, pickup G1.1.2.03) and the class is a C17 row. US "Class A" is not the UK A-class motorhome (G1.1.8.08): C9 homonym rows.
- Body sub-styles and gradients (G-5, G-7, G-8). Roadster, targa, crossover, coupé SUV, shooting brake and coupé utility are `body_substyle` on a named form under its body kind; road and gravel race, endurance, everyday and adventure gradients are `geometry` on G1.1.5.01 and G1.1.5.02, while mountain-bike disciplines are kinds (G1.1.5.03, G1.1.5.04, G1.1.5.10 to G1.1.5.12); inflatable or rigid is `construction`, so an inflatable or packraft kayak stays a kayak (G1.3.1.01), and the RIB (G1.3.3.04) stays a kind.
