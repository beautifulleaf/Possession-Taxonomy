# A3 Jewellery: decided tree

Category: A3 Jewellery (domain A, Worn and Carried). Membership test: an ornament worn on the body, hair or clothing: rings, necklaces, bracelets, body jewellery, brooches, pins, badges and patches worn as ornament, hair ornaments, cufflinks, worn amulets; a part made for one jewellery piece follows the piece (S3).

Structure: sub-family (the part of the body or dress the piece is made to adorn; sub-family 3 is the stated exception defined by the fitting, A-13; sub-family 9 is the parts sub-family, C5(d)), then leaf (form family; in sub-family 3 the fitting form; none in the parts sub-family), then kind (the generic type of piece buyers name and compare). Each object gets one kind. Metal, metal colour, fineness, metal build (solid, plated, filled, vermeil), stone, stone origin (natural, cultured, laboratory-grown), carat weight, stone shape, certificate lab, chain pattern (kihei, ball, station and others), gauge, length, ring size, post or fitting type (threaded, threadless, flat-back, L-shape, screw, hook, clip-on), technique (kundan, polki, meenakari, jadau, filigree), motif, era, region and culture, user, occasion (engagement, wedding) and motive (memorial, birthstone, power-stone) are attributes or tags and never kinds or leaves. Fine / demi-fine / fashion is a tier entry on makers, lines and named forms, never on a kind. Sub-family names below are the memo's; the piece-tab display names (DPH flag 2) are shown on each sub-family as display only. A kind marked † is provisional, named only by the memo and needs a fetched page before it enters the data.

Ids (R-58): A3.n is a sub-family and A3.n.m a leaf; a kind is A3.n.m.kk, or A3.n.kk where a sub-family has no leaf.

## A3.1 Head and hair

Leaf axis: ornament family. Display group: Hair and head jewellery.

### A3.1.1 Crowns and tiaras
Ornamental headpieces made to be worn as jewellery.
- A3.1.1.01 Tiara: jewelled ornamental half-circlet worn on the head, open at the back.
- A3.1.1.02 Crown: closed or full-circle ornamental headpiece worn as jewellery, not as regalia of an office.

### A3.1.2 Forehead and parting ornaments
Ornaments worn on the forehead or along the hair parting.
- A3.1.2.01 Maang tikka: ornament hung on a chain along the hair parting with a pendant resting on the forehead.

### A3.1.3 Hair ornaments
Ornaments fixed into the hair by a pin, comb, clip or slide.
- A3.1.3.01 Hairpin: decorative pin or stick pushed into the hair to hold or adorn it (kanzashi-type pins included).
- A3.1.3.02 Decorative hair clip: jewelled or ornamental clip, comb or slide fixed into the hair (a plain functional clip is H1).

## A3.2 Ear

Leaf axis: form family (earrings made for the lobe; cuffs and climbers). Display group: Earrings and ear cuffs.

### A3.2.1 Earrings
Earrings made for the earlobe, by post, hook, hoop or clip.
- A3.2.1.01 Stud earring: earring whose ornament sits on the lobe, held by a post and back.
- A3.2.1.02 Hoop earring: earring formed as a circle or part-circle of metal through the lobe.
- A3.2.1.03 Drop earring: earring whose ornament hangs below the lobe, still or swinging.
- A3.2.1.04 Jhumka: bell- or dome-shaped hanging earring, often with a fringe of small beads.

### A3.2.2 Ear cuffs and climbers
Ear pieces that follow the outer ear rather than hang from the lobe.
- A3.2.2.01 Ear cuff: piece that clips or wraps round the outer ear, usually without a piercing.
- A3.2.2.02 Ear climber: earring that runs up the edge of the ear from a lobe post.

## A3.3 Body and facial piercing jewellery

Leaf axis: fitting form (barbell, ring, stud or labret, navel curve, stretched-ear piece). Holds every ornament made to be worn through a piercing other than an earring made for the earlobe, whoever sells it. Display group: Body jewellery (piercing).

### A3.3.1 Barbells
Bars with a ball or end screwed or pressed on at each end.
- A3.3.1.01 Straight barbell: straight bar with an end at each side, for tongue, cartilage, nipple and similar piercings.
- A3.3.1.02 Curved barbell: bent bar with an end at each side, for brow, rook, daith and similar piercings.

### A3.3.2 Rings and clickers
Ring-shaped piercing jewellery.
- A3.3.2.01 Captive bead ring: ring held closed by a bead or ball captured between its ends by tension.

### A3.3.3 Studs and labrets
Single-ended posts and nose pieces.
- A3.3.3.01 Labret stud: post with a flat disc back inside and a decorative end outside, for lip and cartilage piercings.
- A3.3.3.02 Nose stud (nose pin, nosepin): small stud worn in a nostril piercing on an L-shape, screw, pin or bone post.
- A3.3.3.03 Nose ring: ring or hoop worn through a nostril or septum piercing.

### A3.3.4 Navel jewellery
- A3.3.4.01 Navel curve: curved barbell made for a navel piercing, usually with a larger or dangling lower end.

### A3.3.5 Stretched-ear jewellery
Pieces for stretched lobes, sized by gauge.
- A3.3.5.01 Plug: solid cylinder worn in a stretched piercing (double, single or no flare).
- A3.3.5.02 Tunnel: hollow cylinder worn in a stretched piercing.
- A3.3.5.03 Taper: tapered spike or crescent used to stretch a piercing or worn as ornament.

## A3.4 Neck and chest

Leaf axis: form family. Display group: Necklaces, pendants and chains.

### A3.4.1 Necklaces
Pieces whose ornament is the strand or collar itself.
- A3.4.1.01 Beaded necklace: necklace of beads strung together, of glass, stone, wood or other material.
- A3.4.1.02 Collar necklace: flat, wide necklace that lies round the base of the neck and over the collarbone.
- A3.4.1.03 Pearl necklace: strand of pearls worn as a necklace.
- A3.4.1.04 Choker: necklace that fits closely round the neck.
- A3.4.1.05 Lariat: long necklace without a clasp, worn looped, knotted or threaded through itself.
- A3.4.1.06 Neck ring (torque): rigid ring of metal worn round the neck, open or closed.
- A3.4.1.07 Mangalsutra: marriage necklace of black beads on a gold chain, usually with a pendant.

### A3.4.2 Chains
- A3.4.2.01 Chain: linked metal chain worn on its own as a necklace (the link pattern is an attribute).

### A3.4.3 Pendants and charms
Ornaments hung from a chain, cord, loop or bracelet.
- A3.4.3.01 Pendant: ornament hung from a bail on a chain or cord, sold on its own or with its chain.
- A3.4.3.02 Locket: pendant that opens to hold a picture or keepsake.
- A3.4.3.03 Charm: small ornament made to hang from a bracelet, chain or pendant loop.

## A3.5 Arm and wrist

Leaf axis: form family (flexible or open bracelets; rigid closed or hinged bangles; upper-arm pieces). Display group: Bracelets, bangles and armlets.

### A3.5.1 Bracelets
Flexible or open-ended pieces worn round the wrist.
- A3.5.1.01 Chain bracelet: bracelet of linked chain.
- A3.5.1.02 Tennis bracelet: flexible line of evenly set stones round the wrist.
- A3.5.1.03 Charm bracelet: bracelet made to carry hanging or threaded charms.
- A3.5.1.04 Cuff bracelet: rigid bracelet with an opening, slipped on over the side of the wrist.
- A3.5.1.05 Beaded bracelet: bracelet of strung beads or stones, stretch or clasped (power-stone bracelets included). †

### A3.5.2 Bangles
- A3.5.2.01 Bangle: rigid bracelet, closed or hinged, worn round the wrist (whatever a maker calls it).

### A3.5.3 Armlets
- A3.5.3.01 Armlet: band or ornament worn round the upper arm.

## A3.6 Hand and finger

Leaf axis: form family. Display group: Rings and hand jewellery.

### A3.6.1 Finger rings
- A3.6.1.01 Band ring: ring of uniform band, plain or set, without a raised head (wedding bands included).
- A3.6.1.02 Solitaire ring: ring set with a single prominent stone (engagement solitaires included).
- A3.6.1.03 Signet ring: ring with a flat face for an engraved crest, monogram or seal.
- A3.6.1.04 Stackable ring: slim ring made to be worn in a stack with others.
- A3.6.1.05 Thumb ring: ring made to be worn on the thumb.

### A3.6.2 Hand ornaments
- A3.6.2.01 Hand harness [haath phool]: ornament joining one or more finger rings by chains to a bracelet across the back of the hand. †

## A3.7 Torso, waist and clothing

Leaf axis: form family (pinned ornaments; shirt and tie pieces; chains round the waist). Display group: Brooches, pins and cufflinks; the waist chain is displayed in Anklets, toe rings and body chains, and its home stays here.

### A3.7.1 Brooches and pins
Ornaments pinned to clothing.
- A3.7.1.01 Brooch: decorative ornament fixed to clothing by a hinged pin and catch.
- A3.7.1.02 Lapel pin: small pin with a post and clutch worn in a jacket lapel. †
- A3.7.1.03 Pin badge: small badge, usually enamelled metal, pinned on as ornament (an identity badge is F5). †
- A3.7.1.04 Patch: embroidered or woven patch sewn or ironed on as ornament. †

### A3.7.2 Shirt and tie accessories
- A3.7.2.01 Cufflink: ornament that fastens a shirt cuff through its buttonholes.
- A3.7.2.02 Tie clip: bar or clip that holds a tie to the shirt (tie bars and tie pins included).

### A3.7.3 Waist and body chains
- A3.7.3.01 Waist chain (belly chain): chain or ornament worn round the waist or hips.

## A3.8 Leg and foot

Leaf axis: form family. Display group: Anklets, toe rings and body chains.

### A3.8.1 Anklets and toe rings
- A3.8.1.01 Anklet: chain or band worn round the ankle.
- A3.8.1.02 Toe ring: ring made to be worn on a toe.

## A3.9 Parts and accessories made for one jewellery piece

Exempt from the level-1 axis (C5(d)); object served, no leaf while the sub-family holds 12 kinds or fewer. Each kind enters on one fetched page showing it sold on its own; otherwise the object is a C4 finding. Display group: Parts.

- A3.9.01 Earring back: replacement back or stopper made for an earring post. †
- A3.9.02 Chain extender: short chain with a clasp made to lengthen a necklace or bracelet. †
- A3.9.03 Ring guard or sizer: insert or band fitted to a ring to make it smaller or keep it in place. †

## Precedence inside A3

- Fitting before site. A piece made to be worn through any piercing other than the earlobe is in A3.3 by its fitting, whoever sells it and wherever it is worn; an earring made for the lobe and ear cuffs and climbers are in A3.2. A small ring or stud sold "for the ear" with no non-lobe placement named is an earring (A3.2.1). A jeweller's nose pin and a gauge-sized nose stud are one kind, the nose stud (A3.3.3.02); a nath is a named form under the nose ring (A3.3.3.03). A cartilage barbell is the straight or curved barbell (A3.3.1.01, A3.3.1.02).
- Bangle test. A rigid closed or hinged piece is the bangle (A3.5.2.01); a rigid open-ended piece is the cuff bracelet (A3.5.1.04). A maker's model is classed by this test, not by the maker's word, which is stored as an alias: a bangle sold as a "bracelet" (Cartier Love) is a bangle; kada is a named form under the bangle.
- A pendant sold on its chain is the pendant (A3.4.3.01); necklaces (A3.4.1) are pieces whose ornament is the strand itself. A chain worn on its own is the chain (A3.4.2.01), its link pattern an attribute.
- A charm has one home, the charm (A3.4.3.01's leaf, A3.4.3.03), whatever it hangs from; a charm made for one kind of bracelet stays in A3 under S3; a charm hung on a bag is A4.
- A jewellery set is filed by its item of greatest value, with contents listed and a unit-of-sale attribute (K-1); there is no set kind, and a bridal necklace set is the necklace kind of its main piece.
- Toe ring and thumb ring are filed by site: the toe ring at A3.8.1.02, the thumb ring at A3.6.1.05.
- Waist chain and belly chain are one kind (A3.7.3.01); the waist chain is displayed with anklets and body chains but its home is A3.7.3.
- Engagement and wedding rings are occasion tags on the solitaire ring (A3.6.1.02) and the band ring (A3.6.1.01), never kinds (A-14).
- Material is a kind only where the material is the form: the pearl necklace (A3.4.1.03) and the beaded bracelet (A3.5.1.05); otherwise metal and stone are attributes.
- Regional names. A regional name is a kind only where no generic kind covers its form: maang tikka (A3.1.2.01), jhumka (A3.2.1.04), mangalsutra (A3.4.1.07) and the hand harness [haath phool] (A3.6.2.01, provisional; if the harvest does not show it sold apart it becomes a named form under the bracelets) are kinds. Nath goes under the nose ring (A3.3.3.03), chandbali under the drop earring (A3.2.1.03), kada under the bangle (A3.5.2.01), bajubandh under the armlet (A3.5.3.01), payal under the anklet (A3.8.1.01), kamarbandh under the waist chain (A3.7.3.01), borla and mandoria under the maang tikka (A3.1.2.01), the ucu strand under the beaded necklace (A3.4.1.01) and the Maasai beaded collar under the collar necklace (A3.4.1.02), each as a named form or alias with a region tag. The Zulu love letter has no home until its worn form is sourced.
- Head ornaments (K-129). A head ornament fixed into the hair by a pin, comb, clip or slide is A3.1.3 (hairpin A3.1.3.01, jewelled clip, comb or bow on a clip A3.1.3.02); one held by its own band, crown, elastic or ties (headband, decorative hairband, fascinator on a band) is A1; plain functional clips, ties and bands are H1. A stick-on bindi or temporary jewel is A3; kumkum and skin powders are H1.
- A crown or tiara made to be worn as an ornament is A3.1.1; a crown that is regalia of an office is F3; a toy crown is E1 (K-123). A real ornament in a child's size stays A3, with size and user as attributes (K-130).
- Loose stones and parts. An unset gemstone or loose pearl is F6 when undrilled and loose, a drilled bead C4, strung or mounted for wearing A3 (K-78); a simulant is A3 or C4 by form (K-77). Loose beads, clasps, findings and jewellery-making tools are C4. A replacement part made for one jewellery piece is A3.9 when a fetched page shows it sold on its own, otherwise a C4 finding. The piece tab shows a "Loose stones" cross-link to F6; there is no A3 kind for them.
- Watches (W1, K-20). A watch, watch strap, watch chain, Albert or fob chain made to attach a pocket watch is A5; a decorative fob, seal or charm worn on a chain without a watch is A3 (charm A3.4.3.03 or pendant A3.4.3.01). Smartwatches and smart rings are D2.
- Religious and memorial objects. An amulet, cross, devotional medal with a chain or bail, or cremation or memorial piece made to be worn is A3 under its form kind (usually the pendant A3.4.3.01); a loose devotional medal and a keepsake urn are F4 (F4-O1, K-107). A strand made for counting prayers is F4; a necklace of the same beads made as jewellery is the beaded necklace (A3.4.1.01) (K-111). A luck or protection charm carried, not worn, is F4; worn, A3; issued for money, F3 (K-117). Worn Masonic and fraternal regalia follow A1 and A3; their ritual implements are F4 (K-115).
- Badges, medals, coins. A badge or patch worn as ornament is the pin badge (A3.7.1.03) or patch (A3.7.1.04); an identity or access badge is F5; an awarded or commemorative medal is F3; a medical alert bracelet or tag is H2. A coin or ingot mounted with a bail, loop or bezel to be worn is A3 (pendant A3.4.3.01); without a mount it is F3 (K-122).
- Dress. Cufflinks (A3.7.2.01), tie clips, tie bars and tie pins (A3.7.2.02) are A3; ties and belts are A1, and a belt buckle is part of a belt, A1 (K-21). Tassels and cords worn on a cap or gown are A1. Keyrings and bag charms are A4.
- Adult and medical. Ornamental piercing jewellery, intimate-site barbells included, is A3.3; a collar made only as an ornament is A3 (choker A3.4.1.04 or collar necklace A3.4.1.02); a collar, cuff or harness with fixing points to restrain a person, and objects made for sexual stimulation, are H3 (K-81); surgical implants and medical retainers are H2.
- Storage and display. Jewellery boxes, trays and stands are B1 (K-191); display ornaments and figurines in precious metal are B4.
