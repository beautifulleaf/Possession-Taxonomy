# C3 Plants & Living Cultures: decided tree

Category: C3 Plants & Living Cultures (domain C, grow, make and maintain). Membership test (CATEGORIES_v0_7, placement key step 6): a living plant or fungus, a living culture kept to grow or propagate (sourdough starter, kombucha culture, mushroom spawn), a fresh-cut plant, or a seed, bulb or spore for growing; a plant sold still growing in a pot, tray or mat is C3 even when sold to eat, and a living plant in any container, basket, kokedama or terrarium is C3 whatever the container's value (K-72 to K-75, K-168). Not here: cut herbs, sprouts and microgreens sold to eat (J4); nuts, seeds, sprouting seed and dried yeast sold as food (J5); kefir or kombucha as a drink (J3, Culinary); dried, preserved and artificial plants and flowers, artificial Christmas trees (B4); pots, compost, plant care, grow lights and greenhouses (C2), plant stands (B1); herbarium sheets and pressed plants (F6); tobacco, cannabis and psychoactive plant products (B9); bird seed and hay, aquarium tanks (B10); live animals (OUT). Structure (Candidate A, decided C-3, C-4, C-15; memo sections 3, 4b and 10): sub-family by what is sold (a rooted living plant; a seed, bulb or other unrooted propagule; a fresh-cut stem, tree or arrangement; a living culture or fungus), then leaf by horticultural group as recognised by a register or trade department in C3.1 (an ordered key of 12 written membership tests, the first that fits wins; never a place or a use in the owner's home), propagule type in C3.2, part cut in C3.3 and organism type in C3.4, then kind by accepted botanical or crop name: the genus by default, the crop for edible plants, the species only in the closed list Philodendron, Monstera, Anthurium, Alocasia, Hoya, Nepenthes, Sarracenia and Ficus (C-4, grows by attested pages), one kind "mushroom spawn" for spawn. Each object gets one kind; the same genus may have different kinds in several sub-families (bulb, potted plant, cut stem), which is three objects, not one kind in three places. Genus and `kind_rank` (genus | species | crop) are fields on every kind, never levels. Species under a genus kind, cultivars, Groups, grexes, clones, hybrids, trade-named and variegated selections and cultivated strains are named forms (grex, Group and clone are nested ranks of one level). Setting (indoor room, balcony or patio, garden border, pond or bog, greenhouse or conservatory, terrarium), `light_need`, `hardiness_rating` (RHS H1a–H7 or USDA zone), `pet_safe`, occasion, region and native range, origin (wild-collected, nursery-grown, tissue-cultured), stage (cutting, plug, rooted start, established), pot size and height, tree size, rootstock and training form, `rose_class`, daffodil division, bonsai size class and style, seed type (heirloom, open-pollinated, hybrid, organic), spawn `carrier` and `species`, grow-kit `substrate`, kokedama, variegation, care or skill level, price band and rarity are attributes, tags or views, never kinds or leaves (memo sections 3, 5, 7; section 14: place and light are the first filter, as tags). C3 receives no S3 parts, so it has no parts sub-family (C5(d), CV-17). Maker role: none (maker_roles.tsv, provisional: plants are chosen by group, genus and cultivar; breeders, registrants and seed houses enter only through registered or protected named forms and seller-owned selection lines). Pending kinds: the memo marks 10 kinds † (named only by the framework key or a decision, without a fetched page); their ids are allocated here in memo order, each line ending in †, and a † kind enters the data only when a fetched page attests it (memo section 10 attestation list, section 14). Leaf C3.1.5 Aquatic and bog plants is decided but holds no kind yet (section 14: its kinds enter when a pond-plant retailer tree is fetched); C3.1.7 and C3.4.3–C3.4.4 hold only † kinds and are provisional with them. Nepenthes and Sarracenia stand as genus kinds until their species kinds are attested (ICPS pages †); Hoya has no row until a species page is fetched. Display groups (navigation proposal, display only, never moving a home; no group ids issued): Houseplants & collector plants (C3.1.1, C3.1.2, C3.1.4, C3.1.8); Bonsai (C3.1.3); Garden plants (C3.1.5, C3.1.9, C3.1.10, C3.1.11, C3.1.12); Grow your own food (C3.1.6, C3.1.7); Seeds, bulbs & cuttings (C3.2); Cut flowers & Christmas trees (C3.3); Mushrooms & living cultures (C3.4). Quality markers (display, judged on the named form, never stored per plant, C16): proven garden or room performance (RHS AGM), health and pest and disease resistance, true to type with consistent colour and form (stable variegation for collector aroids), hardiness rating matched to the site, light and humidity match; collector plants by variegation stability, leaf colour and texture and scarcity; fruit by variety, then rootstock and tree form; seeds by seed type and trial awards; the buying checklist (no brown, yellow or droopy leaves, no gnats or mould, buds over open blooms) is shown on the page only.

Ids (R-58): C3.n is a sub-family and C3.n.m a leaf; a kind is C3.n.m.kk, or C3.n.kk where a sub-family has no leaf.

## C3.1 Growing plants
Leaf axis: horticultural group as recognised by a register or trade department, by the ordered key below (the first test that fits wins; key order only breaks ties). Rooted in a medium and alive: potted, bare-root, plug plants, liners and rooted tissue-culture plantlets, and a plant in any container, basket, kokedama or terrarium (K-73). Kinds: canonical kind first, then by accepted botanical or crop name (memo section 8).

### C3.1.1 Orchids
Membership test: Orchidaceae (AOS). Kind: genus; species, grex, Group and clone are named forms. Display group: Houseplants & collector plants.
- C3.1.1.01 Phalaenopsis [kochoran, 胡蝶蘭]: living moth orchid of the genus Phalaenopsis, the canonical orchid kind.
- C3.1.1.02 Cattleya: living orchid of the genus Cattleya.
- C3.1.1.03 Paphiopedilum (slipper orchid): living slipper orchid of the genus Paphiopedilum.
- C3.1.1.04 Cymbidium: living orchid of the genus Cymbidium.

### C3.1.2 Carnivorous plants
Membership test: a genus in the ICPS denomination classes. Kind: genus, except Nepenthes and Sarracenia, whose kinds are species (C-4); until their species kinds are attested they stand here as genus kinds. Display group: Houseplants & collector plants.
- C3.1.2.01 Dionaea (Venus flytrap): living Venus flytrap, genus Dionaea.
- C3.1.2.02 Nepenthes: living tropical pitcher plant of the genus Nepenthes (genus heading until its species kinds are attested).
- C3.1.2.03 Sarracenia: living North American pitcher plant of the genus Sarracenia (genus heading until its species kinds are attested).

### C3.1.3 Bonsai (trained miniature trees)
Membership test: a tree or shrub sold as a trained bonsai, the sale stating a style, a size class or a bonsai pot; untrained or pre-bonsai material is C3.1.9 or C3.1.10. Kind: species group. Display group: Bonsai.
- C3.1.3.01 Juniper bonsai: living juniper trained and sold as a bonsai.
- C3.1.3.02 Pine bonsai: living pine trained and sold as a bonsai.
- C3.1.3.03 Tropical bonsai: living tropical tree or shrub (Ficus, jade and others) trained and sold as a bonsai, usually kept indoors.

### C3.1.4 Cacti & succulents
Membership test: Cactaceae, and the genera on an attested succulent genus index, except Tillandsia and other Bromeliaceae (C3.1.8). Kind: genus. Display group: Houseplants & collector plants.
- C3.1.4.01 Echeveria: living rosette succulent of the genus Echeveria.
- C3.1.4.02 Haworthia: living succulent of the genus Haworthia.
- C3.1.4.03 Sempervivum: living hardy rosette succulent (houseleek, hens and chicks) of the genus Sempervivum.
- C3.1.4.04 Lithops: living stone-like succulent (living stones) of the genus Lithops.
- C3.1.4.05 Aloe: living succulent of the genus Aloe.
- C3.1.4.06 Mammillaria: living cactus of the genus Mammillaria.

### C3.1.5 Aquatic & bog plants
Membership test: plants sold to grow in water or boggy ground. No kind yet: the leaf is attested by headings only and its kinds enter when a pond-plant retailer tree is fetched (memo section 10, section 14). Display group: Garden plants.

### C3.1.6 Fruit & nut plants
Membership test: plants grown for edible fruit or nuts, strawberry and blueberry included, whatever the setting. Kind: crop. Display group: Grow your own food.
- C3.1.6.01 Apple tree: living apple tree (Malus) grown for fruit.
- C3.1.6.02 Citrus tree: living citrus tree or bush (lemon, orange, lime and others) grown for fruit, compact ones kept as houseplants included.
- C3.1.6.03 Fig tree: living fig tree (Ficus carica) grown for fruit.
- C3.1.6.04 Blueberry bush: living blueberry bush grown for fruit.
- C3.1.6.05 Strawberry plant: living strawberry plant grown for fruit.

### C3.1.7 Vegetable & culinary-herb plants
Membership test: plants grown for edible leaves, roots, stems or seeds, or listed as culinary herbs on an attested herb list †. Kind: crop. Provisional with its only kind. Display group: Grow your own food.
- C3.1.7.01 Basil plant: living basil plant sold growing in a pot or tray, also when sold to eat. †

### C3.1.8 Tropical foliage & flowering plants (by genus list)
Membership test: Araceae; Bromeliaceae including Tillandsia †; Hoya; tropical Ficus; palms; tender ferns †; Marantaceae †; Gesneriaceae †; not defined by where the plant is kept. Kind: species in the closed list of C-4 (Philodendron, Monstera, Anthurium, Alocasia, Hoya, Ficus), genus elsewhere, species then being named forms. Display group: Houseplants & collector plants.
- C3.1.8.01 Monstera deliciosa: living Swiss cheese plant, species Monstera deliciosa.
- C3.1.8.02 Philodendron gloriosum: living Philodendron of the species P. gloriosum.
- C3.1.8.03 Anthurium clarinervium: living Anthurium of the species A. clarinervium.
- C3.1.8.04 Alocasia reginula: living Alocasia of the species A. reginula.
- C3.1.8.05 Epipremnum: living climbing aroid of the genus Epipremnum (pothos, E. aureum, as a named form).
- C3.1.8.06 Dracaena: living plant of the genus Dracaena (snake plant, D. trifasciata, formerly Sansevieria, as a named form).
- C3.1.8.07 Ficus elastica: living rubber plant, species Ficus elastica.
- C3.1.8.08 Tillandsia (air plant): living epiphytic bromeliad of the genus Tillandsia, grown without soil; species are named forms.

### C3.1.9 Trees
Membership test: woody plants sold to grow on a single trunk, conifers grown as trees included. Kind: genus or common tree name. Display group: Garden plants.
- C3.1.9.01 Japanese maple: living Japanese maple tree (Acer palmatum and allies).
- C3.1.9.02 Flowering cherry: living ornamental cherry tree (Prunus) grown for its flowers.

### C3.1.10 Shrubs & climbers
Membership test: woody plants grown multi-stemmed, climbers, roses, hydrangeas, lavender, conifers grown as shrubs. Kind: genus; Rosa is one kind with `rose_class` as an attribute. Display group: Garden plants.
- C3.1.10.01 Rose (Rosa): living rose plant, bare-root or potted, of any class (hybrid tea, floribunda, climbing, shrub, miniature).
- C3.1.10.02 Hydrangea: living shrub of the genus Hydrangea.
- C3.1.10.03 Camellia: living shrub of the genus Camellia.
- C3.1.10.04 Clematis: living climber of the genus Clematis.
- C3.1.10.05 Lavender: living lavender plant (Lavandula).

### C3.1.11 Perennials, grasses & garden ferns
Membership test: herbaceous perennials, grasses (bamboo as a grass †), garden ferns, cultivated turf †, live moss †. Kind: genus or common name. Display group: Garden plants.
- C3.1.11.01 Hosta: living herbaceous perennial of the genus Hosta.
- C3.1.11.02 Peony: living peony plant (Paeonia), herbaceous or tree peony.
- C3.1.11.03 Bamboo: living bamboo plant, any genus.
- C3.1.11.04 Turf (cultivated sod): living grass sold as rolls or slabs of cultivated sod to lay as a lawn. †
- C3.1.11.05 Moss (live): living moss sold to grow, as sheets, cushions or moss balls. †

### C3.1.12 Annuals, biennials & bedding

- C3.1.12.01 Petunia: living bedding plant of the genus Petunia.
- C3.1.12.02 Cannabis plant: living cannabis plant (Cannabis) sold to grow; cultivars in the shared variety table. †

## C3.4 Living cultures & fungi

## Precedence inside C3

- State sold decides the sub-family: no roots and dormant is a propagule (C3.2); rooted in a medium and alive is a growing plant (C3.1); cut is a cut plant (C3.3). A tulip is three objects: a dormant bulb C3.2.2.01, a potted tulip in flower in C3.1 by its group, a cut tulip in C3.3.1. A plug plant, liner or rooted tissue-culture plantlet is C3.1 in its group (a petunia plug is C3.1.12.01, `stage` plug); only unrooted cuttings and divisions are propagules (C3.2.3).
- In C3.1 the first membership test that fits wins, in leaf order C3.1.1 to C3.1.12; every test is a horticultural group, never a place or a use in the owner's home. A plant on no list falls to C3.1.9 to C3.1.12 by growth form.
- Tillandsia (air plants) is C3.1.8.08: C3.1.4 excludes Bromeliaceae and C3.1.8 names them.
- A fruit plant kept as a houseplant goes to the leaf whose test defines it, whatever the setting: a Meyer lemon is a named form of C3.1.6.02; a fig tree is C3.1.6.03, while tropical Ficus is C3.1.8 (Ficus elastica C3.1.8.07).
- A tree or shrub sold as a trained bonsai (the sale states a style, a size class or a bonsai pot) is C3.1.3 (a Ficus bonsai is C3.1.3.03, before C3.1.8); pre-bonsai or untrained material is C3.1.9 or C3.1.10.
- Rose: a bare-root or potted garden rose, gift roses in a pot included, is C3.1.10.01 with `rose_class`; cut roses are C3.3.1.01.
- Lavender is C3.1.10.05, not C3.1.7, until an attested culinary-herb list places it; potted basil is C3.1.7.01.
- Kind rank (C-4): the genus is the kind by default (the crop for edible plants); species are kinds only in Philodendron, Monstera, Anthurium, Alocasia, Hoya, Nepenthes, Sarracenia and Ficus, and the list grows when one fetched page gives a genus's species their own pages. Elsewhere species are non-maker named forms (pothos under C3.1.8.05, snake plant under C3.1.8.06).
- P1: the plant is C3 and anything made for growing it is C2: a bonsai tree is C3.1.3, its pot, wire, tools and soil are C2, a plant stand is B1.
- P2 (K-73): a living plant in any container, basket, kokedama or terrarium is filed as the plant whatever the container's value; the container sold empty or alone, and a cachepot (K-179), is C2. The S3a greatest-value rule is not used for planted containers.
- P3 (K-74): a fresh-cut stem, bouquet, wreath or garland of living plant material is C3.3 (a fresh wreath is C3.3.3.01); dried, preserved (stabilised) or artificial ones, and artificial plants of every kind, are B4. Floral foam, florists' wire and tape are C4 (K-169); a made-up bouquet of living material stays C3.3.
- P4 (K-72): a real Christmas tree, fresh-cut or pot-grown, is C3 (C3.3.2.01, C3.3.2.02); an artificial tree, baubles and tinsel are B4.
- P5: a plant sold still growing in a pot, tray or mat is C3 even when sold to eat (potted basil C3.1.7.01, living microgreens); the same herb cut, sprouts and microgreens cut for eating are J4.
- P6 (K-75): a seed, bulb, corm, tuber, cutting or spore sold for growing is C3.2; the same seed sold as food (nuts and seeds, hemp seed) is J5; sprouting seed sold to eat as sprouts is J5, sold to grow on is C3.2.1. A packet that names both uses goes by the use named first. A seed potato is C3.2.2.03; potatoes for eating are J4.
- P7 (C-15, K-1, K-58): a living culture kept to grow or propagate is C3.4 even when what it makes is eaten (sourdough starter C3.4.2.01, kombucha SCOBY or JUN culture C3.4.2.02, kefir grains C3.4.2.03 and C3.4.2.04, mushroom spawn C3.4.1.01, beer and wine yeast cultures C3.4.3.01 and C3.4.3.02, algae cultures C3.4.4.01); the loaf, kefir or kombucha as a drink and fresh mushrooms are Culinary or J; dried baking and brewing yeast and home-brewing ingredients are J5, their equipment B5. A mushroom grow kit sold and compared as one object is C3.4.1.02 (section 14); spawn sold to inoculate stays C3.4.1.01. A seed-starting set stays a mixed set filed by its item of greatest value (C2 when pots, tray and lights outweigh the seed).
- P8 (K-75, K-168): a live plant, and a seed or spore for growing, is C3 (step 6 before step 27): living cannabis and tobacco plants are C3.1.12.02 and C3.1.12.03. Leaf, flower, resin, extracts, prepared products and every plant or fungus product taken for a psychoactive effect are B9; kava is J6. Legal regimes and CITES status are jurisdiction-ledger rows (C17), never a tree flag.
- P9: a plant kept dead and mounted or pressed as a specimen is F6; a living plant is C3 however rare or collected.
- P10: aquatic plants are C3 (C3.1.5); the aquarium tank, filter and substrate are B10; garden fish ponds, pond liners and pond pumps are C2 (K-104); any live animal is OUT.
- P11: a tree or hedge standing in the ground is part of the land (G2) until it is sold as a plant (a nursery tree, a lifted hedge, a bare-root tree).
- P12: bird seed and hay are B10, not C3.
- P13: cultivated turf and live moss are C3.1.11.04 and C3.1.11.05; lawn seed is C3.2.1; artificial turf is C2; preserved or dried moss is B4.
