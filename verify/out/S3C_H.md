# S3C_H source census, domain H (Body)

Table: verify/out/S3C_H.tsv (83 rows: H1 32, H2 25, H3 26). Every kept source was fetched at least once (WebFetch, or the Parallel_Auth web_fetch where WebFetch returned 403/401/429). Counts are as the page stated; where a figure came only from a search excerpt it is marked as such in the row.

- **H1 Personal Care & Beauty.** Anchor: Fragrantica designers index (142K perfumes, brand list with per-house counts) for fragrance houses and named forms, with Basenotes and Parfumo as cross-checks; EWG Skin Deep (155,108 products, 7,780 brands, a cosmetic category tree) and Open Beauty Facts (12,367 brands) for skin care, make-up, hair and body makers; Sephora, Douglas, Lookfantastic and Cosmetify brand A-Z for retail-listed makers; Olive Young brand facet for K-beauty; ONEcosme and biteki.com for Japanese brands; B&B ShaveWiki and West Coast Shaving for razors and blades. Gaps: no catalogue was found for electric toothbrush heads, shaver foils, hair-dryer and clipper makers as a maker list (only retailer facets such as Lyko, 654 hair tools, and Wikipedia kinds); baby care (pacifiers, baths, potties, changing mats) has no maker catalogue (a Babylist store view was fetched but only as a single-brand page, so not kept); wigs and hairpieces only a US directory of 36 makers (paywalled contacts); hot-water bottles, heat packs, massagers and body scales have no database; @cosme has no brand index at the URL tried and Hwahae and Meilixiuxing are app-first; Chinese, Korean and Japanese product-level depth needs app access.
- **H2 Health, Medical & Mobility.** Anchor: the GKV Hilfsmittelverzeichnis via rehadat-gkv.de (about 56,000 aids in product groups, by maker) for aids and braces, with TAIS Japan (20,021 items), EASTIN (ISO 9999 classes, five national databases) and Living Made Easy (UK) for the same layer in other regions; AccessGUDID plus the FDA database index (Devices@FDA, Product Classification, Registration and Listing) and EMDN for device makers and kinds; DailyMed, the French public medicines database, MHRA Products and PMDA for medicines; NIH DSLD (200,000+ labels) for supplements; HearingTracker (104 models, 21 brands, plus the 133-brand industry map) for hearing aids. Gaps: spectacle frames have only group portfolio pages (EssilorLuxottica, Kering, Safilo, Marcolin, Marchon via W Optics) and no optician or frame database; contact lenses only the Lens.com tree (nine manufacturers); pregnancy tests, fertility monitors, breast pumps, first-aid kits and medical ID jewellery have no catalogue beyond retailer trees (Boots Clearblue page did not render products); sports-nutrition powders and bars have no database apart from ConsumerLab brands (members only for ratings); AbleData could not be reached (connect error) so it is not in the table; WHO ATC lists classes, not products; Chinese NMPA and Korean MFDS registers were not located as fetchable pages; Rote Liste and Gelbe Liste are paywalled, no German medicines page was kept.
- **H3 Adult Objects.** Anchor: Orion Germany (1,964 toys, 70 brand entries, full type tree) and Babeland (3,651 toys, 185 brands, 28 categories with counts) for kinds and maker lists, then Lovehoney's brand index, Adam and Eve and Healthylife for English-language makers; NLS, Bestvibe, Night World (Taiwan) and Pleasure Point (HK) for Japanese and Chinese-language makers and series; Wikipedia Category:Sex toy manufacturers (25 pages) and List of BDSM equipment (about 90 items) as seeds; ISO 3533:2021 for the scope definition. Gaps: no maker directory or trade-association member list is public (ANME, Asia Adult Expo and the European wholesalers Orion Wholesale, EDC Wholesale and Eropartner show only press items; the Asia Adult Expo JETRO page returned 503); no independent review database covers all brands (Lovehoney's own claim of 200,000 reviews is unverified); BDSM-specialist makers (Mr S Leather, The Stockroom, Rouge and similar) and doll makers (RealDoll, Orient Industry, WM, 6YE) have no fetched catalogue, only Wikipedia names and Orion's 45 dolls; Korean and Spanish/Italian/French retail trees were searched but not fetched as usable pages; Vac-U-Lock and 3XLR owners remain unattested.

Notes for the harvest
- INCIDecoder's brands URL redirects (301) to a different domain (inkeedecoder.com); it was not kept.
- Fragrantica, Basenotes, Parfumo, Badger and Blade, Sephora, Douglas, Olive Young, West Coast Shaving, FDA, NIH ODS, EASTIN, ConsumerLab and ISO pages return 403/401 to WebFetch but load through the Parallel_Auth fetch; use that route.
- Several figures (Esxence 298 brands, REHADAT 56,000 products, EASTIN 70,000 products, AbleData sizes) come from search excerpts or older papers and are labelled as such in the table.
