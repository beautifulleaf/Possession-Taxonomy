Kinds: no kind rows written. All eight task kinds (F3.5.1.01, .02, 2.01, 2.02, 3.01-3.04, 5.01) are already in data/kinds.tsv. No new kinds proposed.

Named forms: 81 rows, all non-maker (issuer owns). By kind: definitive 50, commemorative 7, miniature sheet 3, booklet 2, stamped envelope 5, postal card 2, aerogram 3, revenue 9 (including Duck Stamp and Red Revenue). Continents covered: Europe, North and South America, Africa, Asia, Oceania. Of the names the task listed, Penny Black, Penny Red, Machin, Wilding, Marianne (two series), Germania, Sower (Semeuse), Columbian Issue and Zeppelin issues (Germany 1928, US 1930, USSR 1931) are recorded. Inverted Jenny is excluded as a single error, and the Treskilling Yellow was left out for the same reason.

Issuers: 30 rows in DEP_F3_4_issuers.tsv (data/issuers.tsv was empty, so every id is new). Makers: none. F3 maker role is "owner only" and every form is issuer-owned, so the makers file holds the header only. Printers such as Perkins Bacon and De La Rue are not owners.
