# F2 Art: decided tree (kind-level test)

Category F2 Art: pictures made to be looked at on a flat or hung surface (paintings, drawings, prints, posters, art photographs, calligraphy, tapestries and textile hangings, collage and mosaic pictures) and free-standing sculpture, including ornamental figurines of people and animals in ceramic, glass, metal, stone, wood or resin, whatever the edition or price.

Levels below the category: sub-family, leaf (only where stated), kind. A kind is the medium, technique or material, never the subject, size, edition, price, maker, style or period. Place each object at one kind id.

## F2.1 Paintings
Leaf axis: none. Kind axis: binder or medium of the paint.
- F2-K01 oil painting: pigment bound in a drying oil, on canvas, panel or similar support.
- F2-K02 acrylic painting: pigment bound in acrylic polymer emulsion.
- F2-K03 watercolour: transparent pigment in gum arabic, applied wet, normally on paper.
- F2-K04 gouache: opaque, chalk-bodied water-based colour, normally on paper.
- F2-K05 ink wash painting: black or coloured ink brushed in graded washes on paper or silk, in the East Asian manner (named form: ink-and-colour painting).
- F2-K06 mineral-pigment painting (nihonga): ground mineral pigments fixed with animal glue on paper or silk.
- F2-K07 tempera painting: pigment bound in egg yolk or another egg or glue medium, usually on a prepared panel.
- F2-K08 encaustic painting: pigment in hot beeswax, fused to a support.
- F2-K09 fresco (detached or transferred): pigment applied to wet or dry lime plaster, now removed from its wall.
- F2-K10 mixed-media painting: a painting in which several paint media or added materials are used together and the image is painted, not built from pasted elements.
- F2-K11 spray-paint painting: image made mainly with aerosol or spray paint (graffiti-derived work on canvas, panel or other support).

## F2.2 Drawings
Leaf axis: none. Kind axis: drawing medium.
- F2-K12 graphite (pencil) drawing: image drawn with graphite pencils.
- F2-K13 charcoal drawing: image drawn with charcoal.
- F2-K14 pastel drawing: image drawn with pigment sticks (soft pastel, oil pastel) on paper or similar ground.
- F2-K15 coloured-pencil drawing: image drawn with pigmented pencils.
- F2-K16 pen-and-ink drawing: image drawn with pen, nib or marker in ink; illustration and comic pages in this medium are here.

## F2.3 Prints
Leaf axis: family of printing process, oldest first. Kind axis: the technique. Every print is filed by how it was printed.
### F2.3.1 Relief (ink taken from the raised surface of a block)
- F2-K17 woodcut: image cut with knives and gouges into the side grain of a wood block (named form: ukiyo-e colour woodblock print).
- F2-K18 linocut: image cut into a linoleum sheet.
- F2-K19 wood engraving: image cut with gravers into the end grain of a hardwood block.
### F2.3.2 Intaglio (ink taken from lines or pits cut or bitten into a metal plate)
- F2-K20 etching: lines bitten into a coated metal plate with acid.
- F2-K21 line engraving: lines cut into a metal plate with a burin.
- F2-K22 drypoint: lines scratched directly into a plate with a needle, no acid.
- F2-K23 mezzotint: plate roughened all over with a rocker, then burnished from dark to light.
- F2-K24 aquatint: tone made by acid biting through a porous ground of resin dust.
### F2.3.3 Planographic (printed from a flat surface)
- F2-K25 stone lithograph: image drawn with greasy medium on limestone and printed from the stone.
- F2-K26 offset lithograph: image transferred from a plate through a rubber blanket to paper, as a fine-art edition.
### F2.3.4 Stencil
- F2-K27 screenprint: ink pushed through a mesh stencil, one stencil per colour (named form: paper screen).
### F2.3.5 Other and hybrid processes
- F2-K28 monotype: a single unique impression pulled from a painted or inked blank surface.
- F2-K29 monoprint: a unique impression from a reusable matrix, varied by inking or additions each time.
- F2-K30 collagraph: print from a plate built up by gluing materials onto a board.
### F2.3.6 Digital
- F2-K31 inkjet print: image sprayed from a print head with pigment or dye inks onto paper, canvas or another sheet (aliases: giclee, digital print; fine-art reproductions of paintings made this way are here).

## F2.4 Photographs
Leaf axis: process class, ordered by chronology of invention. Kind axis: the process. Every photographic print is filed by process.
### F2.4.1 Silver
- F2-K32 gelatin silver print: silver halide in a gelatin layer on paper, exposed and developed in a darkroom.
- F2-K33 daguerreotype: image on a silvered copper plate.
### F2.4.2 Chromogenic and dye
- F2-K34 chromogenic print: colour dyes formed in layers of colour paper by development (C-print, Lambda print).
- F2-K35 dye-transfer print: colour made by transferring dyes from matrices onto paper.
- F2-K36 silver dye bleach print: colour dyes already in the paper, bleached away where not wanted (named form: Ilfochrome).
### F2.4.3 Pigment and digital
- F2-K37 archival pigment print: pigment inkjet print of a photograph on fine-art paper.
### F2.4.4 Alternative and historic
- F2-K38 platinum or palladium print: image in platinum or palladium metal on paper.
- F2-K39 cyanotype: iron-salt print giving a blue image.
- F2-K40 tintype: positive image on a thin iron plate.
### F2.4.5 Camera-less and instant
- F2-K41 photogram: image made without a camera by placing objects on light-sensitive material.
- F2-K42 instant print: self-developing print from an instant camera or film pack (named form: Polaroid).

## F2.5 Sculpture
Leaf axis: material family that makes the form. Kind axis: the material or material-defined type. Figurines are kinds in these leaves; there is no separate figurine sub-family. Form (freestanding, wall-object, bust, relief, outdoor) and size are attributes, not kinds.
### F2.5.1 Metal
- F2-K43 bronze sculpture: cast copper-tin alloy, usually patinated.
- F2-K44 steel and iron sculpture: sculpture of steel, iron or other ferrous metal that is welded, forged, cut or bent (welded or forged is an attribute).
- F2-K45 pewter and other cast-metal figurine: ornamental figure cast in pewter, zinc, tin or other non-bronze metal.
### F2.5.2 Stone
- F2-K46 stone carving: marble, limestone, alabaster or other stone carved to form.
- F2-K47 jade carving: figure or object carved from jade.
### F2.5.3 Wood and other carved organic material
- F2-K48 wood carving: carved from wood (named form: netsuke).
- F2-K49 ivory and bone carving: carved from ivory, bone, horn or antler (named form: scrimshaw).
### F2.5.4 Ceramic
- F2-K50 terracotta sculpture: modelled or moulded clay fired without a glaze.
- F2-K51 glazed earthenware and stoneware sculpture and figurine: glazed fired clay other than porcelain.
- F2-K52 porcelain and bone china figurine and sculpture: figure or sculpture in porcelain or bone china.
### F2.5.5 Glass and crystal
- F2-K53 glass and crystal sculpture and figurine: blown, cast, fused or cut glass or lead crystal figure or sculpture (named form: Murano glass).
### F2.5.6 Resin, plaster, wax and plastic
- F2-K54 resin sculpture and figurine: cast resin figure or sculpture, usually painted.
- F2-K55 plaster sculpture: cast or modelled plaster.
- F2-K56 wax sculpture: modelled or cast wax.
### F2.5.7 Textile and soft
- F2-K57 soft sculpture: three-dimensional sculpture of fabric, fibre or other soft material, stuffed, wrapped or stitched.
### F2.5.8 Found and mixed
- F2-K58 found-object assemblage: sculpture assembled from found, salvaged or discarded objects, or from several materials none of which governs (named form: junk sculpture).
### F2.5.9 Light
- F2-K59 neon sculpture: sculpture in which a lit gas-discharge tube or equivalent light source is the medium.

## F2.6 Textile and fibre hangings
Leaf axis: none.
- F2-K60 tapestry: picture or pattern woven on a loom to hang on a wall.
- F2-K61 embroidered picture: picture worked in thread with a needle on a fabric ground (named form: sampler).

## F2.7 Calligraphy
Leaf axis: none.
- F2-K62 brush and pen calligraphy: writing made as the picture, in brush or pen script (East Asian script styles, Arabic calligraphy, Western calligraphy); hanging scroll and handscroll are mount attributes.

## F2.8 Collage, mosaic and mixed work
Leaf axis: none.
- F2-K63 collage: picture built from pasted or bonded elements (paper, cloth, photographs, found flat material).
- F2-K64 mosaic picture: picture set in small pieces (tesserae) of stone, glass or ceramic.

## Precedence within F2
1. Pastel is a drawing (F2-K14), not a painting.
2. Gouache is its own kind (F2-K04), beside watercolour (F2-K03).
3. A work whose image is built from pasted elements is a collage (F2-K63); a painting with added materials is mixed-media painting (F2-K10).
4. A print is filed by its printing process. A poster has no kind of its own: a poster printed from stones is F2-K25, a screenprinted poster is F2-K27. A reproduction of a painting made by inkjet is an inkjet print (F2-K31). A print on canvas or metal is filed by its process, with the support as an attribute.
5. A photographic print is filed by its process. Camera-less work is a photogram (F2-K41); a self-developing print from an instant camera is an instant print (F2-K42).
6. Within one material a figurine and a sculpture are the same kind; size, edition, price and maker are never used to choose the kind.
7. A netsuke is filed under the material it is carved from (a wood netsuke is F2-K48; an ivory netsuke is F2-K49).
8. A work that combines animal parts with other materials, or several materials with none governing, is a found-object assemblage (F2-K58).
9. A light sculpture whose medium is the light itself is a neon sculpture (F2-K59). An installation or kinetic work that is a free-standing object is filed under the sculpture leaf of its main material, with several materials in Found and mixed.
10. A cut or forged metal relief hung on a wall is filed under Metal (F2.5.1) with its form as an attribute.
11. Use (illustration, comic art, animation) is a tag, not a kind: an original comic page in pen and ink is F2-K16.
12. Woven to hang is tapestry (F2-K60); worked with a needle is embroidered picture (F2-K61).
