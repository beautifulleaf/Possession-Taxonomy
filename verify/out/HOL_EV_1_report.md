**A1 Clothing.** Shopper ladder: gender > garment form (tops, shirts, trousers, jeans, jackets, coats, knitwear, suits, underwear, swimwear), with brand as the first filter; Zalando also has parallel tabs for Sport, Streetwear, Designer, Pre-owned. END. leads with brand and curated edits. Quality: fabric, then stitching and seams, then fit; denim buyers add mill and weight. Signals: GOTS, OEKO-TEX, Good On You rating, 2-year warranty. Scenes (gorpcore) and makers both matter; scene is a style, not a menu level. Surprise: ethics ratings sit inside "quality" guides.

**A2 Footwear.** Ladder: shoe style (sneaker, boots by kind, lace-up, business, loafers, sport, outdoor) > brand, size, function, material. Fine shoes are sold maker > grade line > last. Quality: construction (Goodyear, Blake, cemented), leather, last, workmanship. Signals: editor tier lists (Gentleman's Gazette A-F), maker grade lines (Crockett & Jones Hand Grade), resale authentication for sneakers. Makers lead for dress shoes and sneakers; use leads for running. Surprise: construction is itself a shop collection.

**A3 Jewellery.** Ladder: item type (ring, necklace, earrings, bracelet) with metal and stone as parallel axes; bridal and diamonds sit first at Blue Nile; Indian shops add purpose and occasion. Quality: the 4Cs for stones, metal purity, maker for antiques. Signals: GIA, IGI, HRD scales; BIS hallmark with HUID (14K to 24K, silver 800 to 990); karat stamps; signed makers. Collectors lead with era and maker (costume vs fine first). Surprise: stone grading and metal marks are separate trust systems.

**A4 Bags & Accessories.** Ladder: what it carries or how (luggage, backpacks, handbags, business, school) > type > brand; facets are litres, kilograms, laptop size, material. Quality: fabric, seams and bar tacks, zippers (YKK), wheels and handles, warranty. Signals: independent tests (OutdoorGearLab), maker tests (Travelpro), bluesign, CORDURA, lifetime warranties. Makers lead for luxury and buy-it-for-life; use leads for travel. Surprise: no formal test standard is named; denier alone is not a quality measure.

**A5 Clocks & Watches.** Ladder: brand > collection > reference; secondary: men's/women's, style, movement, case size, material. Beginners start with taste, wrist size and use. Quality: movement (mechanical, in-house), finishing, crystal, lume, accuracy. Signals: COSC, Geneva Seal, Fleurier, METAS Master Chronometer, maker seals (Rolex, Patek, JLC), ISO 6425 for divers. Makers lead; tool-watch families (dive, pilot, field, chronograph) are the enthusiast genre. Surprise: several top seals are maker-owned.
