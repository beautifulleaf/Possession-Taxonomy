# G2 Property & Structures: decided tree (kind level)

Category G2 holds land, buildings and structures, dwellings that stay in place, and burial places. Four sub-families, filed by what is held. Place each object at one kind (the id in bold).

## G2.1 Land
Leaf axis: land cover and principal use. Land is filed by its principal land use whether or not it carries buildings or a house; dwellings and farm buildings on it are only an `improvements` facet.

- **G2.1.1 Building land**
  - **G2.1.1.1 building plot**: a parcel of land sold to build on, with or without planning permission.
- **G2.1.2 Cropland**
  - **G2.1.2.1 arable land**: land used or sold for growing field crops such as cereals, roots or oilseeds.
- **G2.1.3 Grassland**
  - **G2.1.3.1 grazing land**: pasture, paddock, meadow or ranch land held for livestock or horses.
- **G2.1.4 Permanent crops**
  - **G2.1.4.1 orchard**: land planted with fruit or nut trees.
  - **G2.1.4.2 vineyard**: land planted with grape vines.
- **G2.1.5 Woodland**
  - **G2.1.5.1 woodland**: land under trees, whether managed for timber, amenity or conservation.
- **G2.1.6 Water and wetland**
  - **G2.1.6.1 lake**: a body of water, pond, fishery or wetland held as the property.
- **G2.1.7 Mixed holdings**
  - **G2.1.7.1 mixed holding**: a holding with no single principal use (smallholding, lifestyle block, hobby farm, mixed farm, homestead); area and enterprises are attributes.

## G2.2 Dwellings
Leaf axis: what is owned against the building around it (a whole house; a unit inside a building; a whole building of two or more dwellings; a dwelling kept in place that is not a building).

- **G2.2.1 Houses** (kind axis: attachment to neighbouring dwellings)
  - **G2.2.1.1 detached house**: a whole house standing free of neighbours. Bungalow, courtyard house, cave house, castle or chateau, barn conversion, log house, manufactured or modular home on a permanent foundation, and an annexe, ADU or guest house that meets the dwelling test are forms of it.
  - **G2.2.1.2 semi-detached house**: a house joined to one neighbour on one side.
  - **G2.2.1.3 terraced house**: a house in a row, joined to neighbours on both sides or (end of terrace) on one side; townhouse (UK, AU) and mews are forms of it.
- **G2.2.2 Flats and maisonettes** (kind axis: levels in the unit)
  - **G2.2.2.1 flat**: one dwelling unit on a single level inside a larger building (apartment, condo, studio, penthouse on one level, loft conversion on one level).
  - **G2.2.2.2 maisonette**: one dwelling unit whose rooms are on two or more full levels inside a larger building (duplex or triplex in the UK or Spanish sense).
- **G2.2.3 Multi-unit residential buildings**
  - **G2.2.3.1 multi-unit residential building**: a whole building of two or more dwellings held as one object (block of flats, tower block, US duplex or triplex bought whole).
- **G2.2.4 Dwellings kept in place that are not buildings** (kind axis: physical form; listed from least to most fixed)
  - **G2.2.4.1 moored houseboat**: a hull without its own propulsion and steering, made to stay moored as a dwelling (house barge).
  - **G2.2.4.2 tiny house on wheels**: a small dwelling built on a trailer chassis, towed between sites.
  - **G2.2.4.3 park model**: a small, single-section, trailer-built dwelling or cabin made for a leisure or residential site (park model RV).
  - **G2.2.4.4 static caravan**: a single-section holiday home on a chassis, kept on one pitch (holiday home, mobile home).
  - **G2.2.4.5 lodge or park home**: a twin-unit or wider, factory-built home built to a residential-park standard and kept on a pitch.
  - **G2.2.4.6 floating home**: a dwelling on a float or hull moored in a slip, connected to shore utilities, not made to navigate.

## G2.3 Other buildings & structures
Leaf axis: what the building or structure is for.

- **G2.3.1 Vehicle shelter**
  - **G2.3.1.1 garage**: an enclosed building for vehicles, attached or freestanding.
  - **G2.3.1.2 carport**: a roofed, open-sided shelter for vehicles.
  - **G2.3.1.3 cart shed**: an open-fronted farm building for carts and farm machinery.
  - **G2.3.1.4 boathouse**: a building for keeping boats, on or beside water.
- **G2.3.2 Storage and work**
  - **G2.3.2.1 garden shed**: a small outbuilding for tools and garden storage.
  - **G2.3.2.2 workshop**: a building made for making or repairing things.
  - **G2.3.2.3 shipping container (kept in place)**: an ISO container fixed on a site as a store or room.
- **G2.3.3 Rooms for people**
  - **G2.3.3.1 summerhouse**: a garden building for sitting and relaxing in.
  - **G2.3.3.2 garden office**: an insulated garden building made to work in.
  - **G2.3.3.3 garden log cabin**: a log-walled garden building used as a room or retreat.
  - **G2.3.3.4 garden bar**: a garden building fitted as a bar or pub shed.
  - **G2.3.3.5 sauna cabin**: a sauna that stands as a building.
- **G2.3.4 Animal and crop buildings**
  - **G2.3.4.1 barn**: a large farm building for hay, grain, livestock or machinery (Dutch barn, hay barn, staddle barn are forms).
  - **G2.3.4.2 stable block**: a building of stalls or loose boxes for horses or cattle.
  - **G2.3.4.3 granary**: a building for storing grain.
  - **G2.3.4.4 dovecote**: a building for housing doves or pigeons that a person can enter.
- **G2.3.5 Shade and surface structures**
  - **G2.3.5.1 gazebo**: a freestanding, roofed, open-sided garden structure.
  - **G2.3.5.2 pergola**: an open frame of posts and beams, usually with a slatted or climbing-plant roof.
  - **G2.3.5.3 decking**: a raised timber or composite platform, including one built for a tent, yurt or tipi.
  - **G2.3.5.4 patio**: a laid paved or hard surface outside a building.
  - **G2.3.5.5 jetty or pontoon**: a fixed or floating structure for mooring or access over water.
- **G2.3.6 Pools**
  - **G2.3.6.1 in-ground swimming pool**: a swimming pool built into the ground; construction (fibreglass, vinyl liner, concrete or gunite) is an attribute.
- **G2.3.7 Other buildings by function** (kind axis: the function the building is made for)
  - **G2.3.7.1 office building**: a building made for offices.
  - **G2.3.7.2 shop**: a building made for retail trade.
  - **G2.3.7.3 warehouse**: a building made for storing goods in bulk.
  - **G2.3.7.4 industrial unit**: a building made for manufacturing or light industry.
  - **G2.3.7.5 hotel**: a building made for letting rooms to guests.
  - Finer functions (bank, pub, chapel and the like) are forms of these kinds, not kinds.

## G2.4 Burial places
No leaf level. Kinds are ordered by structure and position (increasing price, with the cremation plot after the ground plot).

- **G2.4.1 ground burial plot**: an in-ground grave space for a casket (single, companion or family capacity).
- **G2.4.2 cremation (urn) plot**: an in-ground plot for cremated remains.
- **G2.4.3 lawn crypt**: an underground pre-built concrete vault set in a lawn section.
- **G2.4.4 mausoleum crypt**: an above-ground chamber for a casket inside a community mausoleum.
- **G2.4.5 private mausoleum**: a freestanding building holding crypts for one family.
- **G2.4.6 columbarium niche**: a compartment in a wall, stele or building for an urn (locker-type and automated indoor halls included).
- **G2.4.7 green burial plot**: a grave space in a natural or conservation setting, often marked by a tree (tree burial).
- **G2.4.8 communal grave space**: a shared burial space for the remains of many people.

## Rules inside G2 (precedence)

1. File what is owned: a whole building of two or more dwellings is a multi-unit residential building; one dwelling of a pair or row is a semi-detached or terraced house; one unit in a block is a flat or maisonette.
2. A unit whose rooms are on two or more full floors is a maisonette, whatever its position (penthouse) or history (loft conversion); one on a single floor is a flat.
3. Land is filed by its principal land use, whether or not it carries buildings; the dwelling and farm buildings on it are the facet `improvements`. A holding with no principal use is a mixed holding. A house with a garden is filed as the dwelling.
4. A unit with its own sink, toilet and entrance is a dwelling (a detached house, with annexe, ADU and guest house as forms); otherwise it is a garden building (G2.3).
5. "Log cabin" names two objects: a garden log cabin is G2.3; a log house that meets the dwelling test is a detached house.
6. An integral garage is part of the house, which is filed as a whole; a garage building of its own is G2.3.1.
7. A factory-built home on a permanent foundation is a detached house; one kept on its chassis or on a pitch is a static caravan or a lodge or park home.
8. Dwellings kept in place are separated by physical form. A dwelling made to stay in one place is here: a floating home or house barge without propulsion, a static caravan, a lodge or park home, a park model, and a tiny house on wheels however often it is towed. A hull with its own propulsion and steering made to navigate is not in G2.
9. A shipping container kept in place as a store or room is G2.3.2.3.
10. A pergola, gazebo or decking kit is filed by the structure it makes.
11. Decking and patio laid are G2; the platform built for a tent, yurt or tipi is decking.
12. A sauna cabin that stands as a building is G2.3.3.5; a portable sauna is not here.
13. A greenhouse and an above-ground pool are not G2; an in-ground pool and a stable block a person can stand in are.
14. A dovecote or granary that is a building a person can stand in is G2; a bird box is not.
15. A shed a person can enter is G2; a garden storage box is not.
16. The plot, crypt or niche is G2.4; the casket, urn, headstone and marker are not here.
17. A burial place is G2 however it is held (owned, leased or on a use right); a rented bay, berth, mooring or permit is a right and not here.
18. Commercial, industrial and institutional buildings are filed by what the building is made for in G2.3.7; market never divides them.
19. Bungalow, courtyard, cave, castle and barn-type are forms of a house, not kinds; style (cottage, villa) and regional names (riad, finca, cortijo, longere, trullo) are forms under the attachment kind, else under detached house.
