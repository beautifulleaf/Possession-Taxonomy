Outputs: MKH_D5_ASIA_makers.tsv (1218 rows), MKH_D5_ASIA_maker_kinds.tsv (159 rows), MKH_D5_ASIA_named_forms.tsv (141 rows), fetch log verify/work/MKH_D5_ASIA/fetched.tsv (125 lines, 12 failed). Build script verify/work/MKH_D5_ASIA/build.py; nothing written to data/, no git.

Makers per country: JP 522, CN 209, KR 117, SG 109, TW 65, TH 65, ID 34, PH 28, HK 26, VN 17, MY 12, MM 7, KH 2, country blank 5 (HTC, ASUS, MSI, Lenovo, Mayflash: their fetched pages do not state HQ). 74 rows reuse an id already in data/makers.tsv; 31 same-name companies of other trades or countries got a "-game" id.

Sources that served best: Wikipedia developer and publisher lists, country and city category pages (Japan, Korea, Taiwan, SEA), zh/ko/ja Wikipedia lists and categories, ja.wikid.org mirror of the Japanese game company list, ChinaJoy 2007 rosters (17173), Gematsu TGS 2026 and Taipei Game Show 2023 exhibitor lists, made-in-china.com factory directories (controllers, handhelds, arcade, shells), Wikipedia console, controller and arcade-cabinet lists (kinds and named forms), Lazada brand wiki and CB Insights for Chinese hardware brands.

Failed or empty: ChinaJoy and CESA official lists not found; globalsources (429), medcom, bisnis, frontiermyanmar, cambodiadaily, vsdaily (403/404/503 or bot check); gamebiz TGS list (503); Wikipedia Video games in Taiwan page (404). Not done: Steam developer pages, KOGIA/JOGA/Taiwan association member directories (none found), Vietnamese and Thai national lists.

Weaknesses: CN 209 is thin against its 15% reference share (consumer-electronics and arcade makers are mostly small B2B sellers); JP is over-covered; most rows carry only country. Companies whose names are only in kana, hangul or hanzi were romanised or given pinyin by hand when no English name was shown (native_name filled); a few were skipped as too uncertain. Trading companies on made-in-china were left out. Conflicts left for the loader: Sony Interactive Entertainment (JP here, US in NAO), Mattel163 (HK here, US in NAO), Enlight Software (CN here, US in NAO), Mistwalker (JP here, US in NAO), Ayaneo (HK per Wikipedia, CN per an earlier row; retailer says Shenzhen), Razer omitted (SG category vs US row).
