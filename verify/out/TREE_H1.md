# H1 Personal Care & Beauty: decided tree

Category: H1 Personal Care & Beauty (domain H). Membership test (step 18): made to be used on the body for grooming, hygiene, cosmetics, fragrance, or bodily comfort and soothing: grooming tools and powered tools, grooming mirrors, bathroom and body scales, cosmetics attached to the body, wigs and hairpieces, hair ties and clips, sponges and pumice, massagers, hot-water bottles, heat packs and hand warmers, pacifiers and teethers, baby baths, changing mats, potties and toilet-training seats.

Structure: sub-family (the area of the body or purpose served; the closing parts sub-family is exempt, C5(d)), then leaf (the job or method inside the area, with one final tools and devices, preparations or equipment leaf; Fragrance, Oral care, Grooming implements and Comfort have no leaf; in Parts the leaf is the object served), then kind (the retail product type; in Fragrance, the format). Each object gets one kind. Body area, skin type, hair type, colour and shade, finish, scent family and notes, SPF and PA, volume, formulation and format (stick, pad, pump, cushion, mist, wipe, roll-on, balm, soap form), user (women, men, unisex, baby, child, teen, adult and the rest of the one user list), power source, `disposable`, head design, brush hair, region and channel are attributes and never kinds or leaves; fragrance strength is a C3 concentration variant; K-beauty and J-beauty are scene tags. A kind marked † is provisional, named only by the framework key or a decision, and needs a fetched page before it enters the data. Display groups (navigation proposal, display only, one to one with the sub-families): Skincare; Makeup; Hair; Fragrance; Shaving and hair removal; Bath, body and hygiene; Oral care; Grooming tools, mirrors and scales; Comfort and massage; Parts and refills.

Ids (R-58): H1.n is a sub-family and H1.n.m a leaf; a kind is H1.n.m.kk, or H1.n.kk where a sub-family has no leaf.

## H1.1 Skin care

Leaf axis: the job in the care routine, for any body area (cleanse, tone, treat, moisturise, mask, protect); the last leaf holds the tools and devices. Face, eye, hand, foot and body products are one kind with `area` as the attribute.

### H1.1.1 Cleansing
- H1.1.1.01 Face cleanser: rinse-off product made to wash the skin of the face.
- H1.1.1.02 Cleansing oil: oil-based cleanser that dissolves makeup and sebum and emulsifies on rinsing.

### H1.1.2 Toning and prep
- H1.1.2.01 Toner: watery leave-on product applied after cleansing to prepare the skin (a toner pad is a toner with format = pad).

### H1.1.3 Treatment
- H1.1.3.01 Serum: concentrated leave-on treatment applied before moisturising (ampoule and essence are formulations).

### H1.1.4 Moisturising
- H1.1.4.01 Cream: thick leave-on moisturiser for any area (face, eye, hand, foot, body by `area`).
- H1.1.4.02 Lotion or emulsion [乳液]: fluid leave-on moisturiser for any area.
- H1.1.4.03 Lip balm: solid or soft balm with no colour that softens and protects the lips.
- H1.1.4.04 Body oil or massage oil: oil applied to the skin to moisturise it or for massage. †

### H1.1.5 Masks
- H1.1.5.01 Sheet mask: single-use sheet soaked in a treatment, laid on the face.
- H1.1.5.02 Sleeping mask: leave-on mask worn overnight.

### H1.1.6 Sun care and skin protection
- H1.1.6.01 Sunscreen: product applied to the skin to protect it from UV, any area and any format (stick, lotion, spray).
- H1.1.6.02 Skin repellent: insect repellent applied to the skin. †

### H1.1.7 Skin tools and devices
- H1.1.7.01 Cotton pad: pad of cotton or fibre for applying or removing skin-care products and makeup. †

## H1.2 Makeup

Leaf axis: the area of the face or body coloured; the last leaf holds the tools and devices. A product that leaves visible colour, or that prepares or fixes makeup, is Makeup.

### H1.2.1 Complexion
- H1.2.1.01 Foundation: tinted product that evens the colour of the face (cushion is a format).
- H1.2.1.02 Concealer: high-coverage tinted product that hides spots, marks and shadows.
- H1.2.1.03 Blush: coloured powder, cream or liquid that adds colour to the cheeks.
- H1.2.1.04 Primer: product applied before makeup to prepare the skin for it. †
- H1.2.1.05 Setting spray: spray applied over makeup to fix it.

### H1.2.2 Body paint and skin powders
- H1.2.2.01 Skin paint or powder: powder or paint applied to the skin as colour, including kumkum and sindoor. †

### H1.2.3 Eyes
- H1.2.3.01 Mascara: coloured product brushed onto the lashes.
- H1.2.3.02 Eyeliner: pencil, liquid or gel that draws a line along the lash line.

### H1.2.4 Brows and lashes
- H1.2.4.01 Eyebrow gel: tinted or clear gel that shapes and sets the brows.
- H1.2.4.02 False eyelashes: strip or individual artificial lashes attached to the lash line.
- H1.2.4.03 Lash and brow serum: leave-on serum applied to lashes or brows to condition them.

### H1.2.5 Lips
- H1.2.5.01 Lipstick: coloured product applied to the lips (gloss and liquid forms are formats).

### H1.2.6 Nails
- H1.2.6.01 Nail polish: coloured or clear lacquer painted on the nails.
- H1.2.6.02 Artificial nails: press-on or glued nail tips or covers worn over the natural nail. †

### H1.2.7 Makeup tools and devices
- H1.2.7.01 Makeup brush: brush made for applying makeup.
- H1.2.7.02 Eyelash curler: hand tool that clamps and curls the lashes. †

## H1.3 Hair care and styling

Leaf axis: the job (wash and condition, colour and chemical, styling, hairpieces); the last leaf holds the hair tools, manual or powered (power source is an attribute).

### H1.3.1 Wash and condition
- H1.3.1.01 Shampoo: rinse-off product that washes the hair and scalp.
- H1.3.1.02 Conditioner: rinse-off or leave-in product that softens and detangles hair after washing.
- H1.3.1.03 Hair mask [Haarkur]: intensive treatment left on the hair for a time and rinsed.

### H1.3.2 Colour and chemical
- H1.3.2.01 Hair dye: product that changes the colour of the hair.

### H1.3.3 Styling products
- H1.3.3.01 Hairspray: spray that holds a hairstyle in place.

### H1.3.4 Hairpieces
- H1.3.4.01 Wig: full head covering of real or synthetic hair worn as hair. †
- H1.3.4.02 Hairpiece: partial piece of hair (extension, topper, fall) attached to the wearer's hair. †

### H1.3.5 Hair tools
- H1.3.5.01 Flat iron: heated plates that straighten hair by clamping it.
- H1.3.5.02 Curling iron: heated barrel around which hair is wound to curl it.
- H1.3.5.03 Hair dryer: appliance that blows heated air to dry hair. †
- H1.3.5.04 Hot brush: heated round or paddle brush that dries or styles hair. †
- H1.3.5.05 Hairbrush: brush with bristles or pins for brushing hair. †
- H1.3.5.06 Comb: toothed strip for combing and parting hair. †
- H1.3.5.07 Hair tie: plain elastic band that holds hair in place. †
- H1.3.5.08 Plain hair clip: plain clip or grip that holds hair, without jewels or ornament. †

## H1.4 Fragrance (no leaf)

Kind axis: the format (H-4). The scent (house plus name) is a maker-bound named form; parfum or extrait, eau de parfum, eau de toilette, eau de cologne and eau fraîche are its concentration variants.

- H1.4.01 Fine fragrance (alcohol-based): fragrance in an alcohol base, sprayed or splashed on the body.
- H1.4.02 Solid perfume: fragrance set in a wax or balm base, rubbed on the skin.
- H1.4.03 Perfume oil: fragrance in an oil base with no alcohol, applied to the skin. †
- H1.4.04 Body mist: light fragrance spray made for the body. †
- H1.4.05 Hair perfume: fragrance made to be sprayed on the hair. †

## H1.5 Shaving and hair removal

Leaf axis: the method of removing or trimming hair; the last leaf holds the preparations and aftercare. Blades, cartridges, foils, heads and guards made for one razor or shaver are H1.10.

### H1.5.1 Blade shaving
- H1.5.1.01 Straight razor: single open blade that folds into its handle, honed and stropped by the user.
- H1.5.1.02 Shavette: open-blade razor that holds a disposable blade.
- H1.5.1.03 Single-edge safety razor: guarded razor holding a blade with one cutting edge.
- H1.5.1.04 Double-edge safety razor: guarded razor holding a blade with two cutting edges.
- H1.5.1.05 Cartridge razor: razor with a handle that takes a multi-blade cartridge (a disposable razor is this kind with `disposable` = yes).
- H1.5.1.06 Shaving brush: brush made for lathering soap or cream on the face.
- H1.5.1.07 Wet-shaving kit: starter kit of razor, brush and preparation sold and compared as one object. †

### H1.5.2 Electric shaving and trimming
- H1.5.2.01 Electric shaver: powered shaver with foil or rotary heads.
- H1.5.2.02 Hair clipper: powered clipper that cuts head hair to a set length. †
- H1.5.2.03 Beard trimmer: powered trimmer made for beards and body grooming. †

### H1.5.3 Depilation and epilation
- H1.5.3.01 Tweezers: hand tool that grips and plucks single hairs. †

### H1.5.4 Shaving preparations and aftercare
- H1.5.4.01 Shaving soap: soap made to be lathered with a shaving brush, sold as a hard puck or tub, a soft soap (croap) or a shaving stick. †
- H1.5.4.02 Shaving cream: cream applied before shaving that lathers by hand or brush.
- H1.5.4.03 Aftershave: product applied to the skin after shaving (an aftershave balm is this kind with format = balm).

## Precedence inside H1

- Colour decides Makeup against Skin care (H1-6). A product that leaves visible colour, or that is applied to prepare or fix makeup, is Makeup: BB and CC cream, tinted moisturiser and tinted sunscreen go to Complexion (H1.2.1), primer is H1.2.1.04 and setting spray H1.2.1.05. A product with no colour is Skin care, whatever it claims. Lash and brow serums are H1.2.4.03. Within H1 this decides the leaf, not the category.
- Skin care holds every skin-care product for any area. Body lotion, hand cream, foot cream and eye cream are cream (H1.1.4.01) or lotion (H1.1.4.02) with `area` as the attribute; body and face sunscreen are one kind (H1.1.6.01); massage oil and body oil are one kind (H1.1.4.04); a face mist with no colour is Skin care with format = mist.
- Format is an attribute, never a kind (stick, pad, pump, cushion, mist, wipe, roll-on, balm). The one exception is masks, where the mask type is the kind (H1.1.5.01, H1.1.5.02). A toner pad is a toner (H1.1.2.01), a sun stick a sunscreen (H1.1.6.01), a deodorant wipe a deodorant (H1.6.2.01), an aftershave balm an aftershave (H1.5.4.03).
- Fragrance (H-4): one kind per format (H1.4.01 to H1.4.05). The scent is a maker-bound named form, a flanker its own named form linked by `flanker_of`, and the printed strength label (parfum or extrait, eau de parfum, eau de toilette, eau de cologne, eau fraîche) a C3 concentration variant. Scent family (Edwards wheel, H-5), notes, user label and volume are attributes.
- Tools follow their area (H-7): makeup brushes and eyelash curlers are H1.2.7; hot brushes, hair dryers, irons, hairbrushes, combs and plain ties and clips are H1.3.5; hair clippers, beard trimmers and body groomers are H1.5.2; tweezers are H1.5.3.01. Items that serve no single area are H1.8: nail clippers (H1.8.03), nail files (H1.8.04), manicure sets (H1.8.05), toiletry and make-up bags (H1.8.06, H1.8.07) and barber chairs (H1.8.08); massage chairs are H1.9.04 (K-190). Nail polish and artificial nails are H1.2.6.
- Parts follow the whole (S3, CV-17): blades, cartridges, foils, heads, guards and attachments made for one razor, shaver, clipper, toothbrush or dryer are H1.10 under the object served, and a strop made for the straight razor is H1.10.8.01. The shaving brush (H1.5.1.06) is not made for one object and stays in Blade shaving; a shavette takes disposable blades and is not served by a strop.
- Razors are filed by mechanism. A disposable razor is the cartridge razor (H1.5.1.05) or the single-edge safety razor (H1.5.1.03) with `disposable` = yes. Closed comb, open comb, slant and adjustable heads are values of `head_design` on the double-edge and single-edge safety razors (H1.5.1.04, H1.5.1.03), and maker models (Merkur 37C slant, Gillette Slim adjustable) are named forms. Brush hair is the attribute `brush_hair` on the shaving brush.
- Shaving soap against shaving cream: a soap made to be lathered with a brush (hard puck or tub, soft soap or croap, shaving stick) is H1.5.4.01; a cream that lathers by hand or brush is H1.5.4.02. The soap form is the attribute `format`.
- Sets and kits (S3a, K-1): a set of objects of one kind is filed as that kind; a mixed set is filed by its item of greatest value, with its contents listed. A kit sold, standardised and compared as one object is a kind: the manicure set (H1.8.05) and the wet-shaving kit (H1.5.1.07).
- Baby goods are placed by function, and baby is a user tag (H-10): baby shampoo is shampoo (H1.3.1.01), baby wipes are the wet wipe (H1.6.1.03), and pacifiers (H1.9.03), baby baths (H1.6.5.01), changing mats (H1.6.4.03), potties (H1.6.4.01), toilet-training seats (H1.6.4.02) and nappies (H1.6.4.04) are H1.
- Kumkum, sindoor and other powder or paint applied to the skin are the skin paint or powder (H1.2.2.01), kumkum and sindoor as non-maker named forms with culture = South Asia (K-129); a stick-on bindi is A3.
- Wigs (H1.3.4.01), hairpieces (H1.3.4.02), false eyelashes (H1.2.4.02), artificial nails (H1.2.6.02), hair ties (H1.3.5.07) and plain clips and bands (H1.3.5.08) are H1; a decorative or jewelled hair comb, pin or clip is A3; period underwear is A1; a toiletry bag and a make-up bag are H1 and not A4 (K-132).
- H1 against H2 (K-31): a product is H2 when its labelled main purpose is to treat, relieve or prevent a named condition, or when it holds an active ingredient sold only as a medicine in every sourced jurisdiction. Cosmetic purposes (clean, perfume, change appearance, protect, keep in good condition, correct body odour) are H1, sunscreens and anti-dandruff and anti-blemish products included; legal status goes to C17 and a pharmacy channel or the label "dermocosmetic" does not move the object. Supplements for skin, hair or nails, contact lenses of every kind and their solution, condoms, incontinence products, breast forms and packers are H2. Lubricant (H1.6.3.05), anal douche (H1.6.3.06), massage oil (H1.1.4.04), intimate wash (H1.6.3.01), menstrual products (H1.6.3.02 to H1.6.3.04), nappies (H1.6.4.04), baby wipes (H1.6.1.03) and cotton pads (H1.1.7.01) are H1 (K-33).
- Massagers (K-32): a powered massager is H3 when its maker names sexual use, H2 when made to treat a condition (TENS, EMS units sold for pain), and H1 for comfort (H1.9.02), percussion and neck massagers included. The test rests on what the maker says on the object or its packaging. An oil sold as an intimate oil is H3 (K-165).
- Fragrance worn on the body is H1; room sprays, plug-ins, diffusers, incense and candles are B4, and incense made for a rite is F4. A grooming or lit makeup mirror is H1.8.01; a wall or dresser mirror is B4.
- Soap, bath and shower preparations, bath salts, and sponges, loofahs and pumice sold for the body are H1 (H1.6.1, H1.6.5); laundry, dish and cleaning soaps and cleaning sponges are B6; towels are B2; bathtubs and sanitaryware are B7.
- Repellent applied to the skin is H1.1.6.02; sprays, coils and traps for rooms are B8 (K-138). Any cannabinoid product, balm and lotion included, is B9 (K-165). Grooming goods made for an animal are B10. Baby bottles and feeding gear are B5; breast pumps are H2.
