# HOL_EV_8 report (F1 to F6)

Evidence: verify/out/HOL_EV_8_evidence.tsv (47 rows); fetch log: verify/work/HOL_EV_8/fetched.tsv. Many big retailers (Waterstones, Fnac, HMV, Amazon, Saatchi, APMEX, NGC, Discogs, Numista) returned 403 or were rate-limited, so retailer navigation is thinner than asked; hosts without a fetched page are not used. Regions: US, UK, Germany, France, Italy, Japan, India, Australia, Switzerland/Czech. No community pages were fetched for any category.

**F1 Media & Publications.** Shopper ladder: product line (books, ebooks, audiobooks, records) > genre/subject > binding or format; Japan puts carrier sub-formats (SHM-CD, LP, 7 inch) as filters, audiophile shops lead with pressing (weight, speed, mastering). Quality: edition, condition, completeness, provenance for books; playing surface, label and sleeve for records. Signals: Goldmine grades (M, NM, VG+...), Disk Union's own S/A/B+/B/C. Genre leads in general shops; carrier leads in specialists. Surprise: records graded disc and jacket separately.

**F2 Art.** Ladder: medium (painting, print, photography, sculpture, drawing) > subject/style > size/price. Quality: authenticity and provenance, condition, artist reputation; for prints edition size, hand signature, original technique. Signals: certificate of authenticity, CAA edition and proof rules (proofs about 10 percent). Medium leads, artist is a parallel tab. Surprise: German and Japanese guides ask original versus reproduction first, and split Japanese/Western tradition.

**F3 Coins, Stamps, Medals & Trading Cards.** Ladder: stamps vs coins/bullion > country/denomination > condition. Quality: wear and strike for coins; centering and gum for stamps; slab grade for cards. Signals: Sheldon 70-point scale with NGC/PCGS, PSA/BGS/SGC/CGC cards, no official stamp grader. Origin and grade lead. Surprise: Japanese buyer ranks issue era and full sheet first.

**F4 Religious & Ceremonial Objects.** Ladder: object (rosaries, crucifixes, butsudan, puja items) > material or sect > occasion; devotion and tradition are facets. Quality: fit for rite, certification (sofer for scrolls), craftsmanship. Signals: rabbinical certification only. Use and object lead. Surprise: Japanese beads divided by sect and wearer.

**F5 Documents & Personal Records.** Ladder: type (letters, postcards, photographs, documents) > period/condition. Quality: writer identity, rarity, condition, content; postcards age, motif, print process. Signals: ALS/LS/TLS letter classes; no grade scale found. Surprise: marketplaces file ephemera in one flat class.

**F6 Natural History & Specimens.** Ladder: natural class (mineral, fossil, meteorite, gem) > species/type > locality. Quality: color, form, condition, luster, locality, provenance; fossils authenticity and disclosed restoration. Signals: GIA/SSEF/Gubelin/AGL lab reports, Meteoritical Society database. Type and species lead. Surprise: no formal grades for minerals or fossils.
