# B4 Decor: decided tree

Category: B4 Decor (domain B, Home). Membership test (placement key step 30, last branch, CATEGORIES_v0_7): made to decorate a room, surface or garden and not a picture, sculpture or lamp: vases, decorative bowls and objects, picture frames, wall and dresser mirrors, candles and home fragrance (including incense), decorative lighting, seasonal, festival and party decorations, Christmas crackers, party poppers, sparklers and fireworks, artificial and dried flowers, preserved and artificial plants, garden ornaments, statues and gnomes, wind chimes, flags, decorative windsocks and signs (including illuminated and shop signs), decorative replica weapons and deactivated display ordnance, and parts made for one B4 object. Branch B4 is tried last, after B5, B6, B7, B8, B3, B1 and B2. Not here: sundials, garden sundials included, and wall clocks (A5, R-28); pictures, posters, canvas prints, tapestries and textile hangings, and free-standing ornamental figurines (F2); maps and decorative globes (F1); plates and serving trays of every kind (B5, K-182); trays for small items on a surface, bookends, jewellery boxes and storage boxes (B1, K-191); lamps, bulbs, light strips sold to light a space, carried lanterns and electric wall sconces (B3, K-180, K-181); objects made for worship or a rite (menorah, diya, Advent wreath, home nativity set, shimenawa, bon-chōchin, rite incense, burners and candles, grave lights: F4, K-113, B4-O2); living plants and real Christmas trees (C3, K-72); planters, cachepots, plant supports, pond and fountain pumps (C2, K-175, K-179); bird baths, feeders and nest boxes (B10, B-33); regulatory and safety signs, candle lighters and matches (C8, K-192); weather and airfield windsocks (C6); candle-making supplies, craft shells and feathers, wrapping and noticeboards (C4, C5); grooming mirrors and fragrance worn on the body (H1); oils sold to treat a condition (H2); odour removers for surfaces and fabrics (B6, B4-O3); humidifiers (B7); real, sport and toy weapons (E4, E2, E1). Structure (Candidate A, decided B-5; memo sections 3 and 10, DPH section 14): sub-family by the family of ornamental object, closed by a parts sub-family exempt from that axis (CV-17); leaf by generic type, stated per sub-family; B4.6, B4.7, B4.9 and B4.10 have no leaf; kind at the product form where buyers compare quality or makers (memo section 4b). Each object gets one kind. Scent family (Michael Edwards wheel, H-5), wax, material, technology (LED, neon: rule T), size, colour, shape, style, occasion, placement, region or tradition, period, maker and price band are attributes, tags or generated views, never kinds; occasion (Christmas, Diwali, Hinamatsuri, New Year, Children's Day) is a tag. Attributes stored (DPH): `holder_form`, `scent_family`, `wax_type`, `burn_time_h`, `chime_tuning`. Flag designs are a register linked to B4.8.1.01 and B4.8.1.02 (CV-45). A kind marked † is named by the key, a decision or the review without a fetched page in the memo; its id is fixed and it is attested in the stage-3 harvest (removed, not kept empty, if none is found). Maker role: owner (maker_roles.tsv, provisional): decor is chosen by object and style; makers come through named designs (candle scents, ornament series), creators lead only in the dealer market. Display groups (navigation proposal, display only, no id changes): Vases, bowls & ornaments (B4.1); Mirrors & frames (B4.2); Candles & holders (B4.3); Home fragrance & incense (B4.4); Seasonal & party decorations (B4.5, shown first in season); Artificial & dried plants (B4.6); Garden ornaments (B4.7); Flags & signs (B4.8); Decorative lighting (B4.9); Flagpoles & parts (B4.10); level 2 follows the leaves. Quality markers (display, provisional): candles by scent throw (cold and hot), clean even burn (no soot or tunnelling), scent quality and house, burn time, wax and fragrance load; home fragrance by scent and house, throw and longevity; mirrors by fit to the space and placement, flat undistorted glass, silvering and a sealed backing, frame, mounting and warranty; vases and ceramics by material and craftsmanship, finish, maker or creator, age and period, form and size; flags by material grade and construction; wind chimes by tuning; collectible ornaments by maker series and era (condition codes only in the grading registry).

Ids (R-58): B4.n is a sub-family and B4.n.m a leaf; a kind is B4.n.m.kk, or B4.n.kk where a sub-family has no leaf. No memo section 13 or frozen audit tree gives B4 ids; these are first assignments.

## B4.1 Vessels & table ornaments
Leaf axis: the vessel type (vase family), then other bowls and decorative objects. Display group: Vases, bowls & ornaments.

### B4.1.1 Vases & jars
Kind axis: vessel type (the retail "Type" facet); silhouettes are named forms, and bud, cylinder, trumpet, tulip and amphora are shape attributes.
- B4.1.1.01 Vase: open vessel made to hold cut or artificial flowers or to stand as an ornament; a tulip vase is a vase.
- B4.1.1.02 Ginger jar: rounded lidded jar made for display.
- B4.1.1.03 Decorative urn: large footed vessel made as an ornament, indoors or in the garden (a cremation urn is F4).
- B4.1.1.04 Decorative bottle: bottle made to be displayed, not to hold a drink.

### B4.1.2 Bowls & decorative objects
Kind axis: object form.
- B4.1.2.01 Decorative bowl: bowl made to be looked at or to hold small decorative items (a bowl made to hold food is B5).
- B4.1.2.02 Snow globe: sealed liquid-filled globe holding a scene and loose particles that fall when shaken.
- B4.1.2.03 Decorative wall panel: carved, fretted or moulded panel made to hang on a wall as ornament. †
- B4.1.2.04 Wall sticker or decal: a self-adhesive vinyl or film motif, pattern or lettering applied to a wall, window or furniture to decorate it (DPH; provisional until a shop page shows wall stickers sold as a type).
- B4.1.2.05 Decorative replica weapon: replica sword, gun or other weapon made for display that cannot injure, decorative wushu implements included (K-86, K-87). †
- B4.1.2.06 Deactivated ordnance (display piece): bomb, grenade, mine or artillery piece made inert and kept for display (K-85). †

## B4.2 Mirrors & frames
Leaf axis: mirrors by placement; frames. Display group: Mirrors & frames.

### B4.2.1 Mirrors
Kind axis: placement, kept as kinds under rule M(a) because the body differs; round and convex are shape attributes.
- B4.2.1.01 Wall mirror: mirror with a hanging fitting, made to hang on a wall.
- B4.2.1.02 Floor mirror [leaner mirror]: full-length mirror made to lean against a wall from the floor, with no hanging fitting.
- B4.2.1.03 Standing mirror [cheval mirror]: full-length mirror held in its own free-standing frame, often tilting.
- B4.2.1.04 Table mirror [dresser mirror]: plain mirror on a stand made to sit on a table or dresser (a grooming mirror is H1).

### B4.2.2 Frames
Kind axis: object form.
- B4.2.2.01 Picture frame: empty frame made to hold and display a picture or photograph.

## B4.3 Candles & holders
Leaf axis: candles by form; holders; tools. Display group: Candles & holders.

### B4.3.1 Candles
Kind axis: candle form, canonical form first, then ascending size; wax, scent, colour and size are attributes.
- B4.3.1.01 Container candle [jar candle]: candle poured into and burned in its own jar or vessel.
- B4.3.1.02 Tealight: small candle in a shallow metal or plastic cup.
- B4.3.1.03 Votive candle: short candle made to burn standing in a holder that contains the melted wax.
- B4.3.1.04 Taper candle: long, slender candle made for a candlestick.
- B4.3.1.05 Pillar candle: thick free-standing candle that burns without a container.

### B4.3.2 Candle holders
Kind axis: one provisional kind (K-180); the form is the attribute `holder_form` (candlestick, candelabrum, tealight holder, votive holder, candle plate, wall sconce for candles, hurricane).
- B4.3.2.01 Candle holder: open holder made to hold a lit candle; holder form is an attribute (provisional: a holder form becomes a kind if fetched shops sell and compare it as its own category).

### B4.3.3 Candle tools
Kind axis: tool form.
- B4.3.3.01 Candle snuffer: tool with a small bell on a handle made to put out a candle flame.

## B4.4 Home fragrance
Leaf axis: product form of the scent carrier; incense by form. Display group: Home fragrance & incense.

### B4.4.1 Diffusers, melts, sprays & oils
Kind axis: product form; scent family is an attribute (Edwards wheel, H-5).
- B4.4.1.01 Reed diffuser: vessel of scented oil with reeds that draw it up and release it into a room.
- B4.4.1.02 Electric diffuser [plug-in diffuser]: powered device made to release fragrance into a room (K-183, B4-O3).
- B4.4.1.03 Wax melt: scented wax piece made to be melted in a warmer, not burned.
- B4.4.1.04 Wax warmer: burner or electric warmer made to melt wax melts or oil (K-183).
- B4.4.1.05 Room spray: aerosol or pump spray made to scent a room.
- B4.4.1.06 Potpourri: mixture of dried scented plant material displayed to scent a room.
- B4.4.1.07 Diffuser oil: essential or fragrance oil sold for a diffuser or burner (K-183; an oil sold to treat a condition is H2).

### B4.4.2 Incense
Kind axis: form by burn length, then the burner, then the smudge bundle.
- B4.4.2.01 Stick incense: incense formed on or as a stick, burned to scent a room.
- B4.4.2.02 Cone incense: incense pressed into a cone.
- B4.4.2.03 Coil incense: incense formed as a long-burning coil.
- B4.4.2.04 Incense burner: holder or burner made to burn home-fragrance incense (a rite burner is F4).
- B4.4.2.05 Smudge bundle: bound bundle of dried herbs burned to scent or cleanse a room, not made for a named rite (K-115).

## B4.5 Temporary display decorations
Leaf axis: how it is displayed or used: hung or strung, standing, used up at a party, or operated for an effect; occasion is a tag. Display group: Seasonal & party decorations.

### B4.5.1 Hung & strung
Kind axis: object form.
- B4.5.1.01 Tree ornament [bauble]: ornament made to hang on a tree or festive display, whatever its theme.
- B4.5.1.02 Tree topper: ornament made to sit on the top of a tree.
- B4.5.1.03 Garland: long strand of foliage, tinsel, beads or other decoration made to drape or hang.
- B4.5.1.04 Bunting: string of pennant-shaped flags hung as a festive decoration. †
- B4.5.1.05 Paper streamer: long strip or roll of paper hung or thrown as a party decoration.

### B4.5.2 Standing displays
Kind axis: object form.
- B4.5.2.01 Artificial Christmas tree: manufactured tree made to be decorated for Christmas (a real tree is C3, K-72).
- B4.5.2.02 Festival doll display: set of dolls made to be displayed on tiers for a festival, not made for worship.
- B4.5.2.03 Entrance decoration [kadomatsu]: seasonal arrangement made to stand at an entrance for a festival (B4-O2, K-116). †

### B4.5.3 Party consumables
Kind axis: object form; legal classes of pyrotechnics go to the jurisdiction ledger (B-19, C17).
- B4.5.3.01 Christmas cracker: paper tube pulled apart by two people with a snap, holding small gifts.
- B4.5.3.02 Party popper: hand-held device that bursts with a bang and throws out streamers. †
- B4.5.3.03 Sparkler: hand-held wire coated with a slow-burning pyrotechnic that throws sparks (B-19). †
- B4.5.3.04 Firework: pyrotechnic device made to give a display of light, colour or noise (B-19). †

### B4.5.4 Party effects equipment
Kind axis: effect device.
- B4.5.4.01 Fog or haze machine: machine that makes fog or haze for a party or display effect (K-145). †

## B4.6 Artificial, preserved & dried plants (no leaf)
Leaf axis: none (leaf = null, C5(b)); kind by plant form; stem or arrangement is a unit-of-sale attribute, species and colour facets. Display group: Artificial & dried plants.
- B4.6.01 Artificial flower: manufactured imitation of a flower, single stem or arranged.
- B4.6.02 Artificial plant: manufactured imitation of a plant other than a flower, foliage and trees included (K-74).
- B4.6.03 Preserved plant [stabilised plant]: real plant or moss treated so that it keeps its look without growing or care (K-74). †
- B4.6.04 Dried flowers: real flowers dried for display.

## B4.7 Garden & outdoor ornaments (no leaf)
Leaf axis: none (leaf = null); kind by ornament form; material is a facet and chime tuning the attribute `chime_tuning`. Display group: Garden ornaments.
- B4.7.01 Garden statue: statue made to stand outdoors as a garden ornament.
- B4.7.02 Garden gnome: figure of a gnome made as a garden ornament.
- B4.7.03 Gazing ball [yard globe]: reflective sphere on a stand made as a garden ornament.
- B4.7.04 Weathervane: rotating ornament on a rod that turns to show the wind direction.
- B4.7.05 Wind chime: set of hanging tubes, rods or bells that sound when moved by the wind.
- B4.7.06 Garden fountain or water feature: ornamental fountain or flowing-water feature for a garden (B4-O1; its pump is C2, K-175).

## B4.8 Flags & signs
Leaf axis: flags by use form; signs by how displayed. Display group: Flags & signs.

### B4.8.1 Flags
Kind axis: use form; each polity's design is a row in the flag register (CV-45); material grade is an attribute.
- B4.8.1.01 National flag: flag of a country, made to fly or hang.
- B4.8.1.02 State or territory flag: flag of a state, province or territory.
- B4.8.1.03 Garden flag: small flag made to hang from a garden stake or stand.
- B4.8.1.04 Pennant: tapering flag, usually triangular.
- B4.8.1.05 Decorative windsock or wind streamer: windsock or streamer made as a garden, festive or decorative ornament (DPH rename; a windsock made to show wind direction or strength is C6).

### B4.8.2 Signs
Kind axis: how the sign is displayed; material (enamel, tin, wood) and technology (neon, LED) are attributes.
- B4.8.2.01 Hanging sign: sign made to hang from a bracket, chain or cord. †
- B4.8.2.02 Wall sign or plaque: flat sign or plaque made to fix to or stand against a wall. †
- B4.8.2.03 Illuminated sign: neon or LED sign made to show a lit word or motif, a decorative light that spells a message included.

## B4.9 Decorative lighting (no leaf)
Leaf axis: none (leaf = null); kind by decorative light form; technology is an attribute (rule T). Display group: Decorative lighting.
- B4.9.01 String light [fairy light]: string of small lights made mainly as decoration (K-181). †
- B4.9.02 Flameless candle: battery or LED light made in the shape of a candle.
- B4.9.03 Lava lamp: lamp in which heated wax rises and falls in a liquid, made mainly as decoration. †
- B4.9.04 Decorative lantern: lantern made to stand or hang as decoration, lit or unlit, stone garden lanterns included (K-116, K-180). †

## B4.10 Parts and accessories made for one B4 object (no leaf)
Leaf axis: none (leaf = null); axis: the object served (C5(d), CV-17). Display group: Flagpoles & parts.
- B4.10.01 Flagpole: pole made to fly a flag (K-192).

## Precedence inside B4

- Family first: an object is filed by what it is as a display object (B4.1 to B4.9); a part or accessory made for one B4 object is B4.10. Occasion, placement, tradition and style are tags and generated views, never homes, so a lantern, garland or bauble used for several festivals has one kind.
- A flameless LED candle is a light (B4.9.02), not a candle (B4.3.1). A neon or LED sign is the illuminated sign (B4.8.2.03), not decorative lighting, and a decorative light that spells a message is that kind too; neon and LED are one kind (rule T).
- A light strip sold to light a space is B3; string, fairy and novelty lights made mainly as decoration are B4.9.01 (K-181). A lantern made to stand or hang as decoration, a stone garden lantern and a Diwali lantern included (occasion tag), is the decorative lantern (B4.9.04); an enclosed lantern with a handle made to be carried as a light is B3; a lantern made as a shrine offering by its form, and a bon-chōchin made for Obon, are F4 (K-116, K-180, B4-O2).
- An open holder for a candle (candlestick, candelabrum, tealight holder, votive holder, wall sconce for candles, hurricane) is the candle holder (B4.3.2.01), with the form in `holder_form`; an electric wall sconce is B3. A holder form becomes its own kind only if fetched shops sell and compare it as a category. A candle snuffer is B4.3.3.01; candle lighters and matches are C8, pocket lighters B9, candle-making supplies C4.
- A candle made for a rite or devotion, grave lights included, is F4 (K-113); home candles are B4.3.1. Home-fragrance incense and its burner are B4.4.2; incense and burners made for a rite are F4. A smudge bundle is B4.4.2.05 unless made for a named rite (F4) (K-115).
- An essential or fragrance oil sold for a diffuser or burner is the diffuser oil (B4.4.1.07), not a B4.10 part, because it is not made for one object; one sold to treat a condition is H2 (K-183). Wax warmers and electric diffusers are B4.4.1.04 and B4.4.1.02; a device sold to scent the air is the electric diffuser and one sold to humidify it is B7. A product made to scent a room (B4.4.1) is B4; one made to remove odour from or clean a surface or fabric is B6 (B4-O3).
- A decorative urn has one home (B4.1.1.03) and is listed in the garden view (C14); a cremation urn is F4. A snow globe is B4.1.2.02 and appears in the festive view; a decorative globe is F1.
- A decorative bowl made to be looked at is B4.1.2.01 and one made to hold food is B5; every plate and every tray made to hold food is B5 with display a tag, a tray for small items on a surface is B1, and B4 holds no plate or tray kind (K-182, K-191). A cachepot, jardinière or planter is C2 (K-179); a tulip vase is a vase (B4.1.1.01).
- A self-adhesive motif applied to a wall, window or furniture is the wall sticker or decal (B4.1.2.04); a carved or fretted panel hung on a wall is B4.1.2.03; pictures, posters, canvas prints, tapestries and textile hangings are F2 (step 20, K-139), and an empty frame is B4.2.2.01.
- A decorative replica weapon that cannot injure, decorative wushu implements included, is B4.1.2.05; a battle-ready reproduction is E4, one blunted for sport E2, a foam or toy one E1 (K-86, K-87). Deactivated bombs, grenades, mines and artillery kept as display pieces are B4.1.2.06 (K-85).
- Mirrors are filed by placement (B4.2.1.01 to B4.2.1.04); a round or convex mirror is the wall mirror with a shape attribute. If a fetched source shows one body sold as both wall and leaner mirror, B4.2.1.01 and B4.2.1.02 merge into one kind with a placement attribute (rule M). A mirror made to magnify or light the face for grooming is H1; a mirror cabinet is B1; a mirror with built-in lights is a mirror, and a wall light fitted beside a mirror is B3.
- A figure made to hang on or stand under a tree, wreath or festival display is B4.5.1.01 or B4.5.1.02 whatever its theme; a ceramic figure made to stand on a shelf is F2 even when it shows Santa. A tree, ornament or festival display not made for worship is B4.5 (a festival doll display is B4.5.2.02); a menorah, diya, Advent wreath or home nativity set is F4. Kadomatsu is B4.5.2.03; shimenawa is F4 (B4-O2).
- An artificial Christmas tree is B4.5.2.01 and a real one, cut or potted, is C3 (K-72); artificial plants of every kind and preserved plants are B4.6.02 and B4.6.03 (K-74); a plant sold still growing is C3.
- Christmas crackers, party poppers, paper streamers, sparklers and fireworks are B4.5 kinds (B4.5.3.01 to B4.5.3.04, B4.5.1.05), with legal classes in the C17 ledger (B-19); a toy cap gun or toy firework replica is E1. A fog or haze machine is B4.5.4.01 (K-145); stage lighting is B3.
- A garden ornament with no function is B4.7. Sundials, garden sundials and the armillary sundial included, are A5 (R-28) and appear in the garden view only as a cross-category link; wall clocks are A5. A garden fountain or water feature is B4.7.06 and its pump C2 (B4-O1, K-175); an arch, obelisk or trellis for climbing plants is C2; a bird bath, feeder or nest box is B10 (B-33).
- A flag is B4.8.1 whatever it is made of; a polity's design is a flag-register row under B4.8.1.01 or B4.8.1.02, not a kind (CV-45). A flagpole made for a flag follows the flag and is B4.10.01 (K-192). A windsock or wind streamer made as a garden, festive or decorative ornament is B4.8.1.05; one made to show wind direction or strength (airfield, weather or site use) is C6 (step 23).
- A sign made as decoration, advertising memorabilia or shop identity is B4.8.2; a regulatory or safety sign is C8 (K-192). An enamel or tin advertising sign is the wall sign or plaque (B4.8.2.02) with a material attribute; the advertised subject is a view.
