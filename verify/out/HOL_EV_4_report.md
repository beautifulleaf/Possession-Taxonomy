# HOL_EV_4 report (C1 C2 C3 C4)

Caveat: many pages were blocked (403/429) or the domain safety check was rate-limited; sources per category are 5-8 fetched, but several kinds are thin (noted).

**C1 Tools & Workshop Equipment.** Ladder: trade shop > Power Tools > tool type (drills, saws, sanders...) > filters brand, voltage, corded/cordless, brushless, battery. Hand-tool enthusiast shops lead with type then maker. Quality: Consumer Reports puts power (torque score), handling, chuck and run time ahead of voltage; CHOICE weights ease of use 40%, drilling 30%, torque 30%; test.de adds durability and safety. Buyers trust independent lab scores. Makers lead for cordless: the battery platform locks the choice (Makita, Milwaukee, DeWalt). Surprising: voltage is called a poor guide; a cheap model beat brands in test.de.

**C2 Garden & Grounds.** Ladder: lawn size > power type (corded, cordless, petrol, robot, ride-on) > grass conditions > features > brand last (Toolstation). French retailer groups machines by garden job; Coolblue filters power source, cutting width, battery platform. Quality: cut quality (Which? 50%), ease of use 30%, battery and noise. Signals: Which? Best Buy/Don't Buy, Consumer NZ. Hand tools (secateurs) lead by maker line and hand size. Reddit-derived views name Honda, Toro, EGO. Surprising: brand is explicitly the last step.

**C3 Plants & Living Cultures.** Ladder: where and how much light > plant type/genus > condition of the individual plant. Dutch shop filters care level, room, light before genus; mushroom shop leads with grow place then species, with skill level. Quality: health, no pests, match to light; RHS AGM (trial-based, hardiness rated) is the main award. Collectors identify by genus and cultivar (variegated aroids); scarcity drives price. Gaps: AAS judging and Thompson & Morgan not fetched.

**C4 Craft & Art Supplies.** Ladder: craft discipline (art, sewing, knitting, papercraft...) > craft type > product; grade, difficulty and quality tier are attributes, not levels. Quality: lightfastness (ASTM I-II), pigment density, single pigment, artist vs student grade; yarn by fibre then weight; machines by type, skill, brand. Signals: ASTM lightfastness (maker-applied), Colour Index codes. Ravelry shows yarn company and fibre as the community axis. Gaps: art-supply retailer pages (Blick, Utrecht) were blocked; ASTM definitions only from a search excerpt, not a fetched page.
