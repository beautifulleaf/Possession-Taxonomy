# S3C_A1S: fashion subcultures and their brands (A1-A4, scene tags C8)

43 rows, 33 distinct fetched sources (A1 33 rows, A2 4, A3 3, A4 3). Japanese-language: 9 sources; Korean: 2 (KREAM, Global MUSINSA); Chinese-language: 3 (CNPP, two Baidu Baike pages) plus one English-language Chinese lolita list (Stephano). Every URL was fetched through mcp__Parallel_Auth__web_fetch (allow_live_fetch on). Namu Wiki (分류:대한민국의 패션 브랜드) was blocked as an IDC address and returned no content, so it is not kept. Mercari JP brand index and Grailed designers index are kept but rendered empty (script-loaded).

Format per scene: anchor source to harvest first; gaps no fetched catalogue covers.

- **Streetwear and hypebeast:** anchor Streetwear Directory (135 labels, filters by country, decade, style); then Grailed designer index, KREAM, ZOZOTOWN. Gap: no catalogue assigns a scene tag per brand; Grailed Market facet (Hype) is not visible on the index page.
- **Skate:** anchor Wikipedia List of skateboarding brands (apparel and shoe sections). Gap: small and non-US skate labels (Polar, Palace-type) are covered only by Streetwear Directory; no Japanese skate source.
- **Surf:** anchor Wikipedia Category: Surfwear brands (56). Gap: Japanese, Australian and Brazilian surf labels thin; no surf community wiki found.
- **Techwear:** anchor disco Techwear guide (43+ brands, tiers) plus Aesthetics Wiki Techwear page for the definition. Gap: guide is commercial; Japanese techwear makers (Goldwin-type, White Mountaineering) not listed in a neutral catalogue.
- **Gorpcore and outdoor:** anchor disco Gorpcore/Outdoor guide (120+, named in the techwear page, not itself fetched) and MFA wiki Gorpcore guide. Gap: Japanese outdoor labels (Nanamica, Goldwin) and Korean outdoor brands only in prose; fetch the gorpcore guide before use.
- **Japanese Americana and heritage denim (Ametora):** anchor Heddels Japanese-denim guide (makers with lot numbers) plus AVEND 6-lineage map. Gap: Heddels brand tag pages and Denimhunters (S2) hold the long tail; Toyo Enterprise family and Real McCoy's lots need a per-maker source.
- **Ivy and trad / preppy:** anchor Ivy Style and the MFA wiki Preppy guide. Gap: no brand index; Japanese Ivy (VAN, J. Press Tokyo, Kamakura Shirts) is not covered.
- **Workwear:** anchor AVEND work lineage plus Streetwear Directory workwear filter; Wikipedia skate list gives Carhartt, Dickies, Ben Davis. Gap: no neutral workwear maker list (disco Workwear/Heritage guide exists, not fetched); European workwear (Blaklader, Le Laboureur) unlisted.
- **Mod, punk, goth, metal, emo, rave, glam, casuals, rockabilly (Western subcultures):** anchor Subculture Wiki hubs plus the Aesthetics Wiki; Subculture Wiki Casual Brands and 90s Hip Hop pages name makers. Gap: Subculture Wiki gives scene definitions, not brand lists; punk/metal/rave makers (Tripp, Killstar, Cyberdog, Demonia) need retailer or press pages.
- **Goth (Western) and gothic lolita:** anchor the Gothic Lolita Wiki (atwiki) plus ja.wikipedia ゴスロリブランド一覧. Gap: Western goth makers (Killstar, Restyle) not found in a catalogue.
- **Emo and scene:** definition only (Subculture Wiki emo hubs, Aesthetics Wiki). Gap: no maker catalogue.
- **Rockabilly and pin-up:** definition in Aesthetics Wiki Rockabilly (seen in search, not fetched) and Subculture Wiki rock 'n' roll hub. Gap: no maker catalogue (Hell Bunny, Collectif, Pinup Couture, Re-Mix); only a thin affiliate list appeared in search.
- **Lolita and J-fashion (lolita, gyaru, decora, mori, visual kei):** anchor Lolibrary for lolita brands and lines; Gothic Lolita Wiki for sub-tags; Stephano list (about 400 Chinese lolita brands with style tags). Gap: gyaru (109 labels), decora/fairy kei, mori kei, visual kei makers only in the Hatena gal/natural/rock pages (not all fetched) and Subculture Wiki prose.
- **Ura-Harajuku:** anchor ja.wikipedia 裏原宿 (definition and about 9 labels); extend with Fashion Press street filter and KREAM. Gap: Fashion Press filter pages are katakana-only and have no founders; Fragment/Sophnet/WTAPS/Neighborhood are found only on those pages.
- **Avant-garde (Antwerp Six, Japanese designers):** anchor Wikipedia Antwerp Six plus Streetwear Directory avant-garde filter. Gap: Japanese Big Three (Kawakubo, Yamamoto, Miyake) and Margiela-era Paris houses have no fetched list (a Museum at FIT study and a Selvane article were found in search only); Grailed avant-garde-archive tag page is a search result, not a catalogue.
- **Y2K:** no dedicated catalogue. Streetwear Directory has a Y2K filter; Depop brands A-Z carries Y2K-era labels; Aesthetics Wiki lists Y2K. Gap: McBling-era makers (Von Dutch, Juicy, Ed Hardy, Baby Phat) appear only in non-fetched blogs.
- **K-fashion:** anchor Global MUSINSA brand index (style facets Street Mood to Heritage Look) plus KREAM brand index. Gap: Korean-language Musinsa brand page and ZOZO K-FASHION section (link seen) not fetched; Namu Wiki list blocked.
- **Chinese guochao:** anchor Baidu Baike 国潮 for the definition and Poizon (得物) page for platform context; CNPP 潮牌 ranking for names. Gap: no neutral guochao maker list; CNPP is paid-listing; Poizon app brand index not reachable as a page.
- **Western and cowboy:** only hatena amekaji (Wrangler) and MFA wiki Western guide (linked posts). Gap: no maker catalogue fetched (Stetson, Resistol, Ariat, Lucchese appear only in a retailer blog found in search).
- **Hip-hop:** anchor Subculture Wiki 90s Hip Hop (FUBU, Karl Kani, Phat Farm, Timberland); Vestiaire lists Coogi. Gap: Japanese B-girl and 2000s labels (Rocawear, Sean John, Mecca) have no fetched list.
- **Menswear forums and brand wikis:** MFA wiki guides, Styleforum Streetwear and Denim forum, Die, Workwear!, Heddels. Gap: Reddit wikis hold links, not brand tables; Styleforum needs per-thread fetches; Die, Workwear has no index.
- **Resale catalogues with brand indexes:** Grailed (script-loaded), Vestiaire (A-Z), Depop (A-Z letter pages), Mercari JP (script-loaded, empty), KREAM, ZOZOTOWN. Gap: Poizon and Mercari JP brand lists did not render; none of the six assigns scene tags, only brand names.
- **Japanese and Korean brand directories:** Fashion Collect (8,962 Japanese labels), Fashion Press (style/category filters), ZOZOTOWN (11,633), KREAM, Global MUSINSA. Gap: neither Fashion Collect sub-pages by item/designer nor Fashion Press filter result pages were opened.
