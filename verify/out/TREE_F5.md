# F5 Documents & Personal Records: decided tree

Category: F5 Documents & Personal Records (domain F, Culture, Records & Meaning). Membership test (placement key step 19): a document issued to prove identity, status, ownership or achievement (passport, licence, certificate, deed, diploma, share certificate, identity and access badges); a card, pass or token issued to give access to an account, service, place or stored value (payment, gift, transit, membership and key cards), whether live, used or expired; or a personal record or keepsake paper: letters, written diaries, filled photo albums, photographic prints, developed negatives and slides, home recordings on a physical carrier, filled scrapbooks, picture postcards used or not, autographs, kept tickets and programmes, house plans, and other kept printed ephemera (menus, invitations, trade cards, receipts, labels, bookplates). Not here: published works and blank or published recording media (F1); a photographic print issued as an edition or signed or stamped by its maker as a work, and posters (F2, K-139); coins, banknotes, stamps, medals, tokens, scrip, postal stationery, administration-issued and first-day covers, numbered collectible card series (F3, K-118, K-121); prayer and holy cards (F4, K-112); blank notebooks, diaries, greeting cards and calendars (C5); blank scrapbook albums (C4); empty multi-object albums, binders, stock books, boxes and trays (B1, K-9); worn badges and patches (A3); medical alert tags (H2); wallets, card holders and passport covers (A4); hardware and security keys and blank RFID cards (D1); the entitlement behind a pass, membership or season ticket (I3, F-22) and the stored balance behind a card (OUT); photographs and recordings held as files (I2). Structure (Candidate A, function first, decided; memo sections 3, 4b, 9, 10, 12 and 14): sub-family by what the object is for (it proves a fact; it gives access; it keeps a record), then leaf (fifteen leaves, the leaf level kept in all three sub-families, F-21; one stated axis each), then kind (the document or card type, the carrier for photographic and recorded records, what the record holds for filled albums, the form of paper for written records and ephemera), then named form (ICAO passport codes, issuer designs in the issuer-design register, F5-O1; transit card systems as maker-bound forms tied to C13 rows; photographic mount formats; the ketubah; not listed here). Maximum depth below the category: four levels. Issuing state, issuer, era (postcard eras Pioneer, Divided Back, golden age 1895-1914 as `postcard_era`), material, card `technology` and `acceptance`, ISO/IEC 7810 and ICAO size classes, album `page_mechanism`, `standard_met` (ISO 18902, PAT), photographic `process` and `mount_format`, `print_process` (chromolithograph, collotype, halftone, real photo; on the picture postcard and the photographic print), `document_class` (ALS, LS, TLS, DS, AL, AQS, AMQS, ANS, SP, cut signature: one classification row, stored on the kinds that admit it, letter, autograph and the issued documents; never a named form), `motif` (subject tags on publisher-series named forms), edition (signed), occasion, motive and subject are attributes, tags or views, never kinds or leaves (section 14). Condition, balance, PIN, serial number and provenance of one object are not stored (C16); legal classes (licence categories, visa and permit types) go to the C17 ledger. Tier type: preference, no tier on any kind and no price band (section 5). Maker role (maker_roles.tsv, provisional): none; issuers sit in the shared issuer table, and card makers, security printers, postcard publishers and album makers appear only through named forms. Display groups (navigation proposal L2, display only, no id change, no group ids issued): Letters, autographs and manuscripts (F5.3.1, with the autograph F5.3.5.04 cross-listed); Postcards and printed ephemera (F5.3.5, picture postcard first, with the real photo postcard named form of F5.3.2.01 cross-listed); Photographs, slides and negatives (F5.3.2); Certificates, deeds and financial papers (F5.1.2, F5.1.3, F5.1.4); Identity and travel papers (F5.1.1, F5.1.5); Cards and passes (F5.2.1 to F5.2.5); Albums and scrapbooks (F5.3.4); Home recordings (F5.3.3). Quality markers (display, judged on named forms and classes, never on the kind; provisional): letters and autographs by the writer's identity (a view through named forms only), rarity, content, condition, with the document-class ladder as dealers list it (ALS > LS > TLS > DS > AL > AQS > AMQS > cut signature, provisional order); postcards by age (golden age 1895 to the First World War), motif and artist, condition, print process, intact stamp; ephemera generally by historical insight, notable creators or signers, beauty and design, rarity and condition, significance; one's own documents and cards: preference, no ranking. Pending kinds: the memo marks 21 kinds † (twenty from section 3.4 and the section 14 printed advertisement or handbill); their ids are allocated here in memo order, each line ending in †, and a † kind enters the data only when a fetched page attests it. Leaves F5.1.5 (two of three), F5.2.4 (one of two) and F5.2.5 (both) are thin on attestation; F5.2.5 holds only † kinds and is provisional with them. Provisional levels: bookplate, timetable, bookmark and the printed advertisement or handbill are kinds only if a fetched seller or collector source compares each as its own category (C2(a)), otherwise named forms of the nearest kind (R-19; the advertisement of broadside). No parts sub-family: none attested; it opens at harvest on one fetched page for a holder or sleeve made for one F5 object (CV-17), and its kinds then take the next free sub-family number.

Ids (R-58): F5.n is a sub-family and F5.n.m a leaf; a kind is F5.n.m.kk, or F5.n.kk where a sub-family has no leaf.

## F5.1 Issued documents
Leaf axis: the fact the document proves (identity and right to travel; a life event; a right or obligation; a standing conferred; who the holder is or what site they may enter). Sub-family axis: what the object is for, it proves a fact. `document_class` DS applies to the issued documents.

### F5.1.1 Identity & travel documents
Leaf axis: the claim (who you are, the right to travel). Display group: Identity and travel papers. ICAO passport codes and each issuer's own designs are named forms; ISO/IEC 7810 and ICAO TD sizes are attributes; the issuing state is a tag.
- F5.1.1.01 Passport: booklet issued by a state to identify its holder for international travel; ICAO codes (PP, PE, PD, PO, PR, PT, PS, PL, PM) and issuer designs are named forms.
- F5.1.1.02 National identity card: card issued by a state to prove the holder's identity and citizenship or residence (Jumindeungrokjeung, Nafnskírteini, CURP card, Carteira Nacional de Identificação as named forms; My Number card and Personalausweis named forms †).
- F5.1.1.03 Driving licence: document issued to permit its holder to drive; licence categories are a C17 entry, issuer designs named forms.
- F5.1.1.04 Residence permit: document issued to a non-citizen to prove the right to reside (Taiwan's Alien Resident Certificate as a named form).
- F5.1.1.05 Visa: label, stamp or sheet issued to permit entry to or stay in a country; visa types are a C17 entry.

### F5.1.2 Civil-status certificates
Leaf axis: the life event recorded. Display group: Certificates, deeds and financial papers.
- F5.1.2.01 Birth certificate: certificate recording a person's birth.
- F5.1.2.02 Marriage certificate: certificate recording a marriage (ketubah a named form †; F2 when made as a picture).
- F5.1.2.03 Death certificate: certificate recording a person's death.
- F5.1.2.04 Divorce papers: decree or certificate recording the dissolution of a marriage.
- F5.1.2.05 Baptism certificate: certificate recording a baptism or christening (F2 when made as a picture, K-112). †
- F5.1.2.06 Vaccination certificate: paper record or card certifying vaccinations received (F-22; H2 holds only devices and supplies). †

### F5.1.3 Title & legal instruments
Leaf axis: the right or obligation created. Display group: Certificates, deeds and financial papers (share and bond certificates also in the scripophily view).
- F5.1.3.01 Deed: document that conveys or proves title to property (the land itself is G2).
- F5.1.3.02 Will: document setting out how a person's estate is to be disposed of after death.
- F5.1.3.03 Promissory note: signed written promise to pay a sum to a named party.
- F5.1.3.04 Share certificate: printed certificate of ownership of shares in a company (F5, not F3, K-27).
- F5.1.3.05 Bond certificate: printed certificate of a bond or debt owed by its issuer.
- F5.1.3.06 Cheque: printed order to a bank to pay a sum, kept as an object (K-27). †
- F5.1.3.07 Traveller's cheque: pre-paid cheque in a fixed amount for travellers, kept as an object (K-27). †
- F5.1.3.08 Postal order: money order issued by a post office, kept as an object (K-27). †
- F5.1.3.09 Tax paper (return, assessment): paper tax return or assessment notice kept as a record (F-22). †

### F5.1.4 Achievement & membership certificates
Leaf axis: the standing conferred. Display group: Certificates, deeds and financial papers.
- F5.1.4.01 Diploma: document issued by a school or university to certify a degree or completed course.
- F5.1.4.02 Certificate (of achievement, training or membership): document certifying an achievement, a qualification or membership of a body.
- F5.1.4.03 School report: report issued by a school on a pupil's progress (F-22). †

### F5.1.5 Identity & access badges
Leaf axis: what the carried credential shows (who the holder is, or what site they may enter). Display group: Identity and travel papers. A badge worn as ornament is A3.
- F5.1.5.01 Identity badge: badge or card bearing the holder's name or photograph, carried to show who the holder is (a door-opening function is an attribute, P5). †
- F5.1.5.02 Access badge: badge or pass bearing the holder's name or photograph, carried to show what site or event the holder may enter. †
- F5.1.5.03 Crew member certificate: identity document issued to airline crew under the ICAO and ISO/IEC 7810 formats.

## F5.2 Cards, passes & tokens
Leaf axis: what the card gives access to (funds, stored value, transit, a benefit, a lock or network). Sub-family axis: what the object is for, it gives access to an account, service, place or stored value. F5 whether live, used or expired; the balance or entitlement behind the card is OUT or I3. `technology` (magnetic, chip, optical, contactless), `acceptance` and ISO/IEC 7810 format are attributes.

### F5.2.1 Payment cards
Leaf axis: how the funds are held. Display group: Cards and passes.
- F5.2.1.01 Credit card: card that draws on a line of credit from its issuer.
- F5.2.1.02 Debit card: card that draws directly on the holder's bank account.
- F5.2.1.03 Charge card: card whose balance must be paid in full each period.
- F5.2.1.04 ATM card: card that gives access to a bank account at cash machines only.

### F5.2.2 Stored-value cards & vouchers
Leaf axis: what the value buys. Display group: Cards and passes.
- F5.2.2.01 Gift card: card holding a prepaid value redeemable for goods or services (`acceptance` closed-system or semi-closed is an attribute).
- F5.2.2.02 Gift certificate: paper voucher or gift token redeemable only with its issuer (circulating scrip is F3).
- F5.2.2.03 Telephone card: prepaid card for public telephones (`technology` optical, chip or magnetic is an attribute).

### F5.2.3 Transit cards
Leaf axis: the journey the stored value or pass pays for. Display group: Cards and passes.
- F5.2.3.01 Transit farecard: stored-value or pass card for public transport fares (Suica, Octopus, EZ-Link, China T-Union, SUBE, Beep, Washington Metro farecard as maker-bound named forms tied to C13 rows).

### F5.2.4 Membership & loyalty cards
Leaf axis: the benefit the card carries (standing in a body, or discounts and points at a shop). Display group: Cards and passes. The membership itself is I3 (F-22).
- F5.2.4.01 Membership card: card issued to show the holder's membership of a club or body. †
- F5.2.4.02 Loyalty card: card issued by a retailer to collect discounts or points.

### F5.2.5 Key & network-access cards
Leaf axis: the lock, place or network the card opens. Display group: Cards and passes.
- F5.2.5.01 Key card: card that opens a lock and names no holder (a hotel key card, P5). †
- F5.2.5.02 SIM card: physical subscriber identity module card for a mobile network (an eSIM profile is part of its I3 plan, K-28). †

## F5.3 Personal records & keepsake papers
Leaf axis: how the record was made or what it holds (written or drawn; photographic; recorded; compiled; printed and kept). Sub-family axis: what the object is for, it keeps a record of a person's life. Being written on, drawn on or recorded on is not condition.

### F5.3.1 Written & drawn records
Leaf axis: a record written, typed or drawn on paper, by its form. Display group: Letters, autographs and manuscripts. `document_class` (ALS, LS, TLS and the rest) is an attribute of the letter.
- F5.3.1.01 Letter: written or typed message sent or kept on paper, signed letters included (`document_class`).
- F5.3.1.02 Diary or journal: written personal diary or journal.
- F5.3.1.03 Kept greeting card (written in): greeting card that has been written in and kept (a blank one is C5).
- F5.3.1.04 Filled notebook: notebook that has been written in and kept (a blank one is C5). †
- F5.3.1.05 House plans: drawn or printed plans of a dwelling kept as a record. †
- F5.3.1.06 Postally used cover: envelope that passed through the post, neither administration-issued nor a first-day cover (K-118). †

### F5.3.2 Photographic records
Leaf axis: photographic carrier. Display group: Photographs, slides and negatives. `process`, `mount_format` and `print_process` are attributes; any subject; an edition, or a print signed or stamped by its maker as a work, is F2 (K-139).
- F5.3.2.01 Photographic print: photograph printed on paper or card (carte de visite, cabinet card, real photo postcard, snapshot as named forms).
- F5.3.2.02 Slide: developed transparency mounted for projection or viewing.
- F5.3.2.03 Negative (developed): developed photographic negative on film or plate.

### F5.3.3 Home recordings
Leaf axis: recorded carrier. Display group: Home recordings. A recording held as a file is I2; blank media and published recordings are F1.
- F5.3.3.01 Home film reel: film shot by the owner or family on a reel (8 mm, Super 8, 16 mm). †
- F5.3.3.02 Home videotape: videotape recorded by the owner or family.
- F5.3.3.03 Home audio recording: sound recorded by the owner or family on a physical carrier (tape, disc). †

### F5.3.4 Compiled records
Leaf axis: what the compiled record holds (prints, or papers of every kind). Display group: Albums and scrapbooks. `page_mechanism` (slip-in or pocket, ring-binder, post-bound, stick-in, accordion, box) and `standard_met` are attributes; the same album empty is B1 (K-9).
- F5.3.4.01 Photo album (filled): album holding photographic prints.
- F5.3.4.02 Scrapbook (filled): album filled with kept papers of every kind (a blank scrapbook album is C4).

### F5.3.5 Kept printed ephemera
Leaf axis: printed paper kept after use, by its form; types are kinds by the C2 choice-level test, otherwise named forms (K-140). Display group: Postcards and printed ephemera (the autograph also under Letters, autographs and manuscripts). Topic, period and subject are tags.
- F5.3.5.01 Picture postcard: printed picture postcard, used or not (`postcard_era` and `print_process` are attributes; a real photo postcard is F5.3.2.01).
- F5.3.5.02 Ticket stub: used ticket kept after the event or journey.
- F5.3.5.03 Programme: printed programme of a performance or event, kept.
- F5.3.5.04 Autograph: signature collected on a card or page (`document_class` cut signature, SP and the like).
- F5.3.5.05 Menu: printed menu of a restaurant, banquet or voyage, kept.
- F5.3.5.06 Invitation: printed invitation to an event, kept.
- F5.3.5.07 Advertising trade card: card issued by a business to advertise it (a card issued in a numbered series to be collected is F3).
- F5.3.5.08 Printed advertisement or handbill: single printed advertising item kept after use (handbill, flyer, leaflet, a detached magazine or newspaper advertisement page); a large public notice is a broadside, a poster F2, a catalogue or booklet F1 (section 14; a named form of broadside if not attested as its own category). †
- F5.3.5.09 Business card: printed card giving a person's or firm's name and contact details.
- F5.3.5.10 Receipt (billheads included): printed or written receipt or billhead, kept.
- F5.3.5.11 Label: printed product or trade label, kept.
- F5.3.5.12 Broadside: large single printed sheet issued as a public notice or announcement.
- F5.3.5.13 Bookplate: printed label pasted in a book to mark its owner (level provisional). †
- F5.3.5.14 Timetable: printed timetable of a transport service, kept (level provisional). †
- F5.3.5.15 Bookmark: printed or card bookmark, kept as ephemera (level provisional). †

## Precedence inside F5

- One home per object: a named form, issuer design, card system or signed state is a ledger row or attribute under one kind and never a second home; occasion, motive, subject and topic never decide a home.
- P1. A blank notebook, diary, calendar or greeting card is C5; once written in it is a personal record in F5 (F5.3.1.02 diary or journal, F5.3.1.03 kept greeting card, F5.3.1.04 filled notebook); a picture postcard is F5.3.5.01 used or not; a blank scrapbook album is C4 (K-9), and the filled scrapbook is F5.3.4.02.
- P2 (K-139, K-141). A photographic print issued as an edition, or signed or stamped by its maker as a work, is F2; every other photographic print (F5.3.2.01), slide (F5.3.2.02) or negative (F5.3.2.03), whoever is shown, is F5, so a stranger's cabinet card is F5.3.2.01 with the named form cabinet card. Home film, tape and audio recordings on a physical carrier are F5.3.3.01 to F5.3.3.03; the same pictures or recordings held as files are I2; posters are F2; unexposed film is D4; blank recording media and published recordings are F1. A real photo postcard is a photographic print (F5.3.2.01), listed also in the postcard view (C14). Every photographic print carries `process`, `mount_format` and `print_process` as attributes. A studio imprint on a cabinet card is read as a maker's mark, not an edition mark (reading risk, K-139 probe undecided).
- P3 (K-27, K-28, K-118, F-16, D-23). Payment, gift, telephone, transit, membership and key cards (F5.2.1 to F5.2.5) are F5 whether live, used or expired; the balance or entitlement behind them is OUT. A physical SIM card is F5.2.5.02 and an eSIM profile is part of its I3 plan. Money as an object (coins, banknotes, bullion, stamps, medals) is F3; a certificate or deed that proves a right (F5.1.3.04 share certificate, F5.1.3.05 bond certificate, F5.1.4.01 diploma, F5.1.4.02 certificate) is F5, and a printed cheque, traveller's cheque or postal order kept as an object is F5.1.3.06 to F5.1.3.08. A voucher or gift certificate redeemable only with its issuer is F5.2.2.02; a token issued to stand for money or a service, and paper issued to circulate as money (scrip, emergency money, Notgeld), are F3. Postal stationery with a printed stamp, and a cover issued by a postal administration or prepared as a first-day cover, are F3; any other used envelope is F5.3.1.06, a letter F5.3.1.01 and a picture postcard F5.3.5.01, whatever stamps it carries. Blank RFID tags and cards are D1; issued cards are F5; a code unlocking one game is D5, a subscription code I3.
- P4 (F-22). The entitlement (the subscription, membership or season right) is I3 and the physical card, pass or token issued to carry it is F5 (a gym membership card is F5.2.4.01); vaccination, school and tax papers are F5 (F5.1.2.06, F5.1.4.03, F5.1.3.09) and H2 holds only devices and supplies; a hardware key, security key or smartphone wallet is D1 or D2; the wallet, card holder and passport cover are A4.
- P5 (K-112). An identity or access badge is F5.1.5.01 or F5.1.5.02 and a badge or patch worn as ornament is A3; a medical alert tag is H2; a deed or certificate is F5 whatever it concerns (the land is G2, the sacrament's objects F4, the occasion a modifier). A ketubah (named form of F5.1.2.02) or a baptism certificate (F5.1.2.05) is F5, or F2 when made as a picture; a prayer card is F4. A card that shows the holder's name or photograph and is carried to show who the holder is or what site they may enter is an identity or access badge (F5.1.5.01, F5.1.5.02); a card that only opens a lock and names no holder (a hotel key card) is a key card (F5.2.5.01); the other function is an attribute.
- P6 (K-9). A holder, case, sleeve or stand made for one object follows it (S3). An album, binder, stock book, box or tray made to hold several objects is B1 when empty; a filled photo album (F5.3.4.01) or scrapbook (F5.3.4.02) is F5; a blank scrapbook album is C4. Document binders, boxes and folders made to hold papers of every kind are filing supplies (C5) or storage (B1) and never F5. The filled photo album is one kind with `page_mechanism` as an attribute; the empty product and its lines are B1.
- P7 (K-140, R-0). An advertising or trade card issued by a business is F5.3.5.07; a card issued in a numbered series to be collected is F3 (F3-O3, K-121). A single printed advertisement or handbill is F5.3.5.08, a large public notice F5.3.5.12, a poster F2 (section 14). Published booklets, pamphlets, catalogues and sheet music are F1.
- P8 (K-140). Types of kept printed ephemera (F5.3.5.05 menu, F5.3.5.06 invitation, F5.3.5.10 receipt, F5.3.5.11 label, F5.3.5.13 bookplate, F5.3.5.14 timetable, F5.3.5.15 bookmark, F5.3.5.12 broadside, F5.3.5.08 printed advertisement or handbill) are kinds only by the choice-level test (C2(a): collectors or sellers compare the type as its own category); otherwise they are named forms of the nearest kind. One fetched page admits a type either way.
- A signed photograph or autograph letter is a photographic print (F5.3.2.01) or a letter (F5.3.1.01) with `document_class` and a signed tag; only a signature collected on a blank card or page is an autograph (F5.3.5.04). The letter classes (ALS, LS, TLS, DS and the rest) are one classification row, never named forms (section 14).
- Postcard motifs (place views, Jugendstil, railway and ships, military) are `motif` tags on publisher-series named forms and drive views only, never kinds (section 14).
