# HOL_DP_7 report (E1 E2 E3 E4)

Stage-2 proposals from evidence batch HOL_EV_7 (35 rows; retailer menus thin for E2 and E4, no community source for E2–E4) and the memos. Outputs: `HOL_DP_7_proposals.tsv` (4 rows) and `HOL_DP_7_groups.tsv`. Only E1 has a tree in data/nodes.tsv, and it has no kinds yet, so its 9 level-1 and 23 level-2 groups list node ids. E2, E3 and E4 are not built and have no group ids. No kind ids change. Every call rests on 6 to 10 fetched pages and is **provisional**.

**E1 Toys & Games.** People choose by type of toy (play family): 9 display groups over the existing sub-families. Age is a filter. Games go by their component set. Genre, mechanic and theme never divide E1, so the evidence's genre-first ladder (Hexagamers) is not used. What makes the best one: kit fit and skill grade, finish class for figures, and component quality. Safety marks are a pass-or-fail gate. Quality signals: Spiel des Jahres, maker skill grades, Gunpla grades. Maker role revised to **way in**, because BRIEF lists toys among the regional maker harvests, and a brand or publisher tab sits beside the main path. Flags: player count and playing time could be facets (a partial review of E-9); is faction a tag or an IE link?; diorama kits.

**E2 Sports.** People choose by sport or activity, then the equipment. Women, men and kids is a user tag. What makes the best one: for safety gear, certification comes first, then fit; for fitness machines, flywheel and stability; for boots, comfort, support and traction. Quality signals: the UIAA Safety Label and its database, CE, OutdoorGearLab and RunRepeat scores, and ISO 20957 (search excerpt only). Maker role stays owner, with a brand tab inside each sport. Flags: spin, upright and recumbent bikes; pickleball and padel leaves are missing. The display order is the thinnest-evidenced call.

**E3 Music.** People choose by instrument family or gear job (11 display groups; controllers join synths). Below that, guitars go by named body design. Music genre never divides E3, so the sound engine and form factor stand in for it. What makes the best one: playability and build, pickups, construction; for pianos, soundboard and action; for interfaces, EIN and dynamic range. Quality signals: maker line ladders, the player_level scheme and piano reviewer ladders. Price tiers are bands only. Maker role stays way in for instruments; owner-only is proposed for studio, PA and effects gear. Flags: more body-design named forms; groovebox and desktop synth kinds.

**E4 Weapons & Armour.** People choose by weapon family, with a use view beside it (hunting, sport, self-defence, collecting, martial arts). Use is never the tree. Arrows and pellets are shown with bows and air guns; swords and armour get an antique / modern toggle. What makes the best one: for knives, steel and heat treatment, then tang; for bows and rifles, fit and use; for armour, NIJ level as a floor. NBTHK papers go to the scale registry only. Maker role split: way in for blades and armourers, owner for the rest. Flags: an everyday-carry knife kind?; a steel reference table.
