## Task 3: navigation
**By shopper path.** B9 is not scene-driven. Makers are the way in only for cigars, pipes and pipe tobacco, and there they are concentrated: Cornell & Diehl has 9 of 11 blends. Every harvested shop (hookah.com, Vape Superstore, Leafly, SmokingPipes) opens on the product family.

The view:
1. **What do you smoke or use?** The 10 display groups.
2. **Which family?**
   - Cigars: house, or Parejos / Figurados
   - Pipes: shape family or maker
   - Pipe tobacco: blend family
   - Vapes: kit type / e-liquids / parts
   - Cannabis: flower / pre-rolls / concentrates / infused / glass
   - Hookah: hookahs / shisha / bowls, hoses, heat and coals
3. **Kinds, then named forms or houses.**
