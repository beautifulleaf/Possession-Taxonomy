# S3C_E source census: Play, Sport & Music (E1 to E4)

Table: `verify/out/S3C_E.tsv` (77 rows: E1 31, E2 11, E3 19, E4 16). Candidate sources carried from `S2_E*_sources.tsv` (Hamleys, Smyths, Zatu, Decathlon, Thomann product pages, Frankonia, Sportsman's and others) were not re-fetched here and are not in the table; use them as retail cross-checks.

Owner rule (2026-10-17) applied to E1: board game genres, mechanics and themes go to the future Interactive Entertainment taxonomy. BoardGameGeek is kept as a source for objects only (titles as named forms, publishers, editions, components). Its mechanic, category and family browse pages were left out of the table.

**E1 Toys & Games.** Anchor: Scalemates brands (kits, scale, makers) for models; Brickset for LEGO lines; BGG publishers and titles for games; WOPC for card makers and suit patterns. Gaps: no single catalogue covers plush, wooden or preschool toys, outdoor play equipment (IPEMA gives only a certification page), gambling tables and slot makers beyond a trade directory, or ride-on toys. Model railway makers have only a Wikipedia category (a German H0 directory with 195 makers returned 403). Diecast outside Hot Wheels (Matchbox, Majorette, Tomica) and TCGs other than Magic and Pokémon have no census entry.

**E2 Sports, Fitness & Recreation.** Anchor: governing-body registries (USGA, ITTF, World Athletics, FIBA) for makers and approved models by sport, plus Intersport's 300-brand A to Z for brands in retail. Gaps: no cross-sport catalogue of models. Fitness machines, camping and outdoor gear, water sports, skiing, cycling shoes, climbing and team-sport apparel are only covered through retailer trees from S2 and a Wikipedia category. Racket sports beyond table tennis (tennis, badminton) have only maker catalogues. Chinese and Korean sport catalogues were not found.

**E3 Musical Instruments & Music Gear.** Anchor: Reverb (240,000-product price guide and category tree), with Thomann's A to Z brand list and ModularGrid for synth and effects modules. Kinds: MusicBrainz instrument list plus MIMO; makers of violin-family instruments: Cozio (paywalled). Gaps: woodwind makers (no list found, brass has one), orchestral percussion beyond Wikipedia lists, traditional instruments of India, the Middle East and Africa (only a Chinese-language list was found), and luthiers and small workshops, which no open catalogue covers. Vintage Guitar's price guide is paywalled.

**E4 Weapons & Armour.** Anchor: Modern Firearms and IMFDB for firearm models (IMFDB includes fictional props), Wikipedia brand lists for makers, CIP and SAAMI for cartridges. Cold steel: Royal Armouries and Touken World; knives: KnifeCenter brand filter. Armour: NIJ compliant products list. Gaps: bows and crossbows (two Wikipedia list pages tried returned 404), ammunition makers (SAAMI lists standards, not makers), historical armour makers, non-Japanese swordsmiths, European and Asian firearm catalogues, and ready lists of knife makers outside one retailer. Legal-class data (C17) was not in scope for this census.

No category lacks a usable catalogue, but E3 woodwinds and world instruments and E4 bows are the weakest.
