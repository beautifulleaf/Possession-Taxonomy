# B8 Home Safety & Security: decided tree

Category: B8 Home Safety & Security (domain B, Home). Membership test (placement key step 30, fourth branch, CATEGORIES_v0_7): made to secure or protect a home, its people and its contents, to hold doors and windows, or to keep pests out: locks and safes for any contents, smoke, carbon-monoxide and gas alarms, fire extinguishers and fire blankets, intruder alarms, security cameras, doorbells and door entry, baby and room monitors, stair gates and child-safety latches and guards, letterboxes and parcel boxes, door stops, anti-climb spikes and security window film, pest traps, baits, swatters, zappers, room sprays, coils, vaporisers and room repellents, mosquito nets, insect screens, bird spikes and bird netting for buildings, water-leak detectors, and parts made for one B8 object. Not here: locks, chains and anchors made for a bicycle, motorbike, caravan or car (G1; a ground anchor fixed in a floor or wall is B8); devices that spray, shock or strike, and firearms (E4); worn alarms that call help after a fall or medical event, breathing or health-sensing monitors and first-aid kits (H2); skin-applied insect repellent (H1); cameras made for making pictures, wildlife trail cameras included (D4); general network drives (D1); gates, pens, cameras and GPS trackers sold only for pets (B10); lights without a camera (B3); lockers, lockable and filing cabinets, cots, bed guard rails, playpens and freestanding play yards (B1); hinges, plain handles, cabinet knobs and pulls, door closers, doors, windows, shutters, fences, fence gates and panels, pool barriers, letter plates fitted in a door, and regulatory and safety signs (C8); gravel of any kind, anti-burglar gravel included (C2 or C8 as stone); plant protection, weed killers and garden crop netting (C2); field and livestock pest control (C7); snares, leg-hold and body-grip traps for game or fur (E2); lock picks and pick sets (C1); cleaning and laundry products (B6); cloud video plans (I3). Structure (Candidate A, decided B-9; memo sections 3 and 10): sub-family by the hazard defended against, closed by a parts sub-family exempt from that axis (CV-17); leaf by the device job (B8.3 by the site of the hazard and the job there, B8.4 by control method), stated per sub-family; B8.5 and B8.6 have no leaf; kind at the form where buyers compare grade or maker (memo section 4b). Each object gets one kind. Lock mechanism (pin tumbler, wafer, disc detainer, lever, dimple), lever count, opening and access method (key, code, card, fingerprint, app; DPH `access_method`), connectivity (smart), power source, sensing technology, installation (freestanding, wall, floor, furniture), setting (indoor, outdoor), size, capacity, material, colour, keyed-alike, target pest, active ingredient, refill system and the user (baby, child, older adult, pet) are attributes or tags, never kinds; "smart lock", "keypad lock", "wall safe", "floor safe", "fire safe", "fire-resistant safe", "burglary-resistant safe" and "gun safe" are not kinds. Measured grades are attributes with the standard named (DPH attributes `burglary_rating`, `fire_rating`, `lock_security_rating`, `attack_resistance`, `alarm_sensor`, `alarm_power`, `video_storage`), pass/fail standards are `standard_met`, graded certifier levels (Sold Secure) are `certification`. Kits (intruder alarm system, camera system kit) are kinds only when sold, standardised and compared as one object (K-1), provisional until attested. A kind marked † is named by the key, a decision or a neighbour memo without a fetched page in the memo; its id is fixed and it is attested in the stage-3 harvest. Maker role: owner (maker_roles.tsv, provisional): devices are chosen by function and grade, makers come through models; high-security lock makers are an identity pocket and camera and doorbell ecosystems a side view (C13). Display groups (navigation proposal, display only, no id changes): Break-in and theft (B8.1); Fire, smoke and gas (B8.2); Injuries at home (B8.3; child, baby, older adult and pet safety as a user view); Pest control (B8.4); Water leaks (B8.5); Parts and refills (B8.6); level 2 follows the leaves. Quality markers (display): locks, padlocks, safes and anchors by the third-party rated grade first (EN 1143-1, UL RSC and TL ratings; TS 007 stars, BS 3621, Sold Secure Bronze to Diamond, CEN 1 to 6, ANSI/BHMA grades, VdS classes), then the attacks resisted (anti-snap, pick, drill, bump), then the fire class (a fire rating does not make a burglary safe), then fit; LPU belts for enthusiasts; smoke and CO alarms by sensor type and certification (EN 14604, AS 3786), sealed 10-year battery, interlink; cameras and doorbells by day and night video quality, alert speed, smart detection, data security and local or cloud storage (Consumer Reports); extinguishers by fire class rating (13A, 34B); gates and guards by standard met (EN 1930, ASTM F1004) and mounting for the top of stairs; pests by target pest and form (no grade).

Ids (R-58): B8.n is a sub-family and B8.n.m a leaf; a kind is B8.n.m.kk, or B8.n.kk where a sub-family has no leaf. B8's builder ids (memo section 13, frozen tree audit/KL_B8_tree_v2.md) are adopted as built under R-58 and kept exactly, with unpadded kind numbers (B8.1.1.1, B8.5.1).

## B8.1 Break-in, theft & attack

Leaf axis: the job the device does (lock a door; restrain a door or window; lock a loose item; store securely; detect and sound; watch and answer the door; receive mail; deter climbing and glass break).

### B8.1.1 Door locks
- B8.1.1.1 Night latch: a rim-mounted latch lock on the inside face of a door, with a latch bolt and a key cylinder or snib.
- B8.1.1.2 Rim lock: a box lock fixed to the surface of the door, with a bolt that shoots into a staple or keep.
- B8.1.1.3 Euro cylinder: a profile cylinder (the key-operated barrel) that fits a euro-profile mortice lock case.
- B8.1.1.4 Mortice deadlock: a lock case sunk into the door edge whose bolt is thrown by key only and does not spring back.
- B8.1.1.5 Mortice sashlock: a lock case sunk into the door edge with both a spring latch and a key-thrown deadbolt.
- B8.1.1.6 Deadbolt: a stand-alone bolt lock (single or double cylinder, or thumb-turn) fitted through the door with a throw bolt.
- B8.1.1.7 Knob or lever lockset: a handle set (knob or lever) that includes a lock, fitted through the door.
- B8.1.1.8 Multipoint lock: a door lock that throws bolts or hooks into the frame at several points along the door edge.
- B8.1.1.9 Patio door lock: a lock made for a sliding or French (patio) door.

### B8.1.2 Window & door restraints
- B8.1.2.1 Door bolt: a sliding bolt fixed to a door to hold it shut.
- B8.1.2.2 Door chain: a short chain that limits how far a door can open.
- B8.1.2.3 Window lock: a lock or catch fixed to a window or sash to stop it opening, lockable window handles included.

### B8.1.3 Padlocks, chains & anchors
- B8.1.3.1 Open-shackle padlock: a padlock whose shackle is largely exposed above the body.
- B8.1.3.2 Closed-shackle padlock: a padlock whose body shrouds most of the shackle to resist cutting.
- B8.1.3.3 Hasp and staple: a hinged strap and eye fixed to a door and frame, through which a padlock is passed.
- B8.1.3.4 Security chain: a heavy hardened chain made to secure gates, sheds or loose items with a padlock.
- B8.1.3.5 Cable lock: a flexible steel cable with a lock, used to secure loose items.
- B8.1.3.6 Ground anchor: a heavy anchor fixed in a floor or wall that an item is chained or locked to.

### B8.1.4 Safes & secure storage
- B8.1.4.1 Safe [金庫]: a steel strongbox with a lock, built to resist forced entry (and often fire), in any installation (freestanding, wall, floor, furniture) and any grade.
- B8.1.4.2 Fire-resistant safe, document box or chest: a container rated against fire only, cabinet or portable lid-opening chest, made to protect papers and media from fire.
- B8.1.4.3 Key safe (outdoor key box) [キーボックス]: a small lockable box, often wall-mounted outdoors, holding a door key.
- B8.1.4.4 Key cabinet: a lockable cabinet fitted with hooks for many keys, sold among security goods.
- B8.1.4.5 Cash box: a small portable lockable box for money.
- B8.1.4.6 Firearms cabinet or gun safe [Waffenschrank]: a lockable steel cabinet or safe made to store guns.

### B8.1.5 Alarms & sensors
- B8.1.5.1 Intruder alarm system: a kit of control panel, sensors and sounder sold and compared as one burglar-alarm system (provisional until attested, K-1).
- B8.1.5.2 Motion sensor: a detector that senses movement in a room and signals or sounds.
- B8.1.5.3 Door and window contact alarm: a magnetic contact that sounds or signals when a door or window opens.
- B8.1.5.4 Personal attack alarm: a hand-held device that makes a loud noise to deter an attacker.

### B8.1.6 Cameras, doorbells & door entry
- B8.1.6.1 Security camera: a fixed camera made to watch and record a place for protection.
- B8.1.6.2 Pan-tilt security camera: a security camera with a motor that turns and tilts it.
- B8.1.6.3 Floodlight camera: a security camera built into a floodlight.
- B8.1.6.4 Video doorbell: a doorbell with a camera that shows and records the caller.
- B8.1.6.5 Doorbell and chime: a push button with a sounder that announces a caller, without a camera.
- B8.1.6.6 Door phone: an entry panel with speaker (and screen) linked to an indoor unit for speaking to a caller.
- B8.1.6.7 Camera system kit: a set of cameras and recorder sold and compared as one system (provisional until attested, K-1).

### B8.1.7 Mail & parcel boxes
- B8.1.7.1 Letterbox: a freestanding, wall or post mailbox for letters.
- B8.1.7.2 Parcel box: a larger lockable box for receiving deliveries.

### B8.1.8 Anti-climb & glazing protection
- B8.1.8.1 Anti-climb spike [忍び返し]: spikes fixed on top of a wall or fence to deter climbing.
- B8.1.8.2 Security window film: a clear film applied to glass to hold it together when struck.

## B8.2 Fire, smoke & gas

Leaf axis: the device job, detect or suppress.

### B8.2.1 Fire & gas alarms
- B8.2.1.1 Smoke alarm: a ceiling alarm that senses smoke and sounds.
- B8.2.1.2 Heat alarm: an alarm that senses a rise in temperature and sounds, for kitchens and garages.
- B8.2.1.3 CO alarm: an alarm that senses carbon monoxide gas.
- B8.2.1.4 Combined smoke and CO alarm: one alarm unit that senses both smoke and carbon monoxide.
- B8.2.1.5 Gas detector: a detector for natural gas or LPG leaks. †

### B8.2.2 Fire suppression
- B8.2.2.1 Water extinguisher: a portable extinguisher discharging water.
- B8.2.2.2 Water-mist extinguisher: a portable extinguisher discharging a fine water mist.
- B8.2.2.3 Foam extinguisher: a portable extinguisher discharging foam.
- B8.2.2.4 CO2 extinguisher: a portable extinguisher discharging carbon dioxide gas.
- B8.2.2.5 Dry-powder extinguisher: a portable extinguisher discharging dry powder.
- B8.2.2.6 Wet-chemical extinguisher: a portable extinguisher discharging wet chemical, for cooking-oil fires.
- B8.2.2.7 Extinguisher spray: a small aerosol-can fire-extinguishing spray.
- B8.2.2.8 Fire blanket: a fabric sheet in a pouch that is pulled out to smother a small fire.

## B8.3 Household injury hazards

Leaf axis: the site of the hazard and the job done there (stairs and openings; cabinets, appliances and furniture; edges, sockets and knobs; windows and doors; watching a room). Child, baby, older adult and pet are user tags.

### B8.3.1 Gates & barriers
- B8.3.1.1 Hardware-mounted gate: a gate screwed to the wall or frame, usable at the top of stairs.
- B8.3.1.2 Pressure-mounted gate: a gate held in an opening by pressure alone, with no screws.
- B8.3.1.3 Retractable gate: a gate whose panel retracts into a housing at the side of the opening.

### B8.3.2 Latches & restraints for cabinets, appliances and furniture
- B8.3.2.1 Cabinet and drawer latch: a latch or catch that keeps a cabinet door or drawer from being opened.
- B8.3.2.2 Toilet lock: a catch that holds a toilet lid down.
- B8.3.2.3 Furniture anti-tip strap: a strap or bracket that fixes furniture or a television to a wall.

### B8.3.3 Guards for edges, sockets & knobs
- B8.3.3.1 Corner and edge guard: a soft cushion fitted on furniture corners or edges.
- B8.3.3.2 Outlet cover: a cover or plug that blocks the holes of a wall socket.
- B8.3.3.3 Stove knob cover: a cover over the control knobs of a cooker.

### B8.3.4 Window & door guards
- B8.3.4.1 Window guard: a bar, grille or restrictor that stops a window opening wide enough for a fall.
- B8.3.4.2 Door stop: a wedge, spring or holder that holds a door open or keeps it from slamming or being pushed.

### B8.3.5 Room monitors
- B8.3.5.1 Video room monitor (baby monitor): a camera and receiver or app for watching and hearing a baby or person in another room.

## B8.4 Pests

Leaf axis: the control method (trap; bait; spray or vaporise; repel; kill on contact; exclude). Target pest is a tag.

### B8.4.1 Traps
- B8.4.1.1 Snap trap: a spring-loaded trap that kills a mouse or rat.
- B8.4.1.2 Insect trap: a sticky, baited or light trap that catches insects.
- B8.4.1.3 Live-catch cage trap: a cage trap that catches an animal alive. †

### B8.4.2 Baits & poisons
- B8.4.2.1 Rodenticide bait: poison bait for mice and rats.
- B8.4.2.2 Bait station: a box or tray that holds bait so that only the pest can reach it.
- B8.4.2.3 Insect bait: a gel or paste bait for ants, cockroaches or similar.

### B8.4.3 Sprays, foggers & vaporisers
- B8.4.3.1 Aerosol insecticide: an insecticide sold in a spray can.
- B8.4.3.2 Insecticide foam: an insecticide that is applied as foam.
- B8.4.3.3 Insecticide powder: an insecticide dust.
- B8.4.3.4 Fogger: a can that releases insecticide as a fog to fill a room.
- B8.4.3.5 Mosquito coil: a burning spiral that gives off insecticidal smoke.
- B8.4.3.6 Electric vaporiser: a powered unit, plug-in or battery, that releases insecticide into room air from a liquid, mat or cartridge refill, by a heater or by a fan without heat; sold to kill or to repel.

### B8.4.4 Repellents
- B8.4.4.1 Pest repeller (electronic): a powered device that drives pests away by ultrasound or similar signals alone, with no insecticide or chemical refill.
- B8.4.4.2 Moth repellent: mothballs, cedar blocks or sachets that keep moths away. †

### B8.4.5 Zappers & swatters
- B8.4.5.1 Electric fly killer: a lamp-and-grid or racket device that electrocutes insects.
- B8.4.5.2 Fly swatter: a hand-held paddle for killing insects. †

### B8.4.6 Nets, screens & spikes
- B8.4.6.1 Bed mosquito net: a net hung over a bed to keep insects off the sleeper.
- B8.4.6.2 Insect screen: a mesh screen fitted to a window or door opening.
- B8.4.6.3 Bird spike: spikes fitted on ledges to stop birds perching. †
- B8.4.6.4 Bird netting for buildings: netting and bird-proofing nets that keep birds out of buildings and structures. †

## Precedence inside B8

- The leaf decides, not the word "lock": a lock, latch, bolt, chain, hasp, window lock, lockset that includes a lock, or door stop is B8, and its fitting decides the kind; a cabinet latch against a household injury is B8.3.2.1, not a door lock.
- Opening and access method (key, code, card, fingerprint, app), connectivity, mechanism, power source and installation are attributes, not kinds: a fingerprint smart euro cylinder is B8.1.1.3; a push-button code lock on a night-latch body is B8.1.1.1; a biometric access lock is filed by its lock body (B8.1.1.3, B8.1.1.6, B8.1.1.7, B8.1.1.8) with `access_method`; a wall, floor or furniture safe is B8.1.4.1.
- A lockable window handle is a non-maker named form of B8.1.2.3; 補助錠 (supplementary lock) is an alias with two targets, B8.1.2.3 and B8.1.1.2.
- Secure storage is filed in B8.1.4 whatever it resists: a fire-resistant document box is B8.1.4.2, not B8.2. A safe with a burglary grade is B8.1.4.1 with its fire rating as `fire_rating`; a container rated against fire only is B8.1.4.2; "fire safe" is a homonym alias with both targets.
- A gun safe or gun cabinet is B8.1.4.6; its burglary and fire ratings and long-gun capacity are attributes.
- A key safe (outdoor key box, Testsieger key box) is B8.1.4.3, a key cabinet (hooks, sold among security goods) B8.1.4.4, a cash box B8.1.4.5.
- A padlock or chain sold for gates, sheds and general use is B8.1.3 even if it carries a cycle rating; a ground anchor fixed in a floor or wall is B8.1.3.6.
- A device that only makes noise to deter attack is B8.1.5.4.
- A camera made to watch and record a place for protection, including a video doorbell (B8.1.6.4) and a floodlight camera (B8.1.6.3, a light with a camera), is B8.1.6; a baby or room monitor that reports sound and picture is B8.3.5.1; a recorder sold only to run security cameras is B8.6.1.
- An intruder alarm system (B8.1.5.1) and a camera system kit (B8.1.6.7) are kinds only when sold, standardised and compared as one object; otherwise the set is filed by its item of greatest value with its contents listed.
- A smoke, heat, CO or gas alarm is B8.2.1 by the signal it detects; the heat alarm (B8.2.1.2) is its own kind because it detects a different signal and is sited apart. Extinguishers are kinds by agent (B8.2.2.1 to B8.2.2.6).
- A gate for stairs and doorways sold for children, or children and pets, is B8.3.1 with user tags; mounting (hardware, pressure, retractable) is the kind (B8.3.1.1 to B8.3.1.3).
- Mouse, rat and insect traps and live-catch cage traps are B8.4.1; sprays, coils and traps for rooms are B8.4; bird spikes and bird nets for buildings are B8.4.6 (B8.4.6.3, B8.4.6.4); mothballs and moth repellents are B8.4.4.2.
- A powered unit that releases an insecticide or repellent chemical into a room from a liquid, mat or cartridge refill is B8.4.3.6, whether it heats or uses a fan, runs on mains or batteries, and is sold to kill or to repel; a pest repeller (B8.4.4.1) releases no chemical; "repel" on the pack does not move a vaporiser to B8.4.4.
- A part or consumable made for one B8 object is B8.6: a recorder for security cameras B8.6.1, a holder for a mosquito coil B8.6.2, a refill or cartridge for one maker's vaporiser units B8.6.3.
- A lock sold as a locksport practice lock is a lock (filed by its form in B8.1).
