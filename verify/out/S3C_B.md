# S3C_B source census, domain B (B1 to B10)

Table: verify/out/S3C_B.tsv (98 rows, every URL fetched in this batch except where the notes say otherwise). Anchor = harvest first; gaps = what no fetched catalogue covers. Chinese and Korean catalogues were searched for but none was fetched, so those regions are a gap in every category.

- **B1 Furniture & Storage.** Anchor: 1stDibs furniture sitemap (styles, creators, design series) with Wikipedia List of chairs for named forms; makers from DGM, FederlegnoArredo Vetrina Soci and the British Furniture Association lists; mattresses from the CalRecycle brand registry. Gaps: no open register of modular-system platforms (USM, Vitsoe) or of mass-market own-label lines; Japanese, Chinese, Indian and Spanish maker directories not found; bed-size names are only in Wikipedia and retailer pages.
- **B2 Soft Furnishings.** Anchor: Imabari Towel Industrial Association member list (towels) and Rug Wiki (rug kinds and weaving names). Gaps: no catalogue of bed-linen, curtain or blind makers beyond trade-fair and Europages listings; hand-woven rug workshops by name; cushion, throw and table-linen lines; Heimtextil search needs a form.
- **B3 Lighting.** Anchor: Little List of Lights (4,850 flashlights) for torches; Lights.co.uk and Leuchtenland brand lists plus Wikipedia Category: Lighting brands for makers; Prolamp base-type page for bulb caps. Gaps: no open model database for designer lamps (only brand lists), none for decorative lanterns or oil lamps, no collector index for Dietz, Aladdin or Tiffany.
- **B4 Decor.** Anchor: Flags of the World (91,000 pages) for flags; Hallmark Ornament Factory for series ornaments; Basenotes Directory for fragrance houses; Robert Zueblin lexicon and Wikipedia porcelain list for studio ceramics. Gaps: no catalogue for candles and diffusers beyond Fragrantica's 15 candle brands; none for mirrors, picture frames, artificial flowers, signs, party decorations or fireworks; art-glass makers only as stub registers.
- **B5 Kitchen & Dining.** Anchor: Replacements Ltd brands A-Z (china, crystal, silver, collectibles; about 450,000 patterns) plus its piece-type guides; Wikipedia lists of cooking vessels, utensils, glassware and Japanese utensils for kinds; AHAM, Hessburg and Kakaku for appliance makers and types; Japan Knife Makers, Madingo and Wikipedia knife categories for knives; espresso spec databases and SCA certified brewers for machines. Gaps: no open register of cookware lines or sets, no catalogue of Chinese, Korean or Indian kitchenware, commercial-kitchen makers, tea ware (Yixing) or pod and capsule platforms; knife steel names are not a maker list.
- **B6 Cleaning & Laundry.** Anchor: EPREL (washing machines, washer-dryers, tumble dryers, dishwashers) for models and suppliers; Wikipedia Category: Vacuum cleaner manufacturers and Cleaning product brands for seeds; Kakaku for Japanese appliance types. Gaps: no register of consumables (detergents, polishes, bin bags) outside US and UK Wikipedia brand lists; vacuum bag, filter and belt fit tables; Chinese robot-vacuum brands; mops, brooms and hand tools by maker.
- **B7 Climate, Water & Energy.** Anchor: MCS Product Directory (75,000+ products) and Clean Energy Council inverter and battery lists (3,800+ models) for solar, batteries and heat pumps; EPREL and AHRI for air conditioners and water heaters; Kakaku for Japanese water heaters, toilet seats and kotatsu. Gaps: no maker list for sanitaryware (baths, basins, toilets), boilers, stoves or ceiling fans; small portable heaters and fans; hot tubs and saunas; Chinese split air-conditioner brands.
- **B8 Home Safety & Security.** Anchor: Lockwiki manufacturer list and Sold Secure approved search (graded padlocks, safes, cylinders). Gaps: nothing found for safe makers outside Sold Secure, smoke-alarm makers beyond LPCB Red Book PDFs, extinguisher makers, baby gates and monitors, camera and doorbell ecosystems, or Japanese insecticide lines; pest products have no open register in this batch (EPA and MAFF searches only reached search excerpts).
- **B9 Smoking & Vaping.** Anchor: Pipedia (pipes, brand and maker lists by country) and Wikipedia List of cigar brands with the Habanos vitolario for cigars; Wikipedia lists for cigarettes, rolling papers and e-cigarette brands; Leafly and CannMenus for cannabis brands. Gaps: no catalogue of e-liquid or disposable-vape brands, hookah and shisha makers, lighters (Zippo collector index), grinders, bongs or ashtrays; no heated-tobacco device list beyond Wikipedia's.
- **B10 Animal Equipment.** Anchor: Dog Food Advisor (reviews by brand) and CatFoodDB for feed brands and lines; SmartPak brand facet and The Saddle Bank list for tack makers; Dadant and Mann Lake catalogues for hive parts and frame sizes; Pet Circle and Rakuten for aquarium and Japanese goods. Gaps: no open register of aquarium and terrarium equipment makers, bird feeders, hay and livestock feed, antique tack by maker mark, or reptile and small-animal housing; APPA's member directory is login only.

Categories with no usable single catalogue: none is empty, but B6 (consumables), B7 (sanitaryware) and B8 (safes, alarms, pest products) rest on partial sources only.
