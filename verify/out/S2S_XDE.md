### What conformance changed
- D1: Tier ledger split into attributes (80 PLUS, PCIe), lines (Core, RTX) and few entries; parts sub-family D1.9 added, D1.7 renamed, partner cards became variants (§11).
- D2: Radios re-cut from regional services to form kinds; rugged phone, e-reader size and tracker-by-user kinds became attributes; parts sub-family D2.8 added (§11).
- D3: Powered and portable speaker leaf and headset leaf deleted for cabinet-form kinds; TV technology an attribute; tiers off kinds; provisional kinds and parts sub-family D3.8 added (§11).
- D4: Lenses re-cut by optical design (leaf null); film cameras by viewing system, instant by film type, film by process; drones and ring lights added; phone accessories sent to D2 (§11).
- D5: Pro, third-party and retro pad kinds, retro leaf by era and VR-by-host kinds removed; controllers and cabinets by form; tier and grade columns dropped; software-per-platform kept (§11).
