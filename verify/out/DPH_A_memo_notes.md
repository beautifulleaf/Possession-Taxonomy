## A1

Built category. Points from proposals.tsv structure_flags, decided (rows in DPH_A_kinds.tsv; no kinds added, no named form moved, no id changed).
- Flag 0, "no kind is missing; groups regroup for display only": **noted**, agreed. The garment-tab groups (groups.tsv G-A1-1 to G-A1-38) hold every A1 kind.
- Flag 1, gender toggle: **display_only**. User stays a C6 tag. It is stored as the attribute `user` (kind and named form; the C6 list) in data/attributes.tsv when a source gives it. The page shows a site-wide department toggle (women, men, unisex/all, child) before the scene door and the garment tab. A brand's departments are derived from its named forms. A brand with no named form shows under "all" only.
- Flag 2, Traditional & regional dress cluster: **display_only**. The 69 scenes move out of the one cluster into six clusters by dress form, all prefixed "Traditional dress:" so they sort together (verify/out/DPH_A_scene_changes.tsv, tools/apply_scene_changes.py). The six are: draped and wrapped (7); kimono, robes and over-garments (13); dresses, suits and sets (22); shirts and tunics (6); handwoven, printed and embroidered cloth (12); folk costume and heritage revival (9). The first three match garment groups G-A1-33, -34 and -35. No scene is renamed, merged or invented, and parent links stay; a child scene in another cluster shows "in <parent>". A nation word in a scene name stays because it names the tradition (Pakistani lawn, Iranian manteau, Sudanese toub, Algerian couture). There is still no country list or country sort anywhere. Modest fashion goes with abaya and manteau (robes and over-garments).
- Flag 3, Luxury & designer: **display_only**. SC-haute-couture and SC-luxury-houses move to a cluster "Designer houses & couture". The page builder shows that cluster as the cross-cutting Designer edit beside the genre door (the Zalando or END. tab), not among the genre clusters. A brand's place in it comes from tags plus price band. SC-old-money is a genre (quiet luxury, classic clothes) and moves to Tailoring & classic. The Designer edit is not a tier (C4).
- Flag 4, thin clusters: **noted**. Revival & retro (5 scenes, 64 tags), Online aesthetics (13, 217), Festival & club (6, 69) and Minimal & modern (3, 61) all hold defined genres, so none is merged. Genre fills (R-114 b) go first to the scenes with no tags (new rave, Antillean madras, shamate, cyberpunk hanfu) and then to those under 5 (Tecktonik, vaporwave, Herero dress, seshoeshoe, garçonne, Newar revival, acubi, gabber, cybercore, Kurdish dress).
- Flag 5, named-form scene tags: **display_only**. The scene page already shows a tagged brand's signature pieces through the maker join, so a core brand's pieces appear without new tags. Stored tags (C8, no inheritance) are added in two cases. (a) Kind tags, when a scene's fetched definition page names the garment (cut-off vest under punk and metal, bondage trousers under punk, souvenir jacket, dirndl under Alpine Tracht, hanbok, kimono). A later tooling batch can do this, with the definition_source as the source, only where the fetch log holds it. (b) Named-form tags, only when a fetched page places that piece in that scene, for example a non-maker piece or a piece outside its maker's core scenes. The SIG harvests should record that page.
- Flag 6, Workwear and safety view: **display_only**. PPE stays in A1, one home each. G-A1-9 is renamed "Workwear and safety" (DPH_A_groups_changes.tsv). The memo attribute `hazard_rating` becomes `protection_class`, the same name as in A2, with the standard named in scope. Once A2 kinds are loaded, the view adds A2 named forms that carry `protection_class` (EN ISO 20345) as a generated cross-link, never as A1 members.
- Attributes decided for A1 (DPH_A_attributes.tsv): user, fibre_content, denim_oz, fabric_weight_gsm, selvedge, sanforized, loom_type, fit, size_range, fill_type, fill_power, fill_weight, wool_super_s, canvas_construction, membrane, face_fabric, protection_class, uniform, formality. The fabric mill and the membrane maker are maker-relations rows (supplier), not attributes.
- Needs (DPH_A_needs.tsv): techwear tiers, denim lineage groups and the Good On You rating as schemes; Grailed Market as a platform label; GOTS, OEKO-TEX, Fairtrade and the PPE standards as standards (all provisional until fetched); the Grailed, Vinted and Mercari condition scales.
