# Possession Taxonomy — Top-Level Categories v0.1

*Stage 1 draft, revision 1 (session 1, 2026-10-17). Replaces v0 after blind placement round 1, the source crosswalk and cold audit AU1 (see STATUS.md). Only the top level is fixed here. Each category's inner structure is researched separately in stage 2.*

## How to read this file

**What is in scope.** Every generic type of object a person can own and keep: worn, carried, furnished, used, ridden, lived in, grown, displayed, played, collected or held as data. Plants are in. Animals are out, but equipment for animals is in. Food and drink are out (the Culinary taxonomy covers them), except bottled alcoholic drinks and tobacco, which are kept as stock (category B10).

**Levels in stage 1.** Domain, then Category. Domains are a reading aid that groups neighbouring categories; they are not placement tests. **Placement is always to a category, by the placement key.**

**The axis: a short status filter, then designed function.** Steps 1 to 7 first sort objects by status: outside scope, health, real property, data only, living, natural specimen, or part of another object. From step 8 on, an object is filed only by its **primary designed function**: what it is made to do, or to be, for its owner. It is never filed by material, power source, value, age, maker, brand, room or setting, installation, connectivity, the user's age or gender, how it was acquired, or why the owner keeps it. Those are modifiers (Appendix A).

**The placement key is authoritative.** Category descriptions and precedence notes restate the key. Where they differ, the key wins and the description is corrected.

**Shared rules for every category.**
- **S1 One home.** Every object type has one category. Run the placement key and stop at the first *yes*.
- **S1a Branches.** When a step's lead question is *yes* but none of its branches fits, the answer is *no*: continue to the next step.
- **S2 Generic types.** A kind is a generic type someone has one of. Models, cuts and designs are named forms under a kind. Unique individual items are out.
- **S3 Parts follow the whole.** A component, attachment, accessory or consumable **made to be fitted to or used up in one kind of object** goes to that object's category: a graphics card to Computers, a lens to Cameras, guitar strings to Music, printer ink to Computers, a child car seat to Vehicles. **Cases, bags, covers and stands made to hold, carry or protect one kind of object are parts too:** a phone case to Phones, a guitar case to Music, a golf bag to Sports, a camera bag to Cameras. **Not parts:** furniture and storage made for a class of things (a bookcase is Furniture, a wine fridge is Kitchen & Dining, a gun safe is Home Safety), and anything for growing plants (pots, compost, plant food, grow lights are Garden & Grounds).
- **S4 Modifiers never split the top level.** See Appendix A.
- **S5 Test the object, not the motive.** A signed football is a football; a collected teapot is a teapot; an antique chair is a chair.
- **S6 Attestation.** Every name added later has an attested source.

## Placement key (stop at the first *yes*)

1. **Not an ownable object?** A living animal (including live bait and fertilised eggs); food, drink or another substance made to be eaten or drunk by people or animals, including supplements and pet food (bottled alcoholic drinks are step 25; medicines are step 2); a service, membership, course, subscription or account; a **right, claim or financial instrument in itself** (ticket or pass, licence to use a place, timeshare, share, bond, insurance policy, loyalty points, intellectual property); money held as an account balance, stored value or digital currency (bank balance, gift-card value, cryptocurrency); a change made to the body itself (tattoo, hairstyle, cosmetic surgery). **OUT**. (Physical tokens of these are objects: a printed share certificate or a kept ticket is F5; coins and banknotes are F3; devices implanted in the body are H2.)
2. Made to **diagnose, treat, monitor or compensate for an illness, injury or disability**, or for first aid: medical devices and monitors, medicines and medical supplies, mobility and assistive aids (including ones installed in a home, such as a stairlift or grab bar), hearing aids, spectacles and contact lenses that correct vision, contraceptives, incontinence products, first-aid kits? **H2 Health, Medical & Mobility** (Protective gear goes by what it is: steps 9 and 15. A device that counts steps or heart rate for fitness is D2.)
3. **Land**; a **building or other permanent structure made to be occupied, entered or enclosed** (house, flat, garage, barn, shed, garden room, carport); a **building element** made to form part of one (walls, roofs, windows, doors, stairs, fitted flooring, fitted cabinetry and kitchens, built-in baths and sanitaryware, fixed plumbing and wiring, in-ground pools, fences); a vehicle or vessel made to **stay in one place as a dwelling** (houseboat, static caravan, tiny house on wheels); a burial plot held as land? **G2 Property & Structures** (A machine, lamp, safe or alarm that is installed keeps its own category: 'built-in' is a modifier. Greenhouses are C2; animal housing is B11; play structures are E1.)
4. Exists **only as data**, held as a file, licence entitlement or registered token: e-book, digital music or film, software, font, digital artwork or photograph file, in-game item, domain name, NFT? **I1 Digital Goods** (The account or platform it sits in is OUT; the item held in it is in. Fungible money tokens are OUT.)
5. A **living plant or fungus**, a living culture kept to grow or propagate (sourdough starter, kombucha culture, mushroom spawn), a fresh-cut plant, or a seed, bulb or spore for growing? **C3 Plants & Living Cultures** (Dried and artificial flowers are B4.)
6. **Made by nature, not by people**, and kept as found or prepared as a specimen: minerals, rocks, fossils, meteorites, shells, unset gemstones, skeletal and taxidermy specimens, pinned insects, herbarium sheets? **F6 Natural History & Specimens**
7. A **part, attachment, accessory, case or consumable made for one kind of object** (S3)? → that object's category.
8. Made for **sexual stimulation, or for restraint during sexual activity**? **H3 Adult Objects** (Garments of every kind, including lingerie and fetish wear, are A1; contraceptives are H2.)
9. **A weapon**: made to injure or kill a person or animal by striking, cutting, shooting or firing (firearms, air guns, bows and crossbows, swords, fighting and hunting knives, martial-arts weapons, pepper spray), including such weapons made for target shooting or fencing; **armour** made to protect against such weapons; ammunition? **E4 Weapons & Armour** (Toy, foam, airsoft and paintball replicas are E1 or E2; decorative replicas that cannot injure are B4; fishing tackle and hunting accessories other than the weapon are E2; pest traps are B9; kitchen and utility knives are not weapons; kendo and other padded sport armour is E2.)
10. Made **mainly to transport people or goods from place to place** on land, water, rail, snow or air, however moved (engine, motor, muscle, wind, animal or tow): cars, motorcycles, bicycles and e-bikes, e-scooters and other powered personal transport, boats and sailing craft, aircraft, trailers, touring caravans and motorhomes, horse-drawn vehicles, prams and strollers? **G1 Vehicles** (A machine that carries its operator while it does other work, such as a tractor, ride-on mower or excavator, goes by that work. Hand carts and wheelbarrows are C2 or C1. Wheeled luggage is A4. Unpowered boards, skates, skis, sledges and gliders made for a sport are E2. Ride-on toys, tricycles and balance bikes for play are E1. Baby carriers and slings are A4.)
11. Made for **keeping, feeding, housing, carrying, training or caring for animals, kept or wild**: housing and hutches, tack, carriers, beds, feeders, nest boxes, aquariums, beekeeping equipment, pet toys, grooming and care equipment? **B11 Animal Equipment** (Riders' clothing and helmets are E2.)
12. Made for **religious or spiritual practice**, or for a **rite of passage or funeral**, **worn or not**: altars, shrines, icons, prayer beads, tefillin, amulets and talismans, ritual vessels, lamps, instruments and implements, incense and incense burners, religious festival objects (menorah, Advent wreath, diya, festival display dolls), cremation urns and grave goods? **F4 Religious & Ceremonial Objects** (Religious dress and vestments are A1. Jewellery in a religious form, such as a cross pendant, is A3. Secular seasonal and party decorations are B4. Instruments also used outside worship, such as an organ or harmonium, are E3.)
13. **Money, postage or an award in physical form, or an object made only to be collected**: coins, banknotes and bullion (in circulation or not), postage stamps and postal stationery, medals, decorations and trophies, tokens, non-game trading cards, commemorative series made for collecting? **F3 Coins, Stamps, Medals & Collectibles** (Figures, dolls and model vehicles sold as collectible series are E1. Pins, badges and patches are A3.)
14. A **timepiece**: watch or clock of any kind? **A5 Clocks & Watches** (Smartwatches are D2.)
15. **Worn on the body.** First: a powered device whose main function is computing, communication, sensing or media (smartphone, smartwatch, fitness tracker, earbuds, e-reader) is not filed here: go to step 22. A garment, shoe or accessory with heating or lights stays here. Wigs, hairpieces, false eyelashes and artificial nails are H1.
    - a garment, headwear, glove, scarf, tie, belt, hosiery, uniform, costume, or protective clothing and personal protective equipment for work (hard hat, safety glasses, respirator, ear defenders, hi-vis)? **A1 Clothing**
    - footwear? **A2 Footwear**
    - an ornament worn on the body, hair or clothing: rings, necklaces, body jewellery, brooches, pins, badges and patches, hair ornaments, cufflinks? **A3 Jewellery**
    - a bag, luggage, wallet or purse, umbrella, sunglasses or other non-corrective eyewear, hand fan, keyring, fashion cane, baby carrier or sling? **A4 Bags & Accessories** (An object that is only portable, such as a pocket knife, lighter, pen or water bottle, is filed by what it does.)
    - (**Made for one sport** means designed for that sport's specific demands: football boots, ski boots, swimming costumes, wetsuits, padded cycling shorts, riding helmets, kendo uniform. These are not filed here: go to step 24. General trainers, leggings, team and fan jerseys, and outdoor jackets stay A1 and A2.)
16. **Made to be used on the body** for grooming, hygiene, cosmetics or fragrance, including powered tools (hair dryers, shavers, electric toothbrushes), grooming mirrors, cosmetics attached to the body, massagers for comfort, pacifiers and teethers? **H1 Personal Care & Beauty** (Towels of every kind are B2. Massagers made to treat a condition are H2.)
17. A **document issued to prove** identity, status, ownership or achievement (passport, licence, certificate, deed, diploma, share certificate), or a **personal record or keepsake paper made by or for its owner**: letters, written diaries, photo albums, personal photographic prints, home recordings, scrapbooks, written postcards, autographs, kept tickets and programmes, house plans? **F5 Documents & Personal Records** (Published works are F1. Art photographs and posters are F2. Blank notebooks, cards and calendars are C5.)
18. Made to be **looked at as a picture** on a flat or hung surface (paintings, drawings, prints, posters, art photographs, calligraphy, tapestries and textile hangings), **or as free-standing sculpture**, including figurines, whatever the edition or price? **F2 Art** (Character figures and bobbleheads are E1. Vases, bowls and other vessels are B4 or B5, however artistic.)
19. A **published work fixed on a physical carrier**, made to be read, viewed or listened to: books (including colouring, puzzle and activity books), comics, magazines, maps, atlases and globes, sheet music, records, CDs, tapes, films and discs? **F1 Media & Publications** (Video games on any carrier are D5.)
20. A **musical instrument**, acoustic or electronic, or equipment for making, amplifying, performing or recording music (amplifiers, effects pedals, mixers, microphones, audio interfaces, stage and PA speakers, DJ equipment, metronomes, music stands)? **E3 Musical Instruments & Music Gear** (Toy instruments are E1. Hi-fi and speakers for listening are D3.)
21. Made to **observe, magnify or measure** things or natural phenomena: binoculars, telescopes, microscopes, magnifiers and loupes, weather instruments, laboratory glassware and instruments, science-education kits? **C6 Optical & Scientific Instruments** (Building and trade measuring tools such as tape measures, levels and multimeters are C1. Rifle scopes are E4 by S3.)
22. A **powered electronic device whose main function is computing, communication, picture-taking, gaming, or playing or receiving sound and pictures**:
    - a **desktop, laptop or convertible computer**, its components, peripherals (keyboards, mice, monitors, printers, scanners, webcams, drawing tablets, 3D printers), storage or networking, and loose electronic components and maker boards? **D1 Computers & Components**
    - a **phone, tablet, e-reader, smartwatch, fitness tracker, smart ring, smart glasses, GPS unit or tracker**, and power banks? **D2 Phones, Tablets & Personal Devices** (Children's tablets are here.)
    - a **TV, projector, hi-fi, record player, radio, headphones or earbuds of any kind, speakers for listening, smart speaker, voice assistant or smart-home hub**? **D3 Audio & Video**
    - a **camera** or equipment for taking and making pictures: lenses, flashes and photo lighting, ring lights, tripods, film, darkroom equipment, drones made for filming? **D4 Cameras & Photography** (This branch also takes non-powered photographic equipment.)
    - a **games console, handheld, controller, VR headset or arcade machine**, or a video game on any carrier? **D5 Video Games**
    (A connected version of a household object, such as a smart lock, thermostat, bulb, plug, alarm, camera or robot vacuum, is filed by its task at step 29: connectivity is a modifier. A powered device that fits no branch continues to step 23.)
23. Made for **play or for games**: toys, dolls, figures (including collectible series), puzzles, board, card, dice and tile games (including trading card games), tabletop and coin-operated games, toy vehicles and ride-on toys, model railways and ready-made models, dress-up toys, fireworks, children's play equipment (swings, slides, climbing frames, playhouses, trampolines made for play), gambling equipment? **E1 Toys & Games**
24. Made for a **sport, physical exercise, or an outdoor recreation such as camping, hiking, climbing, angling or snow sports**: balls, rackets, cues, darts and dartboards, table-tennis tables, fitness machines, boards, skates and skis, paragliders, tents, sleeping bags, camp furniture and stoves, climbing, fishing, winter and water sports gear, airsoft and paintball markers, and garments, footwear and protective gear made for one sport (step 15)? **E2 Sports, Fitness & Recreation**
25. **Bottled alcoholic drinks kept as stock** (wine, spirits, sake, beer), **tobacco products**, and **implements for smoking or vaping** (cigars, pipe tobacco, pipes, hookahs, lighters, vapes, ashtrays, cigar cutters)? **B10 Cellar & Smoking** (The drink as a drink is described by the Culinary taxonomy; here it is the stock owned. Wine racks and humidors are B1 or B7; wine fridges are B5; decanters are B5.)
26. Made for **textile, fibre, paper, bead, clay, paint and other decorative crafts**: sewing and embroidery machines, knitting and needlework tools, yarn, fabric and notions (buttons, zips), artists' paints, brushes, canvases and easels, artists' drawing materials, model kits (built or not), beading and loose beads, scrapbooking and paper-craft supplies, pottery tools and kilns? **C4 Craft & Art Supplies** (Tools for shaping wood, metal and stone, and for electronics work, are C1 whoever uses them.)
27. Made for **growing plants or keeping gardens, grounds, paths, pools and drives**, powered or not: garden tools, mowers and ride-on mowers, trimmers, leaf blowers, pressure washers, snow clearers, wheelbarrows, planters and plant pots, plant care products, grow lights, irrigation, greenhouses and polytunnels, compost bins, artificial turf, pool cleaning equipment and pumps? **C2 Garden & Grounds** (Garden furniture is B1; garden ornaments are B4.)
28. Made for **farm, forestry, trade or shop work** and not for a household: tractors, harvesters and farm implements, forestry machines, commercial kitchen and catering machines, retail, vending and payment equipment, commercial signage and display stands? **C7 Farm & Trade Equipment**
29. Made for a **household task, furnishing or domestic use**, wherever it is used (home, office, garden, caravan or boat), powered or not:
    - made to **prepare, cook, cool, store or serve food and drink**: cookware, kitchen tools and machines, cooking appliances, grills, refrigerators and wine fridges, dishwashers, tableware, glassware, barware, tea and coffee equipment, food storage and lunch bags? **B5 Kitchen & Dining**
    - made to **clean, wash or dry** the home, its surfaces or textiles: vacuum cleaners, brooms, mops, washing machines, dryers, irons, airers, laundry baskets, cleaning products and tools, bin bags? **B6 Cleaning & Laundry**
    - made to **heat, cool, ventilate, purify or humidify air, or heat, soften or filter water**: heaters, fans and ceiling fans, air conditioners, dehumidifiers, air purifiers, water heaters, softeners and filters, thermostats? **B8 Climate & Water**
    - made to **secure or protect** a home and its contents, or to keep pests out: locks, safes for any contents, smoke and carbon-monoxide alarms, fire extinguishers, security cameras, doorbells and alarm systems, pest traps and repellents, mosquito nets and coil holders, door stops? **B9 Home Safety & Security**
    - made to **give light**: lamps, light fittings, bulbs (including smart bulbs), lanterns and torches? **B3 Lighting**
    - **furniture**: made to sit, lie, work at, display or store things on or in, indoor or outdoor, including mattresses, bean bags, playpens, plant stands, wine racks, movable partitions? **B1 Furniture**
    - made to **cover, soften or dress** beds, floors, windows, seats and tables, or towels for drying the body: bedding, mattress toppers, rugs and carpets that are not fitted, curtains, cushions, throws, table linen, towels, swaddling cloths? **B2 Soft Furnishings**
    - made to **decorate** a room, surface or garden and not a picture, sculpture or lamp: vases, decorative bowls and objects, mirrors, candles and home fragrance, seasonal and party decorations, artificial and dried flowers, garden ornaments, flags and wall signs, decorative replica weapons? **B4 Decor**
    - made to **store, organise or carry household things**: boxes, baskets, bins, hooks, shelving inserts, garment and shoe bags, tool boxes? **B7 Storage & Organisation**
30. Made for **writing, everyday drawing, paper and desk work**: pens, pencils, paper goods, notebooks, diaries and planners, calendars, greeting cards, stickers, gift wrap, desk accessories, calculators, office machines that do not connect to a computer (copiers, shredders, laminators, label makers)? **C5 Office & Stationery**
31. A **tool, instrument or machine made to cut, shape, join, measure, test, lift, power or repair**, or **materials, hardware and supplies** for building, repair and electrical work: hand and power tools, workshop machines, ladders, measuring and testing tools, generators, pumps, timber, bricks, tiles, paint, adhesives, fasteners, hinges and fittings, lubricants, fuels, packing materials, cables, extension leads, plugs, fuses, household batteries? **C1 Tools, Hardware & Materials**
32. None of the above? Hold in **X0 Unplaced** and list the object in the review log. X0 must be empty before stage 2 starts.

## Categories

### A — Worn
- **A1 Clothing** — garments and wearable accessories of any material worn on the body or head: everyday, traditional and subculture dress, underwear, uniforms, costume, team and fan jerseys, protective clothing and personal protective equipment for work.
- **A2 Footwear** — anything worn on the feet, except footwear made for one sport (E2).
- **A3 Jewellery** — ornaments worn on the body, hair or clothing, including pins, badges and patches.
- **A4 Bags & Accessories** — bags, luggage, wallets, umbrellas, non-corrective eyewear, hand fans, keyrings, fashion canes, baby carriers.
- **A5 Clocks & Watches** — timepieces of every kind, worn or not.

### B — Home
- **B1 Furniture** — pieces to sit, lie, work at, display or store things on or in, indoor or outdoor, including mattresses.
- **B2 Soft Furnishings** — bedding, loose rugs, curtains, cushions, throws, table linen, towels.
- **B3 Lighting** — lamps, light fittings, bulbs, lanterns and torches.
- **B4 Decor** — ornamental objects that are not pictures, sculpture or lamps, and seasonal and party decorations.
- **B5 Kitchen & Dining** — everything made to prepare, cook, cool, store or serve food and drink, powered or not.
- **B6 Cleaning & Laundry** — everything made to clean, wash or dry, powered or not, and cleaning products.
- **B7 Storage & Organisation** — containers and organisers for household things.
- **B8 Climate & Water** — heating, cooling, air treatment and domestic water equipment.
- **B9 Home Safety & Security** — locks, safes, alarms, extinguishers, security cameras, pest control.
- **B10 Cellar & Smoking** — bottled alcoholic drinks and tobacco kept as stock, and smoking and vaping implements.
- **B11 Animal Equipment** — housing, tack, carriers, feeding, care and toys for animals, kept or wild.

### C — Making, Growing & Work
- **C1 Tools, Hardware & Materials** — hand and power tools, workshop machines, measuring and testing tools, building and repair materials, hardware, electrical supplies, household batteries, fuels.
- **C2 Garden & Grounds** — tools, machines and supplies for growing plants and keeping grounds and pools.
- **C3 Plants & Living Cultures** — living plants and fungi, fresh-cut flowers, seeds and bulbs, living cultures.
- **C4 Craft & Art Supplies** — tools, machines and materials for textile, paper, bead, clay, paint and model crafts.
- **C5 Office & Stationery** — writing instruments, paper goods, desk accessories, calculators, standalone office machines.
- **C6 Optical & Scientific Instruments** — instruments to observe, magnify or measure, and science-education kits.
- **C7 Farm & Trade Equipment** — farm, forestry, commercial catering, retail and trade machinery.

### D — Electronics
- **D1 Computers & Components** — computers, components, peripherals, storage, networking, loose electronic components.
- **D2 Phones, Tablets & Personal Devices** — phones, tablets, e-readers, smartwatches and wearables, GPS units and trackers, power banks.
- **D3 Audio & Video** — TVs, projectors, hi-fi, record players, radios, headphones, speakers for listening, smart speakers and hubs.
- **D4 Cameras & Photography** — cameras, lenses, photo lighting, tripods, film and darkroom equipment.
- **D5 Video Games** — consoles, handhelds, controllers, VR headsets, arcade machines, video games on any carrier.

### E — Play, Sport & Music
- **E1 Toys & Games** — toys, figures, dolls, games of every kind, models, children's play equipment, fireworks, gambling equipment.
- **E2 Sports, Fitness & Recreation** — equipment for sport, exercise and outdoor recreation, and garments and footwear made for one sport.
- **E3 Musical Instruments & Music Gear** — instruments of every tradition, and equipment to amplify, perform and record music.
- **E4 Weapons & Armour** — weapons that can injure or kill, armour against them, ammunition.

### F — Culture, Records & Meaning
- **F1 Media & Publications** — published works on physical carriers: books, comics, magazines, maps and globes, sheet music, recordings, films.
- **F2 Art** — pictures and free-standing sculpture, including figurines, whatever the edition or price.
- **F3 Coins, Stamps, Medals & Collectibles** — physical money and bullion, stamps, medals and trophies, tokens, non-game trading cards, commemorative series.
- **F4 Religious & Ceremonial Objects** — objects for religious practice, rites of passage and funerals, worn or not, except dress and jewellery.
- **F5 Documents & Personal Records** — issued documents and personal records and keepsake papers.
- **F6 Natural History & Specimens** — natural objects kept as found or prepared: minerals, fossils, unset gemstones, shells, meteorites, biological specimens.

### G — Vehicles & Property
- **G1 Vehicles** — anything made mainly to transport people or goods, with its fitted parts and electronics.
- **G2 Property & Structures** — land, buildings, building elements, fixed dwellings, burial plots.

### H — Body
- **H1 Personal Care & Beauty** — grooming, hygiene, cosmetic and fragrance tools, devices and products.
- **H2 Health, Medical & Mobility** — medical devices and monitors, medicines, mobility and assistive aids, corrective eyewear, hearing aids, first aid.
- **H3 Adult Objects** — objects made for sexual stimulation or restraint during sex. Clinical, descriptive register.

### I — Digital
- **I1 Digital Goods** — files, licence entitlements and registered tokens owned by a person.

### X — Holding
- **X0 Unplaced** — review bin; must be empty before stage 2.

## Appendix A — Modifiers (never top-level categories)
Region and culture · era (antique, vintage, retro) · price band and quality tier · brand and maker · scene and subculture · user (baby, child, adult, gender, size) · setting (indoor, outdoor, travel, office, garden) · installation (freestanding, built-in, fitted) · power source (manual, electric, gas, battery) · connectivity (smart, connected) · occasion (wedding, festival, season) · motive (collecting, heirloom, gift, investment, memorabilia, display) · material · condition · edition (limited, signed).
