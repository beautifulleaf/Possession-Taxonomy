# Top-level conflicts found by stage-2 memos
Logged for the next framework revision; not yet applied.

- **Lunch bags** (flagged by A4): the B5 branch lists lunch bags, but step 17 "a bag or luggage of any kind" is tried first, so they land in A4. Fix: add "insulated lunch bags and cool bags are B5" to the A4 branch note.
- **Real Christmas trees** (flagged by C3): the step-14 note sends Christmas trees to B4, but a cut or living tree reaches step 6 (plants) first. Fix: state that real cut and potted Christmas trees are C3 and artificial ones B4.
- **Plant-and-pot sets, preserved plants, sprouting seed, live tobacco and cannabis plants** (flagged by C3): no explicit rule. Proposed: plant-and-pot sets by the plant (C3); preserved (stabilised) plants B4; sprouting seed for eating J5, for growing C3; living tobacco and cannabis plants C3 (the plant, not the product).
- **Animal kind as first axis in B10** (flagged by B10): retail opens with the animal, but Appendix A lists 'user' as a modifier. Owner question: allow the kind of animal served as B10's first axis.
- **Pool pumps** (flagged by C2): step 29 lists pool pumps under C2 while S3 could send a pump made for one pool system to that system; harmless because both are C2.
- **Candle-making, resin, glass and basketry crafts** (flagged by C4): the C4 test names textile, fibre, paper, bead, clay, paint and model crafts only, so these supplies have no home. Fix: add 'candle and soap making, resin, glass and basketry' to step 28's list.
- **Worn optical instruments** (flagged by C6): step 17 (worn or carried) runs before step 23 (observe, magnify, measure), so head-worn magnifiers and binocular loupes can land in A4 eyewear. Fix: add 'head-worn magnifiers and loupes are C6' to the A4 branch note.
- **Sport helmets, goggles, court shoes, swimwear, archery and clay-shooting accessories** (flagged by E2): the 'made for a sport' note and the A1 PPE list overlap for helmets and goggles; archery accessories sit between E2 and E4 (S3). Fix: state that helmets and goggles made for one sport are E2, general cycling and motorcycle helmets A1; archery and shooting accessories follow the weapon under S3 (E4) except targets and ranges gear (E2).

## From the domain C review (framework/structure/_review_C.md, section 3)
- **X1. (high) Chainsaws, axes, log splitters and chippers: three memos, three answers.** C1 section 9 sends "tree chainsaw" to C2 and keeps the "wood axe for splitting logs" in C1. C2 section 9 sends "chainsaw, felling axe, splitting wedge" to C1 (and its question 3 repeats this). Step 29 (C2) is tried before step 32 (C1), so a tool "made for keeping gardens [and] grounds" stops at C2. *Propo
- **X2. (high) Earth-moving and materials-handling machines have no home.** C7 P7 sends "excavator, wheel loader, skid steer, telehandler, forklift" to C1. C1 section 7 says "ride-on construction machines are not tools and go by their work (step 11 note)", and its tree has no place for them. Their work (lifting, digging and moving material) is in C1's test ("lift"). *Proposed:* C1 adds them to C1.8 Lifting, access & materials handling (mini excavator, skid steer, telehandler, forklift, dumper, plate compactor, cement mixer), with the sentence *"A self-propelled machine made to dig, lift, load or move material (excavator, skid steer, telehandler, forklift, dumper) is C1, unless it does a farm or forestry job (C7) or keeps gardens and grounds (C2)."* Section 5, Q20.
- **X3. (medium) Cordless platform batteries fit neither S3 reading.** C1 files "cordless tool battery pack" in its accessories sub-family under S3. C8 boundary 1 says that "a pack shaped for one tool or device follows that tool or device". But a platform pack (Makita LXT, Ryobi ONE+, Einhell Power X-Change [C1 S35], Bosch POWER FOR ALL [C1 S6], Stihl AK [C2 S33]) runs drills, mowers and blowers alike, so it is not made for "one kind of object", and S3 does not apply. *Proposed:* *"A battery pack or charger made for a maker's cordless platform is C1, whatever tools or garden machines the platform includes; a standard-size cell (C8 boundary 1) is C8."* One platform ledger row serves C1 and C2 (convention K3). Section 5, Q23.
- **X5. (medium) Irrigation at farm scale.** C7 question 6 asks whether reels, centre pivots and field sprinkler sets are C7. C2 treats irrigation as household equipment and says nothing about farms. Step 12 names "commercial crop ... equipment", so field irrigation already reaches C7 first. *Proposed:* *"Irrigation made for field crops (hose reels, centre pivots, linear moves, field sprinkler lines) is C7; irrigation for gardens, glasshouses, allotments and grounds is C2."* Section 5, Q22.
- **X9. (medium) The household line for tractors.** C2 keeps the key's wording ("garden and compact lawn tractors" C2) and lists "lawn tractor †" as a kind. C7 P1 proposes a design test instead (rear PTO plus a Category 1 or larger three-point hitch makes a machine C7, whoever buys it), because the step 12 note decides by user, which Appendix A treats as a modifier. Both memos agree on the lawn tractor. They differ on a sub-compact tractor with a loader on a lifestyle plot, and on its implements and snow blade (C7 P2). *Proposed:* adopt C7 P1 and P2 on both sides, and amend the step 12 note to *"... including lawn and garden tractors without a rear power take-off and a Category 1 or larger three-point hitch"*. Section 5, Q21.
- **X10. (medium) Packing materials, envelopes and boxes.** C8.11 "Packing & protection materials" is unsourced. C5 lists "packing envelopes" among its boundary objects. B1 holds "boxes" as storage. *Proposed:* *"Envelopes, padded envelopes and mailing tubes for letters and post are C5; parcel tape, bubble wrap, stretch film, void fill and cardboard boxes sold flat for packing or moving are C8; boxes made for storing things are B1."* Section 5, Q34.

## From the domain G review (framework/structure/_review_G.md, section 4)
- **Houseboats, narrowboats, tiny houses on wheels (X-1, high).** The G1 and G2 memos give opposite rules, and the G2 test depends on use (S5). Proposed step-4 note: "Test the object as made (S5). A hull with its own propulsion and steering made to navigate (narrowboat, Dutch barge, cruising or pontoon houseboat), and a trailer, caravan or motorhome made for road towing or driving, are G1, however long they are kept in one place and whether or not someone lives in them. A dwelling made to stay in one place is G2: a floating home or house barge without propulsion, a static caravan, lodge or park home, a park model, and the tiny house on wheels that step 4 names." The key's example "houseboat" would then mean the moored, non-navigating one (owner to confirm; G review questions 22 and 23).
- **Park model RV (X-2).** RVIA lists it as an RV type; proposed "a park model RV is G2" in the step-4 note.
- **Vehicle-mounted electronics (X-10a).** Car audio, dashcams, mounted sat-navs and bike computers made for vehicle mounting are G1 under S3 (domain D O-10); the G1 memo disagrees on the mounted sat-nav.
- **Carriers that power implements (X-10b).** Proposed: "A vehicle made mainly to carry people or goods (Unimog truck, ATV, quad, utility vehicle) is G1 even when it powers implements" (also domain C F37).
- **Mobility scooters (X-10e)** stay H2.
- **Commercial, industrial and institutional buildings** have no stated scope (G review question 16): in G2 by function, or out.

## From the domain H review (framework/structure/_review_H.md, section 4)
- **Sets and kits (X11).** The framework's only set rule is the grocery one (step 3: the item of greatest value). Proposed general sentence: "A set of objects of one kind is filed as that kind. A mixed set is filed by its item of greatest value, with its contents listed. A kit that is sold, standardised and compared as one object (first-aid kit, manicure set) is a kind of its own."
- **H1/H2 line (X3).** The step note uses "licensed as a medicine", which moves an object by jurisdiction. Proposed: "A product is H2 when its labelled main purpose is to treat, relieve or prevent a named condition, or when it contains an active ingredient sold only as a medicine in every sourced jurisdiction. Cosmetic purposes (clean, perfume, change appearance, protect, keep in good condition, correct body odour) are H1, sunscreens and anti-dandruff and anti-blemish products included. Legal status goes to C17 and does not move the object." (H review question 11.)
- **Massagers (X4).** Proposed: "A powered massager is H3 under the H3-1 test; otherwise it is H2 when made to treat a condition (TENS, EMS units sold for pain), and H1 for comfort, including percussion and neck massagers."
- **Intimate, menstrual and body-replacement goods (X5)** need explicit homes: lubricant and menstrual products H1; condoms and dental dams H2; period underwear A1; pelvic-floor trainers and vacuum devices H2 when a medical purpose is stated, else H3; packers and breast forms H1 with wigs or H2 with prostheses (owner question 8).

## From the domain F review (framework/structure/_review_F.md, section 4)
- **Religious medals (X1, high).** F3 and F4 give opposite answers, and "blessed" is a motive (S5). Proposed: "A medal made for devotion (a saint's or scapular medal, a Miraculous Medal) is F4 under personal devotion aids when loose, and A3 when it is made with a chain or bail to be worn; a medal struck to commemorate or to reward, whatever its subject, is F3."
- **Cards, scrip and postal covers (X2, high).** F3 and F5 use motive tests ("collected for itself", "kept for its stamps"). Proposed: "Payment, gift, telephone, transit, membership and key cards are F5 whether live, used or expired. A token of metal, plastic or other material issued to stand for money or a service is F3. Paper issued to circulate as money (scrip, emergency money, Notgeld) is F3; a voucher or gift certificate redeemable only with its issuer is F5. Postal stationery with a printed stamp, and a cover issued by a postal administration or prepared as a first-day cover, are F3; any other used envelope, letter or picture postcard is F5, whatever stamps it carries." (Owner may prefer F3 for any cover that has passed through the post: F review question 34.)
- **Photographs (X3).** Proposed: "A photographic print issued as an edition, or signed or stamped by its maker as a work, is F2; every other photographic print, slide or negative, whoever is shown, is F5." This needs step 19 to drop "of the owner's own life".
- **Devotional images (X4).** Both F2 and F4 test intent. Proposed kind list: "icon, thangka and other devotional scroll paintings, altarpiece, devotional statue made for a shrine, crucifix are F4; a painting or sculpture with a religious subject made in another form is F2 or B4."
- **Albums, binders and holders (X5).** Proposed: "A holder, case, sleeve or stand made for one object follows it (S3) into that category's parts sub-family. An album, binder, stock book, box or tray made to hold several objects is B1 when empty. A filled photo album or scrapbook is F5; a blank scrapbook album is C4."
- **Sets, rolls and lots (X10).** Same rule as the H review's sets sentence (review C K6): like items filed as the item with a quantity; mixed kits by the item of greatest value; owner-approved exception kinds (mint and proof sets, mineral teaching sets, factory card sets).
- **Car first-aid kits (from H2 conformance, P17).** A car kit is carried in the vehicle, not fitted to it, so it is H2; but S3 could be read as sending it to G1. Proposed: state "first-aid kits are H2 whatever vehicle, workplace or place they are made for" (owner question Q16 in H2).
- **Coloured plano contact lenses (from H2 conformance).** If the owner chooses H2, step 2 needs "contact lenses of every kind, corrective or not, are H2"; otherwise A3 (step 17) must exclude them.
