## 0. Category

**Code and name:** B4 Decor, in domain B Home.

**Membership test (placement key step 30, last branch, framework/CATEGORIES_v0_6.md):** made to **decorate** a room, surface or garden and not a picture, sculpture or lamp: vases, decorative bowls and objects, picture frames, wall and dresser mirrors, candles and home fragrance (including incense), decorative lighting, seasonal, festival and party decorations, Christmas crackers, party poppers, sparklers and fireworks, artificial and dried flowers, garden ornaments, statues and gnomes, sundials, wind chimes, flags, windsocks and signs (including illuminated and shop signs), decorative replica weapons. Branch B4 is tried last, after B5, B6, B7, B8, B3, B1 and B2.

**Precedence sentences and notes in the framework that touch B4:**
- Step 6: dried and artificial flowers are B4; a plant sold still growing is C3.
- Step 7: dried flowers are B4; craft shells and feathers are C4.
- Step 10: decorative replica weapons that cannot injure are B4; real weapons are E4.
- Step 14 note: decorations for any festival, religious or secular (Christmas trees and ornaments; Easter, Diwali, Halloween and Hina-matsuri displays) are B4 unless made for use in worship (menorah, diya, Advent wreath, home nativity set are F4). Home-fragrance incense is B4; incense made for a rite is F4.
- Step 16 note: sundials made as garden ornaments are B4.
- Step 20 note: ornamental figurines are F2; garden statues and gnomes are B4; vases, bowls and other vessels are B4 or B5, however artistic.
- Step 22 note: wind chimes are B4 (music gear is E3).
- Step 29 note: garden ornaments, statues and gnomes are B4 (growing and grounds equipment is C2).
- Step 30, B3 note: candles, string and fairy lights, lava lamps and neon signs made mainly as decoration are B4.
- Step 18: grooming mirrors, and fragrance made to be used on the body, are H1 (Personal Care & Beauty); a wall or dresser mirror is B4 and so is fragrance made to scent a room.

### 1.2 Enthusiast and maker sources
- **Fragrance wheels.** The Edwards wheel (S5) has four families (Floral, Amber, Woody, Fresh) and 14 subfamilies. CandleScience (S4), a candle-maker supplier, uses ten families (Spicy, Gourmand, Amber, Woody, Aromatic, Floral, Citrus, Marine/Ozonic, Green, Fruity) and defines "kindred" and "complementary" neighbours on the wheel. The market report taxonomy (S23) uses eight fragrance types; Nippon Kodo's store (S22) uses ten for incense (Aromatic, Fruity, Citrus, Fresh, Floral, Herbal, Spicy, Perfumy, Sweet, Woody). **The sources agree that scent is a facet that cuts across product forms and disagree on how many families there are (4, 8 or 10).**
- **Incense makers.** Nippon Kodo (S21) describes forms by burn behaviour: stick (mild, private room), cone (intensive, short, larger room), coil (about two hours). - **Wind chimes (S34).** The enthusiast quality dimension is tuning (major or minor pentatonic, Gregorian, 432 Hz, Japanese yo and in scales); untuned decorative chimes ring random intervals; the guide tells buyers to check tubes with a tuner.

### 1.4 Standards bodies and trade classifications
- **HS heading 9505 (S16, S36)** collects festive, carnival and entertainment articles, split at subheading level by occasion (950510 Christmas articles, with glass and non-glass lines; 950590 other). The WCO notes list garlands, lanterns, tree decorations, artificial Christmas trees, nativity scenes, crackers, stockings, streamers and confetti, and **exclude** candles (34.06), textile flags and bunting (63.07), lighting strings and electric garlands (94.05), natural trees, tree stands, statues for places of worship, and utilitarian articles that merely carry a festive motif (tableware, linen).
- **US customs ruling N125135 (S17)** puts tealights in 3406 candles, a ceramic Santa figurine in 6913 ornamental ceramics, and a candle holder in 9405 non-electrical lighting fittings; a festive motif does not make a utilitarian article a festive article. This is direct evidence that candle, candle holder and ornament are three different things to the trade.
- **Result for B4:** the trade has no heading for "decor". B4 is a retailer concept, bundled by the framework's function test. The trade splits it by material and function (candles, festive articles, lighting, textile flags, ceramic ornaments).
- **Market research (S23, S24)** segments home fragrance by product type (candles, air fresheners, reed and electric diffusers, essential oils, sachets, wax melts, room sprays, incense, potpourri) and separately by fragrance type, ingredient, packaging and channel; candles by product (container, pillar, other), wax (paraffin, soy, blended, beeswax, palm) and category (mass versus premium). Form, wax and tier are three separate axes.

## 2. The first axis people choose by

Evidence from navigation order:
- IKEA, John Lewis, Westwing, Pepperfry, Rakuten and 1stDibs all open with **object form** (S1, S2, S6, S7, S8, S26). Filters follow in the order colour, material, style, price (S7, S35).
- Target puts **Type** first and then Brand, Price, then Fragrance Type and Scent Type, Color and Holiday (S3). Scent comes after the form choice.
- Wayfair's vase facets begin with size, colour and material, then Type and Shape (S35): buyers narrow by fit to the space first and by type second.
- Occasion comes first only where the retailer sells only occasions (Home Depot seasonal S11, Matsuya S25, Old World Christmas S10).
- Collectors start with **maker and period** (S26 creator and period facets; S27 series, era and condition).
- For flags the first choice is **whose flag** (S33) and for signs it is **material and type** (S20).

Reading: owners and buyers choose first by **what the object is** (form), then by fit (size, colour, material), then by scent or style; maker and era are the first choices only for collectors and for luxury fragrance (S28).

## 3. Candidate structures

All three keep the category as B4 and divide below it. The framework allows sub-family, leaf, kind, named form (four levels).

### Candidate A (recommended): ornamental family, then generic type, then kind, then named form
- **Level 1, sub-family. Axis: the family of ornamental object, meaning what the thing is as a display object** (vessel, mirror or frame, candle, scent, festive piece, artificial flora, garden ornament, flag or sign, decorative light). This is the axis the generalist retailers use for their top tabs (S1, S2, S6, S7).
- **Level 2, leaf. Axis: generic type within the family** (for example vases and jars; mirrors; candles; incense).
- **Level 3, kind. Axis: the form a buyer compares at** (see 4b), such as pillar candle, cheval mirror, ginger jar.
- **Level 4, named form: a model, line or iconic design** (meiping, Diptyque Baies, Voluspa Baltic Amber).
- Facets, never levels: scent family, wax, material, size, colour, style, occasion, tradition, period, maker, condition, tier.

**Where A breaks (edge objects):**
1. **Candle holder and wall sconce.** IKEA, John Lewis, Target and 1stDibs pair them with candles (S1, S2, S3, S26) but the framework key reaches B3 first for lanterns and lights, and customs treats a holder as a lighting fitting (S17).
2. **Artificial Christmas tree.** It is a festive piece (S16) and an artificial plant; the sub-family axis puts it in B4.5, the artificial flora family would not take it.
3. **Neon sign.** A flag-and-sign object (S20), a decorative light (framework B3 note), and a collectible; the sub-family axis puts it in Signs because the signage purpose is what it is made to be.
4. **Bird bath and garden fountain.** Garden ornaments to Wikipedia and garden retailers (S12, S13, S14) but with a function (water for birds, water feature) that may belong in B10 or C2.

### Candidate B: sensory or display effect first (sight, flame, scent, sound, movement, message)
- **Level 1 axis: the effect the object has on the room** (static form, flame and light, scent, sound, wind movement, message).
- Sub-families: Static form (vases, bowls, objects, mirrors); Flame (candles); Scent (diffusers, incense); Sound (wind chimes); Wind movement (spinners, windsocks, weathervanes, flags); Message (signs, flags).
- Gains: it explains why incense, candles and chimes feel like their own products, and why a buyer of one does not compare it to a vase.
- **Breaks:** a scented candle has flame, light and scent; a mirror has reflection and light; a flag has wind movement and message; a wind spinner has movement and ornament. No retailer uses this axis (S1, S2, S6, S7), so a person looking for a vase would not look under Static form. Edge objects: scented candle, flag, wind spinner, snow globe.

### Candidate C: occasion or placement first (seasonal, wall, table, floor, garden)
- **Level 1 axis: when or where it is displayed.**
- This is what Home Depot, Matsuya, Old World Christmas and HS 9505 do for seasonal goods (S10, S11, S16, S25), and what Pepperfry's room-wise guide does for everything else (S6).
- **Breaks, and not allowed:** occasion and setting are modifiers in Appendix A, so they cannot divide inner levels. In practice a lantern, string light, wreath, garland or bauble serves several occasions; a mirror is wall or floor; a candle holder is table or wall. Customs shows the same failure: a festive motif does not change what the article is (S17, S36).
- Use as **views and tags**, not levels.

**Recommendation: Candidate A**, with occasion, placement and tradition as generated views and tags.

## 4. Depth and size

- **Generic kinds:** about 120 to 170 (the 54 shown plus the kinds in the sub-families not shown in full: more mirror forms, more incense and fragrance products such as fragrance oils and sachets, tree and festival pieces of other traditions, more sign types, flag kinds, garden pieces such as spinners, stakes, rain chains).
- **Named forms:** about 300 to 700 if flag designs are held in a register outside the tree, and over 900 if every national and state flag is a named form (one US retailer lists 612 world flags and 257 state flags, S33). Other named forms: about 15 vase silhouettes (S18), about 8 antique mirror forms (S19), candle lines such as Baies and Baltic Amber (S28, S29), ornament series (S27), IKEA and designer vase models (S1, S9).
- **Where depth concentrates:** home fragrance (forms times scent times maker), festive decorations (many traditions, S6, S25), garden ornaments (S13, S14), and flags (design register).
- **Broad leaves** (many kinds, many buyers): candles, vases and jars, mirrors, tree and home display, artificial flowers, garden statues. **Medium:** incense, signs, flags, frames, wind chimes. **Narrow:** candle tools, snow globes, windsocks, weathervanes, burgees (each a single kind with a few named forms).

| Candles | one kind per candle form (container, pillar, taper, votive, tealight, LED) | wax, scented or unscented, scent family, colour, size are facets; lines such as Baies are named forms | S24, S31; quality compared by maker at the product-line level (S28, S29) |
| Home fragrance | one kind per product form (reed diffuser, wax melt, room spray, potpourri, stick/cone/coil incense, burner) | scent family is a facet | S3 form first, scent later; S22 form first; S23 treats scent as a separate segmentation |
| Festive | one kind per object (ornament, topper, tree, garland, hina set, koinobori) | occasion is a tag; theme collections (S10) are views | S10 themes, S11 holidays are views; S36 notes the object, not the holiday, defines the article |
| Artificial flowers | stem, arrangement, dried arrangement | species, colour are facets | S7, S2 |
| Garden ornaments | statue, gnome, gazing ball, sundial, weathervane, chime | material (stone, metal, resin) is a facet even though S14 uses it first | S12, S13, S14 |
| Flags | one kind per use form (national flag, garden flag, pennant, windsock) | each polity's design is a named form held in a register | S15, S33 |
| Signs | one kind per sign construction (enamel, tin, neon) | advertised subject is a view | S20 |

The choice level differs: kinds are deep (per product form) in fragrance, candles and mirrors, and shallow (one kind with many named forms) in vases and flags, because vase buyers compare by maker and silhouette while flag buyers compare by design.

## 5. Quality and tier

**Overall: preference (price band).** Pepperfry's budget bands (S6) are the only price ordering most of the category has.

**Exceptions:**
- **Home fragrance and candles: consensus-ranked by maker.** Specialists place houses in tiers: Voluspa owns the 30 to 50 USD tier, Diptyque and Trudon set the luxury template, Jo Malone, Byredo and Le Labo are premium benchmarks (S28). The market report splits candles into mass and premium categories (S24). Review sites compare single product lines at cost per ounce and wax type (S29). The measurable specs (fragrance-oil load, burn hours, wax) exist but are not a standard ladder, so this is not spec-ranked.
- **Wind chimes:** tuning is checkable with a tuner but labels such as Gregorian or 432 Hz are used loosely (S34); store as an attribute (scale, tuned or untuned), not a tier.
- **Flags:** manufacturers sell material grades (standard nylon, polyester, reinforced, commercial quality, S33); store as material and grade attributes.
- **Collectible ornaments and signs:** condition codes and era (S27, S20) are tiers of condition, which Appendix A already treats as a modifier.

**How tier appears:** a `tier` modifier (mass, mid, premium, luxury) on the maker ledger row and on named forms, and a condition modifier on owned items. Never a level.

## 6. Makers and brands

**Centrality: identity** in fragrance (the house is what is sought: S28), in art glass and studio ceramics (1stDibs creators facet S26, SSENSE designers S9) and in series ornaments (Hallmark, S27). **Incidental** for flags (S33), garden ornaments (S14), signs, and generic vases, mirrors and frames. No platform dependence: holders and warmers fit by standard size, not by maker.

**Recommendation: yes, a maker ledger**, scoped to fragrance houses and candle makers (a 60-plus brand shelf in one specialist, S28), art-glass and studio-ceramic makers (S26), ornament makers (S27) and incense houses (S22). Columns: maker, known-for kinds, scene tags, tier, price band, country, era, attestation URL. Named forms carry the maker; kinds do not.

## 8. Order inside a leaf

**Canonical form first, then ascending scale or burn, then named forms by lineage or chronology.** Reasons:
- Buyers and retailers start from the generic form (S1, S2, S35).
- Candle forms read naturally as tealight, votive, taper, pillar, container; incense as stick, cone, coil by burn length (S21).
- Vase silhouettes carry lineage (bronze-derived gu, zun, hu versus the plum-blossom meiping) and period (S18), so chronology orders named forms.
- Mirrors order by placement: tabletop, wall, leaner, standing (S19, S30).

## 9. Boundaries

Closest cross-boundary objects and the precedence sentence for each. Items marked (framework) restate an existing note; items marked (proposed) are new and need the owner.

**B3 Lighting**
1. (framework) A candle, string or fairy light, lava lamp or neon sign made mainly as decoration is B4; a lamp, bulb or lantern made to give light is B3.
2. (proposed) A holder made to carry a candle (candlestick, candelabrum, tealight holder, wall sconce) is B4 with candles, because IKEA, John Lewis, Target and 1stDibs pair them with candles (S1, S2, S3, S26); a lantern stays B3. Customs puts a holder with lighting fittings (S17), so the owner should rule.

**F2 Art**
3. (framework) A free-standing ornamental figurine is F2; a garden statue or gnome is B4; vases, bowls and other vessels are B4 or B5 however artistic.
4. (proposed) A figure made to hang on or stand under a tree, wreath or festival display (tree ornament, tree topper) is B4 whatever its theme; a ceramic figure made to stand on a shelf is F2 even when it shows Santa. Customs separates a Santa figurine (ornamental ceramic) from a festive article (S17).
5. (proposed) A decorative plate made to hang is B4; the picture painted on it is not a separate object; a framed picture is F2 and the empty frame is B4.
6. Decorative globes (S2) are F1 under the framework's "maps and globes", not B4.

**F4 Religious & Ceremonial, E1 Toys**
7. (framework) A menorah, diya, Advent wreath or home nativity set is F4; a tree, tree ornament, Diwali lantern or Hina-matsuri display not made for worship is B4. Incense made for a rite is F4; home-fragrance incense is B4.
8. (proposed) A Christmas cracker, party popper or streamer is B4; a toy cap gun or toy firework replica is E1.
9. Matsuya (S25) sells new-year decorations, dolls and memorial lanterns together; the owner should say where the line between festive display and worship sits for items such as shimenawa and bon lanterns.

**C2 Garden, C3 Plants, B10 Animals**
10. (framework) A living plant is C3; an artificial or dried flower is B4; planters and plant pots are C2; vases are B4.
11. (proposed) A cachepot, jardinière or planter is C2 even when sold as decorative (IKEA and John Lewis already separate them, S1, S2); a tulip vase (tulipiere) is a vase.
12. (proposed) A garden ornament with no function (statue, gnome, gazing ball, sundial, weathervane, chime, spinner) is B4; a fountain or water feature, a bird bath or bird feeder, an arch, pergola or obelisk is C2 or B10. Wikipedia lists bird baths and fountains as ornaments (S12) and gardenornaments.com sells gazebos with them (S14).

**B1/B5 and B6, H1**
13. (framework) A vessel made to hold food or drink is B5; a vessel made to be looked at is B4.
14. (proposed) A tray made to carry or serve is B5; a tray made to stand things on for display (Westwing Deko-Tablett, S7) is B4.
15. (framework) Grooming mirrors are H1. (proposed) A mirror cabinet is B1 and a lighted makeup or vanity mirror is H1; IKEA files both under Mirrors (S30).
16. (proposed) A product made to scent the air (room spray, diffuser, wax melt, potpourri) is B4; a product made to remove odour or clean a surface or fabric is B6. Target files air fresheners under home fragrance (S3).
17. (proposed) Essential oils sold for a diffuser or burner are B4; oils sold for therapy are H2. Target mixes them (S3).

**Flags and signs**
18. (proposed) A flag is B4 whatever it is made of; customs splits textile flags (63.07) from festive articles (95.05) by material (S36). A flagpole is a part of the flag under S3 only if sold with it; otherwise C8 hardware. FlagsUSA treats poles as their own branch (S33).
19. (proposed) A sign made to be looked at as decoration, advertising memorabilia or shop identity is B4; a safety, traffic or regulatory sign is B8 or C8.

## 10. Recommendation and open questions

**Recommended structure:** Candidate A. B4 Decor → 9 sub-families (Vessels and table ornaments; Mirrors and frames; Candles and holders; Home fragrance; Festive and party decorations; Artificial and dried flowers; Garden and outdoor ornaments; Flags and signs; Decorative lighting) → leaf by generic type → kind at the product-form level where buyers compare → named form for models, lines, silhouettes and designs. **Maximum depth: four levels below the category**, but most leaves need only three (leaf, kind, optional named form).

**Modifiers held as facets or views, not levels:** scent family, wax, material, size, colour, style, occasion, tradition, period, maker, condition, tier.

**Shared vocabulary:** scent family should be one controlled list shared with the body-fragrance kinds in H1 (placement key step 18). Recommended canonical list: the Edwards wheel (4 families, 14 subfamilies, S5), with mapping tables to the 10-family lists of candle suppliers and incense houses (S4, S22) and the 8-type market segmentation (S23).

**Maker ledger:** yes, for fragrance houses, art-glass and ceramic makers, ornament makers and incense houses.

**Owner decisions needed:**
1. Candle holders (candlestick, candelabrum, tealight holder, wall sconce): B3 or B4 (boundary 2).
2. Garden pieces with a function (bird bath, feeder, fountain, arch): B4, B10 or C2 (boundary 12).
3. Whether national and state flag designs are named forms in the tree or sit in a separate design register (about 900 rows).
4. Cachepots and jardinières: C2 (proposed) or B4 (boundary 11).
5. Where the festive/worship line falls for shimenawa, kadomatsu and memorial lanterns (boundary 9).
6. The canonical scent-family vocabulary and whether it is shared with H1 body fragrance.
7. Essential oils and air-freshener sprays: B4, H2 or B6 (boundaries 16, 17).
8. Flagpoles and regulatory signs (boundaries 18, 19).
9. Fireworks, sparklers and party poppers sit in B4 in the key; they are consumed in use and safety-classed. Keep them as kinds in Festive and party, or move them to a consumable leaf?
10. Decorative lighting (string lights, lava lamps) is in the key but no source was fetched; attestation pending.
