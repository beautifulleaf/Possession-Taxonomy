Research, not decision. Written from fetched pages only (40 sources, listed in `verify/out/S2_B8_sources.tsv`; keys S1 to S40 below are the row numbers of that file). ## 0. Category

**Code and name:** B8 Home Safety & Security, in domain B Home.

**Membership test (placement key step 30, fourth branch, framework/CATEGORIES_v0_6.md):** made to **secure or protect** a home, its people and its contents, to hold doors and windows, or to keep pests out: locks and safes for any contents (locks made for a bicycle or vehicle are G1 under S3), smoke and carbon-monoxide alarms, fire extinguishers, security cameras, doorbells and alarm systems, baby monitors, stair gates and child-safety locks, letterboxes and parcel boxes, door stops, pest traps, swatters, zappers, poisons and repellents, mosquito nets and coil holders. Branch B8 is tried after B5, B6 and B7 and before B3, B1, B2 and B4.

**Precedence sentences and notes in the framework that touch B8:**
- S3: locks made for a bicycle or vehicle are G1; safes are never "parts" (so a safe is B8 and not B1, "whatever they are made to hold").
- S3: cleaning products are B6 and fuels are C8, whatever they are used in.
- Step 10 note: tools made to kill pests are B8; slaughter and stunning tools for livestock are C7; pepper spray, stun guns and tasers are E4.
- Step 24 note: a connected version of a household object (smart lock, alarm, camera, doorbell) is filed by its task at step 30, so it is B8; connectivity is a modifier. Baby monitors are B8.
- Step 32 (C8): hinges and fittings, plumbing and electrical supplies, fencing and windows and doors are building hardware, tried after B8.
- Step 30 (B1): cots, high chairs and playpens, lockers and cabinets are furniture and storage; B1 is tried after B8.
- Step 2 (H2): first-aid kits and monitors of an illness or pregnancy are H2.
- Step 5 (I1, I3): software, accounts and subscriptions are I; a cloud video plan is a subscription.

### 1.1 Retailers and marketplaces (UK, US, Canada, Switzerland/Austria, Japan, Australia)
- **Screwfix, UK trade counter (S1).** One "Security" department holds: Padlocks, Key Safes & Cabinets, Vehicle & Outdoor Security, Door Locks & Bolts, Safes, Post Boxes, Garage Security, CCTV, Commercial Security, Window Locks, Security Lights, Camera Doorbells, Lockers & Cabinets, Smart Security. So locks, safes, cameras and post boxes share one department, divided by object form. Below it, Door Locks & Bolts (S2) lists mortice locks (including 5-lever deadlocks), multi-point locking systems, night latches, cylinder (Euro) locks, door bolts, push-button locks, patio door locks, smart or digital locks, with padlocks, cam locks and knob locks treated as separate guides. A search excerpt of a Screwfix smart-security landing page (not fetched, so unverified) links CO, heat and fire alarms under `safety-workwear` and burglar alarms under `electrical-lighting`, which would put fire and burglar alarms in different departments.
- **Toolstation, UK (S3).** "Security" is split into CCTV & Surveillance (CCTV and systems, IP cameras, doorbells and chimes), Locks, Chains & Barriers (padlock bolts and hasps, padlocks, shackles and chain, security cables and chains, door locks and latches, safes and cabinets, safety barriers, smart locks) and Alarms & Sensors (smoke and fire alarms, burglar alarms). Fire alarms and burglar alarms are together here.
- **Safe.co.uk, UK specialist (S4).** Safes page: home and office safes versus high-security safes, with an insurance cash-rating label ("insurance rated to £4k") used as the tier cue.
- **Home Depot US, Safes (S5).** 525 results; facets in order Category, Protection Against, Lock Type, Safe Size, Features, Includes, Safe Capacity. "Protection against" (fire, water, burglary) and "lock type" come before size.
- **Home Depot Canada, Safety & Security (S6).** Top groups: Safes, Padlocks, Child Safety, Smart Technology, Fire Resistant Safes. Child safety sits beside safes and padlocks.
- **OBI Switzerland, Germany-language DIY (S26).** Sicherheitstechnik: Alarmanlagen, Brandschutz, Kameraüberwachung & Videoüberwachung, Kindersicherheit, Profilzylinder & Kabazylinder, Riegel & Sperren, Tresore, Türschlösser & Türtechnik, Türsicherung & Fenstersicherung, Türspione, Vorhängeschlösser. The listing shows powder and foam extinguishers, a fire blanket, extinguisher spray and smoke alarms (Brandschutz is the matching sub-category); Alarmanlagen is a separate group. Child safety (corner guards) is here, as in Canada.
- **OBI Austria, Tresore (S27).** 220 safes; filters Verfügbarkeit, Feuerschutz, Art, Marke, Preis. Kinds named in the description and listing: Möbeltresor, Wandtresor (walled in), Feuerschutztresor (fire), Laptop-Tresor, Schlüsseltresor, Geldkassette, Waffenschrank (gun cabinet, conforming to § 36 of the weapons law).
- **Eisenbach Tresore, Germany specialist (S28).** Sorts "Wertschutzschrank/Wertschutztresor" by EN 1143-1 resistance grade 0 to V with VdS class 0 to 5 as the same ladder, plus fire classes S60P and S120P (EN 1047); Wandtresor as its own named kind.
- **Safelincs, UK fire-safety specialist (S11, S33, S34).** Extinguishers split by agent: water mist, CO2, water, foam, dry powder, wet chemical; separate Fire Blankets; smoke alarms, CO alarms (battery, mains, digital display, interlinked, boat/camping), combined smoke and CO alarms, and jurisdiction-specific "landlord regulation" alarm categories for England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. Smoke-alarm facets: Sensor Type (electro-chemical, heat, ionisation, optical), British Standard Grade (D1, D2, F1, F2), Connection (standalone, radio-interlink, wired), battery life, battery type, product range.
- **IKEA US (S18).** Baby proofing & child safety sits under Home improvement > Home safety locks, latches & more, next to Home safety. Items: multi-latch drawer straps, corner bumpers, outlet safety plugs, door stop, bed guard rail.
- **Cainz and a specialist shop, Japan (S13, S14).** Cainz groups home-security goods first by the principle by which they deter (light, eyes, sound, time) and also by place (outdoor and entrance, windows, other); kinds named: outdoor camera, sensor light, anti-burglar gravel, door phone, security film, window alarm, auxiliary lock, indoor camera, personal alarm and whistle, safe. The Japanese specialist shop lists 金庫, 防犯砂利, 電子錠, 鍵・南京錠, 補助錠, キーボックス, キーキャビネット, 防犯・監視カメラ, センサーライト・アラーム, 忍び返し (fence guard spikes), 防災グッズ. The Japanese layer adds supplementary locks (補助錠), noisy gravel and fence guards, which the UK and US trees lack.
- **Ace Hardware US, pest control (S22).** Insect and animal control > insecticides > wasps; facets Brand, Price, Product Form, Pest Type, Safe for Edibles; forms are spray, foam, liquid, aerosol, trap, repellent.
- **Letterboxes, Australia (S36); mosquito nets (S35).** Letterboxes are sold as wall-mounted, freestanding and parcel (pillar) types. Mosquito nets are conical, round or rectangular, with 0, 2 or 4 openings, plus stroller nets and head nets.

### 1.2 Enthusiast communities and wikis
- **Lockwiki (S7).** Separates **Lock Designs** by mechanism (axial/tubular, combination, dimple, disc-detainer, electronic, hybrid, lever, magnetic, pin-tumbler, slider, wafer, warded) from **Lock Types** by form (cam lock, cylinder, padlock). It also holds a "lock cylinder identification" table of named models (Abloy Classic, ABUS EC700, ASSA dp4400, Schlage Everest, Medeco Duracam, CISA C2000, DOM Plura, Kale Kilit 164 BNE, Nemef NF4 and others) by keyway shape, detainer type and quantity. So the enthusiast world already treats mechanism as an attribute and form as the type.
- **Wikipedia category (S38).** 65 pages mixing forms (deadbolt, mortise lock, rim lock, padlock, night latch, door chain, child safety lock, real-estate lock box, bicycle lock), mechanisms (pin tumbler, wafer tumbler, disc tumbler, lever tumbler, warded) and named designs (Bramah lock, Chubb detector lock, Banbury lock, Cooper vane).

### 1.3 Collectors and the resale market
- **History of Locks Research & Social Group forum (S39).** Categories: Keys, Padlocks (combination, key operated, railroad, seal, story padlocks, fakes), Patent & Proprietary Locks (doors, furniture, safes, vaults, time locks), Pre-Industrial Revolution Locks, Safes Strongrooms & Vaults (1,011 discussions, the largest), Safe Plates Badges & Escutcheons. Collectors divide by form, by era (before and after 1778), and by maker marks.
- **Collecting guide (S40).** Collector associations (American Lock Collectors Association, West Coast Lock Collectors Association) are mainly UK and US; museums include the German Museum of Locks and Fittings in Velbert. Era and maker, not mechanism, organise the collector view.

### 1.4 Standards bodies, certifiers and trade classifications
- **Safe standards (S9, S28, S37, S31).** EN 1143-1 resistance grades 0 to XIII (safes grade 0 to X), each about 50% stronger than the last, with a required EN 1300 lock class (A to D); EN 14450 S1 and S2 for lower-risk "security cabinets"; EN 1047-1 and EN 15659 for fire; UL 687 TL-15, TL-30, TL-30x6, TRTL-30x6, TRTL-60x6, TXTL-60x6 for burglary and UL fire classes (350 with 0.5 to 4 hours). China grades anti-theft safes A1, A2, B1, B2, B3, C (A1: 15 minutes; C: 60 minutes with explosives, at least 450 kg). Belgian dealer pairs each grade with an indicative insurable cash value (S9).
- **Lock and cylinder standards (S8, S24, S25, S23, S32).** US ANSI/BHMA Grades 1 to 3 (cycles, strikes, weight) with BHMA letter grades AAA, AA, A (S8). UK BS 3621 for mortice deadlocks and night latches on timber doors; TS 007 stars (3-star cylinder, or 1-star cylinder with a 2-star handle) and Sold Secure SS 312 Diamond for euro cylinders on uPVC and composite doors (S24). Padlocks: CEN grades 1 to 6 versus Sold Secure Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond, with an approximate equivalence CEN 3 to 6 (S25). Sold Secure rates by product category: Domestic Safes, Key Safes, Safes EN 14450 S2, Lock Cylinders, Padlocks & Fittings, Smart Locks (maximum Gold), Firearms Cabinets, Ground Anchors, Security Chains, Audible Accessory Alarms, Personal Attack Alarms, Domestic Buildings, plus cycle, motorcycle and caravan categories (S23). China's mandatory GB 21556.2-2025 (from 1 March 2026) rates whole locks A/B/C with C needing 10 minutes of technical-opening resistance and 100,000 cycles, and adds anti-electromagnetic, local-encryption and 3D-liveness requirements for smart locks (S32; consumer-media source).
- **Domestic fire detection (S10, S11).** BS 5839-6:2019 describes a home system by two independent codes: Grade (A, C, D1, D2, F1, F2, the power and control engineering) and Category (LD1 to LD3 for life protection, PD1 and PD2 for property protection, the coverage). Products are marked to BS EN 14604. Extinguishers are identified by agent type, fire class (A B C D K in UL/NFPA; A B C F in UK) and a numeric rating (S15, S34).
- **Trade classifications (S19, S20, S21).** The baby-proofing market is segmented into Safety Gates (28.4%), Cabinet & Drawer Locks (18.7%), Corner & Edge Guards (14.2%), Outlet Covers (11.8%), Door Locks & Stops (10.6%), Window Guards (8.5%), Others (stove knob covers, furniture straps, toilet locks, pool barriers) (S19). A UK pest-control supplier that belongs to the BPCA divides by target animal first: Bird, Rodent, Insect, Animal; and within rodent: traps, poison, bait stations, proofing, repellers; within insect: public-use versus professional-use insecticides, traps, electric fly killers, repellers, insect screens (S20). The Japanese reference page divides insecticides by formulation (aerosol, oil, emulsion, powder, bait, fumigant, vaporiser) and lists household kinds as aerosol, mosquito coil, electric mosquito vaporiser (liquid and mat), bait, trap, with market shares by maker (Earth about 40%, Kincho about 25%, Fumakilla about 15%) (S21).

### 1.5 Museums, reference works and independent reviewers
- **Consumer Reports (S16, S17, S29).** Safety gates: hardware-mounted, pressure-mounted, retractable, freestanding play yards, with a rule that the top of stairs needs hardware-mounted. Baby monitors: radio-frequency video with a separate display, WiFi video to a phone, and hybrids. Home cameras: three main types, wireless (plug-in or battery) cameras, video doorbells, floodlight cameras; choose camera type by placement, then power source, then WiFi coverage, then cloud storage plan.
- **Maker guide (S30).** Wyze lists plug-in wired, battery, pan-and-tilt, video doorbell and floodlight cameras, ordered by power source and best use.
- **Secom, Japan (S12).** Residential fire alarms are two sensing types: smoke (photoelectric) as the default everywhere, and heat (fixed-temperature) for kitchens.
- **Wikipedia Safe (S37).** Lists the specification axes of a safe: burglar resistance, fire resistance, environmental resistance, type of lock, location (wall, floor). This is the neutral summary of what buyers choose.

### 1.6 Where the sources agree and where they split
- **Agree:** locks, safes, alarms and cameras are separate object groups; fire safety (extinguishers, blankets, smoke and CO alarms), child safety and pest control are each their own group in every region fetched (Safelincs, OBI, Home Depot Canada, IKEA, PestFix, Ace, Japanese guides). Mechanism (pin tumbler and so on) is an attribute, not a type, in enthusiast sources (S7, S38).
- **Split on alarms:** Toolstation and OBI group burglar and fire alarms differently; Screwfix may put fire alarms in Safety & Workwear and burglar alarms in Electrical (unverified, search excerpt only) (S3, S26). Secom treats the fire alarm as a home-security product (S12).
- **Split on child safety:** a lock for a cabinet is a "child safety lock" at OBI, IKEA and Home Depot Canada (S26, S18, S6), but a "cabinet lock" or "latch" at general hardware stores; Wikipedia files "Child safety lock" among locks (S38).
- **Split on pests:** supplier trees go pest-first (S20); consumer sources go product form first (S21, S22). Ace carries both as facets (Product Form and Pest Type).
- **Split on safes:** German and Belgian sources lead with the resistance grade (S28, S9); UK and US consumer sources lead with size and protection against (S4, S5); Sold Secure rates domestic safes by Bronze to Gold (S23).
- **Split on security lights and fencing:** Screwfix lists Security Lights and Garage Security in Security (S1), Japan lists fence guards and gravel (S14); the framework files lights in B3 and gravel and fencing elsewhere (see section 9).
