## 0. Category

**Code and name:** C8 Building Materials & Hardware.

**Membership test** (CATEGORIES_v0_7, step 32, branch 2): *a material, building product, hardware or supply: timber, bricks, roofing, windows, doors, stairs, flooring, tiles, wallpaper, paint, adhesives, fasteners, hinges and fittings, plumbing and electrical supplies, mains cables, extension leads, plugs, fuses, household batteries, lubricants, fuels and fuel cans, firewood, matches, firelighters, and long utility and candle lighters (pocket lighters are B9), packing materials, fencing, acoustic panels.* Category line: "building products, hardware, electrical and plumbing supplies, household batteries, fuels, packing materials."

**Notes and precedence sentences that name C8:**
- Step 4 note (G2): a building is filed as a whole; its elements and products, installed or not, are filed by what they are: structural, envelope and finishing products (bricks, timber, roofing, windows, doors, stairs, flooring, tiles, wallpaper), fixed plumbing and wiring, fencing and acoustic panels are C8. Kitchen and wardrobe cabinetry is B1; carpets, blinds and curtains are B2; baths, showers, basins, toilets and hot tubs are B7; installed machines, lamps, safes and alarms keep their own category. Greenhouses and polytunnels are C2.
- S3 (parts follow the whole): building products are never parts; cleaning products are B6 and fuels are C8, whatever they are used in.
- Step 7 note (F6): a natural material sold for a use is filed by that use; firewood is C8; garden stone is C2.
- Step 27 note (B9): long utility and candle lighters, matches and firelighters are C8; pocket lighters are B9.
- Step 28 (C4): paints and glues made for model-making and picture-making are C4; step 29 (C2): garden stone and artificial turf are C2.
- Step 24 (D1): cables and adapters made to connect electronic devices (USB, HDMI, audio, charging) are D1; step 30 B7 note: wall-box chargers for electric vehicles are G1.
- Step 30 B8: locks and safes, smoke and carbon-monoxide alarms, letterboxes and parcel boxes, door stops are B8; B4 holds signs and house numbers.
- Step 32 branch 1 (C1): tools, ladders, tool boxes, and by S3 the batteries, bits and blades made for tools stay in C1.
- Shared rules S3b (v0.7, decided): a charger for standard-size cells is C8 and a cordless-platform charger C1 (K-3); a loose standard-size cell, 18650 included, is C8, a platform pack is C1 and a one-device battery follows the device (K-4); welding wire and rods, solder, a grinding disc or a sanding sheet cut for one sander are C1, sandpaper and abrasive sheets and rolls for general use C8 (K-12); cabinet knobs and pulls are C8 and a lockset that includes a lock is B8 (K-196).
- Notes at other steps (v0.7, decided): fuel bought in its own container, owned or leased, is C8, and pumped or delivered fuel is out (K-30); boards sold loose for a pergola, gazebo or decking kit are C8 (K-66); gas cylinders and flints are C8, hookah charcoal B9 (K-166); landscaping materials (K-174); pumps (K-175); taps, valves, shower trays, enclosures and bath panels C8 (K-186); door letter plates C8 (K-188); regulatory and safety signs C8 (K-192); packing materials C8 (K-193); fence panels, posts, wire, netting, gates, electric-fence energisers, tape and insulators C8 (K-195). Decided but not yet in the v0.7 key: ceiling roses C8 (B-18) and chimney and flue work C8 (B7-O1); see section 12.

## 1. How the domain already divides itself

**Retailers and marketplaces, by region.**
- **Australia, Bunnings** [S1, S2, S3, S4, S10, S30]: "Building & Hardware" is a department of 15 groups: Timber; Building Boards; Building Materials & Supplies; Concrete & Cementing; Fasteners & Fixings; Door Hardware; Ladders; Doors; Fencing & Gates; General Hardware; Insulation; Roofing; Security & Safety Products; Steel & Aluminium Sections; Windows [S1]. The groups are building elements and materials; ladders (C1) and security products (B8) sit in the same department. Fasteners & Fixings (3,605 results) splits into Nails; Nuts & Bolts; Wedges & Spacers; Rivets; Screws; Post Supports & Anchors; Wall & Floor Anchors; General Fasteners; Timber Connectors; Collated Fasteners [S2]: fastener type first, with use ("timber connectors", "collated" for nail guns) mixed in. Electrical sits in a separate department with Heaters, Air Treatment and Solar & EV Charging [S3]. Paint & Woodcare splits Paint, Woodcare and Wallpaper [S4]; how the Woodcare shelf is divided was not reported. Batteries (123 results) sit under Electrical and split Non Rechargeable; Rechargeable; Specialty; Battery Chargers & Accessories, while the listing is mixed by size (AA, AAA, C, D, 9V, A23, CR2, CR123, 2032, 2450) and by line (Duracell Coppertop and Everyday; Energizer Max and Ultimate Lithium; Varta Longlife Extra) [S10]. BBQ Fuels (45) are briquettes, lump charcoal, binchotan, wood pellets, smoking chips and chunks by wood (apple, cherry, hickory, mesquite, oak) and firestarters [S30].
- **UK, Toolstation** [S5–S9, S11]: the trade-counter categories are Screws & Fixings (Screws; Nails & Staples; Fixings & Fasteners; Chemical Fixings; Nuts, Bolts & Washers), Electrical Supplies & Accessories (Switches & Sockets; Cables & Flexes; Cable Management; Power Distribution; plus Domestic Electricals such as carbon monoxide detectors, timers and EV charging), Plumbing (25 groups, many by joining method: compression, push-fit, solder-ring, end-feed, press-fit, MDPE; plus Pipe, Waste Fittings, Traps & Wastes, Brassware Valves & Taps, Guttering, Underground Drainage, Gas Fittings, Plumbing Consumables), Painting & Decorating (Paint listed by type: emulsion, masonry, fence, spray, radiator, tile, floor, furniture, anti-mould; also by room), and Adhesives (101 products; Grab Adhesive 46, General Adhesives & Glue 34, Wood Glue 15, Wallpaper Adhesive 6) [S5–S9]. Screws are split by use: wood, decking and roofing, drywall, masonry, brass, electrical [S5]. Batteries & Chargers carry a Type facet (AA, C, CR123, Charger, and more) and a separate Battery (chemistry) facet that starts with Alkaline (23); the one listing mixes alkaline AA/AAA/D, lithium coin cells (CR/DL2032, CR2032), CR123A, a 12 V 23A cell and sealed lead-acid blocks (6 V 4 Ah, 12 V 7 Ah) [S11].
- **Germany, Hornbach** [S12, S13]: the URL paths place Befestigungstechnik (fixing technology; 2,587 results) under Eisenwaren (ironmongery) and Schrauben (screws) under Befestigungstechnik; the Befestigungstechnik page lists products from Fischer, Tox and Kreg and links on to Dübel types (Schwerlastdübel, Chemische Dübel) [S12]. Schrauben divides mainly by application (Holzschrauben, Gewindeschrauben, Schnellbauschrauben, Blechschrauben, Bohrschrauben, Betonschrauben, Ringschrauben) with one material group, Edelstahlschrauben (stainless), sitting among them; its popular filters are type (Spanplattenschraube, Terrassenschraube, Schlossschraube, Konstruktionsschraube, Innensechskantschraube), material (A2) and metric diameter M3–M14 [S13].
- **France, Leroy Merlin** [S14]: Quincaillerie > Cheville, vis, clou et boulon > Vis. Screws are described by drive (pozidriv, torx, philips, hex), thread (metric, machine, wood) and use (vis terrasse, bardage, charpente, placo, autoforeuse, béton), with brand sub-groups Vis Standers and Vis Spax [S14].
- **Japan, Komeri** [S26, S27]: 建材・木材・配管材 (building, timber and piping materials) has 19 groups with counts: cement-sand-gravel 153; blocks 20; exterior 262; piping 7,429; taps 4,470; gutters 345; safety and scaffold 7,238; roofing and cladding 236; structural steel 1,033; doors-windows 1,070; fencing 190; ventilation-chimney 467; plywood 36; sawn timber 220; DIY timber 141; insulation-plaster 56; interior 147; joinery-flooring 1,031; curing 144 [S26]. Hardware is a different department, 金物・電材 > 釘・ビス > 釘 > コンクリート釘 (four levels to a nail type), with a maker facet (Yahata Holdings, Trusco Nakayama, Komeri) [S27]. Taps and piping sit with building materials, not with bathrooms [S26].
- **US, Home Depot**: the fasteners page returned no category text (blocked-host log); no US retailer tree could be read. US coverage rests on standards bodies and enthusiast sources (†: one US home-improvement category page for fasteners and electrical is still needed, finding F43).
- **Fuels, UK**: Calor sells LPG bottles under Patio gas (propane), Propane and Butane, with facets cylinder colour, usage (BBQ, patio heater, camping, caravan, commercial), gas type and regulator type (screw-on, clip-on); propane bottles run 3.9, 5, 6, 13, 19 and 47 kg and butane bottles 4.5 and 15 kg, each priced as "New" or "Refill" [S28]. Wickes files Firewood and Firelighters with a Type facet: Coals, Firelighters, Kindling, Logs [S29].

**Enthusiast communities and reference sites.**
- **Candle Power Forums (US)** [S22]: flashlight users choose between the 18650 lithium-ion rechargeable (needs a charger, 4.2 V) and the CR123A primary lithium (3 V, long storage, cold performance); cell size is dictated by the light, and protected cells and flat-top cells are further attributes.
- **Bolt-grade guide (MetricMech)** [S37]: engineers choose a bolt by ISO 898-1 property class (4.6, 5.8, 8.8, 10.9, 12.9; the first number times 100 gives tensile strength in MPa), with stainless A2-70 and A4-80 as a separate code and rough SAE equivalents.
- **Glue Directory** [S23]: titled "Adhesive Types — Chemistry Reference"; the part that loaded lists specialty adhesives by material bonded (PVC pipe cement, polyethylene, polypropylene, porcelain, pottery repair).
- **Pipe-thread chart** [S24]: pipe threads are "a family of standards": NPT, NPSC, NPSM, NPSL (ASME B1.20.1), BSPT/R (ISO 7-1, taper), BSPP/G (ISO 228-1, parallel) and metric.

**Collectors and the resale market.** Evidence is thin. The only fetched page is a reproduction-hardware maker, House of Antique Hardware [S32], which lists Door Sets (locksets, hinges, door accessories), Cabinet Hardware (knobs and pulls), Indoor and Exterior Lighting, antique-style light switches and outlets, and picture-hanging hardware. Era (antique, reproduction) is therefore a modifier on ordinary kinds (hinge, knob, switch), not a separate branch. No collector wiki for building materials was found in the budget; this is a gap.

**Standards bodies and trade classifications.**
- **UNSPSC** [S18, S19, S35, S36]: building materials and hardware are split across segments. Segment 30 (structures, building and construction) has 13 families: Structural components and basic shapes; Concrete and cement and plaster; Roads and landscape; Structural building products; Insulation; Exterior finishing materials; Interior finishing materials; Doors and windows and glass; Plumbing fixtures; Construction and maintenance support equipment; Portable structure components; Underground mining structures; Structural materials [S18]. Segment 31 (manufacturing components and supplies) holds Hardware (31160000), Rope and chain and cable and wire and strap, Adhesives and sealants (31200000), Paints and primers and finishes (31210000), Gaskets, Seals, Fabricated pipe assemblies [S36], and its description names fasteners, industrial adhesives, sealants and solvents, paints, primers and finishes [S19]. Segment 15 holds Fuels, Gaseous fuels and additives, Lubricants and oils and greases and anti-corrosives [S35]. Segment 30's description includes "electrical and plumbing fixtures" [S19].
- **Uniclass (UK, NBS) Pr Products table, v1.43** [S20]: 17 groups: Pr_15 Preparatory; Pr_20 Structure and general; Pr_25 Skin; Pr_30 Opening; Pr_35 Covering and finishing; Pr_40 Signage, sanitary fittings and FF&E; Pr_45 Flora and fauna; Pr_50 Fabric accessory; Pr_55 Manufacturing process; Pr_60 Services source; Pr_65 distribution; Pr_70 outlet; Pr_75 control; Pr_80 general; Pr_82 Vehicle; Pr_85 Process engineering; Pr_90 Soft FM. The first axis is the building element or service function the product performs.
- **Battery designation (IEC 60086, ANSI C18)** [S15, S16]: the IEC code is a letter/number sequence for number of cells, chemistry letter (none zinc-carbon, L alkaline, C lithium manganese dioxide, S silver oxide, P zinc-air), shape (R round, F flat, P other), size code (R6 = AA, R03 = AAA, R14 = C, R20 = D; button cells and lithium-ion round cells use diameter and height codes) and modifiers. Size and chemistry are separate parts of the same designation. The ANSI system has its own letter suffixes. Common names (AA, C, D) are older than both [S15, S16].
- **Plug and socket types** [S21, S38]: 15 lettered types (A to O) assigned by the US Department of Commerce and carried by the IEC to group plugs by rough mechanical compatibility; the letters are arbitrary, and each type maps to national standards (A NEMA 1-15; C Europlug CEE 7/16; D BS 546; E CEE 7/5-7/6; F Schuko CEE 7/3-7/4; G BS 1363; I AS/NZS 3112; J SN 441011; K Danish; L CEI 23-50) [S21, S38].
- **Fastener standards** [S17, S33, S37]: ISO 898-1:2013 specifies bolts, screws and studs "with specified property classes", coarse and fine thread, under ISO/TC 2/SC 11 [S33]; screw drives are catalogued with ANSI and ISO type letters and bit size codes (Phillips PH0–4, Pozidriv PZ0–5) [S17].
- **Paint: EN 13300** [S31]: interior wall and ceiling paints are classified by end use, binder type (acrylic, vinyl, alkyd, epoxy, hydraulic lime, silicate), gloss, largest grain size, wet-scrub resistance, hiding power and cleanability, each as a set of classes [S31].
- **Fuel: EN 228** [S34] and GOV.UK [S25]: EN 228:2025 "Automotive fuels — Unleaded petrol — Requirements and test methods" (ICS 75.160.20, liquid fuels) and the E10 explainer exist; only the titles loaded, so grade detail is not attested here.

**Museums and reference works.** Wikipedia's battery-size list [S15] and screw-drive list [S17] are the working reference works; they divide batteries by shape (cylindrical, button/coin and lithium-ion among others) and screw drives by drive family (cruciform, multiple-square, pentalobular, tamper-resistant and others). No museum collection page for building materials was fetched.

**Where the sources agree.** (1) Every retailer and classification keeps fasteners as one family of its own, split by fastener type (screw, nail, bolt and nut, rivet, anchor) [S2, S5, S12, S14, S27]. (2) Plumbing, electrical and paint/adhesive supplies are separate families from structural materials in every source [S3, S5–S9, S18, S36]. (3) Batteries and fuels are never filed with building elements: they sit with electrical (Bunnings, Toolstation) or outdoor/BBQ and heating, or with fuels in UNSPSC [S3, S10, S11, S29, S30, S35]. (4) Where a standard gives a designation (battery size, plug type, property class, gloss class), buyers use it as the primary description [S11, S16, S21, S31, S37].

**Where they split the same objects differently.** (1) Taps and valves: with plumbing at Toolstation and Komeri [S7, S26], with sanitary fixtures in UNSPSC segment 30 "Plumbing fixtures" [S18]. (2) Screws: by application at Hornbach and Toolstation [S5, S13], by drive/thread/use at Leroy Merlin [S14], by head or drive in standards [S17]. (3) Hardware: Bunnings keeps Door Hardware and General Hardware in Building & Hardware [S1]; Hornbach's URL tree puts fixings under Eisenwaren (ironmongery) [S12]; UNSPSC puts "Hardware" under manufacturing components, away from building products [S36]. (4) Safety devices: Toolstation lists carbon-monoxide detectors and EV charging under electrical [S6]; the framework files these as B8 and G1. (5) Adhesives sit in an "Adhesives & Sealants" department of their own at Toolstation [S9], while tile adhesive and grout are grouped with tiling cutters and tools under Painting & Decorating [S8]: the same product is filed by chemistry in one place and by job in another.

## 2. The first axis people choose by

**Evidence on what buyers decide first.**
1. **The supply family, then the product family.** Every department tree starts from a family of supplies tied to a part of the build or repair: Bunnings Building & Hardware by element/material [S1]; Toolstation by Screws & Fixings, Electrical, Plumbing, Painting & Decorating, Adhesives & Sealants [S5–S9]; Komeri by building element [S26]; Uniclass by structure, skin, opening, covering, services source/distribution/outlet/control [S20]; UNSPSC by structure, finishing, hardware, adhesives, paints, fuels [S18, S35, S36].
2. **Inside fasteners, the application (what it fastens into) beats the drive or material.** Hornbach lists screws by application (wood, stainless, thread, drywall, sheet metal, self-drilling, concrete) [S13]; Toolstation by use (wood, decking and roofing, drywall, masonry) [S5]; Leroy Merlin by use ("pour votre chantier": terrasse, bardage, charpente, placo) [S14]. Diameter (M3–M14), length, drive and material (A2) are filters [S13, S14, S17].
3. **For batteries, the standard size class comes first, chemistry second.** Toolstation's Type facet lists AA, C, CR123 and the chemistry facet is separate [S11]; Bunnings' first cut is rechargeable versus non-rechargeable, but its titles carry the size [S10]; the IEC code separates chemistry letter from size code [S16]; flashlight buyers ask which cell the light takes (18650, 21700, CR123A) [S22].
4. **For paint, the paint type by surface and use, then the finish class.** Toolstation's menu is Emulsion, Masonry, Fence, Spray, Radiator, Tile, Floor, Furniture, Anti-mould and room [S8]; the standard classifies by end use, binder and classes for gloss, grain size, scrub resistance and hiding power [S31].
5. **For fuels, the form it is burned in** (gas bottle, logs, coal, charcoal, pellets, firelighter) and then use and size [S28–S30].
6. **For electrical and plumbing, the standard or joint system.** Plugs by lettered type (G, F, I) [S21, S38]; plumbing fittings by joining method (compression, push-fit, solder-ring, press-fit) [S7]; pipe threads by standard (NPT, BSPT, BSPP) [S24].

**Conclusion for the first axis (restated in the conformance pass, finding F40):** the **supply family** the product belongs to: structural and envelope products, finishes, fixings, hardware, plumbing, electrical, coatings, bonding, cells, fuels, packing. This is one product-class axis; it is what the retailers and classifications show, and it fits the batteries, fuels, packing and lighters that the earlier wording ("role the product plays in a build or repair job") did not. Below that, the leaf and kind axes are stated per sub-family in section 3. Size, material, grade, standard type, brand line and colour are attributes, tier entries on lines or named forms, or ledger rows, never levels. Decided (C-7): the axis is the supply family; the eleven sub-families (twelve before lighters and matches were merged into fuels) are the same under either wording.

## 3. Candidate structures

Common to all three: at most four levels below the category (sub-family, leaf, kind, named form; convention C5a); a sub-family with about 12 kinds or fewer skips the leaf level and stores its kinds with `leaf = null` (C5b). Material, region, price, brand, user, and market (trade or DIY) are never levels.

### Candidate A (recommended): supply family → product group → kind → named form

| Level | Stated axis | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| Sub-family | The supply family the product belongs to (structural and envelope, finish, fixing, hardware, plumbing, electrical, coating, bonding, cells, fuels, packing) | 11 sub-families (lighters, matches and firelighting are a leaf of fuels, C-7) |
| Leaf | Stated per sub-family in the table below (always one axis inside a sub-family) | about 45 leaves |
| Kind | Generic product type a person can own, at the level where buyers compare quality or makers (section 4b) | about 500 kinds (estimate) |
| Named form | A specific design below the kind: a standard designation that names a distinct, non-interchangeable form, or a maker's model or line (convention C2) | several hundred, open-ended |

## 8. Order inside a leaf

Convention C7: kinds go **canonical form first, then the secondary key that the sub-family states**; named forms go non-maker first, by first-attested year and then alphabetically, then maker-bound by maker (maker-ledger `name` order), then first-introduction year, then alphabetically (CV-21); variants by code or year. Secondary keys for C8: batteries, by the designation's size order within the leaf (R6/AA before R03/AAA), leaves in the order cylindrical, button and coin, rectangular and block; fasteners, by application type, then ascending size; plugs, the non-maker named forms go by first-attested year and then alphabetically, which where no year is attested is the Type letter A to O, accepting that "these letters are completely arbitrary" and carry no ranking [S21]; paints, by surface and use; fuels, by form, then bottle size. Reason: every source that offers a designation uses it as the order buyers already read [S11, S16, S21, S37]. Gloss class and property class are attributes and do not order named forms.

## 10. Recommendation (decided)

**Final structure: Candidate A.** Supply family (11 sub-families: structure, envelope and openings; interior finishes and floors; fixings and fasteners; door, window and building hardware; plumbing and drainage supplies; electrical supplies; paints, coatings and wood finishes; adhesives, sealants and fillers; batteries and chargers; fuels, lubricants and firelighting; packing and protection materials) → product group (leaf, axis stated per sub-family, skipped where a sub-family has about 12 kinds or fewer) → generic kind → named form, at most four levels below the category. Property class, paint classes, battery chemistry, rechargeability, rating, material, size, colour, trade or DIY and region are attributes, modifiers or tags, never levels. Plug types and coin-cell designations are non-maker named forms held in registers; screw drives, pipe threads and cell-size standards are attributes with compatibility-ledger rows; a product line spanning several kinds is a lines row, a line inside one kind (Calor Patio Gas) a maker-bound named form; makers, lines, named forms, variants and scene tags are uncapped and seeded from fetched pages and existing catalogues; UNSPSC and Uniclass codes are cross-reference columns on each kind [S18, S20, S36] (C-19). Boundaries follow the v0.7 key sentences quoted in section 9. Decisions that rest on an unattested fact are provisional until attested: the five packing kinds (C-7), plumbing joint systems as kinds (C-19), and the facet-derived kind sets of screws, paints and adhesives (CV-25).
