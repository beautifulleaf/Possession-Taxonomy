**Precedence sentences and notes in the framework that touch H3:**

A device sold on a medical purpose (an erectile-dysfunction ring or vacuum pump, a pelvic-floor trainer) can therefore be taken by step 2 before step 9 is reached; section 9 asks the owner to confirm the reading.
- Step 18 (H1, tried after step 9) and its note: "Massagers made to treat a condition, such as TENS units, are H2"; massagers for comfort are H1. Because step 9 comes first, a vibrator made for sexual stimulation is H3 even though customs law calls it a "massage apparatus" (S14). Which powered massagers count as made for sexual stimulation is decided by the ordered test in section 9, item 2 (OWNER: Q2, shared with H1 and the framework-conflicts note X4).
- Framework rule S3 (parts follow the whole): a component, attachment or consumable made for one kind of object goes to that object's category, so an attachment for a sex machine or a replacement liner for a masturbator is H3. Cases and bags that hold several objects, and storage, are never parts (B1). Cleaning products are B6 whatever they clean. Anything worn on the body is never a part (step 17).
- Framework rule S5 (test the object, not the motive): an antique electro-mechanical massager is a massager (S15); a sex toy kept as a collectible is still a sex toy.
- Appendix A: user (women, men, couples, gay men, adapted), scene (BDSM), region, price and tier, brand, material, power source and connectivity are modifiers, never levels. "Beginner" is not a user value (C6); it is a dated platform tag or a C4 skill-level scheme (section 7).
- Framework rule S2 and BRIEF: a kind is a generic type; an individual item, a theme or a licensed likeness is never a kind. A replica of a performer or character is a maker-bound named form (section 7).
- Step 1 (OUT): a tattoo or change made to the body, and a service, are out; genital piercing is a change to the body (OUT), while the jewellery worn in it is A3.
- Boundary with the Culinary and Pleasure taxonomies: none for objects. The practice of sex is Pleasure's; this category files the object only.

## 2. The first axis people choose by

Maker is the third click for premium vibrators and Japanese masturbators (S2, S22, S25). The tree should follow the first axis and leave every other choice as an attribute or a generated view.

## 3. Candidate structures

## 5. Quality and tier

**Type: preference, with measured spec attributes and one consensus material class.** No fetched source ranks kinds or makers on a recognised ladder.

- **Material class (consensus-ranked attribute).** Two independent guides, one US (S20) and one European (S21), use the same three classes: non-porous and body-safe (platinum-cured or medical silicone, borosilicate glass, stainless steel, ABS), porous "gray area" (TPE, TPR), avoid (jelly, soft PVC). Biocompatibility tests are USP Class VI and ISO 10993 (S21); the EU phthalate cap of 0.1 percent in four phthalates applies since July 2020 (S21).
