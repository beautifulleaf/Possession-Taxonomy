## 0. Category

**Code and name:** C2 Garden & Grounds.

**Membership test** (CATEGORIES_v0_7, step 29): *made for **growing plants or keeping gardens, grounds, paths, pools and drives**, powered or not: garden tools, mowers and ride-on mowers, trimmers, leaf blowers, pressure washers, snow clearers, wheelbarrows, planters and plant pots, plant care products, grow lights, irrigation, greenhouses and polytunnels, compost bins, artificial turf, garden stone, above-ground and inflatable swimming pools, pool cleaning equipment, test kits and pumps.* Notes on the step (v0_7): garden furniture is B1; garden ornaments, statues and gnomes are B4; paddling pools made for play are E1; hand-held chainsaws and pole saws are C2, axes, wedges and log splitters C1, tractor-driven splitters and chippers C7 (K-173); growing media, mulch, bark and soil improvers and stone, gravel and bark sold as decorative ground cover are C2, aggregate, paving, edging, membrane, fencing and gates C8 (K-174); water-moving pumps for watering, irrigation, pools, ponds and water features are C2, field irrigation pumps C7, drainage, transfer, sump and general pumps C1 (K-175); all swimming-pool equipment and chemicals are C2, hot-tub equipment B7 (K-176); water butts and rain tanks are C2 (K-177); made to protect plants or kill weeds is C2, made to kill household pests B8 (K-178); cachepots are C2, plant stands B1, children's garden tool sets E1 or C2 by C15 (K-179). Category line: "tools, machines and supplies for growing plants and keeping grounds and pools."

**Notes and precedence sentences elsewhere that name C2:**
- S3 (parts follow the whole): "Never parts: ... anything for growing plants (C2)". A part, attachment or consumable made for one kind of C2 machine (a mower blade, a pool pump cartridge) follows that machine.
- Step 4 (G2): greenhouses and polytunnels are C2, not G2; in-ground pools, pergolas, gazebos, summerhouses, garden rooms and sheds are G2; pop-up gazebos are B1.
- Step 7 (F6): garden stone is C2 when a natural material is sold for that use.
- Step 11 (G1): wheelbarrows and garden carts are C2; sack trucks and dollies are C1. A machine that carries its operator while doing other work (ride-on mower) goes by that work, so it is C2.
- Step 12 (C7): equipment for a household garden or allotment is C2, "including lawn and garden tractors without a rear power take-off and a Category 1 or larger three-point hitch" (K-99; two-wheel tractors by the same test); commercial and farm machines are C7; irrigation made for field crops is C7, irrigation for gardens, glasshouses, allotments and grounds is C2 (K-100). C1 note (K-194): a self-propelled machine made to dig, lift, load or move material is C1 unless it keeps gardens and grounds (C2) or does a farm or forestry job (C7).
- Step 10 (E4): machetes and axes made for cutting wood or vegetation are "C1 or C2" (the note does not say which). Resolved in section 9: axes are C1 (K-173), machetes C2 (memo recommendation, applied by default); the key wording is queued in section 10.
- Step 13 (B10): garden fish ponds, pond liners and pond pumps are C2 (K-104).
- Step 23 (C6): pool and soil test kits are C2; weather instruments are C6.
- Step 27 (B9) and step 6 (C3): seeds, bulbs, spores and living plants are C3, so "plants" and "seed" are not C2 even when sold in a grow kit.
- Step 30: garden furniture and plant stands are B1 (tried after C2 in the key, but named there); B3 lighting excludes grow lights; B7 holds hot tubs, water heaters, filters and softeners for bathing; B8 holds "poisons and repellents" for the home; B6 holds cleaning products.
- Step 24 note: a connected version of a household object (robot mower) is filed by its task; connectivity is a modifier.
- Step 30 (B8 branch): gravel of any kind is stone (C2, K-174, K-187); netting made to protect plants is C2; anti-climb spikes, security window film and bird spikes are B8 (K-187).
- Step 32: C1 holds tools made to cut, shape, join, measure or power; C8 holds fencing, timber, paving, fuels, firewood.

## 2. The first axis people choose by

Evidence shows no single axis; the first choice depends on the leaf.

- **Job to be done** is the axis of the broad navigation and the how-to content. B&Q offers "Autumn garden jobs" [S3]; Engei Net divides tools into cutting, digging, carrying, measuring [S34]; HS 8201 divides by tool family tied to a task [S20]; Gardena's watering groups are situations (balcony, planted rows, hedge) [S25]. For hand tools, buyers decide "what am I doing" (prune, dig, rake, weed) before maker.
- **Form or mechanism** is the first choice for lawn machines: cylinder, rotary, hover, robotic, ride-on [S12, S13]. The Directive defines the cylinder mower by its shearing action [S31]. A Japanese guide instead orders mowers by lawn area [S35]; UK guides put lawn size second [S13]. Cutting mechanism and operator relation (walk-behind, ride-on, robot) are therefore the first axes, and power source follows (English Garden has a "cordless" list, B&Q and Home Depot filter by power).
- **Fit or size** matters second: cutting width (30 to 35 cm for a small lawn [S13]), lawn area [S35], cutting capacity in secateurs [S14], pot inside dimensions [S27], canopy area for grow lights [S36].
- **Material** is the first visible facet for pots (Bunnings sub-categories terracotta, glazed, metal, lightweight; Hornbach popular filters Keramik, Holz, Beton, Ton) [S4, S9], and for greenhouses (frame and glazing) [S18]. Appendix A makes material a modifier unless it is the form; here it works as an attribute, with bonsai pots (unglazed, glazed, slabs, kusamono) the main case where the container form differs [S27].
- **Performance spec** leads in grow lights (PPFD at canopy, PPE) [S36, S38], pool filters (filter area, cartridge versus DE versus sand) [S15] and cordless machines (battery size) [S33].
- **Maker or system** leads once the owner owns a platform: Gardena combisystem heads fit Gardena handles [S32]; Stihl AK tools take AK batteries [S33]; Gardena Micro-Drip parts connect to Gardena master units [S25]. Platform choice precedes the kind in these cases.
- **Scene** (balcony, allotment, indoor hydroponics, bonsai) is a store-level view, not a first filter: Gardena sets are labelled balcony set and terrace set but sit in one product tree [S25].

## 3. Candidate structures

All three candidates keep the category's four possible levels below C2 (sub-family, leaf, kind, named form; C5(a)) and one stated axis per level, read within each sub-family (C5(c)). Kinds marked † below are named by the framework key or by a category-level page but were not seen as a kind on a fetched page; they need a fetched attestation before entry (rule S6). Every other kind in the worked tree was seen on a fetched page listed in its source ids. Kinds that waited on an owner question are placed by the decisions listed in section 12; their remaining † marks are about attestation only.

### Candidate A (recommended): by job done, then tool or product family, then generic type

| Level | Axis |
|---|---|
| Sub-family | the garden job the object serves (dig and plant; cut and prune; lawn; clear, clean and haul; water; contain, support and cover; feed and protect; protect and force growth; pool); plus one closing parts sub-family (C5(d)) |
| Leaf | stated for each sub-family in the table below |
| Kind | generic type of object, distinguished by form or mechanism where buyers compare within it (cylinder versus rotary mower, sand versus cartridge filter) |
| Named form | a specific design below the kind: maker-bound (a model or line: Felco 2, Okatsune 103) or not maker-bound (a generic or traditional design many makers make: kusamono pot, Dutch light); field `maker_bound` (C2(a)). A platform or system is never a named form; it is a row of the compatibility ledger (C13). |

Power source (hand, battery, petrol, mains), mounting, setting (balcony, allotment, indoor), market (domestic or commercial), brand and price never appear as levels. They are attributes (`drive`: manual, corded electric, cordless, petrol, pneumatic, PTO; `mount`: hand-held, wall, free-standing, ride-on, trailed), tags or views (C10, C12; Appendix A). A manual and a powered version of the same form share one kind (secateurs, cylinder mower); a powered tool of a different form is its own kind (hedge trimmer, brush cutter). Decided (C-1, CV-24, CV-28): `drive` and `mount` are shared attributes with generated "Hand tools", "Power tools" and "Machinery" views; rule T (C10) and rule M (C12) apply, so a technology or a mounting makes a kind only where the form or installation changes and a fetched source compares the types separately.

## 5. Quality and tier

**There is no single ordering for the category; it is mixed.** The tree carries no tier on any kind (C4(a)). Three cases follow.

- **Spec ladders, which are attributes with their standard or measure named (C4(b)), not tier entries:** grow lights by PPE (µmol/J), PPF and PPFD, with top white LEDs at 2.8 to 3.0 µmol/J and a certified list of over 1,200 products [S38]; Hydrobuilder tells buyers to size by PPFD rather than watts (vegetative 400 to 600, flowering 800 to 1,500 µmol/m²/s) [S36]; pool filters by filter area and type, and cleaners by type [S15]; mowers by cutting width, which Directive 2000/14/EC also uses for noise limits [S31]; cordless machines by battery system and run time [S33]; secateurs by cutting capacity [S14]. † The measuring standard behind PPFD and PPE, and the DLC list's scheme name, need a fetched standard before these attributes are entered. The standard met is named on the attribute (`standard_met`, CV-36); a voluntary scheme such as the DLC list is such a standard, and a legal obligation such as the noise limits of Directive 2000/14/EC goes to the jurisdiction ledger (C17), not to the tree.
- **Consensus ranking, held as tier entries on named forms** (`subject_id`, `scheme`, `value`, `source_url`, `date`; C4(a)): Okatsune 103 tops the BBC Gardeners World test (4.8/5, lifetime warranty) [S14], and a garden designer ranks Niwaki/Okatsune for sharpness and Felco for parts and service [S30]. The first is a candidate entry (subject Okatsune 103, scheme "Gardeners World secateurs test", value 4.8/5, source S14, scale class (iii), an editorial grade); it enters on this one fetched source (CV-10, CV-12), and its `date` must be taken from the fetched page (†). The designer's comparison is an opinion without a ladder and is not entered as a tier. A named form does not inherit its maker's tier (C4(a)).
- **Preference, no tier:** pots and planters (colour, style, material), hoses, sieves, plant supports, hand-watering tools. Price band only: the domain-wide USD scheme of C4(d), derived from a dated, sourced list price (RRPs appear on S13 and S14 and have not been entered).

Warranty length (lifetime, 25 years [S14, S32]) is an attribute of the named form. A tier scheme enters the data on one fetched source (C4(e), CV-10); a second source or a second region only adds weight and feeds the `disputed` flag, so the secateurs scheme (one UK source [S14]) enters now. The review's extra tier-entry field `basis` is not added: the scheme registry's `scale_class` (the six classes of C4(c), CV-12) carries it. A tier entry may sit on a variant when it is the maker's own ladder for one named form or an external score of that version (CV-11). Price bands follow the one USD scheme (C4(d), CV-13), computed from a dated, sourced list price; a band is never a tier.

## 8. Order inside a leaf

**Kinds: canonical form first, then the secondary key that the sub-family states (C7; the key for each sub-family is in the table in section 3). Named forms: non-maker named forms first (kusamono pot, Dutch light), by first-attested year, then alphabetically; then maker-bound named forms by maker (in the order of the maker ledger's `name`), then first-introduction year, then alphabetically (C7, CV-20, CV-21). Maker ladders, spec ladders (PPE, filter area) and benchmark orders are generated views, not stored order. Variants: by `variant_code` or year.**
- Tool and machine leaves open with the form every source names first (garden spade, then fork, shovel, mattock; secateurs, then loppers, pruning saw, pole pruner), the same order as HS 8201 and UNSPSC [S20, S11], then follow the job sequence (dig, plant, cultivate).
- Machine and equipment leaves order kinds by working scale, which is how buyers narrow the choice: mowers from cylinder and rotary to ride-on and robot by lawn size [S35, S13]; pumps, filters and cleaners by pool size or filter area [S15]; grow lights by canopy area and PPFD [S36].
- Platform relations are not shown by the order of named forms. A generated view lists named forms by compatibility-ledger row (all combisystem heads together, all AK-system tools together; C13) [S32, S33, S25]. Chronology is used only in an era view.
- Reason: browsing sources already group this way (job, then scale), and an alphabetical order scatters same-maker rows.

## 9. Boundaries

**C1 Tools & Workshop Equipment** (closest objects: axe, hatchet, chainsaw, pole saw, wheelbarrow, sack truck, tool rack, generator, battery pack).
- A tool made to dig, prune, rake or cut grass, hedges, brush or vegetation in a household garden is C2; a tool made to cut or split timber and wood (axe, felling axe, maul, wedge, splitting axe) is C1.
- Decided (K-173, C7-O1): hand-held chainsaws and pole saws are C2 (step 29 is tried first; UNSPSC lists "garden chainsaw" and Hornbach files chainsaws under Gartenmaschinen & Forstbedarf [S11, S7]); axes (felling axes included), mauls, wedges and log splitters are C1; tractor-driven splitters and chippers and every self-propelled forest machine are C7; a wood chipper that is not tractor-driven (towed or self-propelled, for tree surgery and grounds) is C2, and a garden shredder is C2. The step 10 note's "C1 or C2" for machetes and axes resolves as axes C1 and machetes C2 (billhook and sickle C2 as well; the memo's recommendation, applied by default). A self-propelled machine made to dig, lift, load or move material is C1 unless it keeps gardens and grounds (C2) or does a farm or forestry job (C7) (K-194); a vehicle made mainly to carry people or goods (ATV, utility vehicle) is G1 even when it powers implements (K-93).
- A wheelbarrow or garden cart is C2; a sack truck, dolly or hand cart is C1 (key notes at steps 11 and 32).
- A rack, box or bag that stores garden tools is C1 as tool storage, not C2 and not B1 (note at step 30, branch B1).
- Decided (K-4, K-3): a battery pack or charger made for a maker's cordless platform is C1, whatever tools or garden machines the platform runs; a battery made for one device follows it (S3); a loose standard-size cell is C8. The memo enters no battery pack or charger; the platform is a compatibility-ledger row (C13).
- Decided (K-175, K-104, B4-O1): a pump made to move water for watering, irrigation, pools, ponds or water features is C2; a field irrigation pump is C7; a drainage, transfer, sump or general-purpose pump is C1. Garden fish ponds, pond liners and pond pumps are C2; a garden fountain or water feature is B4, its pump C2; a bird bath is B10 (B-33).

**C3 Plants & Living Cultures** (closest objects: seed, bulb, potted plant, grow kit, planted hanging basket, turf, moss).
- A living plant, seed or bulb is C3 even when sold in a decorative pot, basket, kokedama ball or terrarium, whatever the container's value; the empty container, the compost and the feed are C2. K-73 adopts this rule for the key, so the C3 side follows it and the C3 memo's greatest-value test falls. Preserved (stabilised) plants and artificial plants of every kind are B4 (K-74); artificial turf stays C2, because step 29 names it.
- A plant sold still growing, including potted herbs and living microgreens, is C3 (key step 6), so Home Depot's Garden Center plant branches belong to C3, not C2 [S1].
- Cultivated turf (sod) and live moss are living plants and belong to C3 (review F15 adds them to the C3 tree); C2 keeps only artificial turf.
- Kits and sets (K-1): a set of objects of one kind is filed as that kind; a mixed set is filed by its item of greatest value, with its contents listed and a unit-of-sale attribute; a kit sold, standardised and compared as one object is a kind. A seed, bulb or living plant sold with pot and compost for growing is C3 (step 6, K-73), because the living plant is what the kit is for (applied by default; K-1's greatest-value test read that way). A set of unlike garden items with no living plant (for example a grow-tent set of tent, light and fan) is filed by its item of greatest value; no kit kind enters unless a fetched seller compares the kit as a class.

**Pools: B7, G2, E1** (closest objects: in-ground pool, inflatable pool, paddling pool, hot tub, pool pump, spa jet, pool heater, pool chemical).
- An in-ground pool is G2; an above-ground or inflatable pool made for swimming is C2; a paddling pool made for play is E1 (cut point: EN 71-8 water depth of 400 mm, E-13; provisional until the standard is attested); a hot tub or portable sauna is B7, an inflatable hot tub included (B-26) [S10 groups all of these in one retail branch].
