## 0. Category

**Code and name:** C2 Garden & Grounds.

**Membership test** (CATEGORIES_v0_6, step 29): *made for **growing plants or keeping gardens, grounds, paths, pools and drives**, powered or not: garden tools, mowers and ride-on mowers, trimmers, leaf blowers, pressure washers, snow clearers, wheelbarrows, planters and plant pots, plant care products, grow lights, irrigation, greenhouses and polytunnels, compost bins, artificial turf, garden stone, above-ground and inflatable swimming pools, pool cleaning equipment, test kits and pumps.* Notes on the step: garden furniture is B1; garden ornaments, statues and gnomes are B4; paddling pools made for play are E1. Category line: "tools, machines and supplies for growing plants and keeping grounds and pools."

**Notes and precedence sentences elsewhere that name C2:**
- S3 (parts follow the whole): "Never parts: ... anything for growing plants (C2)". A part, attachment or consumable made for one kind of C2 machine (a mower blade, a pool pump cartridge) follows that machine.
- Step 4 (G2): greenhouses and polytunnels are C2, not G2; in-ground pools, pergolas, gazebos, summerhouses, garden rooms and sheds are G2; pop-up gazebos are B1.
- Step 7 (F6): garden stone is C2 when a natural material is sold for that use.
- Step 11 (G1): wheelbarrows and garden carts are C2; sack trucks and dollies are C1. A machine that carries its operator while doing other work (ride-on mower) goes by that work, so it is C2.
- Step 12 (C7): equipment for a household garden or allotment is C2, "including garden and compact lawn tractors"; commercial and farm machines are C7.
- Step 10 (E4): machetes and axes made for cutting wood or vegetation are "C1 or C2" (the note does not say which; see section 10).
- Step 23 (C6): pool and soil test kits are C2; weather instruments are C6.
- Step 27 (B9) and step 6 (C3): seeds, bulbs, spores and living plants are C3, so "plants" and "seed" are not C2 even when sold in a grow kit.
- Step 30: garden furniture and plant stands are B1 (tried after C2 in the key, but named there); B3 lighting excludes grow lights; B7 holds hot tubs, water heaters, filters and softeners for bathing; B8 holds "poisons and repellents" for the home; B6 holds cleaning products.
- Step 24 note: a connected version of a household object (robot mower) is filed by its task; connectivity is a modifier.
- Step 32: C1 holds tools made to cut, shape, join, measure or power; C8 holds fencing, timber, paving, fuels, firewood.

## 2. The first axis people choose by

Evidence shows no single axis; the first choice depends on the leaf.

- **Job to be done** is the axis of the broad navigation and the how-to content. B&Q offers "Autumn garden jobs" [S3]; Engei Net divides tools into cutting, digging, carrying, measuring [S34]; HS 8201 divides by tool family tied to a task [S20]; Gardena's watering groups are situations (balcony, planted rows, hedge) [S25]. For hand tools, buyers decide "what am I doing" (prune, dig, rake, weed) before maker.
- **Form or mechanism** is the first choice for lawn machines: cylinder, rotary, hover, robotic, ride-on [S12, S13]. The Directive defines the cylinder mower by its shearing action [S31]. A Japanese guide instead orders mowers by lawn area [S35]; UK guides put lawn size second [S13]. Cutting mechanism and operator relation (walk-behind, ride-on, robot) are therefore the first axes, and power source follows (English Garden has a "cordless" list, B&Q and Home Depot filter by power).
- **Fit or size** matters second: cutting width (30 to 35 cm for a small lawn [S13]), lawn area [S35], cutting capacity in secateurs [S14], pot inside dimensions [S27], canopy area for grow lights [S36].
- **Material** is the first visible facet for pots (Bunnings sub-categories terracotta, glazed, metal, lightweight; Hornbach popular filters Keramik, Holz, Beton, Ton) [S4, S9], and for greenhouses (frame and glazing) [S18]. Appendix A makes material a modifier unless it is the form; here it works as an attribute, with bonsai pots (unglazed, glazed, slabs, kusamono) the main case where the container form differs [S27].
- **Performance spec** leads in grow lights (PPFD at canopy, PPE) [S36, S38], pool filters (filter area, cartridge versus DE versus sand) [S15] and cordless machines (battery size) [S33].
- **Maker or system** leads once the owner owns a platform: Gardena combisystem heads fit Gardena handles [S32]; Stihl AK tools take AK batteries [S33]; Gardena Micro-Drip parts connect to Gardena master units [S25]. Platform choice precedes the kind in these cases.
- **Scene** (balcony, allotment, indoor hydroponics, bonsai) is a store-level view, not a first filter: Gardena sets are labelled balcony set and terrace set but sit in one product tree [S25].

### Candidate A (recommended): by job done, then tool or product family, then generic type

| Level | Axis |

| Sub-family | the garden job the object serves (dig and plant; cut and prune; lawn; clear, clean and haul; water; contain and support; feed and protect; protect and force growth; pool) |

### Candidate B: by retail navigation (hand tool, powered machine, container, consumable, structure, pool)

| Level | Axis |
|---|---|
| Sub-family | object class as a retailer's top nav shows it (hand tools / garden power tools / pots and planters / soil and feed / irrigation / structures / pools) |
| Leaf | task family (cutting, digging, mowing, clearing) |
| Kind | generic type |
| Named form | model or system |

This copies the most common navigation (B&Q, Bunnings, Leroy Merlin, Wikipedia [S3, S2, S24, S22]). Example sub-family contents: *Hand tools* (garden spade, secateurs, loppers, hoe, rake, watering can), *Garden power tools* (rotary mower, hedge trimmer, leaf blower, string trimmer, pressure washer), *Pots and planters* (plant pot, window box, hanging basket, bonsai pot), *Irrigation* (hose, sprinkler, drip kit, controller), *Pools* (above-ground pool, pump, filter).

**Where it breaks:**
1. It uses power source as the first axis, which Appendix A forbids at inner levels; a manual reel mower and a cordless one would sit in different sub-families, and Wikipedia itself needs a third class "manual or powered" [S22].
2. Battery secateurs and hand secateurs, or a petrol and a hand-pushed cylinder mower, split into two homes for one kind of object.
3. A garden hose and an irrigation controller are neither hand tool nor power tool and need an ad hoc "irrigation" branch, so the first axis is not one axis.
4. UNSPSC and HS group hedge trimmers, brush cutters and shredders with hand tools [S11, S20], so no external standard supports the split.

|---|---|

**Where it breaks:**
1. Spade, hose, fertiliser and wheelbarrow serve every zone, so they need a "general" bucket or duplicate homes.

## 4. Depth and size

**Rough count of generic kinds: about 270 to 320** under Candidate A. Basis: UNSPSC class 27112000 alone lists about 35 commodity names [S11]; Hornbach shows 8 to 17 sub-categories in each of its tools, irrigation, pots and pools branches [S6, S8, S9, S10] and Cainz 17 groups [S5]; applying the choice level of section 4b adds mower types, filter media and cleaner mechanisms. Estimate by sub-family: C2.1 about 25, C2.2 about 25, C2.3 about 30, C2.4 about 20, C2.5 about 35, C2.6 about 30, C2.7 about 35, C2.8 about 25, C2.9 about 45.

**Rough count of named forms: 300 to 600 if admitted only as system lines and iconic models** (Felco 2 and 6, Okatsune 103, Gardena combisystem and Micro-Drip, Stihl AK system, Flymo hover, Husqvarna Automower and similar); **tens of thousands if every catalogue model is admitted.** Scale evidence: Cainz lists 15,268 items under 園芸用品 [S5]; Hornbach lists 1,657 hand tools, 2,512 garden machines, 1,651 irrigation items, 5,403 pots and 2,172 pool items [S6–S10]; Bunnings lists 417 outdoor pots [S4]. The owner should cap named forms at systems and iconic models, since the brief says individual products are named forms and not every product is wanted.

**Where depth concentrates:**
- Pool equipment (cleaners by mechanism, filters by media, heaters by fuel, covers by use) is the deepest per kind [S15, S16].
- Powered machinery, where mowers split by type and a platform (battery system, handle system) crosses kinds [S12, S33].
- Irrigation components, which are many small kinds that only make sense inside a maker system [S25, S26].
- Pots, which are broad in count (5,403 at one retailer [S9]) but shallow: container form is the only kind-level axis; material, size and colour are attributes.

**Broad, medium and narrow leaves.**
- Broad: Pots and planters (C2.6); Powered machines and mowers (C2.3 and the powered part of C2.2 and C2.4); Pool equipment (C2.9).
- Medium: Hand pruners and cutters (C2.2); Digging and cultivating tools (C2.1); Sprinklers, hoses and irrigation systems (C2.5); Growing media and feeds (C2.7).
- Narrow: Protected growing structures (C2.8: greenhouse, polytunnel, cold frame), grow lights and tents, hand-watering tools, artificial turf, sifting and soil-testing tools.

## 5. Quality and tier

**There is no single ordering for the category; it is mixed.** If one label is wanted for the category, "spec-ranked" covers every kind where a recognised order exists; hand cutting tools are consensus-ranked; containers and hand tools without an agreed spec are preference.

- **Spec-ranked:** grow lights by PPE (µmol/J), PPF and PPFD, with top white LEDs at 2.8 to 3.0 µmol/J and a certified list of over 1,200 products [S38]; Hydrobuilder tells buyers to size by PPFD rather than watts (vegetative 400 to 600, flowering 800 to 1,500 µmol/m²/s) [S36]; pool filters by filter area and type, and cleaners by type [S15]; mowers by cutting width, which the Directive also uses for noise limits [S31], and by cutting finish (cylinder over rotary) [S12, S13]; cordless machines by battery system size (AK 10/20/30) and run time [S33]; secateurs by cutting capacity [S14].
- **Consensus-ranked:** cutting hand tools by maker line: Okatsune 103 tops the BBC Gardeners World test (4.8/5, lifetime warranty) and a garden designer ranks Niwaki/Okatsune for sharpness and Felco for parts and service [S14, S30]; Gardena sells combisystem heads with a 25-year warranty on registration [S32].
- **Preference:** pots and planters (colour, style, material), hoses, sieves, plant supports, hand-watering tools; price band only.

**As a modifier, never a level:** a `tier` field on kinds and named forms with `tier_basis` (for example PPE, cutting capacity, battery system size, filter area), `tier_value`, and `tier_source` (a test or standard). Warranty length (lifetime, 25 years) is an attribute of the named form. No `tier` value is attested yet for any row; the table would be built from tests like [S14] and [S38] when named forms are added.
