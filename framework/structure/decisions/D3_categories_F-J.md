Every line is a decision delegated by the owner and made by Claude; the owner may overturn any of them, and until then they bind like owner decisions (BRIEF.md, "Owner decisions on stage 2").

**Rules used, in order:** (1) BRIEF binds, with its standing preferences, the **depth-and-breadth rule** (no caps on maker ledgers, lines or named forms; makers, artisans, houses, perfumers and schools in ledgers off the tree; scene tags wherever communities exist; no boundary may drop a maker community) and the **one-source rule** (one fetched page showing a thing is or was made or sold proves it exists; a second source or region decides only level or tier weight, never inclusion). (2) Test the object, not the motive (S5); legal classes go to the C17 ledger. (3) Adopt the recommendation unless it breaks (1) or (2), hangs a home on a fact nobody can check on the object, or adds kinds or levels buyers do not compare; with no recommendation, take the simpler one-sentence rule that keeps round-5 placements stable. (4) Attributes over kinds, fewer levels, one shared rule, a written precedence sentence over a silent gap. (5) No sourcing pass: a decision resting on an unverified fact is marked provisional; such facts are attested when kinds are harvested in stage 3.
**Prior rulings** (verify/in/S2D_COMMON.txt): the per-category top 15 of the summary (§5a) are adopted as recommended; in this file that covers F-2, F-3, G-13, G-14, H-2, H-8, I-2, I-4, J-1 (option b) and J-11, recorded as "adopted (ruling)".
**Format:** `- **ID** decision. adopted | departed: reason. provisional until attested: … (if any)`. "No rec" means no source gave a recommendation; the choice follows rule 3's fallback.
