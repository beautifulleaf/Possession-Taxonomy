**Rules used, in order:** (1) BRIEF binds, with the standing preferences and the stage-2 owner rules: the **depth-and-breadth rule** (no caps on makers, lines or named forms; makers, artisans and schools in ledgers off the tree; scene tags wherever communities exist; no boundary may drop a maker community), the **one-source rule** (one fetched page proves existence; a second source or region decides level or tier weight only), **no separate sourcing pass** (claims are attested in the stage-3 harvest), and the **Interactive Entertainment rule** (owner, 2026-10-17, BRIEF): board, card, tabletop and video game genres, mechanics and themes go to a future Interactive Entertainment taxonomy; here a game is filed as an object (physical form, publisher, edition, components), and a genre, mechanic or theme is never a level, kind or scene tag, at most a link field. (2) The object, not the motive (S5); legal classes to C17. (3) Adopt the recommendation unless it breaks (1) or (2), hangs a home on an uncheckable fact, or adds kinds buyers do not compare; with none, the simpler one-sentence rule that keeps round-5 placements stable. (4) Attributes over kinds, fewer levels, one shared rule, a written precedence sentence. (5) A decision resting on an unverified fact is marked "provisional until attested".
**Prior rulings** from verify/in/S2D_COMMON.txt (the per-category top 15 of summary §5a: A-7, B-9, C-1, C-4, C-6, D-20, E-1, E-2, E-10 in these domains) are recorded as "adopted (ruling)".
