# Residual decisions (delegated by the owner, made by Claude, 2026-10-17)

Points the memo decision pass (S2U) found that no decision in D1–D3 covers. Same status as D1–D3: delegated, binding until the owner overturns them. Rules as in verify/in/S2D_COMMON.txt.

- **R-1** Backdrop stand: D4 with studio stands (K-17 makes studio lights, stands and flashes D4; the backdrop itself is B2). Raised by S2U_D4.
- **R-0** Blanket rule: a rule, precedence sentence or kind that a memo still marks "proposed" and that no decision in D1–D3 or R-items covers is adopted as the memo wrote it (rule 3 of S2D_COMMON: default to the recommendation). This applies to A1 P5, P8, P9, P14, P15 and P18 (raised by S2U_A1) and to any later case of the same kind.
- **R-2** Authenticity and reproduction classes (F2 `authenticity_class`): the C16 grading-scale registry gains the `scale_type` value "classification", meaning a class a kind or named form belongs to, never a grade of one object. Written into CONVENTIONS_v1. Raised by S2U_F2.
- **R-3** Religious relics against "human specimens are OUT" (K-79): a relic housed in a reliquary, or sealed and documented as a relic (for example a theca with an authentication), is F4 by the step-7 relic sentence. Other human remains and anatomical specimens are OUT. Raised by S2U_F4.
- **R-4** The mezuzah case stays a kind in F4.3c, not in the parts sub-family F4.7: the case is the visible devotional object (rev F X6). Raised by S2U_F4.
- **R-5** Tarot and oracle decks: every published deck design is an uncapped, maker-bound named form under its kind, with its publisher as maker, as for board games (E-6) and per the depth rule. Confirmed as applied by S2U_F4.
- **R-6** Traps: mouse, rat and insect traps and live-catch cage traps are B8.4 (pests). Snares, leg-hold and body-grip traps made for trapping game or fur go with hunting gear in E2 (K-163). Raised by S2U_B8.
- **R-7** Bird netting and bird-proofing nets for buildings are B8.4.6 "Nets, screens & spikes"; garden crop netting stays C2. Raised by S2U_B8.
- **R-8** A freestanding play yard (playpen) is B1. Key sentence for the next framework revision: "A freestanding play yard or playpen is B1; a gate or guard fitted to the home is B8." Raised by S2U_B8 (K-103).
- **R-9** Font superfamilies: each typeface family is a maker-bound named form of the one "digital font" kind, and a superfamily that groups families is a lines row. A line may group named forms inside one kind when the maker sells them as one system. Raised by S2U_I2.
- **R-10** C13 `platform_type` gains: DRM scheme, content locker, game platform for items, messaging platform, blockchain and naming root. Written into CONVENTIONS_v1. Raised by S2U_I2.
- **R-11** Leaves with one kind are allowed where a decided leaf axis produces them (I2 audio, after I-8, I-9 and I-10). Raised by S2U_I2.
- **R-12** H2 homes, by function: examination tables, treatment couches and dental chairs go to the sub-family holding care beds (care and nursing furniture). Therapeutic weighted blankets and pillows go to therapy and pain-relief aids. Medical software (K-37) goes to diagnostics and monitoring as the kind "medical app". Raised by S2U_H2.
- **R-13** Head-worn magnifiers (K-38 against K-135): a head-worn loupe or magnifier on a headband or clip is C6. Magnifying, prismatic or telescopic low-vision spectacles in spectacle frames are H2 with spectacles. Raised by S2U_H2.
- **R-14** Companion apps (K-37 against CV-30): an app that only records and shows a device's readings is part of that device's C13 ecosystem row and is I1 as software. Software that itself diagnoses, treats or is cleared as a medical device is H2 (medical app, R-12). Raised by S2U_H2.
- **R-15** C13 `platform_type` gains "stacking tool-storage system". Written into CONVENTIONS_v1. Raised by S2U_C1.

- **R-18** CV-46 applies to food as well: a brand line spanning several kinds is a lines row; a line inside one kind is a maker-bound named form (C2(d)). This confirms the S2U_J5 treatment.
- **R-19** Kinds named with a regional word (udon, soba, atta, besan, jaggery, and the like in any category) keep their level as provisional until attested (CV-27). Their generic label with the regional name as kind name (C11) is set at stage-3 harvest, not now.

- **R-21** E-13 saddle-height note: it decides only between E1 balance bikes and ride-on toys and G1 bicycles without pedals. Every pedal bicycle is G1 (K-91). Raised by S2U_E1.
