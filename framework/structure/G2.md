## 0. Category

**Code and name.** G2 Property & Structures (domain G, Vehicles & Property). Source: `framework/CATEGORIES_v0_6.md`, category list and placement key step 4.

**Membership test (step 4).** "**Land**; a **building or other permanent structure made to be occupied, entered or enclosed** (house, flat, garage, barn, stable block, shed, garden room, summerhouse, carport, in-ground pool, pergola, gazebo, decking, patio); shipping containers and portable cabins kept in place as stores or rooms; a vehicle or vessel made to **stay in one place as a dwelling** (houseboat, static caravan, tiny house on wheels); a burial plot or niche, however it is held?"

**Notes bound to step 4.** A building is filed as a whole. Its elements and products, installed or not, are filed by what they are: structural, envelope and finishing products (bricks, timber, roofing, windows, doors, stairs, flooring, tiles, wallpaper, fixed plumbing and wiring, fencing, acoustic panels) are C8; kitchen and wardrobe cabinetry is B1; carpets, blinds and curtains are B2; baths, showers, basins, toilets and hot tubs are B7; installed machines, lamps, safes and alarms keep their own category. Greenhouses and polytunnels are C2. Animal housing that is not a building a person can stand in (hutches, coops, kennels, stalls inside a building) is B10. Play structures, including treehouses, are E1. Pop-up gazebos are B1.

**Precedence sentences that touch G2.**
- Step 1 (OUT): "a licence, tenancy, permit, rental, timeshare week or timeshare points giving the person a specific place or space for their own use that they do not hold as land, a building or a burial plot, such as an allotment, mooring or berth, parking space or permit, storage unit or dedicated desk"; human remains; "the urn or box that holds remains is filed as that object".
- Step 11 (G1): "trailers and horseboxes, touring caravans and motorhomes" are vehicles; "every boat, canoe, kayak, bicycle and aircraft is G1". Step 4 names "houseboat" for G2, so the two steps meet at the cruising houseboat (section 9).
- Step 14 (F4): coffins and caskets, cremation urns for people or pets and grave markers are F4, so the plot or niche is G2 and what is placed in or on it is F4.
- Step 25 (E1): "treehouses, playhouses and children's play equipment". Step 29 (C2): greenhouses, polytunnels, above-ground and inflatable pools. Step 30 (B1): pop-up gazebos. Step 30 (B7): hot tubs and portable saunas.
- S3: parts follow the whole, but "building products (step 4's note)" are never parts; so G2 has no Parts-and-accessories sub-family (section 3).
- Appendix A: region and culture, era, price and quality tier, brand and maker, market (domestic, commercial), installation, power source and technology, material, origin (natural, cultured), how acquired (bought ready-made, built from a kit, handmade) and condition never divide a level. The owner's pointer adds: tenure and region are modifiers.

### 1.4 Standards, statistics and trade classifications

- ONS Census 2021 accommodation type (https://www.ons.gov.uk/census/census2021dictionary/variablesbytopic/housingvariablescensus2021/accommodationtype): eight categories, in order: Detached, Semi-detached, Terraced, in a purpose-built block of flats or tenement, part of a converted or shared house, part of another converted building, in a commercial building, a caravan or other mobile or temporary structure. A bungalow is inside "whole house or bungalow".
- US ACS B25024 Units in Structure (https://censusreporter.org/tables/B25024/): 1 detached, 1 attached, 2, 3 or 4, 5 to 9, 10 to 19, 20 to 49, 50 or more, Mobile home, Boat, RV, van, etc. Housing units are binned by the number of units in the structure, and boats and vans count as housing units.
- Japan Housing and Land Survey terms (https://www.stat.go.jp/english/data/jyutaku/pdf/05terms.pdf): a dwelling needs a room, a sink, a toilet and an entrance of its own; building type is detached house, tenement-house (terrace houses included), apartment, other; construction material, storeys, tenure and year are separate fields.
- Australian Land Use and Management Classification v8 (https://www.agriculture.gov.au/abares/aclump/land-use/alum-classification): six primary classes (conservation and natural environments, production from relatively natural environments, dryland agriculture and plantations, irrigated agriculture and plantations, intensive uses, water) with commodity and management fields refining within a class.
- REINZ (above): rural types as a standard glossary for agents.

### 1.5 Museums and reference works

- Wikipedia, List of house types (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_house_types): "A basic division is between free-standing or single-family detached homes and various types of attached or multi-family residential dwellings"; later sections by construction method (log, prefab, modular, container) and a "movable dwellings" group (mobile or park home, RV, tiny house, houseboat, tent, yurt, tipi).
- Historic England FISH Thesaurus of Monument Types (https://collectionstrust.org.uk/resource/thesaurus-of-monument-types-fish): 18 top concepts, including Domestic, Agriculture and Subsistence, Religious Ritual and Funerary, Recreational, Commercial, Industrial, Transport, Defence; i.e. the heritage standard divides buildings by function. Historic England National Farm Building Types (https://historicengland.org.uk/images-books/publications/national-farm-building-types) lists about 30 farm building kinds (barn, cart shed, coach house, granary, hay barn, Dutch barn, staddle barn, stable, mill, dovecote, kennels, well house).

### 1.6 Where the sources agree and where they split the same objects

- **Agree.** Every census and portal opens with the physical relation of the dwelling to neighbours: free-standing, joined to one neighbour, in a row, or a unit inside a larger building (ONS, ACS, Japan, idealista, Chinese encyclopedias, Zillow, realestate.com.au). Every land source divides land by use (LandWatch, LandSale, Vantage, REINZ, ALUM). Every burial source separates in-ground from above-ground and urn spaces from casket spaces. Every relocatable-dwelling source separates what can be moved from what stays (tinyhomesnest, Bawtry, YSN, Lake Life).
- **Split on bungalows.** Rightmove treats a bungalow as its own type and keeps it out of detached, semi-detached and terraced results; ONS puts bungalows inside "whole house or bungalow" and divides by attachment; SeLoger offers plain-pied as a filter. The same object is a type, a sub-type or an attribute.
- **Split on "townhouse" and "unit".** realestate.com.au: a townhouse shares walls under strata; REINZ: a separate dwelling on a multi-title site; Zillow: a single-family subtype sharing at least one wall. REINZ "Unit" is attached on a multi-title site, realestate.com.au "Unit" is grouped with Apartment. Same word, different objects (alias table, section 10).
- **Split on mobile and static dwellings.** ONS and ACS fold caravans and mobile structures into a single "mobile" bin; Rightmove lists Mobile Home, Park Home, Caravan and House Boat as four types; Zillow separates mobile home, manufactured home, motor home, tiny home, houseboat and floating home; the UK trade separates static caravan, lodge and park home by build standard and site licence.
- **Split on composite holdings.** LandSale and PGG Wrightson sell farms, estates and lifestyle blocks as one product that is land plus a dwelling plus buildings; Rightmove lists Smallholding and Equestrian Facility next to houses; no source offers the land and the house as two objects.
- **Split on commercial buildings.** The census treats a flat "in a commercial building" as a home; SeLoger and Rightmove sell offices and shops beside homes; the heritage thesaurus makes Commercial and Industrial top concepts; the Chinese source rates office towers (Grade A, 5A). None of the sources fetched here for owner-held property treats them as a different domain.

## 2. The first axis people choose by

1. **Dwelling: how it sits against neighbours, then whether you own the ground.** ONS asks "What type of accommodation is this?" with detached, semi-detached, terraced first (https://www.ons.gov.uk/census/census2021dictionary/variablesbytopic/housingvariablescensus2021/accommodationtype); idealista opens with one family against several; realestate.com.au defines House, Townhouse and Apartment by walls shared and by title (https://help.realestate.com.au/hc/en-us/articles/900007221983-How-to-search-for-different-property-types). Title and tenure (freehold, strata, condominium) follow as a modifier.
2. **Relocatable dwellings: mobility and legal class before size.** "The category matters more than the square footage" (tinyhomesnest); YSN's advice is "if you want to travel, buy a cruising houseboat; if you ... never intend to move, a floating home may fit better".
3. **Land: use.** LandWatch's Property Types and Land Uses facets, LandSale's types, Vantage Land's uses, REINZ and PGG Wrightson's farm types and ALUM's primary classes are all use classes; price and place are facets beside them, not the divider.
4. **Outbuildings: what the building is for.** Dunster House and Hornbach name the building by use (office, summerhouse, shed, garage, carport, sauna, bike garage); material and roof shape are filters.
5. **Burial: in-ground or above, then capacity.** PlotListings' own advice puts "burial or cremation" first, "above-ground or in-ground" second and "for how many people" third.
6. **Price and place** are the dominant facets on every portal (SUUMO, LandWatch, realestate.com.au) but they are modifiers here. Maker is never the first choice for houses or land.

The evidence supports: physical relation or use first, then attachment or capacity, then price and place as facets.

## 3. Candidate structures

Common to all three: category G2, then at most four levels below it (sub-family, leaf, kind, named form), fixed level names (C5a). G2 has **no Parts-and-accessories sub-family** (C5d): the step 4 note sends every element of a building to C8, B1, B2 or B7, so nothing is received under S3.

### 3A. What is held, then the axis of each part (recommended)

- **Sub-family axis:** *what kind of thing is held*: ground (**Land**), a home (**Dwellings**), another building or structure (**Other buildings & structures**), a place for the dead (**Burial places**).
- **Leaf axis, stated per sub-family (C5c):**
  - Land: the use the parcel is held for (building land, farmland, woodland, recreation and water, working and lifestyle holdings).
  - Dwellings: the form of the dwelling unit against the building around it (whole-building house, unit inside a building, whole building of several units, dwelling kept in place that is not a building).
  - Other buildings & structures: what the covered or enclosed space is for (vehicle shelter, storage and work, garden room, open garden structure, farm building, pool and hard landscape, container).
  - Burial places: where the remains rest (in-ground, above-ground chamber, urn space, shared or natural).
- **Kind axis:** inside Houses, attachment to neighbouring dwellings (detached, semi-detached, terraced); inside Flats, the layout of the unit in the building; inside Farmland, the crop or stock the land is held for; inside In-ground pools, the construction method.
- **Named form:** a form of a kind that many builders or traditions make (end-of-terrace, riad, Dutch barn) or a maker-bound product line (not expected here beyond a few iconic forms).
- Example (kinds in bold): Dwellings > Houses > **semi-detached house** > *semi-detached villa*; Other buildings & structures > Garden rooms and cabins > **garden office**; Burial places > Urn spaces > **columbarium niche** > *locker-type niche*.
- **Where it breaks.** (1) Composite holdings: a farm, ranch or lifestyle block is land, a dwelling and buildings sold as one product (LandSale, PGG Wrightson, realestate.com.au rural agents) and has no single leaf; the tree files it under Land > working and lifestyle holdings and records the dwelling as an attribute (open question O5). (2) Bungalow: a storeys attribute, but Rightmove treats it as a type (O3). (3) A houseboat that cruises under its own engine is a vessel to the YSN guide but a dwelling to the key (O1). (4) A private mausoleum is both a burial place and a building a person can enter; it sits in Burial places because buyers choose it as an interment option (PlotListings), not in Other buildings. (5) Penthouse and loft in Flats mix position with layout.

### 3B. Portal list (the retail pattern)

- **Sub-family axis:** the filter value a portal shows (House, Flat, Bungalow, Land, Park Home, Garage, Plot, Caravan, House Boat); a single flat list, as at Rightmove (68 types) and LandSale (100+ types).
- Example kinds (13): terraced, semi-detached, detached, flat, maisonette, penthouse, bungalow, plot, smallholding, park home, house boat, garages, castle.
- **Where it breaks.** (1) The axes mix attachment (terraced), layout (maisonette), storeys (bungalow), position (penthouse), use (garages), regime (park home), tradition (riad) and size (studio) in one list, so a reader cannot predict a type from a rule. (2) Bungalow overlaps Detached and Semi-detached by Rightmove's own admission. (3) A "cottage" or "villa" is a style, which Zillow says is separate from type ("house style is based on the architectural elements"). (4) It cannot place a burial plot, a pool or a pergola at all.

### 3C. Use class first (planning and land-use pattern)

- **Sub-family axis:** the use class of the property: residential, agricultural, commercial, industrial, recreational, conservation (ALUM's six primary classes, the FISH top concepts, Rightmove's residential/commercial split).
- **Leaf axis:** the type within the class (house, flat; arable, pasture; shop, office).
- Example kinds (12): detached house, flat, arable land, pasture, woodland, vineyard, warehouse, shop, office, workshop, stable, garage.
- **Where it breaks.** (1) Market (domestic against commercial) is a Modifier in Appendix A, and a flat "in a commercial building" is still a home to the census. (2) A farmhouse on arable land sits in two classes. (3) A garage is residential accessory or commercial lock-up depending on the lease. (4) The scheme is built for mapping parcels (ALUM says it is about land use information for users across Australia) and has no home for a static caravan, a pool or a grave.

  - Pools: **fibreglass pool**; **vinyl-liner pool**; **gunite (concrete) pool** (Thursday Pools, Pool & Spa Depot).
- **G2.4 Burial places** (8 kinds)
  - In-ground: **ground burial plot** (single, companion or family by capacity; *Wahlgrab*, *Reihengrab* as allocation regimes); **cremation (urn) plot** (Dignity: in-ground plots "for casket or urn burial", urn plots being much smaller than casket plots); **lawn crypt**
  - Above-ground chambers: **mausoleum crypt**; **private mausoleum**
  - Urn spaces: **columbarium niche** (*locker-type*, *butsudan-type*, *automated*, ayagawa)
  - Shared and natural: **green burial plot** (*tree burial / jumokuso*, *Baumgrab*); **communal grave space** (*goshi-bo*)

Arithmetic: Land 14 + Dwellings 20 + Other buildings & structures 18 (garage, carport, garden shed, shipping container, summerhouse, garden office, log cabin, garden bar, outdoor sauna cabin, gazebo, pergola, decking, patio, barn, stable block, fibreglass pool, vinyl-liner pool, gunite pool) + Burial places 8 = **60 kinds**.

## 4. Depth and size

- **Generic kinds.** Rightmove lists 68 residential types, LandSale says "100+ property types", the REINZ glossary lists 8 rural and 8 residential types, Historic England lists about 30 farm building kinds and PlotListings seven interment options. After folding styles, regional names and filter values into named forms or attributes, the estimate for G2 is **170 to 260 kinds**: Land 25 to 35, Dwellings 40 to 60, Other buildings & structures 60 to 90 (the farm-building and garden-building long tail), Burial places 15 to 25, with commercial and institutional buildings (O2) adding 40 to 80 if the owner admits them.
- **Named forms.** The attested set is a few hundred: Rightmove's styles (cottage, villa, mews, chalet, finca, cortijo, longere, gite, trulli), the Spanish and Chinese attachment words, the farm-building list, the regional burial forms, the Seattle dwelling terms. Product-model forms (a given shed or lodge model) could run to thousands if admitted. Recommendation: admit only attested iconic or traditional forms; leave maker models to the maker ledger, and in practice leave them out (section 6).
- **Where depth concentrates.** Houses and flats (68 Rightmove types, attachment and layout vocabularies in every language), rural land and holdings (REINZ, PGG, LandSale, LandWatch each with 8 to 25 values), farm buildings (about 30 heritage kinds), garden buildings (Dunster, Hornbach).
- **Broad, medium, narrow leaves.** Broad: houses, flats, farmland, garden rooms and cabins. Medium: dwellings kept in place, working and lifestyle holdings, farm buildings, in-ground burial. Narrow: pools (three kinds), cave dwellings, castles, urn spaces, communal graves.

## 4b. Choice level

**A kind stops where buyers compare quality or makers.** For most of G2 buyers do not compare makers; they compare place, size and condition of a unique object. The choice level is therefore the level at which the sources themselves keep a separate category with a separate rule set (census bin, planning use, building code, interment option).

| Sub-family or leaf | Kind stops at | Finer distinctions become | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Houses | attachment: detached, semi-detached, terraced, plus the few physically distinct forms (courtyard, cave, castle, barndominium) | storeys (bungalow), style (cottage, villa, mews, chalet), regional name (finca, cortijo, longere, trullo), conversion, tenure | ONS, ACS, Japan survey, idealista and Chinese encyclopedias all stop at attachment; Zillow separates "home type" from "house style"; Rightmove's style words are filters |

## 5. Quality and tier

**Recommendation: preference, with spec-ranked attributes where a standard exists.** No source fetched ranks houses, land or burial places in a recognised tier order. Where a measured or standard-based ladder exists, it is an attribute with its standard named (C4b), not a tier entry and never a level.

- **Houses and flats.** Price per place, size and condition are the facets (SUUMO, LandWatch, realestate.com.au); there is no maker or brand tier. Era, condition and location are modifiers.
- **Land.** The UK Agricultural Land Classification grades land quality, "the higher the grading, the better and more versatile" (https://www.vantageland.co.uk/land-use.php). This is a measured ladder, so it is an attribute `alc_grade` with the standard named. LandWatch carries parcel size bands and PGG Wrightson carries land-area bands: attributes.
- **Dwellings kept in place.** Build standards separate classes: BS EN 1647 (holiday caravan), BS 3632 (residential park home), HUD code (US manufactured home), RV industry standards (tiny house on wheels, park model), IRC Appendix Q (tiny house on a foundation) (https://bawtrylodgecountrypark.co.uk/guides/lodge-vs-static-caravan-vs-park-home/, https://tinyhomesnest.com/types-of-tiny-homes/). They are attributes `build_standard`, not tiers; they matter because they set licence, finance and placement.
- **Commercial buildings.** The only consensus-ranked tier found is Grade A and 5A for Chinese office towers (https://www.sohu.com/a/235399555_99894660). It applies only if commercial buildings enter G2 (O2) and would be a dated tier entry on a building, not on a kind.
- **Price band.** The shared C4d bands stop at "over 15,000" USD, which almost every house, plot of rural land, houseboat and private mausoleum exceeds (SUUMO lists houses and land at tens to hundreds of millions of yen, YSN puts new luxury houseboats at $600,000 to $1,200,000+, U.S. News puts floating homes up to $1 million, and PlotListings puts a private mausoleum at $50,000 to $500,000+). A G2 price band would therefore sit entirely in one bucket. **Owner question O4:** split the top band for G2 (for example 15,000 to 50,000; 50,000 to 150,000; 150,000 to 500,000; 500,000 to 1,500,000; over 1,500,000) as a category-specific extension of C4d.

## 6. Makers and brands

**Recommendation: incidental for land, houses, flats and burial places; identity-lite for factory-built objects.** The maker is part of what is bought only where the object is a manufactured product.

- **Incidental.** Land, houses, flats and grave spaces are unique and sold by place. A builder, developer or architect may be named on a listing but buyers do not compare makers (Rightmove, SUUMO, LandWatch, realestate.com.au filters have no maker field).
- **Product makers (identity-lite).** Garden buildings are bought from kit makers and retailers (Dunster House, Hornbach, Bunnings are named on the pages fetched); pool shells from manufacturers who "sell through independent dealers" (Thursday Pools, https://thursdaypools.com/inground-pools); houseboats from builders (Stardust, Sumerset, Gibson, Fantasy, Lakeview, per YSN); park homes and lodges from park-home makers (Prestige is named only on a search page, not fetched). Buyers here compare the maker's range, but the range is a named form or a line, not a kind.
- **Platform.** None. Build standards (BS 3632, EN 1647, HUD, IRC Appendix Q) are regulatory classes, recorded as the `build_standard` attribute, not a compatibility ledger (C13 is not needed for G2).
- **Maker ledger (C1).** Recommend a **small** ledger limited to the factory-built sub-families (dwellings kept in place, garden buildings, pool shells, houseboats). Core columns as in C1 (`maker_id`, `name`, `native_name`, `aliases`, `maker_class`, `country`, `place`, `founded`, `ended`, `status`, `parent_id`, `known_for_kinds`, `scene_tags`, `price_band`, `sources`). No G2 extension columns are needed. Maker classes used: kit or product manufacturer, manufacturer-retailer, builder, retailer own-label. Do not build a ledger for house builders, developers, estate agents or cemeteries (a cemetery is a place, not a maker).
- **Variant ledger (C3).** Not needed for G2. A model code (a specific lodge or shed model) would be a named form if ever admitted, and nothing here justifies the depth.

## 8. Order inside a leaf

**Canonical form first, then the secondary key the sub-family states (C7).**
- Houses: detached, semi-detached, terraced (the order of decreasing separation from neighbours, as ONS prints it), then the physically distinct forms (courtyard, cave, castle, barndominium). Named forms by region then alphabetical.
- Flats: flat, maisonette, penthouse, loft.
- Dwellings kept in place: by permanence, from least to most fixed (houseboat, tiny house on wheels, park model, static caravan, holiday lodge, park home, floating home), because the permanence of site and utilities is what the sources use to separate them (Lake Life, tinyhomesnest, Bawtry).
- Land: in the order building plot, farmland (arable, grazing, paddock, orchard, vineyard), woodland, recreation and water, holdings; kinds within a leaf alphabetical after the canonical one.
- Burial places: ground plot first, then lawn crypt, mausoleum crypt, private mausoleum, niche, green plot, communal space: PlotListings' own order of increasing price for the first seven.
- Named forms: by first-attested year where a fetched source dates the form (Wikipedia, the heritage pages), then alphabetical; maker order does not apply because named forms are not maker-bound.

Reason: the sources print canonical forms first (ONS, ACS, PlotListings) and the framework's C7 asks for it.

## 9. Boundaries

Closest cross-boundary objects and the precedence sentence for each. Sentences marked (new) are proposals for the framework, not text of v0.6.

**G1 Vehicles (the largest boundary).** Objects: houseboat, narrowboat, motorhome, touring caravan, tiny house on wheels, static caravan, horsebox, shipping container on a trailer.
- *A vessel or vehicle made to stay in one place as a dwelling is G2; a vessel or vehicle made mainly to travel is G1, including a touring caravan, a motorhome and a cruising boat used for trips.* (restates step 4 and step 11)
- *A houseboat is G2 whether or not it has an engine, because the key names it; a boat used mainly for cruising that happens to sleep people is G1.* (new; YSN's cruising houseboat is "a registered vessel ... that moves under its own power", so owner question O1.)
- *A tiny house on wheels is G2 when it is kept in one place as a dwelling, G1 when it is a trailer used to travel.* (new; tinyhomesnest says it is built to RV standards and mobility is its point, so O9.)

**C8 Building Materials & Hardware.** Objects: timber, roof panels, a porch canopy, shed kit panels, decking boards, fencing, a pergola kit.
- *A shed, cabin, pergola or gazebo sold as a kit and assembled is G2 as the building; the same timber, boards and canopies sold loose as parts of a building are C8.* (new; Dunster House sells "Porches and Canopies" as a product, which the note sends to C8.)
- *Decking and patio laid are G2; decking boards and paving stones are C8.* (key text, restated)

**C2 Garden & Grounds, E1 Toys & Games, B10 Animal Equipment, B1 Furniture & Storage.** Objects: greenhouse, polytunnel, above-ground pool, inflatable pool, treehouse, playhouse, hutch, kennel, chicken coop, stable inside a barn, pop-up gazebo, garden storage box.
- *A greenhouse and an above-ground pool are C2, an in-ground pool and a stable block a person can stand in are G2, a treehouse and a playhouse are E1, a hutch, coop or kennel is B10, and a pop-up gazebo is B1.* (restates notes to steps 4, 25, 29, 30)
- *A garden storage box that holds several objects and stands outdoors is B1; a shed a person can enter is G2.* (new)

**B7 Climate, Water & Energy.** Objects: hot tub, portable sauna, outdoor sauna cabin (Hornbach lists "Aussensauna" with garden houses), bathroom fittings.
- *A hot tub and a portable sauna are B7; a sauna cabin a person can enter and that stands as a building is G2.* (new; B7 says "portable saunas", so the line between portable and building is the owner question O7.)

**F4 Religious & Ceremonial Objects, F5 Documents.** Objects: casket, cremation urn, headstone and grave marker, burial deed, cemetery plan.
- *The plot, crypt or niche is G2; the casket, urn, headstone and marker placed in or on it are F4; the deed or certificate is F5.* (restates step 14 and step 19; ayagawa shows the Japanese "ippan-bo" is a plot plus a stone, which therefore splits into G2 and F4.)
- *A burial place is G2 however it is held (owned, leased or held on a use right), but a mooring, berth, allotment or parking permit is a right and OUT.* (restates step 1; German Reihengraeber and Japanese perpetual-care graves are use rights, so owner question O8 asks whether the "however it is held" wording covers them.)

**Rights OUT (step 1).** Objects: tenancy, timeshare week, mooring, berth, parking permit, floating-home co-op share (Lake Life: floating-home slips are "usually owned (co-op or condo)").
- *A share or membership that only grants use of a slip, pitch or space is a right and OUT; the floating home, static caravan or lodge standing on it is G2.* (new)

**Composite holdings.**
- *A holding sold as one product of land, dwelling and buildings (farm, ranch, vineyard, smallholding, lifestyle block, estate) is filed once, under Land > working and lifestyle holdings; its dwelling and buildings are attributes. A house with a garden is filed as the dwelling.* (new; O5)

## 10. Recommendation and open questions

**Recommended structure (3A).** G2 > four sub-families (Land, Dwellings, Other buildings & structures, Burial places) > leaves stated per sub-family > kinds > named forms. **Maximum depth four levels below the category.** The skip rule (C5b) applies to Burial places (about 15 to 25 kinds, so leaves are optional) and to Pools (3 kinds). No Parts-and-accessories sub-family.

**Choice level.** Attachment (houses), layout (flats), regulatory class (dwellings kept in place), use (land, outbuildings), construction method (pools), interment structure (burial). Styles, regions, tenure, size and place are attributes, named forms or views. **Tier type:** preference, with spec attributes (`alc_grade`, `build_standard`). **Maker centrality:** incidental, with a small ledger for factory-built products only.

**Attributes and tags (not levels).** `tenure` (freehold, leasehold, strata, condominium, co-op, use right), `storeys`, `floor_area`, `plot_area`, `bedrooms` (size attributes with the census standard named), `origin` (new build, conversion), `era`, `region`, `build_standard`, `alc_grade`, `propulsion` (self-propelled, towed, none), `units_in_building` (ACS bins), `capacity` and `depth` (burial), `allocation` (chosen, assigned), `perpetual_care` (yes/no). Size (floor area, plot area, rooms) and user (holiday or residential use) are attributes or tags (C6).

### Convention check (C1 to C13)

- **C1 maker ledger:** small, core schema only, factory-built sub-families (section 6). **C2 kind against named form:** every G2 named form is `maker_bound: no` (end-of-terrace, riad, Dutch barn, locker-type niche); a maker-bound form would be a product line and none is recommended. Traditional and regional types follow C2(c) and C11 (section 7, O10). **C3 variants:** none. **C4 tier and price band:** tier entries only for commercial offices if admitted (O2); spec ladders as attributes; shared bands need extending for G2 (O4). **C5 levels:** four sub-families, leaves stated per sub-family, no Parts-and-accessories sub-family because step 4's note sends every element to another category. **C6 size and user:** floor area, plot area and rooms are attributes; holiday against residential use is an attribute (build standard and site licence). **C7 order:** section 8. **C8 scene vocabulary:** section 7, each tag with a fetched definition source. **C9 alias table:** above. **C10 Rule T:** pool construction (three kinds) meets clause (b) because a fetched comparison source ranks them separately; modular, prefab, panelised and container construction are attributes of a house (Zillow lists modular and prefab as home types but they are built to the same code and set on a foundation). **C11 Rule R:** applied to cave dwelling and courtyard house (kinds) against riad, finca, cortijo, trullo (named forms). **C12 mounting:** attached or detached garage, lean-to or freestanding pergola and wall or free-standing carport are installation attributes, since the body is the same. **C13 compatibility ledger:** not needed.
