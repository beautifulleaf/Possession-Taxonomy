# Stage 2 domain review: F Culture, Records & Meaning (F1 to F6)

*Batch S2R_F. Cold, offline review (no web, no git) of memos framework/structure/F1.md to F6.md and their sources files verify/out/S2_F1_sources.tsv to S2_F6_sources.tsv, against BRIEF.md, framework/CATEGORIES_v0_6.md, framework/STAGE2_MEMO_TEMPLATE.md and the draft shared conventions C1 to C13 in framework/structure/_conventions.md. The conventions were written after these memos (F6 already applies C1 to C9), so every memo is also checked against them and the changes it needs are listed (section 3). Where the other domain reviews proposed a convention that bears on F (review C's K4 lines, K6 kits and sets, K7 parts; review E's trading-card and tarot sentences; review A's crown and prayer-bead questions), this review points to it rather than inventing a second one. This is a reviewer's judgement, not an owner decision. Severity: **high** = breaks a shared rule (BRIEF, Appendix A, a convention) or files the same object in two places across many objects; **medium** = one rule break, one contradiction or a clear split; **low** = wording, evidence or tidy-up.*

**Mechanical checks.**
- *Sources match.* F1 cites S01 to S32 with a URL key; the TSV has 32 rows in the same order. F2 uses tags (AAT-VW, SGL-PA and so on) with a URL key; the TSV has 34 rows, one per tag. F3 cites S1 to S36 (keys by name only, no URLs in the memo); the TSV has 36 rows. F4 cites S01 to S37; the TSV has 37 rows, but the key table names S34 "Dafocheng" and S35 "Kumgang" while the TSV rows are hybuddhamall.com and kkbk.shop (F4-7). F5 cites S1 to S37 with a URL key; the TSV has 37 rows. F6 cites S1 to S38 with URLs inline; the TSV has 38 rows.
- *Evidence status.* None of the six TSVs has a column for fetch status. The memos label excerpts in text: F3, F4 and F6 write "(search excerpt only)" or "(to verify)", F5 writes "[fw]" for framework-only names, F1 and F2 say they did not use unfetched pages. Partial fetches are recorded only in the TSV's free text (F3 rows 6, 11, 13, 18; F4 rows 35, 36, 37). Cross-memo finding X14 proposes a column.
- *Depth and genre.* All six memos recommend at most four levels below the category (sub-family, leaf, kind, named form). None divides by content genre: F1 keeps subject, genre and instrumentation off the tree, F3 keeps sport, league and franchise as tags, F2 keeps subject and style as views.
- *Owner interest.* Brand depth and tiers are never a level in any memo. Two memos still put a tier-like label on a kind (F1 "fidelity class", F4 "tier attributes" for casket gauge and wood), against C4(a) and C4(b). F2 has the one large area where the owner's brand-depth interest is not served: ornamental figurines and their manufactories (F2-1).
