## 3. Main decisions

### Shared conventions (CONVENTIONS_v1)
- One maker ledger serves the whole domain and allows several classes per maker, perfumer or composer among them (CV-2).
- Maker ledgers have no cap, and one fetched page admits a maker (CV-5).
- Named forms are harvested one attested row at a time in stage 3, with no cap (CV-7).
- Variants are recorded wherever a community compares them, and a variant is never a level (CV-8).
- A tier is never put on a kind: each source's tier entry is kept separately, and one fetched source admits a scheme (CV-10).
- There are at most four levels below the category, and the leaf level is optional at about 12 kinds or fewer (CV-16).
- A category that receives fitted parts closes with "Parts and accessories made for one <object>" (CV-17).
- Size is an attribute with its standard, and the user is a tag (CV-18).
- Every domain uses one scene vocabulary, and each tag waits until a page defining it is fetched (CV-22).
- Power, technology and connectivity are attributes unless the object's form changes (CV-24).
- A regional name is a kind only if sellers in two regions list it separately; otherwise it is a named form, and it is in either way (CV-27).
- Platforms go in one compatibility ledger and are never levels (CV-29).
- Legal classes go in the jurisdiction ledger, never on the tree (CV-35).
- Wine and spirit designations such as Champagne and Cognac are kinds, as a written exception (CV-40).
- Brand lines of everyday consumables are lines rows, while fragrances, cigars, teas and wines keep named forms (CV-46).

### Framework changes
Version 0.7 applied the 196 K items. Version 0.7.1 added 63 changes from the residual decisions, and v0.7.2 added R-57(d).
- A mixed set is filed by its item of greatest value, and a kit compared as one object is a kind (K-1).
- Cordless platform batteries are C1, one-device batteries follow their device, and loose standard cells are C8 (K-4).
- Empty multi-object albums are B1, and filled photo albums are F5 (K-9).
- Sealed ready-to-drink alcoholic drinks, hard seltzer included, are J7 (K-24).
- A product labelled to treat, relieve or prevent a condition is H2, and cosmetic products are H1 (K-31).
- Corrective spectacles are H2, and plano fashion frames are A4 (K-34).
- Spreads eaten with other food are J5, and dairy spreads are J3 (K-44).
- Objects are tested as made: a navigating hull is G1, and a tiny house on wheels is G2 (K-60).
- A living plant in any container is C3 (K-73).
- The animal group is B10's first axis, a written exception to Appendix A (K-102).
- A loose devotional medal is F4, a worn one A3, and a commemorative or award medal F3 (K-107).
- Helmets against weapons are E4, one-sport helmets E2, and other hazard helmets A1 (K-125).
- Edition or signed photographs are F2, and all other photographs F5 (K-139).
- Game software is divided by carrier, and the platform is an attribute (K-154).
- Hand-held chainsaws are C2, while axes and log splitters are C1 (K-173).

### Residual decisions that matter most
- A rule a memo still marked "proposed" is adopted as written (R-0).
- Every published tarot or oracle deck is an uncapped named form under its publisher (R-5).
- The key text was amended to match the decisions, making v0.7.1 (R-16).
- Kinds named with a regional word keep a provisional level until attested (R-19).
- Designers are a maker class, and named forms record "designed by" (R-24).
- An automatic fetch_status is never treated as attestation for a contested source (R-40).
- Kind ids follow the tree path and are never renumbered (R-58).
- Where a seller's material word conflicts with a technical source, the technical source wins (R-62).
- The 687 kinds the kind-level test did not reach still need one fetched page each (R-63).
- A seller's style word, such as "Oxford", is an alias, and the tree's construction test decides the kind (R-64).
- The A2, B8 and G2 definitions were tightened after the kind-level test (R-66).
- The G2 lodge is decided by its section count, and width is only an attribute (R-67).

## 4. Test results

**Round 6 (v0.6, top level).** 953 items went to 3 Sonnet placers: 99.8 % unanimous, κ 0.999, no splits, 95 of 96 gold.

**Round 7 (v0.7.2, top level).** Cold Opus checkers passed 480 of 493 regression candidates as they were. They rewrote 8 definitions, changed 1 expected home and dropped 4 duplicates. 470 new probes made 1,423 items. Of these, 1,417 were unanimous (99.6 %), none split, and κ was 0.997. Gold scored 470 of 470 for RG7 and 95 of 96 for RG2. The RG2 miss is T0036, a jar of bolognese sauce: it is J5 under the v0.6 sentence, but the old gold says OUT. Six items were non-unanimous: a bowling bag, a tricycle, an integrated dishwasher, a season ticket, a kite and a ten-class yoga card.

**Kind-level test (11 categories, 330 items, 33 Sonnet placers).**
- Before the rework, 327 of 330 items were unanimous. With item A2-07 corrected, 329 had a correct majority.
- Nine categories were unanimous and 100 % right.
- A2 had 28 of 30 unanimous (κ 0.954): the Kolhapuri sandal and the UGG Tasman each drew one stray vote.
- B8 had 29 of 30 unanimous (κ 0.977, 96.7 % right by majority): the battery emanator was misplaced.
- G2 was fully right, but its placers gave many medium-confidence ratings.
- After the Opus rework, 9 fresh placers retested A2, B8 and G2: 90 of 90 unanimous and right, κ 1.000.

**AU4 (cold Opus audit, R-69).** Of the six non-unanimous items and T0036, three came from definitions that left out the deciding fact (bowling bag, tricycle, ten-class yoga card), two from key wording (season ticket, kite), one was a placer slip (integrated dishwasher), and T0036 was an old gold home. v0.7.3 rewrites the two key sentences (the I3 entitlement note and the sport-kite note) and says that game launchers and their free accounts are OUT; the three definitions are fixed and T0036 moves to J5. v0.7.3 is not yet re-tested.
