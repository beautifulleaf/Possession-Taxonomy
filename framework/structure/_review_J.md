# Stage 2 domain review: J Groceries & Cellar (J1 to J7)

*Batch S2R_J. Cold, offline review (no web, no git) of memos framework/structure/J1.md to J7.md and their sources files verify/out/S2_J1_sources.tsv to S2_J7_sources.tsv. They are checked against BRIEF.md, framework/CATEGORIES_v0_6.md, framework/STAGE2_MEMO_TEMPLATE.md and the draft shared conventions C1 to C13 in framework/structure/_conventions.md. C14 to C17 came later, from the domain E review, and are cited only where they settle a point. The conventions were written after these memos. J1, J2, J5 and J6 say they applied C1 to C13, and J3, J4 and J7 cite some of them; section 4 lists the changes each memo still needs. The Culinary boundary is checked against the BRIEF rule (products as bought here, dishes there) and against /home/user/culinary-taxonomy/culinary/FRAMEWORK_v1.md (v1.1), read locally. Every Culinary code a memo cites was looked up there. This is a reviewer's judgement, not an owner decision. Severity: **high** = breaks a shared rule (BRIEF, the placement key, Appendix A or a convention), or files the same kind of object in two places across several memos; **medium** = one rule break, one contradiction or a clear split; **low** = wording, evidence or tidy-up. A point that rests on the reviewer's own knowledge, not on a fetched page, is marked "(reviewer knowledge, needs a fetched source)".*

**Mechanical checks.**
- *Sources match.* J1 cites S1 to S37, and its TSV has 37 rows. J2: S1 to S38, 38 rows. J3: S1 to S36, 36 rows. J4: S1 to S36, 36 rows. J5: S1 to S34, 34 rows. J6: S1 to S36, 36 rows. J7: S1 to S32, 32 rows. Every key resolves. Two J2 rows (S28 and S29, the Codex pages) returned only a header or background. The memo cites them only as a gap, but they should be marked "not usable" in the TSV.
- *Depth and rules.* All seven memos recommend at most four levels below the category, with fixed level names. None makes the maker or a tier a level. Region is not a sub-family or leaf anywhere, though J7 uses it inside some kinds (J7-1). Content genre does not apply here.
- *Storage state is handled the same way in every memo.* Fresh, chilled, frozen and live are attributes in J1, J2 and J4, and J3 does not need the field. This is the one shared concept that is already consistent. Every memo also draws the dish line the same way: coated, battered, sauced or ready-to-heat goes to Culinary (J1 P1, J2 P-P, J4 sentence 3, J5 precedence 7).
- *Regional spread of fetched sources* (from the TSV `region` column). J1 is the widest: US, JP, UK, ES, FR, DE, CN, TW, KR and EU. J3 is UK-led: 8 of 36 rows, 7 of them Waitrose pages, and one Japanese page. J7 covers US, UK, SE, FR, DE and NL, plus only the Japanese tax agency's standard: no East Asian retailer and no Italian or Spanish source. J5 and J6 have no Chinese retail tree, and J5 has none from Korea, Spain or Latin America either.
- *Excerpt labelling.* Every memo says when a list came from a search excerpt. One claim relies on an excerpt without saying so in the memo body: the Cup of Excellence score thresholds in J6 (TSV row 30 says "search excerpt gave the score thresholds"; finding J6-5).
