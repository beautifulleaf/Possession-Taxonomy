# Possession Taxonomy — Top-Level Categories v0

*Stage 1 draft (session 1, 2026-10-17). Only the top level is fixed here. Each category's inner structure is researched separately in stage 2.*

## How to read this file

**What is in scope.** Every generic type of object a person can own and keep: worn, carried, furnished, used, ridden, lived in, grown, displayed, played, collected or held as data. Plants are in. Animals are out, but the equipment for keeping them is in.

**Levels in stage 1.** Domain, then Category. Domains are a reading aid that groups neighbouring categories; they are not placement tests. **Placement is always to a category, by the placement key.**

**One axis at the top: what the object is made to do.** An object is filed by its primary designed function: what it is made to do, or to be, for its owner. It is never filed by material, value, age, maker, brand, room, setting, the user's age or gender, how it was acquired, or why the owner keeps it. Those are modifiers (Appendix A).

**Shared rules for every category.**
- **S1 One home.** Every object type has one category. Run the placement key and stop at the first *yes*.
- **S2 Generic types.** A kind is a generic type someone has one of. Models, cuts and designs are named forms under a kind. Unique individual items are out.
- **S3 Parts follow the whole.** A part, attachment or consumable that only works with one kind of object goes to that object's category: a graphics card to Computers, a lens to Cameras, guitar strings to Musical Instruments, a car seat to Vehicles. Storage, display and furniture *for* a thing are not parts: a bookcase is Furniture, a wine fridge is an Appliance.
- **S4 Modifiers never split the top level.** Region, culture, era (antique, vintage), price and quality tier, brand and maker, scene or subculture, user (baby, child, adult, gender), setting (outdoor, office, travel), occasion and motive (collecting, heirloom, gift, investment, memorabilia) are tags or views.
- **S5 Test the object, not the motive.** A signed football is a football; a collected teapot is a teapot; an antique chair is a chair.
- **S6 Attestation.** Every name added later has an attested source.

## Placement key (stop at the first *yes*)

1. **Not an ownable object?** A living animal; food or drink eaten or drunk as such; a service, membership, course, subscription or account; a right to attend or travel (ticket); money held as a balance, stored value or currency holding (bank balance, gift card value, cryptocurrency holding); a body modification (tattoo, hairstyle). **OUT** (food and drink *kept as a stock* for ageing or collecting are in: step 23).
2. Made to **treat, monitor, protect or compensate for the body's health or function**: medical devices, mobility and assistive aids (including ones installed in a home, such as a stairlift), hearing aids, corrective vision aids, first aid? **H2 Health, Medical & Mobility**
3. **Land, a building or a structure**, or something fixed into one so that it passes with it (built-in cabinets, fitted kitchen, in-ground pool, hard-wired fixtures); a vehicle or vessel made to be **lived in rather than to travel** (houseboat, static caravan, tiny house); a burial plot or timeshare? **G2 Property & Structures**
4. Exists **only as data** (domain name, game item, NFT, e-book, digital music or film library, software licence, font, digital artwork file)? **I1 Digital Goods**
5. A **living plant**, fresh-cut plant, seed or bulb? **C3 Plants**
6. A **part, attachment or consumable** that only works with one kind of object? → that object's category (S3).
7. Made for **sexual stimulation or restraint**? **H3 Adult Objects** (lingerie and other garments stay Clothing.)
8. Made to **injure, hunt or defend**, or a sword, firearm, bow or armour of any use? **E4 Weapons & Armour** (kitchen and utility knives are not weapons.)
9. Made to **carry people or goods by moving** on land, water, rail or air, under its own or human power (cars, motorcycles, bicycles, e-scooters, boats, aircraft, trailers, prams and strollers)? **G1 Vehicles**
10. Made for **keeping an animal**: housing, tack, carriers, feeding and care equipment (aquarium, saddle, dog bed)? **B8 Animal-Keeping Equipment**
11. A **powered electronic device** worn or carried (smartphone, smartwatch, fitness tracker, earbuds, e-reader)? Go to step 15. Otherwise, **worn on the body**:
    - a garment, headwear, glove, scarf, tie, belt, hosiery, uniform, costume or protective clothing? **A1 Clothing**
    - footwear? **A2 Footwear**
    - an ornament worn on the body or hair, or a watch? **A3 Jewellery & Watches**
    - a bag, luggage, wallet, case, umbrella, eyewear, hand fan, keyring or other personal accessory carried or worn? **A4 Bags & Accessories**
    (Garments and gear made for one sport, such as ski boots, cycling helmets and wetsuits, go to E2.)
12. Used **on the body for grooming, hygiene, cosmetics or fragrance**, including powered tools such as hair dryers and shavers? **H1 Personal Care & Beauty**
13. Made for **worship, ritual or ceremony** and not worn: altars, prayer beads, icons, ritual vessels and implements, shrines, festival and rite objects? **F4 Religious & Ceremonial Objects**
14. A **record or document** that certifies, records or remembers (identity papers, certificates, deeds, letters, diaries, photo albums, personal photographs, scrapbooks)? **F5 Documents & Personal Records**
15. A **powered electronic device**:
    - a computer, its components, peripherals, storage or networking? **D1 Computers & Components**
    - a phone, tablet, smartwatch, wearable or e-reader? **D2 Phones, Tablets & Wearables**
    - TV, projector, audio equipment, record player, radio, headphones, speakers, smart-home or home-security device? **D3 Audio, Video & Smart Home**
    - a camera or anything for taking pictures? **D4 Cameras & Optics** (non-powered optics too: lenses, binoculars, telescopes, microscopes)
    - a games console, handheld, controller, VR headset or arcade machine? **D5 Video Games** (video game software on any carrier goes here too)
    (Powered machines for household tasks are not here: step 24.)
16. A **musical instrument** or equipment for making, amplifying or recording music? **E3 Musical Instruments & Music Gear**
17. Made to be **looked at as a picture or sculpture**: paintings, drawings, prints and posters, photographs for display, calligraphy, tapestries and textile art, sculpture made as an artwork? **F2 Art**
18. Made to be **collected in a series** and not used otherwise: stamps, coins, banknotes, medals, tokens, bullion, non-game trading cards, pins and badges made for collecting? **F3 Collectibles**
19. A **fixed work on a physical carrier**: books, comics, magazines, maps, sheet music, records, CDs, tapes, films and discs? **F1 Media & Publications**
20. Made for **play**: toys, dolls, figures, puzzles, board, card and tabletop games (including trading card games), model railways, play equipment, gambling equipment? **E1 Toys & Games**
21. Made for a **sport, exercise or outdoor pursuit**: balls, rackets, fitness machines, camping, climbing, fishing, winter and water sports gear, and garments or protective gear made for one sport? **E2 Sports, Fitness & Outdoor**
22. Made for **making things as a craft or hobby**: sewing machines, knitting and needlework tools, yarn and fabric, art supplies, model kits, beading, scrapbooking? **C4 Craft & Art Supplies**
23. Food or drink **kept as a stock** for ageing, collecting or display (wine, spirits, sake, aged tea), tobacco and cigars, and the implements for smoking or vaping (pipes, hookahs, lighters, vapes)? **B7 Cellar, Humidor & Smoking**
24. Made for the **home**:
    - a **powered machine** (electric, gas or fuel) that does a household task: cooking, cooling, cleaning, laundry, climate, water? **B4 Household Appliances**
    - **furniture**: something to sit, lie, work at or store things in, indoors or out? **B1 Furniture**
    - **household textiles**: bedding, mattress toppers, rugs, curtains, towels, cushions, table linen? **B2 Home Textiles**
    - **cookware, kitchen tools, tableware, glassware, barware or food storage**? **B3 Kitchen & Tableware**
    - **decoration, lighting or timekeeping**: vases, ornaments, figurines, mirrors, candles, seasonal decorations, artificial and dried flowers, lamps, clocks? **B5 Decor, Lighting & Clocks**
    - **cleaning, laundry, storage, organisation or home safety** without a motor: brooms, laundry baskets, boxes, safes, locks, smoke alarms, fire extinguishers? **B6 Household Goods & Safety**
25. Made for **writing, drawing on paper or desk work**: pens, pencils, paper goods, notebooks, desk accessories, calculators, office machines? **C5 Office & Stationery**
26. Made for **growing plants or keeping grounds**: garden tools, mowers, planters, irrigation, greenhouses, compost bins? **C2 Garden & Grounds**
27. Otherwise a **tool, instrument or machine for work**: hand and power tools, workshop machines, ladders, measuring instruments, generators, farm and trade machinery? **C1 Tools & Machinery**

## Categories

### A — Worn & Carried
- **A1 Clothing** — garments and textile accessories worn on the body or head, including traditional dress, underwear, uniforms, costume and protective clothing. *Precedence:* garments made for one sport go to E2; heated or lit garments stay here.
- **A2 Footwear** — anything worn on the feet. *Precedence:* sport-specific boots and shoes (ski boots, football boots, climbing shoes) go to E2; sneakers stay here.
- **A3 Jewellery & Watches** — ornaments worn on the body, hair or clothing (rings, necklaces, body jewellery, brooches, hair ornaments, cufflinks) and watches. *Precedence:* a smartwatch is D2; a religious pendant is jewellery; prayer beads not worn as jewellery are F4.
- **A4 Bags & Accessories** — things carried or worn that are not garments, footwear or ornaments: bags, luggage, wallets, cases, umbrellas, eyewear, hand fans, walking sticks, keyrings. *Precedence:* corrective lenses as such are H2; frames and sunglasses stay here.

### B — Home
- **B1 Furniture** — pieces to sit, lie, work at, display or store things in, indoor or outdoor. *Precedence:* built-ins are G2.
- **B2 Home Textiles** — bedding, rugs and carpets, curtains, towels, cushions, table linen. *Precedence:* a rug or tapestry made as an artwork is F2.
- **B3 Kitchen & Tableware** — cookware, kitchen hand tools, tableware, cutlery, glassware, barware, tea and coffee ware without a motor, food storage. *Precedence:* powered kitchen machines are B4.
- **B4 Household Appliances** — machines powered by electricity, gas or fuel that do a household task: cooking (including grills), refrigeration, cleaning, laundry, heating, cooling, water. *Precedence:* personal-care devices are H1; sewing machines are C4.
- **B5 Decor, Lighting & Clocks** — ornaments, vases, mass-made figurines, mirrors, candles, seasonal and garden ornaments, artificial flowers, lamps and light fixtures that are not built in, clocks. *Precedence:* pictures and artworks are F2; plants are C3.
- **B6 Household Goods & Safety** — non-powered cleaning and laundry tools, storage and organising containers, home safety and security objects (safes, locks, alarms, extinguishers). *Precedence:* powered cleaners are B4; networked cameras are D3.
- **B7 Cellar, Humidor & Smoking** — drink, tea and tobacco kept as a stock, and smoking and vaping implements. *Precedence:* the drink as drink belongs to the Culinary taxonomy; wine fridges are B4; perfume is H1.
- **B8 Animal-Keeping Equipment** — housing, tack, carriers, beds, feeding and care equipment for animals (never the animals). *Precedence:* aquarium plants are C3.

### C — Making, Fixing & Growing
- **C1 Tools & Machinery** — hand tools, power tools, workshop machines, ladders, measuring and testing instruments, generators, farm and trade machinery. *Precedence:* garden tools are C2; craft tools are C4.
- **C2 Garden & Grounds** — equipment for growing plants and keeping grounds: garden hand tools, mowers, planters and pots, irrigation, greenhouses, compost bins. *Precedence:* garden furniture is B1; garden ornaments are B5.
- **C3 Plants** — living plants kept by a person (houseplants, bonsai, garden plants and trees), fresh-cut flowers, seeds and bulbs. *Precedence:* dried and artificial flowers are B5.
- **C4 Craft & Art Supplies** — tools, machines and materials for making things as a craft or hobby: sewing, knitting and needlework, painting and drawing supplies, model kits, beading, paper crafts. *Precedence:* finished models and model railways are E1.
- **C5 Office & Stationery** — writing and drawing instruments, paper goods, notebooks, desk accessories, calculators, office machines. *Precedence:* computers and printers are D1; artists' paints and brushes are C4.

### D — Electronics
- **D1 Computers & Components** — desktops, laptops, components, peripherals (including printers and 3D printers), storage, networking.
- **D2 Phones, Tablets & Wearables** — phones, tablets, e-readers, smartwatches, fitness trackers, smart glasses.
- **D3 Audio, Video & Smart Home** — TVs, projectors, hi-fi, record players, radios, headphones and earbuds, speakers, smart-home and home-security devices.
- **D4 Cameras & Optics** — cameras, lenses, photo lighting, drones made for filming, binoculars, telescopes, microscopes.
- **D5 Video Games** — consoles, handhelds, controllers, VR headsets, arcade machines, and video games on any physical carrier.

### E — Play, Sport & Music
- **E1 Toys & Games** — toys, dolls, figures, puzzles, board, card and tabletop games (including trading card games), finished models and model railways, play equipment, gambling equipment.
- **E2 Sports, Fitness & Outdoor** — equipment for sports, exercise and outdoor pursuits, and garments, footwear and protective gear made for one sport. *Precedence:* bicycles are G1; firearms and bows are E4.
- **E3 Musical Instruments & Music Gear** — instruments of every tradition, amplifiers, effects, DJ and studio equipment. *Precedence:* sheet music is F1; hi-fi for listening is D3.
- **E4 Weapons & Armour** — swords, knives made for fighting or hunting, bows, firearms, air guns, armour, martial arts weapons. *Precedence:* kitchen and utility knives are B3 and C1.

### F — Culture, Records & Meaning
- **F1 Media & Publications** — books, comics, magazines, maps, sheet music, recorded music and film on physical carriers. *Precedence:* digital copies are I1; video games are D5.
- **F2 Art** — paintings, drawings, prints and posters, photographs for display, calligraphy, tapestries and textile art, sculpture made as an artwork. *Precedence:* mass-made figurines and ornaments are B5.
- **F3 Collectibles** — objects made to be collected in a series and not used otherwise: stamps, coins, banknotes, medals, tokens, bullion, non-game trading cards, collector pins. *Precedence:* trading card games are E1; a collected object made for another use stays in that use's category (S5).
- **F4 Religious & Ceremonial Objects** — objects for worship, ritual and ceremony that are not worn: altars, shrines, icons, prayer beads, ritual vessels, festival and rite objects. *Precedence:* vestments and religious dress are A1; pendants are A3.
- **F5 Documents & Personal Records** — papers and records that certify, record or remember: identity documents, certificates, deeds, letters, diaries, photo albums and personal photographs, scrapbooks.

### G — Vehicles & Property
- **G1 Vehicles** — cars, motorcycles, bicycles and micromobility, boats, aircraft, trailers, motorhomes made to travel, prams and strollers, and their parts.
- **G2 Property & Structures** — land, houses and flats, outbuildings, fixtures fitted into them, dwellings that float or sit on wheels without travelling, burial plots, timeshares.

### H — Body
- **H1 Personal Care & Beauty** — grooming, hygiene, cosmetic and fragrance tools, devices and products.
- **H2 Health, Medical & Mobility** — medical devices and monitors, mobility and assistive aids, hearing aids, corrective vision aids, first aid.
- **H3 Adult Objects** — objects made for sexual stimulation or restraint. Clinical, descriptive register.

### I — Digital
- **I1 Digital Goods** — items that exist only as data and are owned or licensed by a person: domain names, game items and skins, NFTs, e-books, digital music and film libraries, software licences, fonts, digital artwork files. *Precedence:* accounts, subscriptions and currency holdings are OUT.

## Appendix A — Modifiers (never top-level categories)
Region and culture · era (antique, vintage, retro) · price band and quality tier · brand and maker · scene and subculture · user (baby, child, adult, gender, size) · setting (outdoor, travel, office, garden) · occasion (wedding, festival, season) · motive (collecting, heirloom, gift, investment, memorabilia, display) · material · condition · edition (limited, signed).
